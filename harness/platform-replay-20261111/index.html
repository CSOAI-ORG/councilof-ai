The standard-library script fetches small public files at fixed revisions, verifies their hashes, writes the protocol, runs the existing suite and compares process outputs. It refuses an existing output directory. No credentials, GPU or training are required by the replay.

Where Cloudflare fits

R2 retains complete artifacts; compact records reference them. Workers and Workflows could provide bounded status, verification and job coordination, but no Worker was deployed in this study. Model computation remains on a qualified compute backend. Permission revocation must not depend solely on an eventually consistent cache.

A parallel six-variant Kaggle replay was inspected for overlap and is not pooled into these counts. This is not a claim that cross-platform reproducibility is unique or that all platform workflows are complete.
