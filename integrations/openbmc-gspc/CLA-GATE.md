# OpenBMC contribution gate

OpenBMC's current contribution guide requires an applicable OpenBMC Contributor
License Agreement before contribution. It also says companies operating in the same
technical space may require a Corporate CLA.

A bounded search of the existing CSOAI/Linux Foundation evidence did **not** find an
executed OpenBMC CLA or CCLA.

CSOAI's Linux Foundation / Open Secure AI Alliance membership is separate and is not
treated as proof that the OpenBMC CLA requirement is satisfied.

Before upstream submission:
1. confirm with OpenBMC/LF which CLA type applies to CSOAI LTD;
2. execute it through the official path;
3. record acceptance/authorization;
4. introduce the design through the OpenBMC community route;
5. use Gerrit only after the contribution prerequisites are satisfied.
