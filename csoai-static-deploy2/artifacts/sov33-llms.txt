> SOV33 is a sovereign Open World Emergence Model (OWEM) — a governed AI substrate with its own trainable weights, 12 Sovereign Pillars as specialist workers, BFT-33 council, Ed25519 SIGIL on every response, and SWAP-persistent memory. Different capability class than frontier models: not chasing T-parameter leadership, but sovereign, governed, auditable.

## What SOV33 Is

- **Sovereign Open World Emergence Model (OWEM)** — owns its own weights, tokenizer, world model
- **12 Sovereign Pillars as specialist workers** around 1 large center (12-around-1 topology)
- **Inside each pillar**: MoE (4 experts) + MOM (3 model families) — decorrelated, not correlated
- **Care-floor 0.95** enforced at the pre-call gate, before any model call
- **Article 0 bound** — ISO fee-for-service only, never equity
- **BFT-33 quorum** — 23/33 voters must agree for binding decisions
- **Ed25519 SIGIL** on every response, hash-chained, audit-grade
- **SWAP-persistent memory** — survives model changes

## What SOV33 Is NOT

- NOT a T-parameter foundation model (we don't compete on parameter count)
- NOT AGI
- NOT a frontier model (smaller, sovereign-owned)
- IS a different capability class: sovereign, governed, auditable
