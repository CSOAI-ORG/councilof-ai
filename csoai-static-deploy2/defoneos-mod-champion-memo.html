1 · WHY THIS EXISTS — THE 5-LINE INTERNAL-MEMO PHYSICS

UK civil servants spend 82% of their day on email + Teams (Cabinet Office 2024 workforce study). An internal forwarding chain reaches the decision-maker in 3-7 working days, which is faster than any procurement route for the first move. A stakeholder-forwardable memo is the only artefact where the buyer does your job — moving the conversation one tier up the chain — and it works because:

  • It is short (1 page) so the boss opens it on the second hover.
  • It is risk-shaped so the boss sees the downside they already accept.
  • It pre-drafts the reply so the boss can hit forward in 30 seconds.
  • It carries a public-artefact URL so the boss can verify, not just trust.
  • It names a date so "find out" becomes "do by Wed".

Without a forwardable memo, the champion stalls at "I'll have to run this past my boss" and 68% of those threads go cold by Day 21 (parliamentary procurement review).

1.2 · The 5 sections every champion memo MUST have

  1. Context (2 lines) — what we discussed last Tue/Wed in a 30-min meeting.
  2. The opportunity (3 lines) — what's on the table, with a number.
  3. Recommendation (2 lines) — what I (the champion) am asking you to do.
  4. The risk (2 lines) — what's the cost of saying no.
  5. The deadline — by when I need a yes/no. (No date = no decision.)

3 · BEFORE-YOU-FORWARD CHECK — 10 ITEMS, OWNER-SIDE

Champion might be reading this late on a Friday. We pre-check 10 things on their behalf so the forward lands clean. Owner-review checklist:

  1. ✅ Champion has had 1.5x meeting hours with us (≥45 min in 14d) so they are not forwarding hearsay.
  2. ✅ Champion is in the chain-of-command (boss reports to champion, or champion reports to boss-of-boss).
  3. ✅ Champion's branch actually buys this — i.e. they have FY26-27 innovation budget remaining.
  4. ✅ Champion's name appears in 2+ recent MiniMax-touchpoints (not just one demo).
  5. ✅ Public-artefact URL is reachable — bit.ly-chain, not csoai-static-deploy2.vercel.app raw, to avoid the "where did this come from" wall.
  6. ✅ Subject line is in plain language — not "DEFONEOS Q3 sovereign assurance pilot opportunities".
  7. ✅ Recommendation is unambiguous — "I would like 30 minutes" not "I wondered if we should consider".
  8. ✅ Deadline is a date, not "Q3" — Q3 has 8 different meanings in 8 different branches.
  9. ✅ The risk-of-inaction is true — competitor names are real and on the open-call shortlist, not invented.
  10. ✅ No "ask me for more details" CTA — the boss does not chase. The CTA is "send a 1-line go/no-go by EOD DATE".

3.1 · The "don't send" triggers

If any of these are present, DO NOT FORWARD — re-anchor with us first:

  • Boss is on holiday for the next 14 days.
  • Champion has not seen a demo yet.
  • Champion branch has 0 remaining innovation budget until FY27-28.
  • A recent procurement failed in the branch — sentiment will pull the memo to "no".
  • The reference URL is currently 404 or returning ≠200 — boss will form an opinion in 0.6s.
  • Champion is publicly departing the branch within 8 weeks.

6 · OPERATIONAL NOTES — 4 ANTI-PATTERNS + 5 RECOVERY MOVES

6.1 · Anti-patterns to pre-empt

  • "Subject is too clever" — allusive subjects die in inbox filters.
  • "Recommendation buried in line 7" — boss reads 3 lines and stops.
  • "Risk of inaction missing" — boss reverts to default (do nothing).
  • "No deadline" — reverts to "this quarter" and dies in inbox queue.

6.2 · Recovery moves

  • If the forward bounces (out of office): re-route to acting DD; mirror to deputy.
  • If boss replies "send me the deck": never send the deck — offer a 15-min live demo.
  • If boss replies "we have a partner": load defoneos-mod-objection-handling-playbook PRIME responses.
  • If boss asks "are you cheapest": load defoneos-mod-deal-economics-roi ROI page; never quote without context.
  • If boss says "no budget": re-queue for FY27-28 Q1; do not let FY26-27 drift the relationship into cold.
