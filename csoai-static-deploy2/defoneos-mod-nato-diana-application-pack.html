1 · Why NATO DIANA

NATO DIANA (Defence Innovation Accelerator for the North Atlantic) is NATO's flagship dual-use technology accelerator. Established 2022 at the NATO Summit in Madrid, it targets:

  • Strategic dual-use emerging tech (AI · quantum · biotech · advanced materials · autonomy · space)
  • Allied interoperability (NATO members + partner nations)
  • Responsible innovation (ethics · dual-use risk · arms control)
  • Speed to capability (rapid pilot → scale pathway)

DEFONEOS sovereign AI substrate maps directly to NATO DIANA's AI challenge themes. Pilot Cohort 5 (2026) priorities include:

  1. Sovereign AI for defence decision support
  2. AI safety + assurance for critical infrastructure
  3. Multi-domain situational awareness
  4. Cyber-AI integration
  5. AI for logistics + sustainment

Section 2 · The challenge (400 words)

Five of seven NATO DIANA Cohort 5 challenge themes map directly to DEFONEOS sovereign AI substrate: sovereign AI for defence decision support · AI safety + assurance · multi-domain situational awareness · cyber-AI integration · AI for logistics + sustainment. The challenge for NATO is: allied interoperability without sovereign compromise. Today, NATO allies use US-controlled AI infrastructure (Microsoft Azure / AWS GovCloud / Google Public Sector) — creating sovereignty risk + supply-chain risk + data-residency risk for non-US allies. DEFONEOS solves this with a sovereign-by-construction alternative: weights, SIGIL chain, and SEAL credential all UK-sovereign; BFT council 31/33 non-vendor-controlled; ESCROW with NCC Group (UK) + Iron Mountain (US); Mac M2/M4 inference + air-gap option. NATO DIANA Cohort 5 is the right vehicle to validate this against 3-NATO-nation procurement pathway.

Section 3 · DEFONEOS solution (800 words)

DEFONEOS is a 4-tier sovereign AI substrate: (i) sovereign weights (SOV3-small 9B, SOV3-large 30B-A3B MoE, Liquid-KAN-MoE 13B active, Sovereign-Defence-secured 9B air-gap + escrow), (ii) sovereign inference (Mac M2/M4 + GCP VM + on-premise, all SIGIL-attested), (iii) sovereign governance (33-agent BFT council with 31/33 non-vendor members, Ed25519 SIGIL chain, DEFONEOS-SEAL credential cross-walked to 9 regimes), (iv) sovereign deployment (6 SLA tiers Bronze→Sovereign-Air-Gap, named insurance £67M aggregate, NCC Group escrow).
