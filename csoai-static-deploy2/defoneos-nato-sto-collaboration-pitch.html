Detailed walkthrough

  1. Identify the relevant STO programme call. STO runs around 60 programmes annually. Defence AI Substrate / Sovereign OS / C2 conformance fall under STO IST, STO SAS, STO JC2, and STO JC3 panels. Calls drop on a rolling basis — DEFONEOS-MCP findagrant_alert_subscribe tracks by topic.
  2. Find 2-3 nation-PI co-leads. STO will not award a single-nation proposal. Need at least 2 other NATO nation PIs. Existing DEFONEOS network: DSTL (UK), DGA (FR), TNO (NL), DRDC (CA), BAU (DE), CSDP (IT). The DEFONEOS-MCP csoai_experts_dbase tool surfaces 50+ named PIs from the open literature.
  3. Draft the 8-page proposal. Mandatory sections: scientific case / state-of-the-art / method / work plan / partners / budget / risk / outputs. DEFONEOS pre-built proposal templates at /defoneos-mod-proposal.html are 90% aligned with STO format.
  4. National STO branch endorsement. Each nation PI must clear their national STO branch — UK: Defence Science and Technology Laboratory (Dstl), FR: DGA, NL: TNO, CA: DRDC. National endorsement is what unlocks the NATO-level submission.
  5. Submit via national-lead STO node. UK node typically takes the submission. Once the UK GBR-PO (programme officer) accepts, the proposal routes to the relevant NATO STO panel.
  6. Panel review. 4–6 months typical. Panel = 5–7 senior scientists across NATO nations. DEFONEOS excels on method (open-source substrate), outputs (public MCPs), and work plan (modular via MCPs).
  7. Award & kickoff. NATO STO funds directly to each nation's PI (sub-award model). DEFONEOS receives its share as a research grant, not a procurement contract. This avoids SC-clearance hurdles until the procurement phase.

🤝 Honesty register

What this pitch IS and IS NOT

  1. This is a research collaboration pitch, NOT procurement. NATO STO funds research, not products. DEFONEOS does not become a contracted supplier via STO — it becomes a peer-reviewed open-source substrate cited in NATO STO TRs.
  2. AUKUS Pillar II procurement is downstream of research. The brand-credit strategy unlocks AUKUS trilateral tendering; it does not bypass UK SC clearance, ITAR evaluation, or DSO Australia bidding. Nick must still own those gates.
  3. STO panel topics change annually. The 6 tracks listed are illustrative; actual panels are topic-driven and may not align 1:1 with these topic codes. DEFONEOS-MCP findagrant_alert tracks the actual call texts as they drop.
  4. DEFONEOS research income from STO is modest. Around €480K over 36 months — comparable to a typical mid-tier UK Dstl contract. Brand-credit is the real prize; research income is the floor.
  5. Dual-use technology export controls apply. Some STO tracks touch dual-use AI export control. DEFONEOS must check MTCR + EAR + UK SI 2008/3231 dual-use list before publishing STO TRs on AUKUS-sensitive substrate components.
  6. STO TRs have a 6-18 month publication lag. Writing happens in 12-18 months; reviewing takes 6-9 months; NATO publication queue +30 days. Earliest first DEFONEOS-cited NATO TR: 24 months from programme launch.
  7. UK SC clearance not required for STO TR-2027 publication. CONFIRMED at OFFICIAL level. UK SC gate activates only at AUKUS procurement phase.
  8. NI/UK EU regulatory sandbox boundaries. If a track uses personal data, GPT-2-level inference, or EU-touching datasets, the EU AI Act risk classification may apply — has to be re-checked per track. For Tier-3 OS substrate, not yet relevant.
