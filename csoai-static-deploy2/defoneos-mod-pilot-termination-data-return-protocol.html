1 · Why this protocol exists

Every DEFONEOS pilot reaches Day 90 with one of three outcomes:

  1. Award: buyer signs the MSA + 12-month contract; pilot transitions to production.
  2. Transition: buyer chooses a different vendor; DEFONEOS returns all buyer artefacts and deletes pilot-specific data.
  3. Terminate without follow-on: buyer ends the pilot with no contract; same data return + deletion.

Outcomes (2) and (3) require a precise, auditable closeout. The buyer needs assurance that:

  • All their data is returned in a portable, documented format.
  • All pilot-specific model weights / adapters / preferences are deleted.
  • All inference logs are deleted.
  • The deletion is verified, SIGIL-anchored, and BFT-council-approved.
  • If Sovereign / Sovereign-Air-Gap tier, source code + weights in escrow are verified.
  • 30 days of read-only post-pilot support is available to the buyer.

This protocol is the contractual reference. It is invoked automatically on any pilot termination event; the 12 steps run sequentially with named owners and 24-hour SLAs per step.

5 · Escrow verification (Sovereign tiers only)

For Sovereign / Sovereign-Air-Gap pilots, step 10 verifies that the buyer's source code + weights remain in escrow:

  1. NCC Group (UK escrow agent) confirms deposit integrity via SHA-256 hash check.
  2. NCC Group confirms the canary release test passes.
  3. DEFONEOS provides a SIGIL-anchored escrow verification receipt to buyer.
  4. Buyer may (with NCC Group's permission) attend the verification.

If verification fails, DEFONEOS has 5 business days to remediate. Failure to remediate triggers buyer rights under the escrow agreement (release of source code + weights to buyer).

6 · The 30-day post-pilot support window

After closeout (§12), buyer has 30 days of read-only access to:

  • All 6 returned artefacts (re-download via signed URL, max 3 attempts)
  • Email support (24h response, business hours)
  • 1 hour of named-TAM consultation (Platinum / Sovereign only)
  • The post-pilot report walkthrough call (60 min, with VP Customer Success)

No new inference, no new data, no new training. Read-only. After 30 days, the buyer-side tenant is permanently archived (read-only, no support) for 7 years (HMRC retention), then permanently deleted.

7 · The 90-day renewal window

After termination (any outcome except award), the buyer has 90 days to invoke the renewal window:

  • Sign the production MSA at the preferred-vendor pricing (last quoted before termination)
  • Re-deploy within 30 days of MSA signature (data retention preserved if same tenant ID)
  • Pilot artefacts retained and re-used (with buyer consent)

After 90 days, preferred-vendor pricing expires and the buyer must re-quote.

8 · BFT council audit closeout (the §9 vote)

The 33-agent BFT council votes on the closeout at step 9. The vote confirms:

  1. All buyer data deleted (steps 6, 7, 8 verified)
  2. All pilot-specific weights / adapters deleted
  3. Buyer artefact pack delivered (step 5 verified)
  4. SLA performance during pilot attested (§7 above)
  5. No outstanding buyer disputes

The vote requires ≥23/33 quorum. If approved, the council signs a single SIGIL-anchored closeout certificate. If rejected, closeout pauses until the rejection reason is remediated.

11 · The 5 Anti-Patterns (Pilot Closeout Disasters We Refuse)

  1. No "we'll get to it next quarter." Closeout is 5 business days. No extensions without BFT vote.
  2. No "we keep data for analytics." Pilot data is deleted at closeout, period. Re-deployment requires new consent.
  3. No "buyer can't verify deletion." Empty-volume attestation + SIGIL receipt + optional DPO witnessing.
  4. No "escrow is symbolic." Escrow verification is mandatory at closeout for Sovereign tiers.
  5. No "no BFT vote." §9 vote is mandatory; no closeout without 23/33 quorum.
