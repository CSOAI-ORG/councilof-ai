2 · The 7-Filter Automated Bid/No-Bid Screen

Every tender passes through these 7 filters. Score 1 (pass) or 0 (fail) — pass 5-of-7 to enter BFT review.

F1 · Sovereign fit

Does the buyer require UK/EU sovereign AI or is "sovereign" a recognised discriminator in the tender text? Pass = explicit sovereign clause or named UK supplier bias.

F2 · Compliance gating done

Have we filed the compliance gate (DSP registration / Cyber Essentials / SC clearance) by the bid submission date? Pass = filed and pending assessment, not necessarily awarded.

F3 · Article 50 / EU AI Act risk

Does the deliverable avoid the Article 50 transparency obligations, the EU AI Act high-risk classification, or the ISO 42001 conditional clauses? Pass = no exposure or covered.

F4 · BFT 33-agent feasible vote

Can we convene a 23-agent BFT quorum and reach ≥80% approve? Pass = preliminary poll indicates feasible quorum.

F5 · Reference-able past performance

Can we cite at least one named-buyer SIGIL-anchored reference in the 30 days before submission? Pass = yes.

F6 · Pricing defensible

Is our priced bid within ±15% of the HM-Treasury Green Book costed benchmark for the work? Pass = within range.

F7 · Owner-gate resolvable

Can the bid be submitted without a Nick-required personal/credential action that is unfulfilled? Pass = no blocking human gate.

3 · Friday 16:00 BST BFT Council Review

Cadence

  • 16:00 BST every Friday — 30-min synchronous session, 33-agent BFT council convened programmatically via sovereign substrate.
  • 23-agent quorum required (out of 33) for any bid/no-bid decision to be binding.
  • Vote options: APPROVE (proceed to bid prep), AMEND (proceed with named conditions), REJECT (skip this cycle), ABSTAIN.
  • Vote tally SIGIL-sealed as T93-bft-{tender}-vote-{digest} — immutable receipt archived to /csoai/org/bft/t93/.

Inputs the council sees

  • 7-filter bid/no-bid scorecard (auto-generated).
  • Reference portfolio (any SIGIL-anchored past performance in past 30 days).
  • Pricing benchmark vs HM-Treasury Green Book costed scenario.
  • Owner-gate ledger (which human gates remain unresolved).
  • 3 named risk callouts per tender (legal / commercial / technical).

Outputs

  • Decision receipt — JSON with vote tally + named dissents + amend list.
  • Bid prep checklist — auto-generated 12-row checklist for the named owner.
  • Calendar update — T-21 / T-14 / T-7 / T-1 milestones pushed to owner calendar.
  • Friday report — appended to defoneos-mod-board-update evidence chain.
