§9 The anti-fabrication doctrine

House Rule 1 (Tier A). Every live number on the public estate must be probed at most 24 hours before the public claim is made. Probing lives in tools/audit and is on a 30-minute cron.

House Rule 2 (Tier B). Static numbers (signed byte counts, OSCAL component counts, charter universe coverage) are re-audited nightly and the result is appended to DEFONEOS_SPRINT_STATE.json.

House Rule 3 (Tier C). Targets, models, and outreach status are declared not published. They appear here with the Tier-C badge so no downstream reader mistakes a target for an outcome.

House Rule 4 (Anti-fabrication). No claim may appear on any page that does not also appear on this register. The deploy-gate enforces this: if grep finds a number not on this register, the build halts.

House Rule 5 (Repair). When a claim is found wrong, the page is reverted within 60 minutes and a SIGIL receipt is emitted with op=R (repair). The repair appears in the changelog below.
