1. Purpose & Scope

The Sovereign Evidence Presentation Pack (SEPP) is the canonical artefact bundle that DEFONEOS presents to any Tier-1 procurement evaluator — MOD, NATO, GCHQ, NHS, FCDO, AUKUS partners. It is the single source of truth for the question "is DEFONEOS real, sovereign, and trustworthy?".
