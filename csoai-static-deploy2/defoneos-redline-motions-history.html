🔁 Red-Line Process Diagram

Red-line motion lifecycle (canonical):

  1. Detection         — red-line vector scanned across 30 MCPs + 569 pages
  2. Motion drafted     — R3 (Compliance) or R5 (Public) sponsors
  3. Quorum check       — 27/33 supermajority required
  4. Ring-distinct gate — R2≥4/6, R3≥5/8, R4≥6/12, R5≥4/6
  5. Amendments         — any ring may amend; rolled into text
  6. Final vote         — recorded with Ed25519 sig + BLAKE3 anchor
  7. Owner cosign       — R1 signs for SEAL-eligibility
  8. Public minutes     — deployed to /defoneos-bft-council-public-minutes.html
  9. SBOM refresh       — if motion changes SBOM category, sbom-index refreshes
 10. SIGIL anchor       — motion nonce appended to SIGIL stream
