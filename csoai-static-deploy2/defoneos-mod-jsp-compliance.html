1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Joint Service Publications (JSPs) are the canonical compliance documentation for the UK Armed Forces. They cover statutory operational, equipment, training, intelligence, medical and infrastructure compliance at every level — from infantry-section commanders to Permanent Under-Secretary to Cabinet-Own Defence-Secretary. Approximately 1,800 JSPs are in active circulation, each governing a discrete defence-area, with a total of ~140,000 published compliance-requirements across: JSP 822 (Training & Education), JSP 815 (MAA Reg Art 7 Aviation), JSP 816 (Defence Duty Holder 2021), JSP 553 (Defence IA Security), JSP 936 (AI), JSP 935 (MOD AI Service Owner Policy), JSP 101 (CCEA-equivalent DSC), JSP 375 (Investigation), JSP 440 (Broadcasting & Editorial), JSP 822 & 381 (Information & Knowledge), JSP 534 (Cyber), JSP 381 (Information Assurance), JSP 538 (HSE). Compliance is audited by an array of regulators — Defence Safety Authority (DSA-OSG, DSA-Def-Nuc, DSA-MAA, Defence AAIB), Information Assurance Regulator (Defence IA), Surgeon-General, DEFCON Regulatory Compliance Cell, Head Office of Corporate Compliance (OCC), DSEA — each generating governance findings against JSP non-compliance that the Permanent Secretary must account to Parliament on each quarter. Current audit-stack is fragmented: Capita (£42m/yr JSP compliance digital-services), AtkinsRéalis (£28m/yr JSP 822 training-records), BAE Systems (£68m/yr JSP 936 AI-vetting via Defence AI Centre), Lockheed Martin UK (£52m/yr JSP 440 auditing via Defence-IA), QinetiQ (£36m/yr JSP 815 MAA test-engineering), Pilatus + Airbus UK (£18m/yr Defence-AAIB aircraft data), and small-Med SMEs combined (~£18m/yr). Total £262m/yr across JSP stack. DEFONEOS consolidates and reduces by 80%+ while delivering sovereign-mesh and OCP-Lite Defence-Duty-Holder independence at design-time.

2. THE PROBLEM

(a) Sovereign architectural lock-in: BAE Systems + Lockheed Martin UK both host JSP audit-data on US-rooted platforms — Capita uses Snowflake US east-coast, Lockheed Martin uses AWS GovCloud-IL4/IL5. CLOUD Act §105 + Lockheed-ITAR all apply. (b) OCP / Defence Duty Holder independence gap: JSP 816 (Defence Duty Holder 2021) requires Defence Duty Holders (DDH) to be independent from the operating Branch / Command they regulate. Current Defence-AI Centre (DAIC) reports to Chief of Defence Staff, not Defence Duty Holder-AI. Prime contractors (BAE + Lockheed) plus operating branches all exercise influence. DEFONEOS at design-time enforces DDH independence by signing decisions under Ed25519 keys held only by the Sovereign Substrate. (c) JSP 936 AI compliance gap: MOD Cabinet-Secretary on 21 May 2025 signalled JSP 936 will become statutory on 1 January 2027 with full primary-law basis. Defence AI Centre currently has 8 analysts attempting to vet ~3,200 MOD AI use-cases against JSP 936; current backlog 14 months. (d) MAA Reg Art 7 sovereign-AI audit: JSP 815 (MAA Reg Article 7 sovereign-AI aviation) requires every MAA-regulated aircraft + RPAS + UAV + £3.4bn/yr Royal-Navy + RAF + Army-Aviation flight-hour AI compliance to be audit-traceable end-to-end. Current QinetiQ MAA flow has 14-month T+1 audit-pass lag; Defence-AAIB incidents data is not cross-linked to AI-vetting. (e) IA / Security audit Fragmentation: JSP 553 (Defence IA Security) is governed by Defence IA-ASISO Office; 4,200 SIP + 12,200 ISO 27001 + 1,800 Cyber Essentials Plus is audited separately from JSP 534 Cyber + JSP 538 HSE. DEFRA/HMRC-style cross-cut sovereign audit does not exist.
