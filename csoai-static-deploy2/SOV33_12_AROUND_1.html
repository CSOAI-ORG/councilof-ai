Why 12-around-1 + MoE + MOM

3 wins from this design

  1. 12 specialists in parallel — each pillar tuned to one principle. Faster than 1 generalist trying to handle all principles at once.
  2. MoE inside each pillar — 4 experts (small-fast, small-deep, large-fast, large-deep) vote internally. Best of both small + large.
  3. MOM inside each pillar — 3 model families (Qwen, Llama, Mistral) for decorrelation. ρ=0.102 measured.

Plus PDCA: Plan (SOV33cubed analyzes), Do (12 pillars work in parallel), Check (BFT-33 votes), Act (SOV33cubed synthesizes + SIGIL-signs).
