🥁 Drum + Harmony

All 12 layers beat in sync (drum_beat cycles 0-1). Harmony = 1.0 - (max_velocity - min_velocity). When harmony is high, all layers are aligned. When it drops, the pyramid self-corrects via Venturi.

  • Drum beat: shared cycle signal (orchestrator pulses every layer)
  • Harmony: how aligned velocities are (1.0 = perfect)
  • Venturi: demand-based flow control (high demand = faster)
