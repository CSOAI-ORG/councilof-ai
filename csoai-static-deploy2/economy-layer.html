⚡ x402 — Per-Outcome Invoicing

Coinbase x402 · HTTP 402 Payment Required · per-action billing

SOV3 issues an x402 invoice for every paid outcome. Not per-seat, not per-token — per verified, SIGIL-signed outcome.

When you call POST /api/x402_invoice, SOV3 returns a JSON invoice priced by service + tier + quantity. Pay via Stripe, crypto, or x402 wallet — and you get a SIGIL-signed receipt hash-chained into the public ledger.

🧠 Wisdom Economy — Gimification

Latin: ludus + sapientia · earn for sovereign actions

Every sovereign action earns wisdom points — game-theoretic rewards for contributing to the network's collective intelligence and integrity.

Gimification — Latin ludus (game) + sapientia (wisdom). Where gamification doles out dopamine hits, gimification pays in cumulative, transferable wisdom that ranks you on the public leaderboard and unlocks Pro-tier features.

🎯 How You Earn Wisdom

📐 Earning Formula

wisdom_earned = base_reward × care_multiplier × rarity_bonus × streak_factor

  • base_reward — set per action (10 to 500 pts)
  • care_multiplier — 1.0× to 3.0×, scaled by your care-weighted impact score
  • rarity_bonus — 1.0× to 5.0×, higher for first-of-kind contributions
  • streak_factor — daily login + activity streak, 1.0× to 2.5× over 30 days

💸 Wisdom Transfer — P2P Economy

peer-to-peer · audit-trailed · instant

Transfer wisdom points between users. Each transfer emits a SIGIL receipt with from, to, amount, reason, prior_hash — hash-chained and publicly verifiable.

🎁 Why Transfer?

  • → Tip a contributor for a useful dataset or training module

  • → Settle a service — pay another user for i-character lease, TwinStore purchase, or wisdom consulting

buy · lease · rent · sovereign digital twins

The first marketplace for sovereign-grade digital twins. Every i-character ships with explicit consent, training provenance, and revocable commercial rights.

TwinStore is the only marketplace where the person the digital twin represents has explicitly opted in to commercial use, set the price, and can revoke at any time. Every sale emits a SIGIL receipt + consent receipt.
