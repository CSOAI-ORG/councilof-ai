5 · The 12 red-line protections (negotiated at every consortium)

  1. IP ownership — DEFONEOS retains 100% of DEFONEOS-developed IP (weights, MCPs, sovereign substrate)
  2. SEAL ownership — DEFONEOS retains 100% of DEFONEOS-SEAL rights (prime cannot re-issue SEALs)
  3. Data sovereignty — buyer data remains in DEFONEOS-controlled tenant; prime has no direct access
  4. Model weights sovereignty — DEFONEOS sovereign weights remain in DEFONEOS escrow (NCC Group); prime has no direct access
  5. Pricing floor — DEFONEOS pricing floor for sub-work: 75% of standalone equivalent (no race-to-bottom)
  6. SIGIL chain integrity — DEFONEOS SIGIL chain remains DEFONEOS-controlled; prime cannot modify or substitute
  7. Sovereign ownership — DEFONEOS Ltd UK 16939677 remains UK-sovereign; no forced sale or JV dilution
  8. No exclusive lock-in — DEFONEOS retains right to work with other primes on other bids
  9. Liability ring-fence — DEFONEOS liability limited to sub-work scope; no liability for prime's separate work
  10. Transparency — consortium agreement + key terms public on csoai.org/partners/ (with commercial redactions)
  11. Exit rights — DEFONEOS may exit on 90-day notice + good-cause (defined in agreement); no perpetual lock-in
  12. Red-line scope — DEFONEOS will NOT participate in bids for kinetic / autonomous lethal / personal surveillance (regardless of prime pressure)

7 · The 5 prime-engagement red flags (refuse the partnership)

  1. Prime refuses to sign 12 red-line protections (§5) — sovereign compromise unacceptable
  2. Prime demands exclusive lock-in beyond 3 years — sovereign independence preserved
  3. Prime demands DEFONEOS share sovereign weights or SEAL control — non-negotiable
  4. Prime bid scope violates DEFONEOS red lines (kinetic / autonomous lethal / personal surveillance) — refuse regardless of value
  5. Prime demands transparency waiver (silent consortium) — public accountability required

8 · The 33-agent BFT council approval workflow

  1. VP Commercial prepares consortium proposal (§2 structure + §4 stage 2 qualification memo + §5 red-line assessment + §7 red-flag check)
  2. Proposal reviewed by GC for legal red lines
  3. Proposal posted to BFT council at csoai.org/bft/consortium/{proposal_id}
  4. 5 BD open comment period
  5. BFT vote: ≥23/33 quorum required to approve
  6. If approved, signed consortium agreement stored at csoai.org/partners/agreements/{agreement_id}
  7. Public summary published on csoai.org/partners/
  8. Quarterly partner review thereafter (§4 stage 5)

10 · The 5 Anti-Patterns (Consortium Disasters We Refuse)

  1. No "yes to everything the prime wants." 12 red lines are non-negotiable.
  2. No "exclusive lock-in." DEFONEOS works with multiple primes.
  3. No "scope compromise." Kinetic / autonomous lethal / personal surveillance = always refused.
  4. No "silent consortium." Public summary always.
  5. No "consortium without BFT vote." ≥23/33 mandatory.
