🌍 NATO DIANA Overview

NATO DIANA (Defence Innovation Accelerator) is NATO's flagship programme for sourcing dual-use deep-tech innovation from Allied nations. It runs themed Challenge Calls where startups and SMEs propose solutions to specific NATO security challenges. Successful applicants receive funding, access to NATO test centres, and a pathway into the NATO procurement pipeline.

Primary Challenge Bid: AI-Enabled Decision Making

DEFONEOS directly addresses NATO's need for sovereign, auditable AI-driven decision-making in contested environments. The platform provides:

  • Multi-source intelligence fusion: 30 data sources (satellite, AIS, air quality, OSINT, IoT, drone feeds) unified into a single 3D Common Operating Picture
  • Explainable AI governance: Every AI decision is logged via SIGIL Ed25519 hash-chain, reviewed by a 33-agent BFT council, and auditable against JSP 936 / EU AI Act
  • Edge deployment: Full platform runs in a single Docker compose — deployable to forward operating bases with limited connectivity
  • AUKUS-compatible sovereignty: UK-sovereign by design, designed for AUKUS Pillar 2 (AI & Autonomy) interoperability
