6. Financials (DASA-audit format)

DASA expects a 2-page cost detail deck. Format requirements:

  • Daily rate per role (£450 lead eng / £350 researcher / £300 dev — band at 2025 UK defence day rates)
  • HDRC markup = 0% (CSOAI Ltd is sole trader-style PSC, no overhead recovery beyond 8.4% audit-grade accounting)
  • Materials clearly split into CAPEX (one-off on-prem HW) vs OPEX (sovereign-cloud subscription)
  • Sub-contracting capped at 30% per DASA policy — all 5 subs below the cap
  • Currency = GBP throughout; if dual-funded via AUKUS, AUD/USD reconciliation at HM Treasury FX rate

7. Submit — and wait

The submission button on the DASA portal writes:

  • One signed .pdf of the proposal
  • One signed .pdf of the cost detail
  • Four signed compliance .pdfs
  • One signed consortium agreement (if multi-supplier)
  • PSC + named ULO declaration

Average DASA review time on a Tier-1 EOI: 4 weeks. Tier-1 panel shortlist: ~6 weeks. Funding decision: 10–14 weeks. Earliest realistic contract start: 14–16 weeks after submission.
