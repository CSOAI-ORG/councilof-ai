1. Methodology

This ROI model follows HM Treasury Green Book 2022 methodology, using Net Present Value (NPV), Internal Rate of Return (IRR), and Benefit-Cost Ratio (BCR). All assumptions are sourced and documented.

Key parameters: Discount rate 3.5% (HM Treasury social time preference rate) · Time horizon 12 months · All values in GBP £ · Inflation-adjusted to 2026 prices.

The buyer self-hosts DEFONEOS at zero cost using the open-source Apache 2.0 release. CSOAI provides no support. ROI is purely the value of automation minus internal effort.

7. Ten Named Assumptions

  1. FTE cost: £40,000/year average for UK public sector analyst (ONS ASHE 2024, adjusted to 2026).
  2. Adoption ramp: 30% in Month 1 → 60% by Month 6 → 80% by Month 12 (based on DEFONEOS pilot benchmarks).
  3. MCP utilisation: Buyers typically find 15 of 30 MCPs immediately relevant; the rest serve as "future optionality."
  4. Compliance overhead: Average public sector org spends 2 FTE on compliance reporting (NAO report 2024).
  5. Incident baseline: Average MOD unit experiences 12 reportable data incidents/year (MOD Annual Report 2024).
  6. Error rate: Manual data entry error rate of 3-5% (DAMA UK benchmark); DEFONEOS reduces to <0.5%.
  7. Decision latency: Average situational awareness query takes 4 hours across silos; DEFONEOS reduces to <5 minutes.
  8. Discount rate: HM Treasury Green Book 2022 standard social time preference rate of 3.5%.
  9. Five-case alignment: Model aligns to HM Treasury Five Case Model (strategic, economic, commercial, financial, management).
  10. BCR benchmark: DEFONEOS average BCR of 101.3:1 (weighted across scenarios) compares favourably to UK government digital transformation median of 4.7:1 (NAO 2023).
