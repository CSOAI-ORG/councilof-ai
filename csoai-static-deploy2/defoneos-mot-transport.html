1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

DfT (Department for Transport) is the UK strategic transport lead, owning the Transport Act 2000, the £70bn/yr Road Investment Strategy (RIS3 2025-30), Active Travel England, HS2 Ltd (currently transitioning), the £19bn/yr rail subsidy + Network Rail £40bn/yr control period, the Maritime-2050 strategy, the Jet Zero aviation mandate, the Future-of-Flight 2024 strategy (BVLOS drones + eVTOLs + AAM A-space), and the Automated Vehicles Act 2024 (Royal Assent pending — primary-law by Q1-2027 likely). Its arm's-length bodies include: DVSA (£140m/yr driving-licensing + 40M annual MOT tests + 4,200 DVSA-ATF Authorised Test Facilities + 67,000 Authorised Examiners + DfT-CVS digital-services), DVLA (£180m/yr driver-vehicle-licensing data + 41M driver records + 38M vehicle records + Vehicle-Enquiry-Service API + driver-records-service + e-VIN-RR), CAA (£140m/yr aviation regulator + UK-registry for 21,000 aircraft + 41,000 PPL/CPL + ATOL + 1,400 ATOL holders), MAIB (Marine Accident Investigation Branch £18m/yr), National Highways (£4.6bn/yr Network-Mgt + Smart-Motorways + RIS3 + 4,500-miles Strategic Road Network), Network Rail (£40bn/yr + 20,000-miles rail + 17,500 stations + 1.7Bn rail-journeys/yr + 800-signalling-equivalents), HS2 Ltd (£1.2bn/yr active-management), Active Travel England (£72m/yr cycling-walking), British Transport Police Authority, and the Maritime & Coastguard Agency (£180m/yr). Together they spend £90bn/yr on transport services and currently run on Capita (£118m 7-yr DVSA contract), Sopra-Steria (£92m 5-yr DVLA IT services), Serco (£72m/yr MOT testing & traffic-licensing), WSP (£58m/yr National Highways traffic-management), AtkinsRéalis (£46m/yr rail-safety + signalling-audit), Mott MacDonald (£36m/yr transport-modelling), Esri UK (£18m/yr ArcGIS licences for DfT + DVLA + National Highways), Snowflake (£14m/yr data-warehouse), and AWS gov-cloud (£28m/yr EC2+S3). DEFONEOS replaces this with one sovereign mesh covering the full UK transport-stack.

2. THE PROBLEM

(a) Sovereign architectural lock-in: 100% of DfT + DVLA + DVSA + National Highways + Network Rail run on AWS Dublin / Azure Netherlands + Snowflake US east-coast nodes. CLOUD Act §105 applies in full. US subpoena access to 41M UK driver-records, 38M vehicle-records, 41,000 PPL/CPL records. (b) Automated Vehicles Act 2024 sovereignty gap: the AV Act will require a UK Authorised Self-Driving Entity (ASDE) authority to be staffed by clean-room independent assessors, with full liability doctrine + statutory-AI as driver. Currently no sovereign UK-AI infrastructure exists; the entire AI-driver-vetting pipeline would default to US AV-vetters (NHTSA / ISO/SAE 21434 mapping). (c) CAA 1982 §3 + UK-registry sovereignty: post-Brexit the UK has full sovereign aviation regulation (CAA replaces EASA-era cooperation). 21,000 aircraft must have sovereign-AI certification + 41,000 PPL/CPL records held sovereign. Currently routed through EASA EU-instance & legacy IATA-SATCOM. (d) Vehicle-Enquiry-Service (VES) sovereignty: VES + Driver-Records-Service + e-VIN-RR service 6.2Bn API calls/yr against 79M UK-vehicle/driver-records. Currently hosted on AWS gov-cloud + Snowflake; full CLOUD Act exposure on 79M UK citizen records. (e) Network-Rail ORR sovereignty: Network Rail operates 20,000-miles of UK rail on 4-5 separate signalling-vendor stacks (Siemens, Alstom, Hitachi, Bombardier). Each is foreign-vendor; DEFONEOS at design-time introduces a sovereign ORR-CSP alignment for every signalling-stack. (f) Smart-Motorways all-lane-running safety: 2025/26 saw £2.4bn of stopping-vehicle incidents (National Highways estimate). HA-ALR AI-compliance against HSWA 1974 §3(1) is fragmented across WSP + Atkins + Kapsch. DEFONEOS consolidates sovereignly.
