1 · Why this pack exists

DASA (Defence & Security Accelerator) runs thematic Open Calls to fund innovative defence AI / cyber / data science projects. The Open Call: AI for Defence (2026 Q3) targets sovereign AI capabilities aligned with MOD's "Defence AI Strategy 2025-2030" priorities:

  • Frontier AI safety + assurance
  • Sovereign inference infrastructure
  • Multi-domain decision support
  • Cyber-AI integration
  • AI for logistics + sustainment

DEFONEOS's sovereign weight family (SOV3-small, SOV3-large, Liquid-KAN-MoE) maps directly to 5 of 7 Open Call themes. This pack is the reusable author template for any of the 5 themes.

  1. Day 1 — Theme selection + buyer sponsor confirmed (5 themes above; DEFONEOS picks 2-3 per quarter based on capacity)

  3. Day 4-5 — Sections 4-6 drafts (sovereignty + delivery + team)
  4. Day 6-7 — Sections 7-9 drafts (cost + risk + compliance)

  6. Day 9-10 — 33-agent BFT council pre-vote (≥23/33)
  7. Day 11 — Final revisions + SIGIL-anchored submission receipt
  8. Day 12 — Submit to gov.uk portal with DEFONEOS UK Ltd UK 16939677 entity + bid pack ZIP
  9. Day 13-14 — Acknowledgement + tracking number logged in DEFONEOS Vault

10 · The 5 Anti-Patterns (DASA Bid Disasters We Refuse)

  1. No "submit without BFT vote." ≥23/33 mandatory pre-submission.
  2. No "vague Annex Q coverage." All 15 controls addressed with named evidence.
  3. No "DSP SC2 incomplete." 9-of-9 required before submission (gate 3 dependency).
  4. No "trust the buyer to fill gaps." Every section complete before BFT vote.
  5. No "skip the debrief." Decline outcomes get a requested debrief to inform next cycle.
