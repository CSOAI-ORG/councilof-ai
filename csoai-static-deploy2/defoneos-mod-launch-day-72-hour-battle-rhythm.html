5. The 3 Hard Abort Criteria

  1. Tech RED: any P0 surface 5xx or 30x-loop for >30 consecutive minutes during T-24 → T-4. → HALT + 33-agent BFT emergency vote (≥25 continue required within 2h).
  2. Press walkback: >3 of 30 embargoed journalists signal walkback or pull coverage during T-12 → T-4. → HALT + OWNER personal call to each walkback.
  3. Adverse legal: external counsel flags any outbound text as legally unsafe at T-2h. → HALT + emergency counsel review + rewrite before T-1h.
