Witness Rotation Policy

Witnesses in Rings 2, 3, 4, and 5 undergo regular rotation, ensuring a dynamic and resilient council. Ring 1 (Owner) is always present due to its foundational role. The rotation schedule is as follows:

  • Ring 1 (Owner): Always Present.
  • Ring 2 (Defence Primes): Quarterly Rotation.
  • Ring 3 (Compliance & Legal): Quarterly Rotation.
  • Ring 4 (Operators & Engineers): Monthly Rotation.
  • Ring 5 (Public & Research): Semi-Annual Rotation.

The selection of new witnesses is a deterministic process, seeded by a combination of the global SIGIL digest and a motion nonce, ensuring verifiability and preventing manipulation. A reference Python VRF implementation (libsodium + BLAKE3) is publicly available for audit.
