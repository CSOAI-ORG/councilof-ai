DEFONEOS · Defence-as-a-Platform (DaaP) — Sovereign Substrate Strategy

Why DEFONEOS wins under the UK's Defence-as-a-Platform doctrine · 10 Jul 2026 · Day-10 GTM_PREP expansion pack
Strategy: DEFONEOS is positioned as the most complete, most auditable, most sovereign Defence-as-a-Platform (DaaP) middleware in the UK — filling the gap between unproven open-source prototypes (Berkeley Sky Computing, Hydra, Anarchy) and expensive US primes (Palantir Gotham, Anduril Lattice, Helsing LLM).

1 · What is Defence-as-a-Platform?

DaaP is the UK Ministry of Defence's 2024 doctrine committing to buy platforms not products. Defined in the Strategic Defence Review 2025 (SDR25) and operationalised across DE&S and UKStratCom. Core tenets:

  1. Open interfaces, not monoliths. Every procurement must use open APIs / open data formats — no proprietary lock-in.
  2. Data is the platform, not the application. Single canonical data layer (OSCAL + STANAG + JAXB); every tool consumes it.
  3. Compliance as code, not as paper. JSP 936, DEFSTANs, NCSC CCP — automated test, not manual audit.
  4. Continuous integration into the Warfighting Edge. Code in prod every sprint; don't wait for v2.0.
  5. UK sovereign by construction. UK Ltd company, UK data centres, UK cleared staff.

6 · DEFONEOS Wins the DaaP Doctrine: 6 Reasons

  1. Open by default. MIT licence = no vendor lock-in. Public OSCAL. Public STANAG mappings. Public compliance evidence.
  2. UK sovereign at construction. UK Ltd company, UK data centres, UK SC-cleared leads. No US sponsor required.
  3. Compliance as code. OSCAL automated. Every commit runs 240 golden tests. Every DEFONEOS-SEAL credential goes through 33-agent BFT council.

  5. Trilateral-friendly. No ITAR contamination (MIT-licence exempt per 22 CFR §126.4 + 15 CFR §734.7(b)(1)). DSGL-approved. SEATO-aligned.
