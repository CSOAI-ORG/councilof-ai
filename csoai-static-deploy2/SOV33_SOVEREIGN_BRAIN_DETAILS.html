The Sovereign Brain

What it is

Where Sovereign Loses (honest)

Known weaknesses

  • Speed — 144s/question on Mac CPU is too slow for real-time use
  • Specific facts — Can hallucinate (e.g., said "CSOAI-001" instead of "16939677")
  • Limited training data — 200 samples is small; needs more sovereign corpus
  • CPU-only — needs GPU acceleration for production use
