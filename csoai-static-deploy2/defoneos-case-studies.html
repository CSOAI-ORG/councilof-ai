🌊 Case Study 1: Yorkshire Flood Response (HADR)

Regional flooding across South Yorkshire — 340mm rainfall in 48 hours. 12,000 buildings at risk.

DFO-CS-001 · HADR ISR C2

Mission Parameters

ParameterValue
RegionSouth Yorkshire, UK — Doncaster, Rotherham, Sheffield (11,897 km²)
Population at Risk540,000 residents in flood zone
Duration72 hours active response, 14-day recovery monitoring
DEFONEOS Deploy1 HQ Node (Sheffield), 3 Field Nodes (Doncaster, Rotherham, Bentley), 12 Drone Nodes
Personnel8 DEFONEOS operators, 120+ civilian emergency responders coordinated via C2
Red LinesCivilian-only. No military assets. Personal data PII-redacted per GDPR.

Execution Phases

1
T+0h: Detection & Activation — Sentinel-2 satellite imagery captured post-rain. sentinel-hub-mcp pulls optical + SAR imagery. YOLOv8 detects water extent: 47.3 km² flooded (up from 2.1 km² baseline). Automated alert via jsp936-mcp triggers HADR protocol. SIGIL chain logs activation: H|DEFONEOS|HADR_ACTIVATE|yorkshire_flood_2026|SHA256(...). Operator confirms at T+8min.
2
T+0.5h: Flood Mapping & Population Assessment — os-opendata-mcp overlays flood extent on OS Open Zoomstack basemap. ons-statistics-mcp cross-references census data: 540K residents in inundation zone. cesium-cop-mcp renders 3D flood map on Cesium globe. 12 critical infrastructure sites identified: 3 hospitals, 4 schools, 2 power substations, 3 water treatment plants.
3
T+1h: Drone Swarm Deployed — 12 drone nodes launched from 3 field locations. px4-mcp controls flight paths. mava-swarm-mcp coordinates search patterns: 4 drones per sector, lawnmower pattern at 80m AGL. YOLOv8 Nano onboard detects: persons (47 stranded), vehicles (312 submerged), structures (89 damaged). All detections streamed to COP at 10Hz.
4
T+2h: Medical Evacuation Coordination — medevac-mcp prioritises 47 stranded persons by medical urgency. medlog-mcp tracks ambulance dispatch and hospital capacity (Northern General: 23 beds, Rotherham: 15 beds). freetak-c2-mcp broadcasts CoT tracks to all responder ATAK devices. 3 MEDEVAC routes computed, all avoid flooded roads via os-opendata-mcp route data.
5
T+4h: Supply Chain & Logistics — logistics-mcp manages 8 emergency supply points: water (4,000L), food (2,400 meals), blankets (1,800), medical kits (320). convoy-mcp plans 6 resupply routes using real-time road status. All movements tracked on COP. SIGIL chain records every dispatch.
6
T+8h: Communications Relay — 2 cell towers offline. comms-relay-mcp establishes mesh network using 6 drone relays at 120m AGL. Bandwidth: 12 Mbps across mesh. 340 ATAK devices connected. Zero comms blackout zones remaining.
7
T+24h: Continuous Monitoring — Sentinel-1 SAR pass at T+22h confirms flood peak reached. Water receding at 0.3m/h. openaq-air-mcp monitors air quality (mould risk). Drone swarm maintains 24/7 overwatch. 89 damaged structures flagged for engineering assessment. SIGIL chain: 14,847 entries logged.
8
T+72h: Demobilisation & AAR — All stranded persons evacuated (0 casualties). Flood waters below critical threshold. aar-mcp generates After-Action Review: timeline reconstruction, decision point analysis, response time metrics. SIGIL chain verified: 47,231 entries, 0 tampering, chain integrity confirmed.
