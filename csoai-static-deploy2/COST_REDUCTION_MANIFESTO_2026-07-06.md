## ARTICLE III — FIVE COST-SAVING MECHANICS (the *why*, not the *what*)

These are the five structural reasons CSOAI is structurally cheaper. They are not "we pay our staff less" or "we're VC-subsidised." They are architecture.

### Mechanism 1 — OPEN-SOURCE SUBSTRATE (MIT LICENSED) = NO LOCK-IN TAX ON EVERY LINE OF CODE

The sovereign substrate (SOV3, the 41-charter universe, the OScal-signed System Card, the verification chain, the bias and drift and data-privacy tooling) is MIT-licensed. The full code is on CSOAI-ORG public repositories. A buyer can:

- Read every line before signing.
- Fork and operate it themselves indefinitely (the "self-host sovereign" path).
- Pay CSOAI only for the assurance envelope (signing, anchoring, monitoring, regulator hand-back).

This is the structural reason a Palantir Foundry deployment does not have a 90% cheaper analogue: the underlying code is proprietary, every line is a billable event, every service call is on a price-list, every training course is a separate SKU. Open-source substrate collapses all four revenue lines into one — *the assurance envelope* — which is the only part where sovereign independence has any marginal cost at all.

**Cost reduction**: ~70% of the per-customer cost line in a typical defence AI programme is the proprietary code + licence + per-seat, not the compute or the assurance. Removing that 70% **before** margin is applied is what produces the median 90–98% reduction.

### Mechanism 2 — SOVEREIGN CHARTER UNIVERSE (41 CHARTERS, WRITTEN ONCE, REUSED PER BUYER)

The 41 charters (00 sovereign root → 39 sovereign ledger + 40 distribution hive) are written **once** and reused per buyer with **no re-derivation cost**:

- Cross-walks against 123 universal compliance frameworks (EU, UK, US, APAC, EMEA, Americas, sectoral, standards).
- 4-tier training pathway (Foundation 40 hrs → Director 1,500 hrs).
- Self-sovereign identity binding.
- SIGIL emit on every significant action.
- Ed25519 + OTS Bitcoin anchoring (already paid, marginal cost).

A Palantir / Anduril deal re-derives the compliance posture for every new buyer (their internal compliance is rebuilt per deployment). PwC and McKinsey re-staff per deal (90% of the buyer's spend is people, not platform). **CSOAI reuses the same 41 charters for every named account.** That reuse collapses customer-acquisition cost and customer-onboarding cost by approximately one order of magnitude.

### Mechanism 3 — FREE TIER FOREVER (MIT = LOCKPROOF, NOT LOSS-LEADER)

The Open Source tier is **£0 forever**. There is no "free for 30 days then upsell," no "free for evaluation then enterprise." The Open Source tier includes:

- All 41 charters in their canonical form.
- Self-host sovereign substrate (Docker compose, Helm chart).

This is **not** a loss-leader or a venture-funded giveaway. There is no venture round. There is no exit. There is no obligation to convert. The Open Source tier is the **consequence** of Charter Article 0: a Certifying Authority that charges for *certification* cannot also gatekeep the *underlying capability* on which it is judging. The Open Source tier is the part that makes the binding not just contractual but mechanical.

**Cost reduction to the buyer**: entire tiers of their cost line go to zero. Their developers, regulators, and assurance team can run the same Code-of-Code CSOAI would have run, with no licence to budget.

### Mechanism 4 — REGULATORY ARBITRAGE BY SUBSTRATE (WE BUILD FOR THE FIRST JURISDICTION, THE REST IS A RECONFIGURATION)

The single most expensive line in a defence / sovereign-AI programme is the **per-jurisdiction re-derivation of compliance posture** — every country, sector, and procurement regime wants its own assurance documentation. The traditional vendor model is to charge per re-derivation.

CSOAI's 123-framework cross-walk matrix is structurally the same problem turned around: we built the cross-walk against all 123 frameworks once, anchored it to the substrate once, and now serve the EU AI Act, UK ICO, US NIST, Singapore IMDA, Australia DISP, Canada AIDA, and Japan AI Promotion Act from a **single substrate configuration**.

The cost-saving here is not "we charge less for the same work." The cost-saving is "the work of mapping one new jurisdiction to our chassis is a 6-hour engineering task, not a 6-month programme." A typical multi-jurisdictional deployment that costs a vendor £600K–£3M, costs us a £2,499/mo Gov-tier subscription.

### Mechanism 5 — SIGIL-EMIT / TIME-ANCHOR = NO DUPLICATE-AUDIT TAX

Every CSOAI charter, framework mapping, training record, regulator hand-back, and named-account verification event emits a SIGIL (signed + OTS-Bitcoin-anchored). This means:

- One audit pass for the lifetime of the system, not per customer.
- One regulator hand-back per assurance period, not per vendor engagement.
- One anchored verification chain a buyer can show to their own regulator.
- One breach-disclosure record a buyer can show to their own DPO.

For a vendor that does not anchor, every audit cycle is a fresh invoice. For CSOAI, every audit cycle is a single repo-state + SIGIL-chain walkthrough. This is what collapses year-2, year-3, year-4 cost lines for our buyers — the year-2+ cost of compliance operations is, on a 5-year model, the single largest line in a Palantir or PwC programme.

2. **High-consequence human-in-the-loop decisioning**. We do not replace the medical, judicial, military, or financial decision-maker. We scaffold the decisioning with assurance, audit, and chain-of-custody. The capability of the human is the customer's, not ours.

3. **Permanent retention of buyer data**. CSOAI sovereignty is **substrate sovereignty + assurance envelope**, not data custody. Buyer data lives in the buyer's sovereign wallet (UK-resident or in a CSOAI-co-located sovereign VPC). We do not retain it. SIGIL-anchored verification is our artefact; the buyer's data is the buyer's.

4. **Per-seat or per-request pricing**. Every CSOAI tier is fixed and published. There is no AI-meter, no request counter, no "API call" line. Sovereign assurance is not a metered service; it is a recurring envelope. Pro at £499/mo is £499/mo; it does not become £5,000/mo when usage doubles.

5. **Capture of the institution we certify**. Charter Article 0 forbids it. ISO fee-for-service only. If you ever find CSOAI holding equity, board seats, revenue-share, or success fees from a customer, file the breach at `proofof.ai/breach` and we forfeit the right to be CA3O.
