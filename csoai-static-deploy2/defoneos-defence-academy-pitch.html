1. Executive Summary

The UK Defence Academy (UKDA), headquartered at Shrivenham (Cranfield University campus) with satellite campuses at the Joint Services Command and Staff College (JSCSC, Watchfield) and the College of Management and Technology (CMT), trains ~5,800 military and civilian students annually across the UK Armed Forces. The Defence Academy also hosts the Conflict, Stability and Security Research Centre (CSSRC), Defence Studies Department, and Defence and Security Accelerator (DASA) outreach. This proposal offers DEFONEOS as an open-source AI-OS research platform and curriculum substrate across 5 research lines and 3 curriculum tracks, jointly governed with the Defence Science and Technology Laboratory (Dstl) and Cranfield University.

Funding pathway: UKRI / EPSRC £1.2M, Dstl £800K (joint call), Cranfield University £600K, NATO STO CPoW €400K = total £3.2M / 3 years.

6. 7 Red Lines (immutable for DEFONEOS research at UKDA)

  1. NO kinetic-targeting research — research is limited to ISR, sensing, fusion, decision-support, NOT strike-package generation or kill-order automation
  2. NO personal-surveillance research — no face-rec, no track-individual, no locate-phone at the individual level
  3. NO human-replacement research — BFT-33 council, HITL/HOTL/HIC enforcement; no autonomous lethal decision
  4. NO foreign-controlled compute — research runs only on UK sovereign compute (Telehouse London / Equinix Manchester / Heriot-Watt Edinburgh) or REL-TO partner compute with written MOU
  5. NO defonos.io reference — known trap domain
  6. NO meok.ai / csoai.org / dagon compartment leakage — defence-AI compartment doctrine enforced
  7. NO export of classification-bound raw data — only SIGIL receipts + aggregation deltas across REL TO FVEY
