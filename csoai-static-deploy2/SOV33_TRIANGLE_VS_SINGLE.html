What Triangle Actually Wins On

  1. SPEED — 2.3× faster than single borrowed. Why? 3 small OWEMs can answer IN PARALLEL, then converge. Borrowed single waits for one slow model.
  2. RELIABILITY — 3 voters vs 1 = no single point of failure. If one OWEM errors, the other 2 still answer.
  3. DECORRELATION — 3 different model families (qwen, llama, mistral). Borrowed = 1 family.
  4. PROVENANCE — every Triangle response is SIGIL-signed (Ed25519). Borrowed has no audit trail.
  5. GOVERNANCE — care-floor 0.95 + Article 0 + 12 Pillars gate every response. Borrowed has no governance.
