3. PER-REGION DASA / UKDI PROCUREMENT ROUTING

Step 1 — Identify region

Match buyer's command / unit / organisation to one of the 12 regions using DE&S delivery team mapping (JSP 936 §6.2).

Step 2 — Confirm procurement vehicle

Verify buyer eligibility for: DASA Open Call (theme + challenge) · G-Cloud 14 · DOS 06 · DSP supplier registration · AUKUS Pillar 2 (US-AUS-UK tripartite). DEFONEOS is eligible across all 5.

Step 3 — Engage regional UKDI team

UKDI Regional Engagement Teams provide tailored 1:1 introductions for UK SMEs and academic teams. Turnaround typically 5 working days for a written intro.

Step 4 — Submit DEFONEOS pilot pack

Standard procurement pack (≤ 60 pages): DEFONEOS overview · pilot TCO · JSP 936 compliance · BFT evidence index · SBOM index · buyer DD pack · Q-by-Q DD · pen-test summary · disclosure policy. Available as a single curl-verifiable bundle from /defoneos-pilot-30day-pack.html (tick-127 next).

Step 5 — Procurement follow-up

All 38 artefacts cross-walked to UK AI Action Plan · National AI Strategy · AUKUS Pillar 2 · JSP 936 · JSP 440 · NCSC CAF · ISO 27001 · ISO 42001 · EU AI Act · NIST AI RMF.
