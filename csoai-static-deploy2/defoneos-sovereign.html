⚠️ The CLOUD Act (2018)

The Clarifying Lawful Overseas Use of Data Act allows US law enforcement to compel US companies to hand over data — even if that data is stored in the UK. If the UK MOD uses Palantir, Anduril, Microsoft, or AWS, US authorities can access UK defence data through legal compulsion on the US company.
