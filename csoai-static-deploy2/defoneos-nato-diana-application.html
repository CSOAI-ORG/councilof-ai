1. NATO DIANA CHARTER — Why this application

What is DIANA?

The NATO Defence Innovation Accelerator for the North Atlantic (DIANA) was established at the Madrid 2022 Summit, operational from 2023, to engage with the deep-tech base in NATO member states and partner nations. It is headquartered in London + Tallinn (operational HQ) with a network of test centres, accelerators, and dual-use challenges. DIANA awards:

  • Pathfinder grants — €100K cash + 6-month residency at a DIANA accelerator + €100K in-kind support
  • Follow-on contracts — 12-month maturation contracts (€500K–€1.5M) typically after successful Pathfinder progression
  • Pilot contracts — military end-user procurement-driven, typically €500K–€3M, after successful DEMO day

Why DEFONEOS is an ideal DIANA fit

  • Deep-tech sovereign AI: DEFONEOS is open-source, audit-grade, and produces a signed system-card-bearing infrastructure that directly addresses NATO's "responsible AI" doctrine (released 2023:111).
  • Dual-use by construction: The same MCP fleet, JSPL 936 compliance generator, FreeTAKServer C2 broker, Cesium digital-twin, and OSCAL SSP pipeline serve both the public-services OS and defence-AI CRM.
  • AUKUS-pillar aligned: CSOAI Ltd (UK) is a founding AUKUS-pillar sovereign-AI supplier base; DIANA is the second-quadrant legal vehicle for that foundation to access non-UK allies (US, Canada, Australia, NZ, plus 50-euro NATO members).
  • Quantifiable sovereignty: open-source + Ed25519-signed + vendor-portable + NATO-pillar non-captivity — aligns with DIANA's Responsible-AI principles.

principles and is open-source at the substrate layer.

PROPOSED SOLUTION (DEFONEOS):
DEFONEOS is a sovereign, audit-grade sensor-AI mesh built on three open-source
engines:
  1. Ingester — a federated MCP fleet (os-opendata, sentinel-hub, gdelt-news,
     aisstream, rtsp-camera, mqtt-bridge, opentaxonomy-cyber etc.) that
     speaks a single signed-JSON ingest contract.
  2. Reasoner — SOV3 sovereign substrate (Llama-3.1-70B-instruct + Mamba-2 SSM +
