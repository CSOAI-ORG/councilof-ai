1. PRICING PHILOSOPHY

DEFONEOS pricing is per-tier run-rate: T1 for solo operators, T2 for departmental pilots, T3 for UK MOD live deployments. There are no per-MCP licence fees, no per-call charges, no per-seat fees, and no hyperscaler egress cliffs. The DEFONEOS composable substrate is OSS-licensed (Apache-2.0 / MIT / OGL-3.0 / CC-BY-4.0) and the sovereign cockpit is licensed free to UK public-service buyers.

Every pound traces to a cost line. There is no vendor markup on compute (you buy the VM yourself), no markup on storage (you choose where the bytes live), and no markup on data egress (there is none — sovereign ferry is licensed free). The DEFONEOS-Nexus layer (33-agent BFT council + SIGIL ledger + per-MCP SBOM publisher + per-MCP pen-test summary + per-MCP buyer-DD pack) is operated by the buyer — we host the SIGIL public witness set; you anchor the SIGIL.

This open-model arithmetic is auditable. Every line below ties to a specific tool, deployment step, and unit cost. The buyer's TCO calculator (runnable in <60s on a fresh VM) is at the bottom of this page.
