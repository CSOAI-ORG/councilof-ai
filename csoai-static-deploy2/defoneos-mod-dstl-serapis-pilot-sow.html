1 · Why SERAPIS

Dstl SERAPIS is the UK's mechanism for trilateral / bilateral collaborative defence science with allied nations. The AI Assurance theme (2026 call) targets:

  • Sovereign AI assurance frameworks
  • UK-FR interoperability of AI safety controls
  • NCSC SC-01 CAF v3.1 compliance for defence AI
  • Adversarial robustness benchmarks (UK + FR red teams)
  • Cross-walk to EU AI Act + UK AISI voluntary commitments

DEFONEOS sovereign AI substrate (SOV3-small + SOV3-large + SEAL credential) maps directly to 5 of 7 SERAPIS sub-themes. This SOW is the reusable template for any UK-FR consortium submission.

10 · The 5 Anti-Patterns (SERAPIS Bid Disasters We Refuse)

  1. No "submit without DGA partner." UK-FR consortium mandatory.
  2. No "vague deliverables." 12 named Phase 1 deliverables each with month + owner.
  3. No "BFT bypass." ≥23/33 mandatory + DGA observer panel review.
  4. No "Phase 2 promise without Phase 1 results." Phase 2 contingent on D12 GO.
  5. No "unilateral decision-making." T1/T2/T3 governance respects UK + FR + DEFONEOS.
