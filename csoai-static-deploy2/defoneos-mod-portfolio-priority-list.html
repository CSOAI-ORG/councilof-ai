5. The 7 routing rules

  1. Always use a named-individual sender — never info@, never partnerships@, never ceo@. Nick's own name & voice. This is non-negotiable.
  2. Always reference a specific public artefact — OSCAL SSP, DaaP doctrine, or the 240-test pytest harness. Vague superlatives ("industry-leading", "best-in-class") are banned.
  3. Always offer the "not now" exit — "If DEFONEOS is not what [org] needs, please reply 'not now' and I will not follow up." This is the highest-converting trust-building line in B2B cold outreach.
  4. Always include a single-sentence subject — under 8 words, no ALL-CAPS, no emoji. Example: DEFONEOS — sovereign AI assurance for Dstl Q4 pipeline
  5. Never send on Friday, Saturday, Sunday, or UK bank holiday Monday — empirical reply-rate drops 35%.
  6. Never follow up more than 4 times — 24h, 72h, 7d, 30d. After that, drop to quarterly milestone ping.
  7. Never cc more than 2 people — cc-spam destroys trust. The cc list is visible: it's the buyer's chain of trust, not a chain of awareness.
