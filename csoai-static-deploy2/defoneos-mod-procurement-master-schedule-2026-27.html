1 · Why this calendar exists

DEFONEOS must align bids, capability investment, BFT council votes, and pilot SOW readiness to a 12-month procurement rhythm. The 26 windows below are the named set; each is annotated with the deadline, the value band, the eligibility constraints, and the DEFONEOS-bid fit (HIGH = suitable for direct bid or consortium lead, MED = consortium partner, LOW = watch only).

Three rules govern this schedule:

  1. No bid is filed outside this calendar. Anything not listed is rejected at intake — focus over breadth.
  2. Bids targeting HIGH-fit windows are reviewed by the 33-agent BFT council. Council quorum 23/33 required to approve file.
  3. Capability investment (which sovereign weights / which MCPs / which pilot SOW) follows the bid pipeline. Reverse direction is a veto.

10 · Bid fit scoring (the 5-criterion model)

Each window is scored against 5 criteria. HIGH = 4-5 score, MED = 2-3, LOW = 0-1.

  1. Mission alignment — does the window's mandate match DEFONEOS sovereign-by-design thesis?
  2. Eligibility headroom — can DEFONEOS meet security / clearance / domicile requirements in time?
  3. SOW readiness — is there a pilot SOW already drafted, or can one be drafted in <30 days?
  4. BFT council capacity — is the 33-agent BFT council available to review bid readiness within the timeline?
  5. Capability investment — does winning this window require capability DEFONEOS has, or capability we can build in <90 days?

12 · The 5 Anti-Patterns (Procurement Calendar Disasters We Refuse)

  1. No "we'll bid everything." Bid only what's HIGH-fit (14 windows). MED = partner only. LOW = watch.
  2. No "last-minute bid." BFT review milestone must be 14+ days before deadline.
  3. No "out-of-scope bid for optics." Biometrics / kinetic / autonomous = red lines, always refused.
  4. No "capability investment lagging bids." Bid pipeline drives capability pipeline, never the reverse.
  5. No "calendar without owners." Every HIGH-fit window has a named DEFONEOS bid owner + BFT liaison.

13 · Owner gates (human-decision items blocking bids)

  1. UK SC clearance — blocks MOD IFS TC-008 (#5), Dstl AI T&E (#7), AUKUS Pillar 2 (#25)
  2. DSP registration — blocks all UK MOD direct bids (#5, #6, #10)
  3. Cyber Essentials + Cyber Essentials Plus — blocks NCSC ACD 4 (#6), ENISA (#16)
  4. US SPAC formation — blocks FedRAMP (#22) and US-led consortium bids (#19, #20, #23)
