1. WHY THIS REGIONAL HUB PACK EXISTS

Northern Ireland (NI) is one of the 12 UK regions in the DEFONEOS regional procurement map (R12). However, NI requires a dedicated hub pack because of its unique defence-industrial landscape: (a) Belfast has a significant maritime-platform manufacturing base (Thales Belfast, Harland & Wolff), (b) the NI Civil Service operates under a different devolved procurement framework than Great Britain, (c) the Good Friday Agreement legacy makes cross-border AI assurance alignment sensitive, and (d) the AUKUS Pillar 2 dual-use lane connects NI to Republic of Ireland defence research via EU Horizon bridges.

This hub pack documents the NI-specific DEFONEOS procurement entry points, force priority mappings, MCP coverage, and cross-border considerations. It is BFT-signed (Ed25519 / RFC 8032 / 2026-Q3 rotation) and curl-verifiable. Every entry carries a public contact and an explicit NI-specific DEFONEOS fit note.

5. NI-SPECIFIC PROCUREMENT ROUTING × 5-STEP WORKFLOW

Step 1 — Confirm NI buyer identity

Is the buyer: (a) NI Civil Service Department, (b) NI-based defence industry prime, (c) NI academic institution, (d) NI-based SME, or (e) MOD unit deployed in NI? Each has a different procurement vehicle (see §6).

Step 2 — Verify dual supplier registration

NI procurement requires registration on BOTH CCS G-Cloud 14 (GB framework) AND NI Procurement Note (NIPN) supplier list. DEFONEOS is registered on both. Verify via curl https://online.marketplace.team/digital-marketplace/services?q=defoneos and curl https://www.finance-ni.gov.uk/topics/procurement.

Step 3 — Engage NI-specific UKDI team

NI does not have a dedicated UKDI regional engagement team — engagement routes through UKDI Scotland (Edinburgh) or UKDI Northern England (Newcastle). Request intro via hello@csoai.org subject "NI UKDI intro".

Step 4 — Submit DEFONEOS NI pilot pack

Standard pack (≤ 60 pages) plus NI-specific addendum covering: NI Data Protection Act 2018 devolved instruments · NI Civil Service procurement preferences · Good Friday Agreement legacy considerations · cross-border data flow consent. Pack at /defoneos-pilot-ni-30day-pack.html (tick-128 planned).

Step 5 — AUKUS Pillar 2 / EU Horizon cross-border approval (if applicable)

If buyer intends to share DEFONEOS data across the NI/ROI border, escalate to UK Foreign Office (FCDO) via hello@csoai.org subject "DEFONEOS NI cross-border". DEFONEOS enforces this gate at the substrate level — no autonomous cross-border.

8. THE 4 ANTI-PATTERNS THIS HUB PACK DEFEATS

  1. No "NI procurement is the same as GB". NI Civil Service runs a devolved procurement framework (NIPN) separate from CCS G-Cloud 14. Dual registration required.
  2. No "Belfast is a regional backwater". Thales Belfast + Harland & Wolff + QUB CSIT + UU = a defence-industrial base with >£1B/year revenue. Tier-1 buyer.
  3. No "cross-border AI assurance is just another compliance box". Good Friday Agreement legacy + AUKUS Pillar 2 + EU Horizon require FCDO explicit approval. No autonomous cross-border.
  4. No "NI data protection = UK GDPR". NI has devolved instruments layered on top of UK GDPR. tk-gdpr-dpia MCP must process devolved consent rules.
