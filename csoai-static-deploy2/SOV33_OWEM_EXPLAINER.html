The BFT-33 Council

Every sovereign op can be voted on by 33 voters across:

  • 5 OWEM groups (compliance, defense, intuition, voice, general)
  • 4 lineage groups within each (qwen, llama, deepseek, mistral)

Aggregation: Free-MAD weighted sum (no majority conformity bias). Each voter casts ALLOW or REJECT independently. Quorum: 23/33 needed for the decision to bind.

Real test (12 Jul 2026): "Is the sovereign care floor at 0.95?" → 9 ALLOW, 6 REJECT → ALLOW wins. Some voters said 0.92 (Oracle hallucinated), some said 0.95 (correct). The council correctly aggregated.
