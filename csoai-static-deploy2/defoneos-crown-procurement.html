4. The 6-Step Pre-Award Pipeline

The Crown procurement process (under Procurement Act 2023) has 6 mandatory steps for any procurement above £139,688 VAT:

1

Step 1 — Pipeline Notice (PA-2023 §13)

Buyer publishes a pipeline notice on the UK e-notification service (Find a Tender Service). This is a forward signal of upcoming procurement, not yet a tender. For DEFONEOS, this typically appears in CCS' Digital & AI pipeline for AI Growth Zone procurements, or in MOD's DE&S forward-look.

2

Step 2 — Planned Procurement Notice (PA-2023 §14)

Buyer publishes a more specific planned procurement notice when tender is imminent. Suppliers can express interest; buyer is expected to engage.

3

Step 3 — Tender Notice + Documents (PA-2023 §18)

Buyer publishes the tender notice (with the competitive flexible procedure) and full tender documents. For AI, the documents MUST include: (a) AI risk classification, (b) data governance plan, (c) assurance pathway, (d) any specific JSP 936 / EU AI Act requirements, (e) evaluation criteria and weights. DEFONEOS-ready template documents: defoneos-mod-proposal.html (UK MOD SoN) and defoneos-dasa-application.html (DASA bid).

4

Step 4 — Supplier Response (typically 30 days)

Suppliers submit their response (technical + commercial). For MOD, this typically includes: (a) a Statement of Need response, (b) a compliance matrix, (c) a commercial proposal, (d) a risk register, (e) team CVs and SC status. DEFONEOS generates the compliance matrix and risk register in 6 minutes via the JSP 936 generator.

5

Step 5 — Evaluation (typically 6-12 weeks)

Buyer evaluates responses against the published criteria. For AI, evaluation usually includes: (a) a technical oral, (b) a 2-day proof-of-concept, (c) Cyber Essentials Plus check, (d) SC clearance check for key personnel, (e) JSP 936 / AISI informal consultation. DEFONEOS supports all of these by providing a 2-day PoC environment and pre-built compliance evidence.

6

Step 6 — Award Notice + Standstill (PA-2023 §31)

Buyer publishes the award notice with the winning supplier and price. A mandatory 8-working-day standstill period follows during which unsuccessful suppliers can challenge. Once standstill passes, contract is signed and procurement closed. The contract is then subject to ongoing performance reporting.

7. The £139,688 / £5,372,609 Thresholds (Procurement Act 2023)

The Procurement Act 2023 has a single threshold for supplies/services (£139,688 VAT) above which the full open competitive procedure applies. Below that threshold, the buyer can use a below-threshold procedure (still subject to fundamental principles of transparency, equal treatment, and non-discrimination).
