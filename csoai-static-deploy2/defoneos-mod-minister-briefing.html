Sovereign open-source legal posture

DEFONEOS releases are MIT-licensed. This is the only licence tier that is automatically not subject to US export controls:

  • ITAR 22 CFR §126.4 — publicly-available carve-out applies to unclassified basic research and open-source code
  • EAR 15 CFR §734.7(b)(1) — publicly-available without restriction is EAR-exempt (no ECCN needed)
  • DFARS 252.227-7014(b) — non-commercial academic/open-source is outside DFARS
