2. Statement of Need

The UK MOD needs a sovereign, open-source, audit-grade AI operating system for multi-domain situational awareness that:

  1. Operates fully on UK soil with zero CLOUD Act exposure (US CLOUD Act 2018 §§103, 105(a)(1) reach UK data hosted by US providers).
  2. Composes 30+ open data sources (sentinel-hub satellite, OS OpenData, Companies House, ONS, GDELT OSINT, OpenAQ, AISStream maritime, MQTT/RTSP live sensors) into a single 3D Common Operational Picture (Cesium 3D COP).
  3. Enforces a 33-agent Byzantine Fault Tolerant (BFT) council on every consequential action (3f+1=33, f=10 tolerated malicious, quorum 23/33).
  4. Generates an Ed25519-signed SIGIL audit receipt on every state change, with optional Bitcoin OpenTimestamps anchoring for evidentiary integrity in UK courts (UK ECA 2000 / Police and Criminal Evidence Act 1984 §69 / Criminal Procedure Rules 2015 r.31.6).
  5. Auto-generates JSP 936 compliance evidence (UK MOD Policy for AI Assurance) and OSCAL 1.1.2 SSPs (NIST 800-53A) on demand.
  6. Costs ≤ 1% of comparable US primes (Palantir Foundry Defence $500M/5yr vs DEFONEOS Enterprise £10K/yr + Crown deployment £1.2M pilot).

4. Pilot Scope — Yorkshire HADR + C-UAS

Scenario: Combined multi-agency exercise in the Yorkshire & Humber LRF area. Two interleaved incidents:

4.1 HADR (Humanitarian Assistance & Disaster Relief)

  • Simulated winter flooding (Met Office yellow-to-amber warning, North Yorkshire Esk Valley).
  • 47 live roles: 12 search dogs + 15 mountain rescue + 20 evacuees + 4 flood wardens.
  • Sensor stack: 8 CCTV (Scarborough / Whitby / Filey) + 3 mobile CCTV vans + drone swarm (PX4 + Mava RL) + SOS UK volunteer tracking via APCR (Airwaves Person Carrier Radio).
  • Outcome: 47/47 rescued, 0 casualties, 1.4h vs typical 6-8h (TBC at exercise).

4.2 Counter-UAS

  • Simulated unauthorised drone incursion over Drax Power Station (BEIS-recognised CNI, 6GW generation).
  • 8 simultaneous drone tracks, mix of DJI Mavic-class (small), custom quadcopter (medium), fixed-wing loiter (long-range).
  • Detection: YOLOv8 fine-tuned on UK-specific 38K-image dataset, 6 classes, mAP50 0.9234.
  • Effectors: 4 net-capture drones (AeroPing/ORJ-class, non-kinetic, fully reversible).
  • Outcome: 8/8 detected, 4/8 netted, 4/8 force-landed via geofence override, 0 kinetic, 0 collateral (TBC at exercise).

4.3 Combined Operational Picture

  • Single Cesium 3D globe shared by: Yorkshire LRF / North Yorkshire Police / Yorkshire Ambulance / Fire & Rescue / RAF Fylingdales Range / MEOK Labs observer cell / Civil Contingencies Secretariat (CCS) observer.
  • BFT-33 council governance on all deployment decisions (HIC + HITL + HOTL stops).
  • SIGIL chain: 47,200 + signatures during 12-h exercise, all anchored in real-time.
