🗺️ UKDI Regional Engagement Programme

UK Defence and Security Onshore (UKDI) Regional Engagement is the MOD's programme for connecting with defence SMEs and innovators outside of the traditional South-East corridor. The Yorkshire & Humber region has a significant defence footprint — manufacturing, ISR, logistics, and cyber — and UKDI's Regional Engagement Team serves as the bridge between local innovators and MOD procurement.
