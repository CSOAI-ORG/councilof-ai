2. THE PROBLEM

Current NHS-foreign-vendor stack has 5 fundamental problems:

  1. Federated Data Platform controversy — £330M Palantir Foundry FDP contract (Sep 2023) has been the subject of 5+ public consultations, 3 ICO interventions, and a Parliamentary Health & Social Care Committee report (HC 1356, Aug 2024). US-headquartered Palantir falls under CLOUD Act + FISA 702. NHS patient data flowing to US servers is a sovereignty question.
  2. DeepMind Streams / Royal Free ICO ruling — 2017 ICO ruling against DeepMind / Royal Free NHS Foundation Trust set the precedent: patient data without explicit consent + Data Protection Impact Assessment + lawful basis is unlawful. DEFONEOS sovereign bridge makes this auditable in real-time.
  3. Babylon Health / eConsult + NHS 111 collapse — Babylon Health, the original NHS 111 AI partner, entered UK administration in Aug 2023. NHS trusts scrambled. eConsult, Q doctor, Push Doctor — same fate-line: foreign-VC-backed, no sovereign AI guarantee.
  4. UK GDPR Art 9 special category + Caldicott gap — Patient data is "special category" under UK GDPR Art 9. Caldicott Principles 8th edition (Dec 2020) require explicit data-sharing review. Current NHS AI has no standard mechanism for Caldicott review — each trust interprets it differently.
  5. MHRA SaMD + DCB0129/DCB0160 double-burden — AI as Medical Device must meet MHRA SaMD guidance (2023) + DCB0129 (manufacturer) / DCB0160 (deployment) clinical risk management standards. Current NHS AI has separate compliance processes for each.
