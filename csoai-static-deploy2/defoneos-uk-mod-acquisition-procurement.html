6 · Honesty Register — Acquisition & Procurement

Honesty Register — UK MOD Acquisition & Procurement Pathways

  1. Budget figures (~£14bn DE&S, ~£700M Dstl, etc.) are public-knowledge estimates from Annual Reports and Accounts (ARA); actual budget varies by Spending Review cycle.
  2. Lead times (16 weeks / 9-18 months) are illustrative averages; actual award times vary 50%-200%.
  3. Tier prices (£145K / £420K / £1.2M / £2.4M) are DEFONEOS's internal pricing bands; final contract values depend on negotiation + lot-specific scrutiny.
  4. The 8-phase Crown Compliance Calendar assumes no surprises; expect 4-6 week extension for any insurance slow-paths.
  5. ATI and Innovate UK are illustrative — specific 2026 competitions may have moved or closed.
  6. AUKUS Pillar II allocations are illustrative; the trilateral pool is being built 2025-2030 — DEFONEOS can apply via existing DIANA / DARPA / DSTG channels.
  7. NATO STO is brand-credit only — real acquisition goes via national channnels (DASA in UK).
  8. Compliance requirements (DSP+CE+/SC/ISO 27001) are the standard MOD core; specific primes may add (e.g. NV1, CTC, Booked Cleared List).
