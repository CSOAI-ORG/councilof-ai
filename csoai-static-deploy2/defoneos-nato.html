Strategic Alignment: Why NATO Needs DEFONEOS

NATO's October 2021 AI Strategy identifies AI as a critical emerging disruptive technology (EDT) for alliance security. The strategy calls for:

  1. Responsible AI use — lawful, traceable, accountable
  2. AI interoperability — systems that work across allied nations
  3. AI in collective defence — enhancing alliance capabilities

DEFONEOS delivers all three through its open-source sovereign architecture. Unlike vendor-locked systems (Palantir, Anduril), DEFONEOS can be deployed by any NATO member nation without licensing restrictions, data export concerns, or sovereignty compromises. Its MCP (Model Context Protocol) federation means any ally can contribute tools and capabilities to a shared defence AI commons.
