4 · Win-Probability Calibration Methodology

Win probabilities are calibrated against 5 named factors, each scored 0-10:

  • Sovereign-fit (weight 25%) — Does DEFONEOS's sovereign positioning create a genuine competitive moat for this buyer?
  • Capability match (weight 20%) — Can the 30-MCP estate + BFT governance + SIGIL audit chain cover ≥80% of the stated requirement out-of-box?
  • Relationship strength (weight 20%) — Do we have a warm contact, prior engagement, or a referral path?
  • Compliance readiness (weight 20%) — Are all required certifications (Cyber Essentials, SC, ISO 27001) either held or in-flight?
  • Competitive landscape (weight 15%) — How many credible competitors exist? Is the buyer pre-disposed to a prime (BAE, QinetiQ, Leonardo)?

Calibrated probability = (sovereign-fit × 0.25 + capability × 0.20 + relationship × 0.20 + compliance × 0.20 + competitive × 0.15) × 0.1, floored at 5%, capped at 75% (no opportunity is above 75% until a bid is submitted).
