10 · The 5 Anti-Patterns (Capability Investment Disasters We Refuse)

  1. No "capability investment lagging bids." Bid pipeline drives capability pipeline, never the reverse.
  2. No "build it and they will come." Every build traces to a named bid window.
  3. No "BFT bottleneck." Council expansion is funded (scenario-dependent) ahead of bid volume.
  4. No "constraint ignored." Disk / SC / PyPI / cash gates are real; not aspirational.
  5. No "build without BFT vote." Every weight + MCP + SOW gets ≥23/33 BFT approval at design close.
