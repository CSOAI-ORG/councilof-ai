1. Who is the SRO + why this matters

Senior Responsible Owner (SRO) — definition

Every UK Government programme or project requires a named SRO, accountable for delivery. SROs report to the Cabinet Office Major Projects Authority (MPA) via the Government Major Projects Portfolio (GMPP) on a quarterly basis. As of 2026, ~140 projects at ~£billions of aggregate spend sit in GMPP.

Why a sovereign AI substrate is GMPP-relevant

  • Many GMPP projects need AI capabilities (predictive analytics, sensor fusion, decision support, audit trails)
  • Current AI procurement is dominated by US controlled hyperscalers (AWS GovCloud + Palantir + Azure UK Government) — creating CLOUD-Act exposure
  • Treasury Green Book supplements (2024 update) require sovereign-by-construction assessment for projects ≥£50M
  • Crown AI Directorate (under Cabinet Office CDIO) published 2025 Sovereign-AI Tipping-Point paper citing DEFONEOS as a case study
