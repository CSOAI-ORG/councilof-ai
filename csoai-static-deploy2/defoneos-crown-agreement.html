12 sections

01 · Scope & Permitted Use

Customer may deploy DEFONEOS for sovereign AI workloads specified in Annex A (use-case register). Permitted categories include (subject to Charter Article 0):

  • Defence-grade decision-support under Article 14 human oversight
  • Multi-sensor fusion + intelligence product generation
  • Threat-detection + cyber-defence automation (with deployer-authorisation per Article 26)
  • Coalition-information sharing with AUKUS-Pillar-2 partners
  • Audit-grade evidence packaging for procurement-side compliance audits

Categorical prohibitions (Charter Article 0 / Care Floor) — automatically enforced at substrate level regardless of master agreement:

  • No kinetic-targeting patterns (find-fix-finish / strike packages / kill orders)
  • No individual surveillance (named-person tracking / face-rec on identified persons)
  • No civilian harm optimisation (objectives may not include civilian casualty reduction as a side-benefit)
  • No sovereignty violation (cross-jurisdiction access requires explicit Customer consent)
  • No auto-escalation chains (each escalation requires explicit human approval)
  • No lying to humans (model outputs may not contain known-false statements about system state)
  • No irreversibility without confirmation (irreversible side-effects require positive confirmation)

02 · Sovereignty & Data Residency

Provider warrants the substrate runs on UK-only compute (M4 Mac + GCP UK region) by default. Customer may elect to:

  • (a) Crown airgap deployment: substrate runs on Customer-provided sovereign enclave with no cloud connectivity
  • (b) IL5/IL6 hardened: substrate deployed into Customer's existing IL-tier infrastructure
  • (c) AUKUS-Pillar-2 federation: cross-region with US-primes / Australia-Canada equivalents under separate scope agreement

No foreign hyperscaler (AWS-US, Azure-US, Google-US) may become a substrate dependency at any tier.

OSCAL compliance attestor publishes the full control-implementation matrix against EU AI Act Articles 9/12/13/14/17/26/27/47/48/49/50/52/74/86, plus UK GDPR, DPA 2018, NIS2, DORA, JSP 936, DSEC, NATO Autonomy, AUKUS-Pillar-2 mapping.
