1. What is NATO AGS?

Alliance Ground Surveillance (NATO AGS)

NATO AGS is the Alliance's primary airborne ISR capability, operated by NATO AGS Force (NAGSF) at Sigonella Air Base, Sicily. Five RQ-4D Phoenix High-Altitude Endurance UAVs (built by Northrop Grumman, derived from Global Hawk) provide wide-area-surveillance over 100,000+ km² per 24h sortie.

3. KLV / STANAG 5527 ingest — the seam

What is KLV

Key-Length-Value (KLV) is the STANAG 4586 / 5527 metadata encoding that wraps every NATO motion-imagery frame with geo + time + sensor-state + classification. DEFONEOS's `ntm-fusion-mcp` parses KLV in real-time and adds the same frame data to the sovereign SIGIL chain.
