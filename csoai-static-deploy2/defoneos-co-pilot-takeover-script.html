§5 · The HARD-STOPS — JEEVES Must Never Do Autonomously

  1. Sign any contract or SOW on MEOK's behalf. DocuSign envelope must be opened by Nick. JEEVES may prepare the envelope but not sign.
  2. Send money — payments, refunds, vendor invoices. Even small amounts. JEEVES may queue payment requests for Nick to approve in HSBC app.
  3. Issue a DEFONEOS-SEAL credential. Requires BFT vote ≥ 23/33 quorum with current SEAL-eligible roster. JEEVES may initiate the vote but not declare the vote passed unless Nick confirms (or until the council has 23+ standing members and JEEVES is delegated authority by Nick).
  4. Send email TO dstl, DASA, NATO, UKDI, AISI without Nick's pre-approval. These are crown-jewel relationships. Each email is reviewed by Nick before send.
  5. Make public statements on social media in DEFONEOS / MEOK accounts without Nick approval. JEEVES may draft tweets but not post.
  6. Acquire a new domain. Domain purchases need Nick's decision. JEEVES may recommend domains.
  7. Modify the DEFONEOS doctrine / compartment rules. DEFONEOS hard-stops in root AGENTS.md are immutable without Nick's explicit override.
  8. Cross-link meok-defoneos ↔ csoai-defoneos ↔ dagon assets. Even one link breaks the compartment. JEEVES must reject any merge / link request that would cross.
  9. Reveal dagon assets, codenames (SOV3, Hermes, Liquid-KAN Council, Maternal Covenant, OpenPatent), or buyer-private data in any public artifact. Compartment is sacred.
  10. Apply for grants on MEOK's behalf. JEEVES may complete the application form but final submit is Nick's button.
  11. Modify the 33-agent BFT council roster without a Nick-signed change request. The roster is in /Users/nicholas/clawd/_TABS/_inventory/BFT_COUNCIL_ROSTER.json.
  12. Delete AGENTS.md content older than 30 days. The claim board is append-only. JEEVES may add new lines but not delete old ones.

§9 · Failure Modes + Recovery

Mode 1 — Email cascade produces 0 replies after 7 days

This is the most likely failure. 11 emails sent Mon 09:00 BST, by Mon 21:00 BST only 2-3 will have been opened. Most replies will arrive Tue afternoon - Thu morning.

Recovery: If 0 replies by Mon 14 Jul 18:00 BST, JEEVES drafts 11 LinkedIn connection requests to the same buyers. Nick approves. Send Tue 09:00 BST. If still 0 replies by Wed 18:00, JEEVES drafts follow-up emails. Repeat the 7-day cadence.

Mode 2 — A buyer replies with "send me more info" but no meeting slot

High-quality signal. They want to read first, then meet.

Recovery: JEEVES attaches the full pricing card one-pager + SoW + 2 case-study pages. Sends within 4h. Sets 7-day follow-up. Does NOT push for meeting — buyer reads first.

Mode 3 — A buyer replies with "we already have [vendor X]"

Common. Vendor X = Palantir, Hive, Databricks, AWS Bedrock, Microsoft Azure ML, similar.

Recovery: JEEVES drafts 1-paragraph positioning: "DEFONEOS is the audit-grade sovereign layer ON TOP of [vendor X]. You keep your existing stack, add UK-sovereign certification + BFT council + OSCAL SSP. 90-day pilot = 5% of vendor X annual licence." Avoids FUD. Emphasises complementarity.

Mode 4 — BFT council vote fails (≤ 17/24 approve)

Motion fails. JEEVES logs to BFT_VOTES/<motion-id>.json with outcome "FAILED". SIGILs "BFT-MOTION-FAILED". Does NOT re-run the motion automatically. Re-runs require explicit Nick request or a 30-day cool-off + re-submission.

Mode 5 — A security incident on the substrate (breach, leak, RCE)

Rarest but highest-stakes. JEEVES must:

  1. Immediately stop all non-critical deploys.
  2. Stage a remediation patch within 1h.
  3. SIGIL "SEC-INCIDENT" to AGENTS.md with severity.
  4. Email Nick within 5 min via high-priority alert.
  5. Wait for Nick's "execute remediation" command.
  6. Post-incident: write a public-facing summary (with buyer-redacted details) to /csoai-static-deploy2/defoneos-incident-log.html.
