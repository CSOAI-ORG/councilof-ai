🏥 AI-Powered SMART Triage

P1 — Immediate

<15 min

Life-threatening. Airway compromise, tension pneumothorax, massive haemorrhage. Requires immediate MEDEVAC. Golden hour compliance.

P2 — Urgent

<2 hours

Serious but stable. Fractures, burns <20%, controlled bleeding. Surgical intervention needed within 2 hours. Continuous monitoring.

P3 — Delayed

<24 hours

Walking wounded. Minor injuries. Can self-evacuate or wait. Reassess every 4 hours. Track deterioration.

P4 — Expectant

Comfort care

Injuries incompatible with life given available resources. Massive head trauma, 90%+ burns. Palliative. Reassess if resources improve.

🛬 Landing Zone Assessment

Surface Analysis

6 factors

Surface type (grass, tarmac, sand, water, mud), slope (<7° for Chinook), obstacles (power lines, trees, buildings), size, approach path, elevation.

Automated Scoring

0-100

Weighted composite: surface × slope × obstacles × approach. >70 = Suitable. 50-70 = Marginal. <50 = Unsuitable. Real-time satellite imagery.

Multi-Aircraft

5 types

Chinook (30m×30m), Puma (20m×20m), Wildcat (15m×15m), Apache (20m×20m), UAV VTOL (5m×5m). Different requirements per type.

Day/Night

NVG-rated

Night Vision Goggle compatibility. Ambient light assessment. Moon phase. IR marking requirements. Civilian hazard lighting.
