⚑ Honesty register — what we will NOT do

The 7 hard stops (these are strengths, not weaknesses)

  • No lock-in. The sovereign substrate can be forked. The fork is sovereign. The fork inherits Care Floor + BFT + SIGIL + DORADO + Article 50 + Crown + MIT.
  • No closed weights. Only open-weights models participate as substrate truth. Vendor models can plug in via bridge_think, but their weights are not the substrate's truth.
  • No foreign cloud. Only citizen hardware (Mac + GCP UK + M2 LAN). No US hyperscaler can become a substrate dependency.
  • No individual surveillance. PII redaction is constitutional. Care Membrane stops it at inference time.
  • No data selling. The substrate's data IS its citizens'. It does not sell. It charges fees for service. (ISO fee-for-service model.)
  • No fork blocking. Anyone can fork. The fork is encouraged. The fork is sovereign.
  • No substrate-paywall. The base substrate is $0 forever to citizens. Crown tier is the only paid tier, and it pays for consulting, not the substrate.

What we do NOT claim

  • Not zero-error. Care Membrane reduces risk; it doesn't eliminate it.
  • Not zero-incident. Serious-incident reporting is a process.
  • Not bypass-proof. Substrate-level attacks can in principle bypass. We test against this.
  • Not all-knowing. The OOWM ingests and learns; it does not see the future.
  • Not vendor-X-compatible-by-default. Any vendor model participates through bridge_think, with output filtered through Care Membrane + SIGIL + BFT. They are muscle, not brain.
