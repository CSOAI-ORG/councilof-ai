External audit hook — how you read the trail from the outside

The DEFONEOS external-audit hook gives you read-only access to a SIGIL-verified stream of all sovereign actions — without requiring write access to the substrate. This is the spine of cooperative regulation.

  • mTLS-pinned — your external audit endpoint signs with your regulator's PKI certificate. No DNS attack can reroute.
  • Read-only — by design. You can verify, you cannot modify.
  • SIGIL-verified — every record you read carries the SIGIL hash. You can re-verify against sigil.csoai.org yourself. False records detectable.
  • Audit-logged at access time — every request your tool makes is logged on both sides. You see what you requested, when, and what you received.
  • Filterable — by deployment, date range, action type, BFT outcome, red-line trigger, kill-switch flag.
  • Bulk-export cap — by default, 100,000 events per request. Higher volumes negotiated as part of cooperative agreement.

Honesty register — what the regulator sandbox doesn't give you

  • No classified material. The sandbox operates on illustrative data, not real sovereign-action telemetry. We don't expose national-security deployments to free sandboxes.
  • No authority to act. Read-only access is a regulator's right; we don't hand you override controls.
  • No exclusivity. Our cooperative agreements are non-exclusive. We cooperate equally with all eligible regulators.
  • No guaranteed availability. We aim for 99.9% but the sandbox is on shared infrastructure. SLA-guaranteed uptime requires a cooperative agreement (4h or 1h SLA).
  • No third-party re-distribution. The evidence we provide is for your regulator's use. Re-distribution requires written permission; on the open-source tier, we publish OSCAL/SPDF to evidence.csoai.org already.
