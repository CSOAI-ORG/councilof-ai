2. Ed25519 Signature Chain

Each SEAL is bound to an Ed25519 detached signature over the canonical SHA-256 hash of the MCP body. The signature is itself bound to:

  1. The MCP body (SHA-256)
  2. The SBOM bundle (SHA-256)
  3. The BFT motion record (tally + owner cosign hash)
  4. The witness-set roster snapshot at time of issuance
  5. The owner-seat R1 cosign signature

Any change to (1)-(5) after issuance invalidates the SEAL — the MCP must be re-voted (unanimous 33/33) and re-SEALed. Reversal of an issued SEAL requires a red-line 27/33 motion (higher quorum).

3. Revocation Policy

Four revocation triggers — any of which initiates a red-line 27/33 motion:

  • SBOM drift: SBOM SHA-256 does not match canonical probe (24h grace).
  • Red-line violation: any of 7 vectors scores >0 in subsequent red-line scan.
  • Witness set compromise: >50% of ring witnesses rotate during grace.
  • Pen-test regression: any CRIT or HIGH finding in subsequent pen-test cycle.

To date: 0 revocations across all 4 SEALed MCPs.
