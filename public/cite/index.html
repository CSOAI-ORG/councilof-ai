How to cite Council of AI and the GSPC board

Council of AI is the public name of CSOAI Ltd, a company registered in England and Wales (UK Companies House 16939677). It measures AI models, agents and the systems they call, and publishes the evidence. A result is a measurement of what was run and when. It is not a certification.

Recommended citation: the GSPC board dataset

Council of AI (2026). gspc-board [Data set]. Hugging Face. https://doi.org/10.57967/hf/10114

The DOI is registered with DataCite for the Hugging Face dataset csoai/gspc-board (licence CC-BY-4.0). It is the DOI that /CITATION.cff names as the one to cite.

@misc{council_of_ai_2026_gspc_board,
  author    = {{Council of AI}},
  title     = {{gspc-board}},
  year      = {2026},
  publisher = {Hugging Face},
  doi       = {10.57967/hf/10114},
  url       = {https://huggingface.co/datasets/csoai/gspc-board},
  note      = {Dataset. Council of AI is the public name of CSOAI Ltd, UK Companies House 16939677. Measurement, not certification.}
}

Citing the live board

The board changes as measurements run. To cite a reading of it, cite the URL with the as_of value the payload carries and the date you read it: https://councilof.ai/api/gspc.

  • BibTeX with today's access date: /cite/gspc
  • CSL-JSON: /cite/gspc?format=csl
  • Both, plus a plain-text reference: /cite/gspc?format=json

The methodology record DOI that the board payload names (10.5281/zenodo.21991104) does not resolve at present; its status is at /interop/zenodo-status.json. Cite the method as https://councilof.ai/methodology/.

Citing Council of AI (organisation and software)

The repository's citation file is /CITATION.cff (GitHub: CSOAI-ORG/councilof-ai, licence MIT).

@software{csoai_council_of_ai,
  author  = {{CSOAI Ltd}},
  title   = {{Council of AI}},
  version = {v0.1.0-evidence},
  date    = {2026-09-12},
  url     = {https://councilof.ai},
  note    = {Source: https://github.com/CSOAI-ORG/councilof-ai. Licence: MIT.}
}

CFF reference block

To list the board in your own CITATION.cff, add this under references:.

references:
  - type: dataset
    title: "gspc-board"
    authors:
      - name: "Council of AI"
    publisher:
      name: "Hugging Face"
    year: 2026
    doi: "10.57967/hf/10114"
    url: "https://huggingface.co/datasets/csoai/gspc-board"
    license: CC-BY-4.0
