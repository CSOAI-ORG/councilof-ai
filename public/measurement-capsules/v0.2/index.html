A published batch is a signed record over a set of capsules; the capsule to batch root is an RFC 9162 audit path, and each capsule's own id is the SHA-256 of its JCS serialisation. Two kinds are outside the measurement.* kind grammar used by the profile this corpus is being written against: the operations batch (236 capsules) and the single measurement.claim_watch capsule are recorded here under their published names, not renamed.

endpoints/00.json to endpoints/ff.json: 256 shards of the capsules about each measured endpoint, keyed by the first two hex digits of the SHA-256 of the normalised endpoint URL.

Verify

  • In the browser: /verify-server/ returns every capsule held about one endpoint and re-derives its inclusion.
  • Over MCP: the tools measurement_index, verify_capsule and server_evidence at https://councilof.ai/mcp/.
  • Offline: the builder and verifier source (Apache-2.0) is at huggingface.co/datasets/csoai/evidence-index/tree/main/source; the daily index chain is in the same dataset under measurement-index/, and the upgraded OpenTimestamps proofs under ots-upgraded/.

A valid signature shows only that the key holder signed those bytes. It does not show that any value is correct.
