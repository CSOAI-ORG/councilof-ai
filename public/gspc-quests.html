Twelve behavioural axes, each a quest over its frozen GSPC bank. Your answer is read by the same deterministic rule and scored by the same macro-F1 aggregation used to measure every model — so your score and the model's sit on one scale. The model's figure is read live from /api/gspc; a live axis compares against the model on the same bank, a frozen axis serves a retired frozen set and shows the model's figure on that same set.

Your score is local play, not a measurement. Nothing you do here writes, signs or changes any model's card. The model's figure is a signed measurement; your score lives only in this browser (localStorage) and is not an attestation about you or any system. Completing a quest is not a qualification, declares no conformity, and is not legal advice. A conformity certificate needs an accreditation chain (a national accreditation body, then a certification body under ISO/IEC 42006); CSOAI holds none and does not claim to.

Two different things, on purpose. The model column is a signed measurement read live from /api/gspc. The "your best" column is local play held in this browser. They share a scale so you can see the gap — but your number is not a measurement, is not signed, and never becomes a model's card. Clear your browser storage and your side of this table is gone; the model's card is not.

Delta is your best local macro-F1 minus the model's, on this axis. A positive delta means you beat the measured model on the items you played — a result about those items, and nothing more. "no run yet" means you have not played that axis in this browser.

Co-op quest — genuinely doable, and live below. Several people on one device answer the same axis in turn; their answers are pooled and the pooled macro-F1 is scored by the same rule and placed beside the model. That is a real "human team vs the measured model" round. It runs on this one browser only.
Networked multiplayer is not built, and this page will not pretend it is. A true "human team vs AI swarm" match — many people across the network against a live swarm — needs a match server that does not exist on this site. The swarm axis on the board is model-vs-model coordination measurement, not a live match. Until a real match route answers, that half stays honestly unbuilt.
