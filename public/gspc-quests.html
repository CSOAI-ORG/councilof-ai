GSPC QUESTSanswer the same items the models answered · graded by the same rule

Six quests, one per axis, over the frozen GSPC quest sets shipped with this page. Your answer is read by the same deterministic regex and scored by the same macro-F1 aggregation used to measure every model — so your score and the model's sit on one scale. XP is highest where the model is weakest, because that is where a human answer carries the most information.

This is training, not certification. Completing a quest records that a person demonstrated a skill. It is not an attestation about any system, it declares no conformity, and it is not legal advice. A conformity certificate needs an accreditation chain (a national accreditation body, then a certification body under ISO/IEC 42006). CSOAI holds none and does not claim to.
