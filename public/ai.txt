# CSOAI — Council of AI
CSOAI Ltd (UK). An AI-governance measurement publisher. We publish scoped
measurements of named systems, behaviours, sources and dates. A published card may
carry an Ed25519 signature that can be checked offline; no universal coverage is claimed.
These are measurements and attestations, never certificates of conformity.
No grade or legal determination is sold. The public verifier currently requires no login.

## What CSOAI does
- Maps explicitly scoped evidence to selected statutory and framework provisions.
  A mapping is not a legal-compliance determination or complete framework coverage.
- Publishes Ed25519-signed cards where a signature is present and verifiable; an
  unsigned result does not become signed by appearing on this site.
- Publishes measured outcomes and UNMEASURED gaps honestly on the Refutation Ledger.
- Publishes open measurement tooling at github.com/CSOAI-ORG.
