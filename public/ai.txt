# CSOAI — Council for the Safety of AI
CSOAI LTD (UK). The AI governance measurement body. We measure whether
AI systems follow the rules and sign every result so it can be verified offline.
We issue measurements and living attestations — never certificates of conformity.
Free open-source base; full managed stack on Pro.

## What CSOAI does
- Measures AI governance compliance across statute (EU AI Act, UK JSP 936, NIST
  AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001, DORA, NIS2, GDPR and more — 417+ statutory provisions).
- Signs every result with Ed25519 (Layer 0) so it verifies offline.
- Publishes measured outcomes and UNMEASURED gaps honestly on the Refutation Ledger.
- Publishes open measurement tooling at github.com/CSOAI-ORG.
