Impartiality and independence

A measurement body is only worth what its independence is worth. This page states the separations we hold, the pressures we have identified, and what we do about each.

One sentence. We measure; we do not certify, accredit, approve or endorse — and no payment changes a state, a rank, a delay or a correction.

The five separations

These are load-bearing. Where two of them are collapsed, the evidence stops meaning anything.

  1. Identity ≠ authority. Knowing which agent acted does not establish that it was entitled to act.
  2. Authority ≠ execution. A delegation in force is not proof that the delegated act occurred.
  3. Execution ≠ outcome. A successful API call is never treated as proof of a real-world result.
  4. Outcome ≠ compliance. A measured behaviour is not a conformity assessment.
  5. Signature ≠ truth. A signature states who signed which bytes. It never states that the content is true.

What we will not do

RefusalWhy
Certify, accredit, approve or endorse a product, model or organisationThose are acts reserved to accredited bodies and regulators. We are neither.
Issue or cover a third-party attestation (for example SOC 2, ISO certification)Only a licensed or accredited issuer can. A measurement mark cannot stand in for one.
Measure our own products as part of a comparisonWhere our own models appear in a run, they are excluded before comparison and never counted in it.
Advise on the thing we measure, for a feeConsulting on a subject you also score is the classic route to capture.
Remove or rewrite a correctionThe correction is the record. Annotations are added; history is not deleted.
Let a payer choose a state, a rank, a delay or a dateCommercial boundary, published in the correction policy.

Pressures we have identified, and what we do

PressureHow it shows upMitigation in force
FinancialA payer or funder expects a favourable result, or expects a competitor's result delayed.No payer may buy a state, rank, delay or correction removal. Revenue is published in aggregate at /api/revenue; no payer is named without consent.
AdvocacyA body wants to be measured only on the axes where it looks good.Axes are published in full, including unmeasured ones. A subject may not select the axes it is measured on.
FamiliarityThe same reviewer and the same vendor for years.Adjudicators declare interests annually and are reassigned on conflict. See the conflicts register.
IntimidationThreat of legal action intended to withdraw a true record.A published complaints route with reasons; a withdrawal must state its reason and its date. A withdrawal without a reason is a disappearance, not a correction.
CompetitionPressure to disparage a rival of a client.No comparative claim is published that is not a measured result with its frame, n and date. Absence of evidence is never published as evidence of absence.
Self-interestThe measurer is also the measured.Own models excluded from comparisons. First-party claims are held to the same statement rules as third-party claims.

Funding

Revenue is settled, non-self, and published in aggregate: distinct paying wallets, number of settlements, and settled amount, with zero-value and self settlements recorded but never counted as buyers. We do not publish a price on the board — metered amounts appear only in a payment challenge.

Where funding is grant or membership based, it is disclosed in aggregate in the annual report. No funder has editorial control over a state.

What we cannot claim

We are not accredited to ISO/IEC 17020 or ISO/IEC 17025, and nothing on this site should be read as a claim that we are. Those standards describe how an inspection or testing body organises itself; this page borrows their principles — impartiality, confidentiality, complaints, management review — because they are the right principles, not because we hold a certificate.

Where our method maps to a framework (EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO/IEC 42001), the mapping is published as relevance metadata. It is not a conformity assessment, and it does not establish that any requirement is met. EU AI Act Articles 14, 15 and 72 apply from 2 December 2027; evidence published before then is readiness, not compliance.

How to challenge our independence

If you believe a decision was influenced by a financial or personal interest, use the complaints route and say so explicitly. Independence complaints are handled by a reviewer with no involvement in the original decision, and the outcome is published in the same aggregate as every other complaint.
