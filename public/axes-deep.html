… Model-comparison · family: gspc

A 19-model fleet answers a frozen item bank, graded deterministically. TIE is TIE. Fleet means the fleet; not a single winner. Where the lead is statistically separated (McNemar p<0.05), it is reported as SEPARATED; otherwise TIE.

… Deterministic-fact · family: financial

Read from public ledgers (XRPL issuer accounts, Companies House, public statistical series) and graded by rule. No model, no fleet, no judgement. Measured is not scored. These axes never have a leader, accuracy, or separation.

Notes for the end user

  1. The lid (22 axes · 22 measured) is the full set we measure. Empty cells are not a missing feature — they are a discipline.
  2. Jail (axis 14) is a containment floor, not a certification. It carries its per-model rows verbatim from the signed living board.
  3. The 8 financial axes are fact runs, not leader scores. None has a fleet, an accuracy, or a separation determination. Measured, not scored.
  4. The two former index slots (ai-adoption-components, labour-components) are measured as component facts, never restored to any retired sticker.
  5. Every score on the board is quoted from a live signed card. A score copied from a frozen artifact is not a number; it is hearsay.
