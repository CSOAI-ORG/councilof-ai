How x402 payment works

What is x402?
An open protocol (x402.org) where an API returns HTTP 402 Payment Required with a challenge describing the amount, network, asset, and payTo address. Any x402-capable agent wallet pays the challenge, retries the request, and receives the paid artefact along with a signed receipt.
What do I pay?
USDC on Base mainnet (eip155:8453). No other token, no other chain. Pay to 0x212686404A7D1E1fD88F35eD6200c3aF7A78ae31 (Council of AI estate).
What if I'm just verifying?
Verification is free. GET any signed artefact, verify the Ed25519 signature against did:web:csoai.org#board-attestation-1, check inclusion in the Merkle root, validate against OpenTimestamps Bitcoin anchoring. All offline-capable, all free.
What does a paid receipt look like?
A JWS-signed (EdDSA) attestation from did:web:csoai.org#board-attestation-1, naming the payer wallet, the amount, the artefact resource, and the timestamp. Verifiable free at https://councilof.ai/api/receipts/verify.
Why are prices this low?
Because the marginal cost of a signed artefact is ~$0.0003 in compute. Pricing at 10-100x cost is enough to fund the mill and keep the rail live. Volume beats margin.
Can I get a volume discount?
Yes — if you're settling more than $100/month, contact nicholas@csoai.org for a bundle deal. We can pre-anchor receipts for known buyers.
