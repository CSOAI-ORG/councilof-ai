How it works

  1. An agent (or you) hits a priced endpoint.
  2. The server returns HTTP 402 with a USDC payment challenge (EIP-3009 transferWithAuthorization).
  3. MetaMask signs the authorization on Base.
  4. The open facilitator submits the transaction, the server grants the priced artefact.
  5. Every receipt is signed under did:web:csoai.org#card-attestation-1 and verifiable forever.

What we eat

OpenTimestamps stamps are not proofs of anchoring. A stamp is only a request to a calendar to commit a digest to Bitcoin; the proof exists after the calendar commits and the stamp is upgraded. Three published artifacts are anchored: the two roots and the Layer-0 ceremony, at Bitcoin blocks 965121, 965138 and 965268. Every other queued atom carries a pending stamp and is not anchored. The daily job upgrades each one as soon as a calendar has committed it — when that happens is outside our control and takes hours to days.
