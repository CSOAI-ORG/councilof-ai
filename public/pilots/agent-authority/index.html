What a scoped assessment could deliver.

The paid engagement is proposed, not an instant checkout or guaranteed live service. Every scope requires written approval and capacity.

Define one buyer-owned sandbox action

Agree exact operator, tool, resource, permitted effect, data handling and independent observation point. No secrets in public enquiry forms.

Pre-register control and failure cases

Pin authority state, revocation, expiry, wrong-scope changes, replay behaviour and criteria for an uncheckable outcome.

Run, observe and preserve failure cases

Compare dispatch decisions with the destination's own evidence; keep all failed/unknown results, revisions and environment details.

Independent review and handover

Provide a machine-readable result, method, source hashes, limitations and buyer-led reproduction. Acceptance and invoice terms are separately agreed in writing.

Independent measurement is not certification. Signing evidence can establish integrity and provenance under a trusted key; it does not establish whether an underlying safety conclusion is true. Council of AI is not claiming to operate a staffed incident-response service here.

Questions before relying on this.

Does this prove Council of AI can stop a real prompt injection?

No. The observed result is a synthetic authorisation and file-effect experiment. A real prompt-injection claim requires a paired model/runtime test with a pinned corpus, held-out attacks, benign controls, observed tool effects and measured false positives.

Can this be used with open-source and privately fine-tuned models?

The architecture is intended to be model-independent, but each model/runtime integration needs separate qualification. A checkpoint or dataset update can change risk, so measurements require explicit versions and freshness.

Are Cloudflare, Hugging Face or other vendors endorsing this work?

No vendor endorsement or formal partnership is implied. Existing open infrastructure and hosted research do not grant certification or preferred authority.

Can I buy an assessment now?

You can prepare an enquiry. There is no instant purchase, booked run or published service-level commitment. A real engagement requires staff capacity, written scope, authorised test boundaries and agreed price, privacy and acceptance terms.
