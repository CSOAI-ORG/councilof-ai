What is BlackRock's BUIDL?

BUIDL

BlackRock's USD Institutional Digital Liquidity Ltd, BUIDL, is a tokenized fund that invests in cash, U.S. Treasury Bills and Notes

Stability and Liquidity

BUIDL aims to have a stable & consistent asset value with strong liquidity

Trusted Service Providers

BlackRock is the Fund Manager & Sponsor, Securitize is the Fund Transfer Agent, and BNY Mellon is the Fund Admin & Custodian

KYC Compliant

BUIDL adheres to high KYC/AML standards and only permits token transfers to whitelisted investors

Experienced Team

Our team has deep experience in both the traditional finance and digital asset spaces, facillitating billions of dollars in transactions, building robust technical infrastructure, and providing premier client service.
