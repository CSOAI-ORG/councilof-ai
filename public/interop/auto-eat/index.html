How the pipeline works

  • Discover: append genuinely new identifiers to the frozen queue as DISCOVERED.
  • Probe: perform bounded, read-only reachability checks and classify outcomes without treating HTTP reachability as model quality.
  • Stage: retain small unsigned evidence atoms for review. Staging is not measurement.
  • Review and sign: the existing owner-controlled OIDC workflow and human release gate remain separate. Nothing is MEASURED merely because it was discovered, reachable or staged.

Interpretation rules

  • DISCOVERED means an identifier entered the queue.
  • LIVE means the specific probe observed a reachable/resolvable endpoint at its observation time—not that the model works or is safe.
  • UNMEASURED remains the default until the applicable evidence, approval, signature and verification requirements are satisfied.
  • A signature field, historical signed-batch reference or valid hash is not, by itself, proof that the current record is signed and verified.
