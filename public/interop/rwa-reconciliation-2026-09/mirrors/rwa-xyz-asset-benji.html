BENJI

BENJI represents one share of the Franklin OnChain U.S. Government Money Fund, a regulated money market fund backed by government securities, cash, and repurchase agreements. Launched in 2021, BENJI is the world’s first and only U.S.-registered mutual fund using blockchain-integrated technology to process transactions and record share ownership fully onchain.
