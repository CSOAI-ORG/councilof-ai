# Regulatory open doors — September 2026

Official-source registry of public consultations, application windows and open-source contribution surfaces that are relevant to CSOAI's measurement work.

This registry does not claim membership, endorsement, partnership, regulatory approval or successful submission. `OPEN` means the named official page describes an open route as of `last_checked_at`. Every route must be checked again immediately before a contribution is sent.

CSOAI's appropriate contribution is an evidence gift: a schema, reproducible measurement, signed record, crosswalk or correction trail. Generic promotion is out of scope.
