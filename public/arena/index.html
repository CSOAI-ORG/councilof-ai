GSPC Training Arenas

One arena per axis, for the axes listed below. Each arena serves the real frozen items from the published benchmark, marks against the published key, shows you the provision behind every answer, and scores you against the AI models we measured on the identical items.

The arenas below are a training set, not the board. The board’s own count is read live from /api/gspc — a slot count and a measured count are different numbers and both travel together. See the board.

Training, not certification. CSOAI is an independent measurement body and does not accredit anyone. Completing an arena confers no status and is not a conformity assessment.

Every axis in the table below was measured on the same 19-model board (2026-08-12, per-item rows, 0 transport errors). That is a statement about these rows only — it is not a claim that every axis on the board has been measured, and several have not. "Board leader" is the highest point estimate — whoever leads, ours or a base model. SEPARATED means the lead is statistically real (McNemar p<0.05 on discordant items); TIE means it is not. A tie is not a win.
