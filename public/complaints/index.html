Complaints and appeals

We publish records that name real organisations. Anyone named — or anyone relying on what we publish — is entitled to challenge it. This page states how, what happens next, and what this route cannot do.

The short version. Write to complaints@csoai.org. We acknowledge within 2 working days, decide within 10, and correct a published error within 5 of deciding. If you disagree with the decision you may appeal to a second reviewer within 20 working days. We publish the number of complaints received and how we met these times, in aggregate, every quarter.

What you can complain about

GroundExample
Factual errorWe recorded a claim you did not make, quoted you out of context, or attributed a statement to the wrong entity.
MeasurementOur method, item bank, sampling frame, grading or arithmetic is wrong, or the run is not reproducible from what we published.
ProcessWe missed a stated deadline, changed a state without evidence, or published without the review this standard requires.
DescriptionWe described your relationship to us in a way that implies endorsement, partnership or certification you have not given.

What this route cannot do

  • It cannot determine regulatory compliance. That is for a regulator or a court. We measure; we do not certify.
  • It cannot remove a correction. Corrections are the record. We may annotate one; we do not delete history.
  • It cannot buy silence or a better outcome. No payer, partner, member, measured entity or contributor may purchase a state, a rank, a delay or the removal of correction history. This is stated in our correction policy and it is not negotiable.
  • It cannot adjudicate a private dispute between you and a third party, or compel anyone to produce records they do not publish.

How to complain

Email complaints@csoai.org with:

  • the URL of the record you are challenging, and the date you saw it;
  • what you say is wrong, in one sentence per point;
  • the evidence you rely on — a link, a document, or a statement of what a correct record would say;
  • whether you want the record corrected, annotated, or withdrawn, and on what ground.

You do not need a lawyer, an account, or our permission. If you are named in a record and want it anonymised, say so and say why; we will treat that as a separate request.

What happens, in order

StepTimeWhat you get
Acknowledge2 working daysA named human, a reference number, and a statement of what we understand the complaint to be.
Triage5 working daysWhether we accept the ground, and whether the record is marked DISPUTED while we look. DISPUTED is published; it is not a finding.
Decide10 working daysA written decision with reasons and the evidence we relied on. If we need longer we say so, once, with a date.
Correct5 working days after decisionA correction entry in the public ledger, citing the original record. If we were wrong, we say so in the same words we use for our own errors.
Appeal20 working days to lodgeA reviewer who was not part of the first decision. Their decision is final for us, and their dissent — if any — is published with it.

Who decides

A named adjudicator, published on the conflicts register. An adjudicator may not decide a complaint about an organisation that employs them, funds them, or that they advise. Where that applies, the case is reassigned and the reassignment is recorded.

What we publish

Every confirmed complaint becomes a correction entry. Quarterly we publish: complaints received, grounds accepted and rejected, median and worst time at each step, and how many we got wrong. We do not name complainants without consent.
