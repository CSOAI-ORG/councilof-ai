Complaints and appeals

We publish records that name real organisations. Anyone named — or anyone relying on what we publish — is entitled to challenge it. This page states how, what happens next, and what this route cannot do.

The short version. Write to contact@csoai.org, the one mailbox for complaints, disputes and corrections. We acknowledge within 3 business days and send a reasoned decision within 20 business days of receipt; if we need longer, we tell you before day 20 and give a revised date. These are the times on appeals and disputes, which is the same route. Today one person, the owner of CSOAI Ltd, handles every complaint and every appeal: the reviewer and adjudicator roles are not filled yet.

What you can complain about

GroundExample
Factual errorWe recorded a claim you did not make, quoted you out of context, or attributed a statement to the wrong entity.
MeasurementOur method, item bank, sampling frame, grading or arithmetic is wrong, or the run is not reproducible from what we published.
ProcessWe missed a stated deadline, changed a state without evidence, or published without the review this standard requires.
DescriptionWe described your relationship to us in a way that implies endorsement, partnership or certification you have not given.

What this route cannot do

  • It cannot determine regulatory compliance. That is for a regulator or a court. We measure; we do not certify.
  • It cannot remove a correction. Corrections are the record. We may annotate one; we do not delete history.
  • It cannot buy silence or a better outcome. No payer, partner, member, measured entity or contributor may purchase a state, a rank, a delay or the removal of correction history. This is stated in our correction policy and it is not negotiable.
  • It cannot adjudicate a private dispute between you and a third party, or compel anyone to produce records they do not publish.

How to complain

Email contact@csoai.org with:

  • the URL of the record you are challenging, and the date you saw it;
  • what you say is wrong, in one sentence per point;
  • the evidence you rely on — a link, a document, or a statement of what a correct record would say;
  • whether you want the record corrected, annotated, or withdrawn, and on what ground.

You do not need a lawyer, an account, or our permission. If you are named in a record and want it anonymised, say so and say why; we will treat that as a separate request.

What happens, in order

StepTimeWhat you get
Acknowledge3 business daysA reply from the owner of CSOAI Ltd with a reference number and a statement of what we understand the complaint to be.
Triage5 business daysWhether we accept the ground, and whether the record is marked DISPUTED while we look. DISPUTED is published; it is not a finding.
Decide20 business daysA written decision with reasons and the evidence we relied on. If we need longer, we say so before day 20, once, with a date.
Correct5 business days after the decisionA correction entry in the public ledger, citing the original record. If we were wrong, we say so in the same words we use for our own errors.
Appeal20 business days to lodgeUntil an appeals reviewer is appointed, the owner reviews the appeal too, and the reply says so. It is not an independent second instance.

Who decides

Today, the owner of CSOAI Ltd. The conflicts register lists the complaints reviewer, the appeals reviewer and both adjudicator roles as OPEN_SLOT: none of them is filled. When a role is filled, the register names the person, and they may not decide a complaint about an organisation that employs them, funds them, or that they advise. Where that applies, the case is reassigned and the reassignment is recorded.

What we publish

Every confirmed complaint becomes a correction entry in the corrections ledger. A count of complaints received, and of how long each step took, is not published yet. We do not name complainants without consent.

Status vocabulary used here and across the site: VERIFIED · REPORTED · OPEN_SLOT · DISPUTED · CORRECTED · WITHDRAWN · UNCHECKABLE. Measurement is not certification.
