# EU AI Act screening helper (deterministic, text-only)

POST https://councilof.ai/api/assess with a system description. Keyword matches name the Annex III categories and Article 5 patterns a human should inspect; no model is consulted. It does not establish legal tier, applicability, exceptions or compliance, and an empty description is UNMEASURED, not a low-risk finding. When no signing key is provisioned the report says UNSIGNED out loud.

## How to call it

This is an A2A skill of the Council of AI measurement agent (agent card: https://councilof.ai/.well-known/agent-card.json).
Send `SendMessage` to `POST https://councilof.ai/api/a2a` with header `A2A-Version: 1.0`; `params.message` carries a `messageId` and `parts`,
and one structured part selects the skill: `{"data": {"skill": "eu-ai-act-screen", "input": {...}}, "mediaType": "application/json"}`.
Calling it needs no account, key or registration (see https://councilof.ai/auth.md).

## Examples

- SendMessage with Part.data {"skill":"eu-ai-act-screen","input":{"system":"<what the system does and for whom>"}}
- POST /api/assess {"system": "<what the system does and for whom>"}
