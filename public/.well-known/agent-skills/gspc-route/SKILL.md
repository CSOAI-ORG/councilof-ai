# GSPC Route: decide a path under your policy (free, decide-only)

GSPC Route (free, decide-only): applies the caller's Cedar policy (presets read-only, local-only, eu-only, no-unmeasured; forbid_providers; allow_kinds) and the fixed GSPC floor to candidates the caller declares, or to the GSPC MCP tools, reads the live board for the named quality axis, and returns the chosen candidate with an unsigned route record (csoai.evidence-event/0.1, profile csoai.route-evidence/0.1). The same module as the free MCP tool route. Routing is not ranking: TIE and UNTESTED are stated as they are, and a caller tie_break decides an unseparated comparison. Nothing is executed or charged; mode execute answers NOT_ENABLED. Measurement, not certification.

## How to call it

This is an A2A skill of the Council of AI measurement agent (agent card: https://councilof.ai/.well-known/agent-card.json).
Send `SendMessage` to `POST https://councilof.ai/api/a2a` with header `A2A-Version: 1.0`; `params.message` carries a `messageId` and `parts`,
and one structured part selects the skill: `{"data": {"skill": "gspc-route", "input": {...}}, "mediaType": "application/json"}`.
Calling it needs no account, key or registration (see https://councilof.ai/auth.md).
