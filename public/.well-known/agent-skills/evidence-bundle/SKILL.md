# Evidence bundle preview: signed cards relevant to one obligation (free)

For ONE obligation (article-50, article-53 GPAI transparency, dora or cra) and an optional subject: the obligation record, its counsel-gate status and the already-signed measurement cards that are relevant to it, read from GET /api/evidence-bundle. The same module as the free MCP tool evidence_bundle_preview. Relevant-to is never a determination: no answer says satisfied or not satisfied, and zero relevant cards is answered EMPTY. Article 53 output is evidence for review, not a legal determination. The assembled OSCAL bundle is the x402 MCP tool evidence_bundle on https://councilof.ai/mcp; this skill never charges. Not a conformity assessment, certification, audit opinion or compliance determination; grants no status.

## How to call it

This is an A2A skill of the Council of AI measurement agent (agent card: https://councilof.ai/.well-known/agent-card.json).
Send `SendMessage` to `POST https://councilof.ai/api/a2a` with header `A2A-Version: 1.0`; `params.message` carries a `messageId` and `parts`,
and one structured part selects the skill: `{"data": {"skill": "evidence-bundle", "input": {...}}, "mediaType": "application/json"}`.
Calling it needs no account, key or registration (see https://councilof.ai/auth.md).

- SendMessage with Part.data {"skill":"evidence-bundle","input":{"obligation":"article-53","subject":"gpt"}}
