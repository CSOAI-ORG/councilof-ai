# MEOK Hive axis specialist audits (free, unsigned)

Read the bounded specialist audit for one exact GSPC axis, or the full index with an empty input. Every axis has its own logical MEOK Hive cluster, mission, evidence checks, negative controls, improvement proposals and human reviewer role. RSI is recommendation-only: no ASI claim, no self-modification, no spend, no deployment and no apply operation. Every proposal stays PROPOSED_NOT_APPLIED under a MEOK H.I.M.-compatible HOLD gate. The existing axis_<id> MCP tools remain the live measurement readers; these audits are unsigned analysis of a captured board, not new measurement or certification.

## How to call it

This is an A2A skill of the Council of AI measurement agent (agent card: https://councilof.ai/.well-known/agent-card.json).
Send `SendMessage` to `POST https://councilof.ai/api/a2a` with header `A2A-Version: 1.0`; `params.message` carries a `messageId` and `parts`,
and one structured part selects the skill: `{"data": {"skill": "axis-specialist", "input": {...}}, "mediaType": "application/json"}`.
Calling it needs no account, key or registration (see https://councilof.ai/auth.md).

## Examples

- SendMessage with Part.data {"skill":"axis-specialist","input":{}}
- SendMessage with Part.data {"skill":"axis-specialist","input":{"axis":"governance"}}
- GET /api/axis-specialists/governance
