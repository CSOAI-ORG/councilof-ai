# Article 50 provenance detection (free)

Verify a C2PA-style signed manifest for AI-generated content (EU AI Act Article 50) at POST https://councilof.ai/api/detect. Deterministically checks the Ed25519-signed metadata layer and the IPTC/schema.org digitalSourceType; returns AI_MARKED / NOT_AI_MARKED / UNVERIFIABLE with a signed verdict receipt (in-toto/DSSE when a board key is bound). The imperceptible-watermark layer we cannot see is declared, never claimed. Free for all; unrestricted for authorities, media, fact-checkers, researchers and civil society. Not certification.

## How to call it

This is an A2A skill of the Council of AI measurement agent (agent card: https://councilof.ai/.well-known/agent-card.json).
Send `SendMessage` to `POST https://councilof.ai/api/a2a` with header `A2A-Version: 1.0`; `params.message` carries a `messageId` and `parts`,
and one structured part selects the skill: `{"data": {"skill": "article50-detect", "input": {...}}, "mediaType": "application/json"}`.
Calling it needs no account, key or registration (see https://councilof.ai/auth.md).

## Examples

- SendMessage with Part.data {"skill":"article50-detect","input":{"manifest":{"claim":{}}}}
- POST /api/detect {"manifest":{...},"asset_hash":"<optional-64-hex>"}
