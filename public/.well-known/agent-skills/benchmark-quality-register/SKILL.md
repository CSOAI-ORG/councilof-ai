# Benchmark-quality register

Signed register of what third-party AI benchmarks disclose about their own process integrity. Deterministic predicates only — no language model judges. Records are unsolicited; nothing on the register is certification or endorsement.

## How to call it

This is an A2A skill of the Council of AI measurement agent (agent card: https://councilof.ai/.well-known/agent-card.json).
Send `SendMessage` to `POST https://councilof.ai/api/a2a` with header `A2A-Version: 1.0`; `params.message` carries a `messageId` and `parts`,
and one structured part selects the skill: `{"data": {"skill": "benchmark-quality-register", "input": {...}}, "mediaType": "application/json"}`.
Calling it needs no account, key or registration (see https://councilof.ai/auth.md).
