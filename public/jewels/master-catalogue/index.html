What this is not

  • Not a certification, a rank, a grade or a score.
  • Not a statement that any axis is MEASURED. The board carries its own status; this catalogue neither moves it nor pre-empts the decision that moves it.
  • Not a claim that any captured artefact is true. It records what was read, when, and what came back.
  • Not a completeness claim about the estate. It catalogues every jewel the state table names, plus the missing jewels the 2026-10-27 catalogue found. A jewel on no list would not appear here.

The three laws

  1. A signed artefact is not a true artefact. Signature and presence prove where bytes came from; they never prove the number inside.
  2. The measurement must not manufacture the evidence it measures. Where finishing a jewel would mean minting the evidence, the jewel stays open and the blocker is named.
  3. Absence is a state. A refused URL, an unreachable endpoint, an unmeasured axis: each is recorded in its own state and never converted to a zero, a failure or a score.
