Three things you can do here

  1. Verify any card — paste a card-v0 attestation, get its signature, hash, and anchors.
  2. Bulk history — the full 50-card root + the bulk-signed stem. Read it offline.
  3. Inclusion proof — for any card, get the merkle path from the leaf to the root, signed under did:web:csoai.org.

The anchors

Every measurement is bound to three independent live anchors. Re-publishing from another vendor requires rebuilding all three. A fourth, Bitcoin OpenTimestamps, is stamped but not yet anchored: a stamp is a request to a calendar, and it becomes a proof only once the calendar commits it to a block.

  1. HuggingFace Hub — huggingface.co/csoai — 60+ datasets, 42 Spaces, 3 models.
  2. Sigstore Rekor — public witness receipt at /api/rekor.
  3. Corrections ledger — public witness at /api/corrections (39 rows live).
  4. Bitcoin OpenTimestamps (planned) — digests are stamped; the published roots carry block attestations, queued atoms do not yet. State at /api/state.
