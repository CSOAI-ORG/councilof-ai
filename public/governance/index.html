CSOAI Governance Fabric

One governance truth, many site experiences. Measurement, never certification.

This surface defines the owner-authorised, permissionless-public, external-governed and private/restricted lanes used across Council of AI / CSOAI sites.

Canonical machine-readable records

  • Governance manifest
  • Authority model
  • Committee and participation registry
  • Contribution routes
  • Correction policy
  • Work Evidence Capsule schema

Boundary

External foundations, projects, committees, vendors and standards bodies remain independently governed. Measurement or contribution does not imply control, partnership, endorsement or certification.

Correction

Material changes to authority, identity, skills, runtimes, sources, evaluations, security state or payment semantics can invalidate dependent claims and trigger bounded re-verification.
