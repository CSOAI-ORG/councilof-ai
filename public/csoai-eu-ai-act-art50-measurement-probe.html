What does Article 50 actually require?

Three duties apply to every human-facing AI and synthetic-content product on the EU market — including UK firms serving EU customers:

  • 50(1) Chatbot disclosure — users must know they are interacting with an AI. The duty attaches to the actual user-facing copy, not your privacy policy.
  • 50(2) Machine-readable marking — every synthetic audio, image, video or text output must carry a machine-readable, detectable mark. A human-visible watermark is not compliance.
  • 50(4) Deepfake labelling — deepfake-class outputs must be labelled, with artistic/parody use registered under the 50(3) exemption evidence class.

Exemptions under 50(3) are not a shield — they are an evidence class. You must be able to demonstrate that the exemption applies.
