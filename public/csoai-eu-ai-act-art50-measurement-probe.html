What does Article 50 actually require?

Article 50 contains different duties for providers and deployers. Applicability depends on the system, output, role and relevant exceptions; a homepage scan cannot determine that scope:

  • 50(1) Chatbot disclosure — providers of systems that interact directly with people inform them of the AI interaction, subject to applicable exceptions, including where this is obvious.
  • 50(2) Machine-readable marking — providers ensure in-scope synthetic outputs are machine-readable and detectable as AI-generated or manipulated, subject to statutory qualifications. A visible label alone does not establish that technical property.
  • 50(4) Deepfake labelling — deployers disclose in-scope deepfake content. Paragraph 4 limits how disclosure is made for evidently artistic, creative, satirical, fictional or analogous works; it does not create a paragraph 3 exemption register.

Paragraph 3 concerns emotion recognition and biometric categorisation. Any CSOAI inventory of scope decisions is an engagement record, not a legally mandated exemption register. Your legal adviser determines applicable duties and exceptions.

Detector coverage is limited to agreed inputs and supported techniques. Undetected markers, unavailable sources and untested methods remain explicit; a detection result is not a whole-system compliance finding.

How do I measure my Article 50 exposure?

With instruments, not memos. The Council of AI Probe suite runs deterministic checks per surface and issues signed measurement credentials — reproducible statements of what our instruments observed, verifiable independently. Measurement credentials, never certification.

  • Marking Probe (→ 50(2)) — per-output check for machine-readable marks (provenance manifests, metadata, watermark detectability). Result per item: PRESENT-MACHINE-READABLE / PRESENT-HUMAN-ONLY / ABSENT.
  • Disclosure Probe (→ 50(1)) — verification of user-facing disclosure at the interaction boundary.
  • Deepfake Label Check (→ 50(4)) — observations of the agreed disclosure surface, with any paragraph 4 artistic treatment documented as a scope decision.
  • Evidence ledger — agreed observations, source identities and verification instructions. Article 12 requirements are separate and are not inferred from this engagement.
  • Quarterly re-measurement — marks drift; a March pass is not a June pass.
