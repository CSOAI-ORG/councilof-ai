What this axis measures

A dated, read-only boundary probe of remote MCP tool-call servers: whether an unauthorised extra argument is refused at the tool boundary. It does not observe backend effects or establish a vendor's security posture.

Why it matters

The GSPC board reports its current count in totals.public_count. A dated snapshot is useful for reproduction when its date and scope stay attached. Read the live board for the latest state.

Scope and exclusions
