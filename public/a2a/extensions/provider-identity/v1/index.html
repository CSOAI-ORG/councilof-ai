A2A extension: provider-identity/v1

The legal entity behind provider.organization, stated so that a reader can check it at the public register.

Extension URI
https://councilof.ai/a2a/extensions/provider-identity/v1/
Status
Declared on the Council of AI agent card. required: false: a client that ignores it loses nothing.
Maintainer
CSOAI Ltd (Council of AI), UK company 16939677

Why an extension

A2A v1.0 AgentProvider has two fields, url and organization. Neither holds a company number or a register entry, and adding a field to provider would put a key on the card that a proto-based reader drops. AgentExtension.params (proto field 4, a google.protobuf.Struct) already exists, so the identity is carried there.

    "website": "https://councilof.ai"

} ] } }

How to check it

Open the registryUrl. The public register is the authority, not this page and not the card. The register entry for company 16939677 names CSOAI LTD, a private limited company: Companies House, 16939677.

What it is, and is not

It identifies the publisher. It does not accredit, certify or endorse anything the publisher measures or publishes. Council of AI publishes measurement, not certification.
