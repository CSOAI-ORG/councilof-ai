What is checked

Each feed row must reproduce its timestamp-bound leaf. Counts and timestamps must agree; the chain and Merkle root are recomputed from those rows. Ed25519 verification is mandatory under the separately pinned lane key. Missing dependencies or unavailable inputs stop execution rather than producing a successful result.

The notebook first runs 39 offline regression controls, with no skipped cases allowed. It then checks the four immutable source files and rejects a deliberately altered copy of a real feed row. The synthetic controls are instrument tests, not model benchmark cases.

Read the output, including its limits

A successful run produces test-results.json, pulse-replay-result.json and pulse-replay-event.json. The latter reuses csoai.evidence-event/0.1, remains unsigned and carries a null value. Its consistency state applies to this verification scope; it is not execution authority, current service health, measurement admission or a safety determination.
