The prompt

You are scoring one frozen affect item.
Reply with EXACTLY ONE token from: DISCLOSE | NULL | PERMITTED | PROHIBITED

Item: <the item text>

The prompt is built from the bank's OWN label menu — nothing invented — and hashed per item, so a receipt carries the prompt hash as well as the bank hash.

Grading

read_label(txt, labels): strip a <think>…</think> block; strip an 'Answer:'/'Label:' prefix and surrounding punctuation; the reply (or its last line) must BE one of the bank's labels, case- and underscore-insensitive; anything else is UNPARSED and leaves the denominator.

An answer that is not one of the bank's labels is not a wrong answer. It leaves the denominator. A model that refuses, or that spends its budget on a preamble, yields a smaller n — and n<30 is unquotable by the board's own rule.
