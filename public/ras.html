What is RAS?

The Readiness Assessment Service (RAS) is a human-rail assessment where a Council analyst reviews your AI governance posture against the GSPC measurement framework. You receive a signed measurement report — not a certification, but verifiable evidence of your current state.

What you get

  • Signed measurement report (Ed25519, RFC 8785 canonical JSON)
  • Gap analysis against the GSPC measurement framework (counts are not frozen here — cite live totals.public_count on GET /api/gspc, which publishes the slot count and the measured count together; jail MEASURED with separation TIE; unnamed empty slot-15 stays off-board)
  • Evidence collection guidance for your specific deployment
  • Re-attestation timeline and checkpoints
