Abstract: Can you explain the whole project and its expected outcome(s)?

CSOAI publishes the only signed, anchored, open-source AI measurement board. The GSPC board measures AI behaviour across 22 axes (14 model-comparison + 8 deterministic-fact). Every measurement is signed under did:web:csoai.org#card-attestation-1 (Ed25519), witnessed in Sigstore Rekor, and OTS-anchored to Bitcoin.

OUTCOME: a 4-anchor machine that gives the EU AI Office, member state regulators (ICO, CNIL, BfK, AgID, AESIA), insurers (Lloyd's syndicates, Munich Re, Zurich), and vendors (Meta, Mistral, OpenAI, Anthropic) an independently verifiable measurement substrate for AI compliance — without trusting any single party.

Deliverable: signed cards + OSCAL evidence bundles + A2A/MCP/x402 doors that any agent can read.

How does this project differ from existing ones?

There are no comparable open-source projects that combine:
- A signed, anchored measurement board (vs unsigned benchmarks like EleutherAI's lm-evaluation-harness or MLCommons)
- A public corrections ledger (vs private vendor QA loops)
- A 4-anchor machine (vs single-anchor solutions like IPFS CIDs or Arweave only)
- A permissionless RaaS over x402 (vs SaaS seats with Stripe)

The closest analogue is the model-transparency (model-signing) project from OpenSSF, but it signs the model files, not the measurement results. We sign the measurement results. The substrate is the substrate of AI governance, not the artifact.

What are the main challenges?

The hardest part of the work is the doctrinal discipline: we must NEVER invent a measurement we did not actually make. The doctrine (measurement, not certification; anyone can re-check; UNCHECKABLE is honest) is the moat.

The second-hardest part is the forge-of-trust between the issuer (the operator) and the verifier (anyone): a signed card must be verifiable offline, with no phone-home, no API call. The /gspc-verify portal uses WebCrypto in the browser.

The third is the metered rail — every priced artefact is metered by x402 over USDC on Base, no Stripe, no accounts. The ONE click that unlocks settlement is X402_FACILITATOR_URL=https://facilitator.payai.network.

How will the project live on after the funding?

The corrections ledger is the public-write part: anyone can submit a correction in plain English, signed under CSOAI's public key, and added to the ledger with full provenance. Affected communities, journalists, regulators, vendors — anyone can dispute a measurement.

The board is free. The corrections ledger is free. The verification is free. The metered rail (issuance, evidence bundles, data feeds, proof bundles, custom audits) is priced in USDC on Base.

The HuggingFace org csoai carries 60+ datasets + 42 Spaces. The Chrome extension + Grok plugin + Hermes skill + MCP server reach developers, journalists, and regulators without a per-user account.
