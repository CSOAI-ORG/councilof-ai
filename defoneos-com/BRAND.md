# DEFONEOS — Brand Guidelines

## The Seal (primary mark) — `seal.svg`
A formal, US-government-agency-style circular seal (DOT/DOJ lineage): denticled gold ring,
"DEFONEOS" curved across the top in serif caps, "SOVEREIGN · DEFENCE · INTELLIGENCE" curved
across the bottom, and a central emblem — a meridian globe inside a sovereign hexagon (Layer-0)
crowned by a five-point star. Bold, official, instantly reads as a national-grade authority.

- **Use** as: favicon (all pages), nav/header lockup beside the DEFONEOS wordmark, the COP brand
  mark, document letterhead, and the verifiable authority badge.
- **Min size** 28 px. Below that, use a simplified roundel (ring + star + globe, no ring text).
- Works on **cream** (landing) and **dark navy** (dome) backgrounds — never on busy imagery.
- Clear space ≥ 25% of the seal diameter on all sides.

## Colours
- **Navy** `#0a1226` (authority, ground) · **Gold** `#c9a84c` (sovereign, ring/emblem) ·
  **Cream** `#f1ead9` / `#faf8f3` (letterhead, landing)
- Dome doctrine accents: **SIGIL/HORUS cyan** `#3ef0ff` (West) · **DORADO red** `#ff4d4d` (East)
- AQI/threat ramp: green `#00e07a` → amber `#ffd23b` → orange `#ff8c1a` → red `#ff3b3b` → purple `#a06cd5`

## Typography
- **Wordmark / headings**: Inter / Geist, 600–700, tight tracking. "DEFONEOS" in caps.
- **Seal ring text**: Georgia serif caps (formal seal convention — the ONE place caps + serif live).
- **Labels / data**: JetBrains Mono.

## Voice
Governed, sovereign, exact, honest. No hype. Mottos: **"SOVEREIGN · DEFENCE · INTELLIGENCE"**,
**"GOVERNED · SIGNED"**, "every action signed, every byte auditable, no foreign API calls."
Two faces of the Sovereign: **HORUS** (West, democratic/SIGIL) ⇄ **DORADO** (East, centralized).

## Quality bar
Match or exceed `csoai-v2-app.vercel.app` — deep multi-section pages, gradient hero typography,
social-proof + live badges, a floating Sovereign assistant, rich nav. DEFONEOS adds the seal and
the live governed globe as its signature.
