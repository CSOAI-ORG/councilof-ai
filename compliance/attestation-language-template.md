## Positioning (public)

We publish **unsolicited + permissionless** cryptographically signed measurement opinions about public artifacts (issuer addresses, contracts, on-chain stats).  

We are **not** an NRSRO / EU CRA. We do **not** issue, custody, or tokenize assets. We do **not** sell grades or placements.

## Suggested disclaimer block (adapt with counsel)

> This measurement card is a **statement of opinion as of its `as_of` timestamp**, produced by deterministic public-artifact grading and signed (SHA-256 + Ed25519). It is **not** a statement of fact, **not** a credit rating, **not** investment advice, and **not** a recommendation to buy, hold, or sell any security or crypto-asset. It does **not** address suitability. CSOAI Ltd is not acting as a fiduciary or investment adviser.  
> The subject issuer did **not** solicit or pay for this card. Acceptance of any on-chain Credential (if attached) is optional and does not change the opinion character of the card.
