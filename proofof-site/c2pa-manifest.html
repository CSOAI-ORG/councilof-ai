What it is

C2PA is the open technical standard for content provenance. It cryptographically signs every artifact (text, image, video, audio) with a manifest that records:

  • Who created it (Ed25519 sovereign wallet)
  • What tool produced it (model ID + version + LoRA rank)
  • When (UTC timestamp, anchored to charter sha256)
  • How (RAG sources, Care Floor score, BFT-33 vote count)
