Why a bridge?

Without owem-bridge, each OWEM (compliance/defense/intuition/voice) would independently consume the shared core. This creates drift — small differences in how each OWEM reads charter_sha256, RAG facts, or BFT quorum math.

The bridge ensures all 4 OWEMs read from the same canonical source. Zero drift. BFT quorum math is identical across all 4. Charter hash is identical. RAG facts are identical.
