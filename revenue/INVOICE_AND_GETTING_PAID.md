# Getting paid — invoice + the 2 simplest ways to take the money (no gated rails needed)

You do NOT need the broken Stripe loop to bill £750. Two clean options that work today:

1. **Bank transfer (fastest, zero fees):** put your business account details on the invoice, "payable on
   receipt." Most B2B clients pay by transfer anyway. Done.
2. **Stripe Payment Link (2 minutes, card payments):** dashboard.stripe.com → Payment Links → New →
   "AI Act Readiness Check £750" (and a separate "AI Compliance Monitoring £299/mo" recurring one). This is a
   *fresh, standalone* link — nothing to do with the gated app loop. Paste it in the invoice/email. Card + Apple Pay.

For the **£299/mo retainer**, the Stripe recurring Payment Link is ideal (auto-charges monthly = real MRR,
the thing that refills the hole).
