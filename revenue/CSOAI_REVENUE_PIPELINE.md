- **£10M+ ARR** = growth-stage / IPO-track *narrative*. Needs category leadership in EU AI compliance tooling + enterprise logos + net-revenue-retention >110%.
> Honest note: "IPO" is a multi-year outcome of sustained ARR growth + retention, not a near-term event. The grounded goal is **£1M ARR**, which the open channels + outreach can credibly reach.
