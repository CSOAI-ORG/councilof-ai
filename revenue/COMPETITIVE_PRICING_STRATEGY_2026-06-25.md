## The verdict (answers "what should we charge")
1. **We are already structurally cheaper — and transparency IS the wedge.** Every competitor hides price behind sales. *Publishing* £/mo is itself the differentiator. Keep it public.
2. **Self-serve (per-MCP £29–79, Pro £99): keep cheap, don't agonise.** It's a land price ~10× below comps; its job is signups, not margin. **→ Pro £99 is fine. Stop blocking on it.**
3. **The margin is in packs + assessment + enterprise — and here we're UNDER-priced.** Room to anchor up *while still being "cheaper than Vanta":*
   - Security pack £199 → **£249–299** (still ½ of Drata standard).
   - **Defence pack £499/mo** = good (≈ LowerPlane, ⅕ of Vanta) — this is the SKU for the King's defense/intel buyer.
   - **Assessment £4,950** = perfectly anchored — sell it as *"a signed, independently-verifiable assessment for less than half of Vanta's annual."*
   - **Enterprise/multi-MCP defence deals: price £10k–30k/yr** — still under Vanta's $28–40k, huge margin vs our cost.
4. **The "100× more effective" is the value story, not the price:** competitors sell **point-in-time, self-attested** snapshots. MEOK is **continuous runtime enforcement + Ed25519-signed, offline-verifiable** attestations. *The proof is signed, not claimed.* That justifies cheaper-AND-better without looking like a discount brand.
