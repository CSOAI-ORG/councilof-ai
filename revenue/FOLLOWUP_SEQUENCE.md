# Follow-Up Sequence — where most of the £ actually comes from
**Rule of thumb: the first email gets ~1-3% reply; the full sequence gets 5-9%. Most "yes"es land on touch 2-4. Send these to non-repliers only, same thread, from nicholas@csoai.org.**

Use for BOTH the direct (Synthesia/ElevenLabs/etc.) and partner outreach — adapt the [bracket].

---

## TOUCH 5 — +21 days (the break-up — surprisingly high reply rate)
**Subject: Should I close your file?**

Hi [name], I don't want to keep landing in your inbox — I'll assume Article 50 isn't a priority for [Company] right now and close this off. If that's wrong and you'd like the free readiness check or a quick call, just reply "yes" and I'll sort it. All the best either way.

---

## Why the break-up works
"Should I close your file?" gets the highest reply rate of any touch — loss-aversion + an easy one-word out. Many "yes"es come here from people who meant to reply earlier.

## Cadence discipline
- Touches at day 0, 3, 7, 14, 21. Stop on any reply (switch to the close playbook).
- Keep follow-ups SHORTER than the first email.
- One thread per prospect — never restart from scratch.
- Track touch number per prospect in your sheet; the sequence is the system, not any single email.
