# EU AI Act Article 50 — 60-Second Readiness Check
**The lead magnet + the live-demo close tool.** Use it 3 ways: (1) free check you offer in outreach, (2) embed as a form on csoai.org → email capture, (3) run live on a discovery call to surface the gap and close the £4,950.

## THE 10 QUESTIONS (yes/no/unsure)

**Scope**
1. Does your product generate or output content (text, image, audio, video) for users in the EU?
2. Do users interact with an AI system (chatbot/assistant/agent) that could be mistaken for a human?

**Article 50(1) — interaction disclosure**
3. Do you clearly tell users, at first interaction, that they're talking to an AI?

**Article 50(2) — machine-readable marking**
4. Is your AI-generated output marked in a machine-readable format (C2PA / embedded metadata)?
5. Does that marking survive editing/compression (robust watermark, not just a metadata tag)?
6. Do you have a detection/fingerprinting layer (the draft Code of Practice says metadata alone isn't enough)?

**Article 50(4) — deepfake disclosure**
7. If you generate realistic images/audio/video of real people or events, is it visibly labelled as AI-generated?

**Evidence**
8. Could you produce signed, independently-verifiable evidence of compliance for an auditor or an enterprise customer's procurement today?
9. Is any of this documented in a technical file?
10. Do you know whether your systems placed on the market before 2 Aug 2026 qualify for the 2 Dec 2026 marking grace period?

---

## SCORING
- Each **"yes" = 1**, "no"/"unsure" = 0. Total /10.
- **8–10 — Ahead:** "You're in good shape. The gap is usually #8 (signed evidence). We turn 'we're compliant' into cryptographically-signed proof in 2 days."
- **4–7 — Exposed:** "You have the basics but real gaps on marking and/or evidence. This is exactly the 2-day audit-prep sweet spot."
- **0–3 — High risk:** "Material exposure before 2 Aug 2026. Fines reach 7% of turnover and your enterprise deals will stall at procurement. Priority audit-prep."
