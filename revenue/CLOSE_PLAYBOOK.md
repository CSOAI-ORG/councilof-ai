# The £4,950 Close Playbook — 15-min call → signed deal
**Why this matters for £3,333/day:** close rate is a multiplier. At 20% close you need ~100 booked calls/mo; at 40% you need ~50. This script is built to push close rate up — fewer sends, same revenue.

## Pre-call (2 min)
- Pull up their product + run the free readiness check on it (so you walk in already knowing their gaps).
- Have the £4,950 Stripe link + a calendar open.

## The call structure (15 min)

### 1. Frame (1 min)
"Thanks for the 15 minutes. Goal here is simple — I'll show you exactly where [Company] stands on Article 50, you decide if our 2-day audit-prep is worth it. No pressure either way."

### 2. Discovery (4 min) — ask, don't pitch
- "Which of your features generate content for EU users — chat, images, video, voice?"
- "Has anyone internally mapped which Article 50 obligations apply to those?"
- "Are enterprise customers starting to ask for AI-Act assurances in procurement?" ← *the pain*
- "Who owns this internally — you, legal, a DPO?" ← *find the budget holder*

### 3. Live readiness check (4 min) — show, don't tell
Run the check on their product live. Narrate the gaps: "So your avatars need machine-readable marking by 2 Dec, and you've no signed evidence layer — that's the two things an auditor or a customer's procurement will ask for first."

### 4. The offer (2 min)
"That's exactly what the 2-day audit-prep fixes — £4,950. You get: gap analysis across all 410 articles, marking review wired in, and a signed attestation pack with public verify URLs your customers and a Notified Body accept. Audit-ready before the cliff, evidence in hand."

### 5. Close (2 min)
"Two ways to start: I can send the engagement link now and we kick off this week, or you loop in [legal/DPO] and we start Monday. Which works?" ← *assumptive, two-option close*

### 6. Payment (2 min)
Send the Stripe link on the call. "I'll drop the link now — once it's in, I'll send the kickoff questionnaire today." Get it paid *on the call* if you can.

## Objection handling
- **"Too expensive."** → "Compared to up to 7% of turnover, or losing an enterprise deal because procurement failed you on AI-Act? £4,950 is the cheapest insurance you'll buy this year."
- **"We'll do it in-house."** → "You can — most teams find it's 3-4 weeks of legal + eng time, and the signed-evidence layer is the hard part. We do it in 2 days and hand you the attestations. Your call on build vs buy."
- **"Aug 2026 is ages away."** → "The marking grace is 2 Dec 2026, and procurement teams are asking *now*. The teams that wait will be scrambling in July competing for the same advisors. Two days now beats a panic later."
- **"Need to think about it."** → "Totally fair. What's the one thing you'd need answered to decide?" → handle that, then re-close. Book a firm follow-up.
- **"Send me info."** → "Will do — and so it's useful, I'll include your readiness-check results so it's specific to [Company], not generic." → send + follow up in 2 days.

## After the close
- Upsell: £499/mo continuous monitoring ("stay compliant as the rules evolve — the Code of Practice is still finalising").
- Ask for a referral / intro to 2 peers facing the same deadline. ← *compounds*

## The number
40% close × £4,950 means **every ~2.5 booked calls = one deal.** ~50 calls/mo = 20 deals = **£3,333/day.** Tighten the script, raise the close rate, lower the sends needed.
