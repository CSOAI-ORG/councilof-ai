## 1. EU AI Act Enforcement News

### 🔴 MAJOR: Digital Omnibus Deal Struck (7 May 2026) — Deadlines Extended

The European Commission, Parliament, and Council agreed a targeted amendment package called the **"AI Omnibus"** on 7 May 2026. This is the most significant change since the Act entered force.

**Deadline changes (not yet formally enacted — trilogue process underway):**

| Original Deadline | New Deadline | Scope |
|---|---|---|
| 2 August 2026 | **2 December 2027** | High-risk AI systems under Annex III (employment, education, health insurance, credit, law enforcement) |
| 2 August 2026 | **August 2028** | AI embedded in physical products (medical devices, industrial machinery) |
| — | **2 December 2026** | New prohibition on AI-generated non-consensual sexually explicit images |

**Other key changes in the Digital Omnibus:**
- **High-risk scope narrowed** — Only systems whose failure creates genuine health/safety risks trigger the full regime. Assistive/optimisation tools no longer automatically qualify.
- **Machinery completely carved out** — Now governed by its own sector-specific regulation (Siemens/ASML lobbied heavily for this).
- **Regulatory overlap trimmed** — Sector-specific legislation (aviation, medical devices, financial services) no longer requires parallel AI Act assessments.
- **Commission to publish "practical implementation guidelines" for Article 6 by 2 February 2026** — this has now arrived.

**Status:** The omnibus deal is agreed at political level but not yet formally enacted. Trilogue negotiations continue. Once finalised, implementing regulations will follow.

### EU AI Office Activity
- **Signatory Taskforce established** — EU AI Office created a taskforce to guide compliance with general-purpose AI (GPAI) rules (source: BABL AI).
- **Draft Code of Practice on AI Labelling and Transparency published** by the European Commission (source: Jones Day).
- **Classification guidelines for high-risk AI systems** — The Commission is consulting on draft guidelines for classifying high-risk systems (source: INSIGHT EU MONITORING).
- **General-Purpose AI Code of Practice** published (source: Jones Day).

## 2. UK AI Regulation Developments

### No Standalone UK AI Bill — Existing Laws Expanded Instead

The UK **has not introduced a standalone AI Bill** in 2026. Key developments:

**King's Speech 2026 (Date TBC):**
- The King's Speech 2026 included a **Digital Regulation and AI** announcement, focused on extending existing regulatory powers rather than a new AI-specific statute.
- The government is pursuing a **sector-by-sector, principles-based approach** rather than the EU's horizontal regulation model.

**Online Safety Act Expansion:**
- The UK is seeking **additional powers under the Online Safety Act** to tackle AI-generated harms (source: Tech Policy Press, June 2026).
- Fast-tracked reforms to address **AI-generated child sexual abuse material, deepfakes, and non-consensual intimate images** (source: Lewis Silkin).

**Data (Use and Access) Act 2025 (DUAA):**
- Received Royal Assent in mid-2025.
- Core amendments to UK GDPR and PECR expected to come into force in **early 2026** (likely any day now).
- Also includes digital verification services provisions.

**Cyber Security and Resilience Bill:**
- Introduced to Parliament, expected to pass in **2026**.
- Phased commencement likely.

**EU Adequacy Decision:**
- **Renewed December 2025** — UK remains adequate for EEA data transfers until at least **27 December 2031**.

**Parliamentary Activity:**
- **House of Lords** debated "human impact of AI" (June 2026).
- Lords Library published briefing on *"Potential future risks from autonomous AI systems"*.
- Short debates on facial recognition technology in policing.

### UK vs EU Approach Summary
| Dimension | UK | EU |
|---|---|---|
| **Regulatory model** | Principles-based, sector-specific | Horizontal, risk-based regulation |
| **Standalone AI law** | No (expanding existing laws) | Yes (EU AI Act + Digital Omnibus amendments) |
| **High-risk classification** | No statutory list | Annex III (now being narrowed via Omnibus) |
| **Enforcement body** | Ofcom (online safety), ICO (data) | AI Office + national competent authorities |
| **Timeline** | Gradual, ongoing | 2 Aug 2026 original deadline (now delayed) |
