### 5.2 · UK — 5 principles, *not* a single statute

The "UK AI Bill 5 principles" framing in some MEOK docs is **policy-paper accurate but not statute accurate**. The Government **Pro-innovation AI Regulation White Paper** (Mar 2023) explicitly carries **5 cross-sectoral principles**:

1. **Safety, security & robustness**
2. **Transparency / interpretability / explainability**
3. **Accountability / governance / contestability**
4. **Fairness**
5. **Redress / challenge**

This is what the launch should reference — not "5-principle Bill". (Source: full UK gov.uk search results page https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-ai-strategy).
