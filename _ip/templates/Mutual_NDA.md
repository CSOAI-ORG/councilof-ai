# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

Between **CSOAI Ltd** and **______________________**, effective **__________**.

## 1. Confidential Information
"Confidential Information" includes, without limitation: source code, algorithms, APIs, system architecture, machine-learning models and outputs, training data, datasets, business and financial plans, projections, pricing models, customer and partner lists, partnership and fundraising discussions, competitive analysis, go-to-market playbooks, and any information marked or reasonably understood to be confidential.

## 2. Obligations
The receiving party will: (a) use Confidential Information only to evaluate or perform the relationship; (b) limit access to those with a need to know who are bound by equivalent obligations; (c) protect it with at least reasonable care; (d) not disclose it to third parties without prior written consent.

## 3. Exclusions
Information that is public through no breach, independently developed, or rightfully received from a third party without restriction.

## 4. Term & return
Obligations survive **5 years** (indefinitely for trade secrets). On request or termination, return or destroy all Confidential Information and confirm in writing.

## 5. No licence
No IP licence is granted by disclosure.
