This Agreement is between **CSOAI Ltd** ("Company") and **______________________** ("Assignor"), effective **__________**.

## 1. Assignment
Assignor hereby assigns to Company all right, title, and interest in and to all inventions, discoveries, improvements, ideas, know-how, formulas, processes, techniques, methods, software code, data, documentation, designs, and other intellectual property conceived, developed, authored, or reduced to practice by Assignor during and in connection with the period of engagement with Company, whether or not patentable or copyrightable (the "Work Product").

## 2. Retroactive coverage
This assignment expressly includes all Work Product created **before** the date of this Agreement in connection with Company's business, including any pre-incorporation contributions.

## 3. Moral rights & further assurances
Assignor waives any moral rights in the Work Product to the extent permitted by law and agrees to execute any further documents reasonably required to perfect Company's ownership.

## 4. Third-party / open-source
Assignor confirms the Work Product is original and does not knowingly incorporate third-party IP or GPL/AGPL-licensed code without prior written disclosure to Company.

## 5. Confidentiality
Assignor will keep Company's confidential information secret (see separate NDA).
