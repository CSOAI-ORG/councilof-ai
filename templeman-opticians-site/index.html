FAQs

Common Questions

How much does a home eye test cost?

NHS-funded (free) for eligible patients. Private tests £95-120.

How quickly can you repair broken glasses?

48-hour emergency response. National providers quote 2-4 weeks.

Do you provide care home block contracts?

Yes — £6,000-25,000/year covering all resident eye care.

Are you DBS checked and GOC registered?

Yes. All optometrists hold enhanced DBS and GOC registration.

What is domiciliary eye care?

Which areas do you cover?

All of Essex — Rayleigh to Colchester and everywhere in between.
