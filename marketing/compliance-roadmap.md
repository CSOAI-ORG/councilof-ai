## 2. The 12 Frameworks (Covered by MEOK)

- **EU AI Act:** Article 50 (transparency), Article 6 (high-risk), Article 9 (risk management), Annex III (high-risk use cases), Annex IV (tech docs).
- **UK AI Bill:** In parliamentary draft. 5 AISI voluntary commitments (safety case, third-party eval, info sharing, vuln disclosure, watermarking).
- **DORA:** Digital Operational Resilience Act. 5 pillars: ICT risk, incident reporting, resilience testing, third-party risk, info sharing.
- **NIS2:** Network and Information Security Directive 2. Article 21 (10 measures): risk analysis, incident handling, business continuity, supply chain security, vuln handling, crypto, access control, asset management, training, secure comms.
- **ISO 42001:** AI Management System. Annex A controls.
- **GDPR DPIA:** Data Protection Impact Assessment. Data subject rights, breach notification.
- **HIPAA:** US healthcare. PHI handling, BAA generator.
- **SOC 2:** Trust Service Criteria. Security, availability, processing integrity, confidentiality, privacy.
- **NIST AI RMF:** US federal. Map / Measure / Manage / Govern.
- **AISI:** UK AI Safety Institute voluntary commitments.
- **CAISI:** California AI Safety Institute voluntary capabilities.
- **Montreal / Toronto Declarations:** Ethical AI principles.
