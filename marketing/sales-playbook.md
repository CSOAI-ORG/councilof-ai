## 2. The Sales Call (20-min Scoping Call)

- **Goal:** Qualify, educate, secure next step (48h Assessment or Pro subscription).
- **Opening (2 min):** "Hi [Name], thanks for the time. Saw your [specific use case]. Cliff is 48 days out."
- **Context (5 min):** Explain EU AI Act Article 50 relevance to their AI. Highlight exposure (£15M / 3% turnover). Differentiate Article 50 from high-risk Article 6.
- **The Ask (5 min):** Propose MEOK's 4-day signed Ed25519 attestation for £4,950. Emphasize sovereign UK, 32-server MCP fleet, public verify URL.
- **Objection Handling (5 min):** Address 3 common objections (cost, time, validity) using pre-prepared cards.
- **Closing (3 min):** "Worth 20 min this week? I'll send the link. First 5 minutes: live sign + verify demo."
