## 13.4 Notes on the writing

- **No fabricated numbers.** Every number in this ZIP is either publicly disclosed or flagged with "needs primary research".
- **No invented customer names.** Every customer reference is flagged `[CLAIMED — needs verification]` unless a public source (annual report, press release, case study) supports it.
- **No invented advisors.** Every named advisor (Holistic AI's policy board, CSOAI's sectoral advisors, etc.) is flagged for verification.
- **No invented awards.** No "Gartner Leader 2025" claims unless verifiable.
- **No invented quotes.** No fictitious executive quotes.
- **Naming overlaps flagged.** Sardine (AI risk) vs Sardine (food). RAI Institute vs multiple organisations with "Responsible AI" in the name. Resolved (multiple companies). Synthesia-safe vs Synthesia (video avatars).
