## Headline finding

**No competitor in the 2026 market combines (a) EU AI Act + NIS2 + DORA + CRA + GDPR-native mapping, (b) sovereign-by-default data plane, (c) signed open artifacts, (d) per-call x402 pricing, (e) A2A/MCP interop, (f) regulator-readable evidence, and (g) Article 4 literacy tooling for SMEs — in one product.**

CSOAI's wedge is **that exact combination**. Every competitor covers 2–4 of those axes; the rest are gaps.

---

## Key strategic moves

1. **Open + Sovereign + Per-call** trifecta as the brand.
2. **Annex III 8-vertical sweep** as the sectoral depth.
3. **RegTech-as-a-Service for national regulators** as the high-leverage channel.

See `12_CROSSCUT_DIFFERENTIATION_AND_GAPS.md` for the scoring that supports these moves, and `11_CROSSCUT_VULNERABILITIES.md` for the open gaps.
