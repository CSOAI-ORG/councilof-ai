# 08 — Tier 3: Emerging, EU-native & Adjacent Players

These are smaller, EU-native, or vertical-specific platforms. They are not the primary competitors today, but each is a credible local champion that CSOAI should be aware of, and several are partnership candidates.

---

## 8.1 Apheris

**Website / hq:** apheris.com · Berlin, Germany. Founded 2019.
**Form:** Series A, ~$25M raised. **needs primary research**.
**Positioning:** "Federated AI for regulated industries" — life sciences, chemicals, manufacturing. **Federated Compute**.
**Modules:** Federated learning infrastructure · Privacy-enhancing computation · Data clean rooms.
**USP:**
- EU-native, deep privacy DNA.
- Federated compute — a real technical moat for regulated cross-org collaboration.
**Weaknesses:**
- Niche to life sciences/chemicals.
- No AI Act mapping.
- No MCP / A2A.
- No per-call x402.
- No Article 4 SME.

## 8.8 Codified / Holistic-as-Service / Micro-SIs

Several smaller EU consultancies and small SaaS shops are building AI-governance-shaped offerings:

- **Holistic-as-service** wrappers (Baringa, Paragon, Elucidate, AI RiskOps) — services, not software.
- **Regional integrators** in DE (TÜV AI Lab, Fraunhofer IAIS spinoffs), FR (LNE, Bpifrance-adjacent), NL (TNO, TÜV Nederland), IT (AgID-adjacent), ES (ISMS Forum-adjacent), BE (Agoria), PL (NASK).
- **National competent authorities** themselves (BSI Germany, CNIL France, AP Spain, Garante Italy, DSB Austria, AP Belgium, etc.) sometimes operate "AI sandboxes" (Article 57 AI Act) and may operate AI governance tooling for their members.

**Implication for CSOAI:** National authorities are a sales channel, not a competitor.

## 8.9 ENISA / EU Agency for Cybersecurity

**Positioning:** EU-level cybersecurity agency with an expanding AI role. **Not a competitor** — a regulator-adjacent partner.

---

## 8.10 EU AI Office / DG-CONNECT

**Positioning:** EU AI Office established under the AI Act (operational since mid-2024, **needs primary research for current 2026 structure**). **Not a competitor** — a regulator.

---

## 8.11 CEN-CENELEC JTC 21

**Positioning:** Joint Technical Committee on AI. Publishes harmonized standards that confer presumption of conformity under the AI Act. **Not a competitor** — a standards body. CSOAI should map its controls to JTC 21 standards as they publish.

---

## 8.12 ISO/IEC SC 42

**Positioning:** ISO/IEC subcommittee on AI. Publishes ISO/IEC 42001 (AIMS), ISO/IEC 23894 (AI risk management), ISO/IEC 27090 (AI security), ISO/IEC 27091 (AI privacy), etc. **Not a competitor** — a standards body.

---

## 8.13 GPAI Code of Practice signatories

**Positioning:** Voluntary instrument from the AI Office. Signatories get a "trusted provider" badge for GPAI providers. Notable signatories include Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, Mistral, Aleph Alpha, IBM, Microsoft, and others. **Not a competitor** — a compliance instrument. CSOAI's GPAI provider tooling should map to this Code.
