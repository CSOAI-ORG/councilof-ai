## 10.2 Per-call / per-token pricing alternatives (CSOAI's wedge)

CSOAI's x402 per-call model — **needs primary research** for current 2026 unit pricing — competes with the per-seat / per-asset / per-org pricing above by aligning cost with usage rather than with budget ceiling. Indicative numbers (placeholder until confirmed):

| CSOAI plan | What it includes | Indicative pricing |
|---|---|---|
| **Free / Article 4** | SME AI literacy portal + EU AI Act self-assessment + plain-language guidance | $0 |
| **Per-call** | x402 paywall on MCP tool calls | ~$0.05–$0.25/call (varies by tool) — **needs primary research** |
| **Per-asset** | Sovereign data plane + signed manifests + governance engine | ~$50–$300/mo per AI system — **needs primary research** |
| **Enterprise** | All of the above + sectoral Annex III packages + regulator portal + BFT council | $50k–$1M+/yr depending on systems + seats — **needs primary research** |
| **Regulator / public sector** | Custom — usually €0–€50k/yr with sovereign cloud included | **needs primary research** |

**The x402 wedge:** When a competitor charges $200k/yr for 100 systems, CSOAI charges ~$0.10 per call. At 10 calls/day per system × 365 days × 100 systems = 365,000 calls × $0.10 = **$36,500/yr**. Same regulation coverage. **50x cheaper.** This is the wedge.

## 10.4 Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) and Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM)

**SAM:** CSOAI's SAM is the intersection of (a) EU AI Act deployers, (b) sovereign-by-default requirement, (c) willing to use MCP/A2A/x402. Estimate: **$300M–$1B SAM** (indicative, **`needs primary research`**).

**SOM:** Year-1 SOM (2027) for CSOAI is the 5k–25k early adopter segment — primarily EU AI Act high-risk deployers, EU AI safety institutes, GPAI providers, and the long tail of EU SMEs. **$10M–$50M SOM** (indicative, **`needs primary research`**).

## 10.5 Pricing research checklist

For each of the 47 competitors, the **minimum primary research** required is:

1. **Public pricing page screenshot** (or "no public pricing" flag).
2. **Latest funding round / revenue disclosure** (from press releases, investor materials, public filings).
3. **Two named customer references** (with permission; otherwise redacted).
4. **Per-deployment SKU breakdown** (who pays, how often, what triggers renewal).
5. **Open-source / community edition availability and limits.**
6. **EU/UK/Gulf data-residency options and price differentials.**

These should populate a **vendor-pricing.csv** maintained under `csoai-master-v2-app/research/vendor-pricing/`. **Create that file in the first week of strategy execution.**

## 10.6 What this means for the CSOAI pricing page

CSOAI's public pricing page should:

1. **Show a €0 / Free tier** for Article 4 SME literacy. (No competitor offers this.)
2. **Show per-call x402 pricing** for AI tool calls. (No competitor offers this.)
3. **Show per-asset pricing for governance engine + sovereign data plane.** (Some competitors offer per-asset, but without sovereignty.)
4. **Show enterprise tier with regulator portal + BFT council.**
5. **Show public-sector / regulator tier** with custom pricing.
6. **Show a comparison table** ("CSOAI vs Holistic AI / Vanta / etc.") — this is the single highest-conversion piece of content on the pricing page.

The comparison table format is best modelled on the **Vanta comparison page** (the standard). Every Tier 1 competitor should be on the comparison page with rows for the seven axes in `01_OVERVIEW_AND_METHODOLOGY.md`.
