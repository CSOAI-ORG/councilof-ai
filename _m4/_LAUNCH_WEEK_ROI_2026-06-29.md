## The 3 specific actions to maximize every day's contribution

1. **Owner move fires Tue 09:00 BST** (28 minutes) — sets the gate on EVERY downstream metric. Without this, the math is 0/279 categories.
2. **Monzo first call = the wedge** — a Monzo pilot + glowing reference gives credibility for HSBC/Cera/Co-op. Without it, the design-partner pipeline stalls.
3. **All 5 upstream PRs merged by Tue Eve** — maintainers respond faster on sunny day. Drives 3 of 5 categories to non-zero. Without them, the answer-engine discovery curve flattens.

## The gap (what closes the spread)

The 5.5× spread between worst-case and best-case revenue is **the same 5.5× spread on PR merges**. If the owner move fires AND 3+ PRs merge AND Monzo replies, the best-case materialises. If 0 PRs merge AND the owner is delayed AND Monzo ignores us — worst-case.
