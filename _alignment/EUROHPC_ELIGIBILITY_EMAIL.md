## On voluntary VAT registration — my honest advice: DON'T, not for this
You *can* register voluntarily below the threshold. **I would not do it just to unlock EuroHPC.** It creates
permanent quarterly VAT returns and admin for a pre-revenue company, to solve a question one email answers.
If your accountant recommends it for other reasons (reclaiming VAT on purchases), that's a separate decision —
and theirs to advise, not mine.
