## THE 4 IMPLICATIONS

### 1. CSOAI is independent, by design
- No equity stakes in certified organizations
- No board seats on certified organizations
- No revenue-sharing with certified organizations
- No success fees on certified organizations

### 2. CSOAI follows ISO fee-for-service model
- Like ISO (195 countries)
- Like CMMC/C3PAO (US Department of Defense)
- Like BSI (UK)
- Like DIN (Germany)
- Like TÜV (Germany)

### 3. S&P and Moody's are the warning
- $1.375B + $864M in settlements after 2008
- They paid the institutions they rated
- Created conflict of interest
- CSOAI refuses this path entirely

### 4. CA3O is the CMMC for AI
- C3PAO = CMMC Third-Party Assessment Organization
- CA3O = CSOAI-Authorized AI Assessment Organization
- Same architecture. Same authority model. Same fee structure.
- This is the binding charter principle.
