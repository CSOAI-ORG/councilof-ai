For each hive, sovereign learns:
1. **Domain vocabulary** — its 5-7 sovereign Mist 12 pillars terms (e.g. grabhire = "permit", "MCIL", "driver", "lorry", "haulage")
2. **Domain agent-card schema** — its 5-7 sovereign skill descriptions
3. **Domain decisions** — its decision rubric (e.g. when to permit a job)
4. **Domain constraints** — its hard safety floors (e.g. cannot dispatch overloaded lorry)
5. **Domain reasoning** — its reasoning chain patterns

All 5 then become sovereign-labelled-data examples for sovereign-merge fine-tunes.

Each absorbed pattern feeds the **per-feature-queen self-improvement loop** (Sovereign Mist 12 pillars P5 from the Mindset Flywheel):
- Queen-CareFloor learns the domain's hard-care rules
