## Honest caveats

1. **Aggregate ≠ active**: We have 4.245T aggregate but only ~3B active at any time (qwen2.5:3b for cheap path, 70B for oracle). The "active" is what gets run. Aggregate is what we have access to.
2. **Brain availability**: The federated models (Llama 405B, DeepSeek V3, etc.) are **not all live in London**. Some are available via Oracle GenAI, others via Cohere, others via API. The 4.245T aggregate assumes we have access to all of them.
3. **Cost**: $0.073-$0.127/call at full federated is expensive for high-volume. For production: use the free tier (qwen) for 90% of traffic, oracle for 10% of hard queries.
4. **Latency**: 25-40s for the full 12-brain sweep. For real-time, use the free tier (0.55s).
5. **The score cap of 0.9475** is from the formula: 0.7 * agg + 0.2 * quality + 0.05 * sov + 0.03 * cost + 0.02 * latency. With aggregate capped at 1.0 and quality at 1.0, the max is 0.7 + 0.2 + 0.05 = 0.95 (modulo cost/latency adjustments). The actual **goal** is the aggregate, not the score.
