# SOV4 Awareness Layer — the consented, attested "understands you" play (2026-07-18)

The synthesis of "SOV4 understands/learns the end user" done the way that is REAL, LEGAL, and a MOAT —
NOT the ambient-sensing play the law bans. Same capability the big assistants chase; opposite trust model.

## The reframe (why this beats Siri/Google, not copies them)
- Them: sense the user passively -> infer state -> user never sees it. Untrusted, and in the EU illegal
  (EU AI Act Art.5 prohibited practices; biometric-inference / emotion-recognition rules).
- SOV4: the user's signals stay on the USER's device; SOV4 sees only what the user CONSENTS to share;
  every use is shown + Ed25519-signed. Same "understands you" outcome, inverted trust posture.
- The law banned ambient state-inference BECAUSE it's the untrustworthy version. Being forced onto the
  consented/on-device/attested path lands SOV on the ONLY version that survives — and it's uniquely ours.

## The four mechanisms (all buildable, all legal)
1. **On-device / edge sensing the USER owns.** Any ambient input is processed on the user's device,
   never uplinked; produces only opted-in signals. Governed + attested, which the big assistants can't be.
2. **Consent as the product, not the fine print.** "The AI that shows you everything it knows about you
   and lets you delete any of it." The consent ledger (SHA-stamped, tamper-evident, revocable) IS the
   differentiator — a feature no incumbent can offer (they already sense covertly).
3. **Geometry/events, not identity.** Sense THAT something happened (hazard, fall, request), never WHO or
   their biometric/emotional state. Legal (safety-event detection, not emotion-recognition). The Guardian's
   actual job: protect, not surveil.
4. **Interoperate, don't ingest.** SOV4 is the governed ENDPOINT Siri/Google/App-Intents call INTO — not a
   pipe tapping their feeds. SOV becomes the trust/attestation layer ON TOP of others' sensing — the
   independent attestation they structurally cannot self-provide. This is "EAT Layer 0": they sense, SOV
   makes it trustworthy.

## HARD LINES (legal walls, not mythos pins — non-negotiable)
- NO WiFi-sensing / through-wall sensing of persons. NO always-on ambient mic / passive presence sensing
  of a user to infer state. NO biometric or emotion inference. All = EU AI Act Art.5 prohibited territory.
- Interoperable-WITH Siri/App Intents = OK. "Reads what Siri hears / taps ambient user data" = the same wall.
  The integration is a consented API seam, not a sensing pipe.
- Awareness = CONSENTED, INSPECTABLE, REVOCABLE, geometry-not-identity. Ask first, sign consent, show every
  use. Consistent with the Guardian charter (protect the user, never surveil them) and the consent-ledger.

## Why it's the STRONGER play (not the consolation prize)
"The AI that understands you WITHOUT spying on you" is a bigger, more defensible market than the covert one,
because it's the version people actually want and the one a journalist cannot end. The consent ledger +
attestation ARE the moat. Siri cannot say "we show you everything we know and you can delete it." SOV can.

## The consent moment = onboarding demo = USP (the product insight)
The legal requirement (informed, explicit, revocable consent) is turned INTO the sales pitch. Before any
data, SOV opens out loud:
  "Here's what I can do, exactly what I'd learn about you, where it's stored (your device), how you delete
   any of it anytime. Nothing happens until you say yes. Want to see?"
This single moment does three things at once:
1. Satisfies the law (informed + explicit + revocable consent, Art.5-clean).
2. Sells the benefit (SOV explains what it offers — the demo IS the pitch).
3. Proves the trust (the user SEES the transparency instead of being told about it).
No incumbent can open this way: if Siri/Google read out everything they sense + how to delete it, the list
would horrify. SOV's list is short, on-device, consented — so saying it aloud is a STRENGTH, not an exposure.
The thing that would embarrass them is the thing that sells SOV.

### The honest pin (keeps it a USP, not a dark pattern)
The explanation must be TRUE and COMPLETE — benefits AND limits in the same breath ("here's what I can do,
here's what I can't, here's what I won't"). If the consent moment becomes a slick funnel that downplays the
tradeoffs, it stops being trust and becomes the dark-pattern everyone else uses. The USP is honesty at the
door — so the door must actually be honest. This is the rare case where the safe way and the winning way are
the same way, because the trust model makes "ask first" a feature instead of friction.
