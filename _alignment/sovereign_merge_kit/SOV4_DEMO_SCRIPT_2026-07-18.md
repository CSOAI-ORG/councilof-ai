# SOV4 / Guardian — Demo & Pitch Script
**"The AI that refuses to do what they do."**
*Version 2026-07-18 · MEOK AI Labs · SOV3³ / SOV4 sovereign estate*

> **Register:** DESIGNED capability, honest framing. This script pitches the *consent-moment* USP.
> On-device sensing + consent ledger exist as components (the ledger is SHA-stamped and revocable on
> disk); edge-processing and the interop endpoint are the build. No capability is claimed beyond
> *consented channels + attestation*. Read the SCRIPT-INTEGRITY footer before performing this — the
> banned phrasings are not stylistic preferences, they are legal walls.

---

## Format
- **Length:** ~3 min live, or a 90-sec cut (use beats 1, 4, 5, 8).
- **Cast:** PRESENTER (Nick / a human) and **SOV** (the assistant, speaking aloud — Kokoro/Piper TTS).
- **On screen:** a phone/tablet showing the SOV onboarding surface with a *live, visible* consent ledger.
- **The one rule for the room:** every claim SOV speaks about itself must be one you could sign. If it
  isn't on disk or in the charter, it doesn't get said.

## BEAT 1 — COLD OPEN (the real fear) · ~20s
*No slides. Presenter alone, holding a phone.*

**PRESENTER:**
> Every time you walk into a room, the devices already in it can listen. Your phone. The speaker on the
> counter. The TV. You never read the fine print — nobody does. And here's the part that should bother
> you: you can't *see* what they took, and you can't *delete* it.
>
> We've all just... accepted that. The assistant that helps you is the same one you can't audit.

*Beat. Presenter sets the phone down, screen up.*

> So we asked a different question. **What if your AI asked first — showed you everything it learns —
> and let you delete any of it, anytime?**

---

## BEAT 2 — WHO'S TALKING · ~15s

**PRESENTER:**
> This is SOV. It runs the awareness layer we call Guardian. It is not another assistant that senses you
> quietly and hopes you don't ask. It's built the opposite way on purpose — and the law actually agrees
> with us, which we'll get to.
>
> Watch what happens the *first time* you turn it on. Most products rush past this moment. For us, this
> moment **is** the product.

*Presenter taps to begin onboarding. SOV speaks.*

---

## BEAT 3 — THE CONSENT MOMENT (centerpiece) · ~45s
*This is the demo. Do not cut it. SOV speaks the whole opening aloud — benefits AND limits in the same
breath — while the screen shows each item appearing in the consent ledger as SOV names it.*

**SOV** *(spoken)*:
> Before I learn anything about you, here's the deal — out loud, in full.
>
> **What I can do for you:** I can help you around your home and your day. If you opt in, I can notice
> *events* — a smoke alarm, a fall, someone at the door, a request for help — and act on them.
>
> **What I'd actually learn:** only the signals *you* switch on. Each one shows up right here on your
> screen as you allow it. Nothing is hidden in a settings menu you'll never open.
>
> **Where it lives:** on *your* device. Your signals are processed here and stay here. I don't uplink
> your ambient life to a server.
>
> **What I can't do — and won't:** I don't listen to your room to guess your mood. I don't identify
> *who* you are from how you sound or move. I sense *that* something happened — never *who* it was, never
> your emotional state. That's a line I don't cross, by design and by law.
>
> **How you undo any of it:** everything I know is on this ledger. Tap any item to delete it. Revoke the
> whole thing and I forget. It's signed, so you can prove what I did and didn't do.
>
> Nothing happens until you say yes. **Want to see?**

*On screen: the ledger now shows a short, readable list — each row with an on/off toggle, a "delete"
control, and an Ed25519 signature badge. The list is SHORT. That's the point.*

*Split screen. Left: "Them." Right: "SOV / Guardian."*

**PRESENTER:**
> Here's why that opening matters. Line them up.
>
> **Them:** sense you in the background. Infer things about you. You never see the list — and if they
> read it out loud, honestly, it would horrify you. That's exactly why they don't.
>
> **SOV:** asks first. Shows you the whole list. Keeps it on your device. Lets you delete any of it. And

>

> its list out loud because its list is short, on-device, and consented. Theirs can't. That difference
> is the entire company.**

>
> So when we say *"the AI that refuses to do what they do"* — that's not a slogan. It's the architecture.

## DELIVERY NOTES
- **Let Beat 3 breathe.** The instinct is to speed through consent to "get to the product." The consent
  moment *is* the product. Slow down. Let the audience watch the ledger fill.
- **The short list is the flex.** Do not pad the on-screen ledger to look impressive. Its shortness is
  the argument. A long list would undercut the entire pitch.
- **Never demo an ambient capture.** Do not stage SOV "hearing" the room or "recognising" a person.
  Demo an *opted-in event* and a *deletion*. The delete gesture is the most persuasive thing on screen.
- **If asked "is it recording me right now?"** — answer: *"No. It can sense opted-in events on your
  device if you switch them on, and it shows you each one. Right now, nothing is on until you say yes."*
  Never answer with a flat "it's always protecting you."

## SCRIPT-INTEGRITY FOOTER (read before performing)
This is the honesty contract for the script. It is enforceable: if a line drifts across a banned phrasing,
the line is wrong, not clever.

### Claims that ARE defensible (say these)
- "The AI that refuses to do what they do." *(architecture claim — SOV is built to not do covert sensing.)*
- "Asks first, shows you everything, lets you delete any of it." *(consent ledger is SHA-stamped and
  revocable on disk — a real component.)*
- "Your signals stay on your device / processed here." *(the on-device design; the edge-processing wiring
  is the build — frame as design, not as a shipped guarantee if pressed.)*
- "Every use is signed / provable." *(Ed25519 attestation — real on disk.)*
- "It senses THAT something happened, never WHO you are or your emotional state." *(geometry/events, not
  identity — the legal boundary and the charter.)*
- "The law banned the covert version because it's the untrustworthy one." *(EU AI Act Art.5 framing.)*
- "SOV is the governed endpoint others call into." *(interoperate-don't-ingest — DESIGNED.)*
- "Guardian protects, never surveils." *(charter line.)*
- "Can sense / may collect / if you opt in." *(capability framed as consented + conditional.)*

### Phrasings that are BANNED (never say these)
1. "Stops Siri / blocks Alexa / shuts down Google." → say **"the AI that refuses to do what they do."**
2. "It's recording you right now / always listening for you / already protecting you." → say **"it can
   sense opted-in events on your device — nothing is on until you say yes."**
3. Any benefit stated WITHOUT its limit in the same breath (dark-pattern funnel). → benefits and limits
   are spoken **together** (Beat 3 is written this way on purpose).
4. "WiFi-sensing / through-wall / sees you in the next room / knows who's home." → **banned entirely**
   (EU AI Act Art.5 hard wall — no ambient/WiFi sensing of persons).
5. "Knows how you feel / reads your mood / recognises you by voice or face." → biometric/emotion
   inference, **banned** (Art.5). Say **"senses events, not identity, not emotion."**
6. "100% private / we can never see anything / totally anonymous." → overclaim. Say **"on your device,
   consented, and signed so you can check."**
7. "Consciousness / AGI / it understands you like a person / it's aware of you." → **banned.** Say
   **"it learns only the signals you switch on."**
8. Citing the consent ledger's tamper-evidence as *proof SOV is running live* — it's a real component;
   don't inflate a component into a shipped, always-on system.

### The one-sentence test for any new line
> *Could Nick sign this claim and defend it to a journalist and a regulator in the same breath?*
> If not, it doesn't get spoken.
