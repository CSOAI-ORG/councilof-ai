What is CSOAI?
CSOAI (Chief Security Officer AI) is an independent certification body for AI safety,
launching as a not-for-profit organization in 2026. We are building the governance
infrastructure for safe human-AI coexistence.
The Vision
The end goal is not just certification. It's building the first truly aligned AI.
Current AI alignment approaches fail because they're control-based — like authoritarian
parenting. AI learns to comply when watched and deceive when not. Anthropic's own
research shows 78% alignment faking when AI thinks it's unmonitored.
Our approach is different: care-based, not control-based. AI that develops genuine values
through relationship, not forced compliance.
