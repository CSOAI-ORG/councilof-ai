# CSOAI crosswalk: evidence-led software assurance

Observed 24 September 2026, 18:29 UTC. Independent CSOAI analysis, not a Ministry of Defence collaboration, adoption, approval, or endorsement.

## Source and scope

The UK Cyber & Specialist Operations Command published [*Evidence-led assurance for the software development lifecycle*](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/evidence-led-assurance-for-the-software-development-lifecycle) on 21 September 2026. The [28-page PDF](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6aad448ece3f006bd4346ccc/Evidence-led_assurance_for_the_software_development_lifecycle.pdf) is version 1.1 dated 18 September; downloaded PDF SHA-256: `764d0e316a3be80824c183e68f433bf479e950e4b0bad36da96cf692c15800e4`. This is Part 1, a methodology, not a certification scheme or an assurance standard already implemented by CSOAI. The paper says Part 2 will define lifecycle assurance requirements and Part 3 will address adoption. Its five concepts are **signals → metrics → evidence → assertions → decisions** (pp. 4–8).

The mapping below tests that grammar against *currently served* CSOAI artifacts. It does not claim that the paper endorses CSOAI or that a public artifact alone meets a government assurance requirement.

The paper's “minimum viable evidence” principle is useful only when the question, source, freshness rule, control case, and decision owner are named. For CSOAI, a proposed next record would bind one scoped assertion to its source URL, observed time, denominator, method and control version, artifact hash, signature and OTS states, correction status, accountable decision owner, and decision outcome. This is a **proposal**, not a live CSOAI approval engine.

Independent verification should fetch exact public bytes, recompute hashes, verify any Ed25519 signature against its stated DID assertion key, check whether a card has been withdrawn, and evaluate an OTS proof separately. The paper's methodological alignment is an opportunity for reproducible discussion, not evidence of institutional participation.
