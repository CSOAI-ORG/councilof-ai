# CSOAI — bootstrap, valuation and operating model

**As of:** 2026-09-14
**Source:** owner (Nick) paste to the governor, 14 Sep ~04:05Z, "learn align learn". Canonical copy for every agent. The governor's daily scoreboard follows the "Canonical daily scoreboard" section verbatim.
**Purpose:** turn the existing evidence estate into a durable business without confusing technical output, publicity, usage or founder-funded tests with customer demand.

## The company in one sentence

CSOAI is a public measurement and evidence network for AI, agents and digital financial rails: it observes versioned subjects, applies declared methods, signs admitted results, publishes corrections and sells fresh computation, proof delivery, monitoring and integration.

The strongest category is **RaaS: receipts and evidence as a service**. The free public index earns discovery and trust. Revenue comes from work that has a buyer-specific cost or continuing value. CSOAI never sells a favourable finding.

## What the recent company analogues teach us

These are business-model analogues, not direct valuation comparables.

| Analogue | Verified recent event | Lesson for CSOAI |
|---|---|---|
| Patronus AI | Announced a $17m Series A around enterprise GenAI evaluation | Buyers pay for repeatable evaluation integrated into operations, not a static leaderboard. |
| Arize AI | Raised a $70m Series C; Dynatrace later agreed to acquire Arize for $915m | Evaluation plus observability can become core infrastructure, but scale and enterprise adoption matter more than the breadth of a public catalog. |
| Vanta | Raised $150m in 2025 while expanding from evidence collection into continuous monitoring and enforcement | The valuable transition is from one-time proof to always-current evidence and workflows. |
| VoidZero | Raised $12.5m with open-source community at the center | Open infrastructure can be the distribution engine while hosted continuity, support and enterprise controls fund the company. |
| Coinbase Bazaar / Agentic.Market | Built discovery and service profiles around x402 payments | Machine-readable discovery, delivery proof and payment attribution are becoming a market surface. CSOAI can independently measure its health. |

CSOAI's combination is unusual: public evidence, signed corrections, machine delivery and coverage across models, agents and financial rails. Its present weakness is commercial proof. The estate shows capability; repeat outside use establishes a company.

## The seven revenue loops

| Loop | Free value | Paid unit | Proof that it works |
|---|---|---|---|
| Commission | Search, preview and method | Fresh subject run and signed evidence pack | Outside request completes queue → mill → signature → root → retrieval |
| Proof delivery | Public summary and digest | Exact bytes, inclusion proof, replay bundle and SLA | Attributed outside settlement and valid delivery |
| Change feed | Public delayed snapshot | Fresh alerts, drift, corrections and webhook/API feed | 30-day retained subscriber |
| Sector evidence | Public crosswalk | Buyer-specific evidence export, control mapping and audit pack | Paid design partner accepts delivery |
| Data licensing | Open samples and documented schema | Bulk longitudinal history, commercial terms and update SLA | Licensed downstream use or citation |
| Legacy bridge | Public compatibility method | Bounded interface inventory, equivalence evidence and monitored wrapper | One paid pilot with measured acceptance criteria |
| Ecosystem intelligence | Public census | Wash-aware, provenance-linked market and protocol health feed | Buyer renews because freshness changes a decision |

Products should share one evidence factory. Stablecoins, MCP, A2A, x402, XRPL, SWIFT targets, models and wrappers are evidence families inside the feed, not dozens of unrelated startups.

## Pricing ladder to test

Prices are hypotheses until an outside buyer accepts them.

1. **Free:** search, delayed summaries, methods, corrections and verification tools.
2. **Machine proof:** $0.01–$1 for an existing artifact or inclusion proof.
3. **Fresh commission:** $10–$250 depending on compute, method and urgency; charge cost plus margin.
4. **Watch feed:** $199–$999 monthly for one evidence family, alerts and history.
5. **Design-partner pilot:** £2,500–£15,000 for a bounded 30-day outcome with explicit acceptance tests.
6. **Enterprise evidence plane:** annual contract after pilots establish support, security, liability boundaries and margin.

Do not discount proof below its variable cost. A launch offer can reduce margin for 30 days, but it must record list price, paid price, compute cost, delivery time and whether the payer returned.

## Market sizing that survives diligence

Avoid adding large industry reports together. Build the market from buyers and transactions:

- **Beachhead:** organizations operating x402 services, AI models/agents or regulated digital assets that need independent continuous evidence.
- **Bottom-up SAM:** reachable named accounts × evidence families per account × believable annual contract value.
- **Initial SOM:** accounts one founder and automated delivery can actually support over 18 months.
- **Expansion:** add a sector only after one buyer, one repeatable method and positive delivery margin.

The first credible spreadsheet needs named accounts, buyer role, trigger, current alternative, expected product, price evidence, probability, next action and source. "All banks," "all models" and "425 stablecoins" describe indexing scope, not revenue.

## Valuation triangulation

No honest valuation can be produced from repository, card or indexed-subject counts alone. Use five methods and disclose the inputs.

### 1. Berkus / risk-factor logic

Score the quality of the idea, working product, execution capability, strategic relationships and initial market evidence. Current fixed dollar amounts from old Berkus examples are obsolete; calibrate the factors to current UK pre-seed transactions. CSOAI scores well on product and technical execution, moderately on defensibility, and weakly on repeat demand and team depth.

### 2. Scorecard method

Start with the median valuation of genuinely comparable UK infrastructure/evaluation rounds, then weight team, market, product, competition, traction, capital need and execution risk. Do not use mature US compliance companies as the median.

### 3. VC method

Model a plausible year-five recurring revenue range, apply a supportable exit multiple, then discount for target investor return and future dilution. Run downside/base/upside cases. This is sensitive to assumptions and must never be the only method.

### 4. Comparable transactions and market multiples

Patronus, Arize, Vanta and VoidZero validate investor interest in evaluation, observability, compliance automation and open infrastructure. They do not establish CSOAI's valuation. Revenue multiples become meaningful only when CSOAI has clean recurring revenue, retention and gross margin. Arize's announced acquisition is category evidence; without verified revenue it supplies no defensible revenue multiple.

### 5. Estate replacement cost

Estimate what it would cost a competent team to reproduce only the tested, documented, legally owned and maintainable components. Apply discounts for duplication, obsolete code, founder dependency, missing rights, operational fragility and lack of adoption. Never capitalize generated files or repository counts at face value.

### Provisional readiness score

This is a diligence checklist, not a valuation:

| Dimension | Current evidence view | Next proof |
|---|---|---|
| Product/technical capability | Strong | Stranger completes the whole commissioned loop |
| Market timing | Strong themes, broad scope | One narrow ICP with urgent trigger converts |
| Defensibility | Promising longitudinal evidence and corrections | Repeat data use, citations and integration costs |
| Distribution | Machine discovery exists; human funnel weak | Qualified replies, external citations and referrals |
| Revenue | Rail proof exists; repeat demand not proven | Second outside payer and one repeat payer |
| Unit economics | Not yet auditable | Cost and margin per delivered evidence family |
| Team/operations | High founder and multi-machine coordination risk | Canonical ledger, on-call rules and reproducible recovery |
| Governance/IP | Needs diligence pack | Ownership, licenses, security, privacy and claims register |

## The bootstrap order

1. Make commission → queue → worker → signed evidence → root → retrieval reliable.
2. Choose one initial buyer and trigger. Best current candidates are x402 operators needing conformance/freshness, AI builders needing independent evidence, and regulated digital-asset operators needing supply/attestation monitoring.
3. Deliver five outside evidence packs manually enough to learn, while the machinery records every cost and delay.
4. Convert one buyer to a 30-day feed.
5. Publish corrections and reproducible methods. Use public measurements as demonstrations tied to a buyer action.
6. Add the next evidence family only when it shares the factory and has a named user.
7. Build a diligence room continuously, whether or not outside capital is pursued.

## Six-month outcomes compressed by agents

Agents compress research, implementation and distribution work; they do not compress elapsed retention, buyer trust, legal deadlines or longitudinal history.

### Next 24 hours

- Prove one real commissioned model end to end and publish exact state.
- Reconcile production coverage, revenue, roots and worker health into one canonical ledger.
- Put one product page in front of one ICP with an explicit commission/feed action.
- Produce a 25-account prospect table from verified public triggers; prepare five artifact-led contacts.
- Record compute cost, human minutes, delivery latency and gross margin for every completed run.

### Next 7 days

- Five outside deliveries, at least two buyer conversations and one paid non-founder transaction.
- One evidence-family watch feed running daily with corrections and change alerts.
- HF, Kaggle, GitHub, browser, API, MCP and x402 surfaces reconcile to the same canonical manifest.
- One public technical note earns an external citation or reproducibility attempt.
- Diligence room contains corporate, IP, security, finance, architecture, traction and claims evidence.

### Next 30 days

- One retained feed subscriber, one repeat payer and three active design partners.
- Published delivery SLA and measured reliability.
- Unit economics by evidence family; kill or reprice negative-margin work.
- A narrow, evidence-backed market model and buyer pipeline.
- A weekly investor/partner update generated from the ledger, with no vanity inflation.

### Six months

- Repeatable customer acquisition in one wedge.
- Clean recurring revenue, retention and margin cohorts.
- Longitudinal evidence that competitors cannot recreate instantly.
- A small team or contractor network with clear operational ownership.
- Capital becomes optional acceleration rather than rescue.

## Series A quality gates

"Series A ready" is an internal standard, not a claim about any investor's threshold:

- complete stranger commission with an SLA;
- at least five attributable outside paying accounts;
- repeat use and three 30-day retained feeds;
- positive and measured gross margin for the lead product;
- one ICP, one urgent job and documented wins/losses;
- uptime, incident and correction history;
- IP, license, privacy, security and corporate diligence clean;
- 18-month plan that links every hire and compute pound to measurable growth.

Until those exist, describe CSOAI as technically advanced and commercially early.

## Capital policy

Prioritize customer cash and non-dilutive funding. Use outside equity when it accelerates a proven loop, not to finance uncontrolled breadth. Wallet funds, self-settlements and founder purchases are operating or testing costs. They never count as revenue, traction or market validation.

## Canonical daily scoreboard

Track only: outside commissions, successful deliveries, unique outside payers, repeat payers, active feeds, qualified buyer replies, external citations, completion latency, variable cost, gross margin, worker uptime, correction rate and admitted signed records. Keep downloads, stars, repositories, indexed subjects and raw card totals as secondary distribution/coverage metrics.
