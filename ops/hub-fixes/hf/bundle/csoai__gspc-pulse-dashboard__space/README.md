# GSPC Live Pulse — public evidence dashboard

Renders the append-only GSPC evidence series live from the CC0 dataset.
Every number carries its source and as_of. Verify everything free at https://councilof.ai/gspc-verify
Data: https://huggingface.co/datasets/csoai/gspc-pulse

## Limitations

**Measurement is not certification.** This Space displays measurements recorded by Council of AI (CSOAI Ltd) under the stated method and date. What it shows is not a certification, audit opinion, conformity assessment or legal determination (including under the EU AI Act).
