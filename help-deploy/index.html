📖 MEOK Glossary — 100+ Terms Across 12 Frameworks

Comprehensive definitions for EU AI Act, UK AI Bill, DORA, NIS2, ISO 42001, GDPR, HIPAA, SOC 2, NIST AI RMF, AISI, CAISI, Montreal, Toronto. AEO/AISEO-optimized.

AI Act
Regulation (EU) 2024/1689. World's first comprehensive AI regulation. Fully applicable 2 Aug 2026.
AI system
Machine-based system that infers, from inputs, how to generate outputs (predictions, content, decisions) that influence physical or virtual environments.
Article 5 (Prohibited AI)
AI practices always banned: subliminal manipulation, exploitation of vulnerabilities, social scoring, real-time biometric ID.
Article 6 (High-Risk AI)
AI in products covered by EU safety law OR listed in Annex III (biometric, critical infra, education, employment, essential services, law enforcement, migration, justice, democratic processes).
Article 9 (Risk Management)
Continuous risk management system across the lifecycle.
Article 10 (Data Governance)
Training + validation + testing datasets must be relevant, representative, free of errors. Bias detection/mitigation required.
Article 13 (Transparency)
High-risk AI must allow users to interpret system output.
Article 14 (Human Oversight)
High-risk AI must allow effective human oversight.
Article 15 (Accuracy, Robustness, Cybersecurity)
High-risk AI must be accurate, robust, secure.
Article 50 (Transparency for non-high-risk)
AI systems interacting with people, generating content, or performing emotion recognition/biometric categorisation must inform users. Deepfakes labelled. AI-generated content machine-readable. Cliff 2 Aug 2026.
Annex III (High-risk use cases)
8 use cases: biometric, critical infrastructure, education, employment, essential services, law enforcement, migration, justice, democratic processes.
Annex IV (Technical Documentation)
11+ documents: general description, design specs, data, training methodology, risk assessment, validation, performance metrics, post-market monitoring plan.
AISI
UK AI Safety Institute. Voluntary commitments. UK AI Bill in draft.
DORA
Digital Operational Resilience Act (EU financial services). 5 pillars. Applies 17 Jan 2025.
NIS2
Network and Information Security Directive 2 (EU cybersecurity). 18,000+ entities. 10 measures in Article 21. Applies 18 Oct 2024.
UK AI Bill
Parliamentary draft (H2 2026). Regulates frontier AI developers + high-risk AI deployers.
ISO 42001
AI Management System standard. Annex A controls. Dec 2023.
NIST AI RMF
US federal. Map / Measure / Manage / Govern.
Ed25519
Public-key signature system. MEOK uses Ed25519 to sign attestations.
MCP
Model Context Protocol. Open standard for AI agent tools. MEOK ships 32 MCP servers.
CAISI
California AI Safety Institute. Voluntary capabilities framework. US alignment.
Montreal / Toronto
Ethical AI principles. MEOK Maternal Covenant absorbs.
