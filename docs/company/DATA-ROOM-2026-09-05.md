### Which DOI to quote, and why it matters

Zenodo mints two DOIs per deposit. The **concept** DOI (`…21991104`) is version-agnostic and always
resolves to the newest version — it is the correct thing to cite in prose, and the estate cites it
everywhere. The **version** DOI (`…21991105`) names one immutable deposit.

**They are not interchangeable in third-party indexes.** Measured 2026-09-06:

| Index | concept `…104` | version `…105` |
|---|---|---|
| OpenAlex | INDEXED | INDEXED |
| OpenAIRE | **none** | **FOUND** |

An earlier pass in this workstream reported *"OpenAIRE has not harvested the concept DOI"* and
treated it as a discoverability gap. **That was wrong.** OpenAIRE has harvested the record; it
indexes it under the version DOI, and the query used the concept one. The record carries
`communities: [{"id": "council-of-ai"}]`, which is the harvest hook, and it worked.

The practical consequence, and the reason both are published here: **anyone checking our OpenAIRE
presence with the DOI we advertise will find nothing and conclude we are not indexed.** That is a
false negative we were handing to reviewers, including grant reviewers.
