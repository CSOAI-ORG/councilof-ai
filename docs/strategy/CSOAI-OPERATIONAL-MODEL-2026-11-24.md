# Council of AI operational consolidation — proposed v1, 24 November 2026

**State: DRAFT BUSINESS/OPERATIONAL PLAN.** This document does not adopt the human council, appoint any officer, change a scheduler, authorize keys/spend/sends/actuators, grant a review mandate, create a product/price, merge code or represent a customer outcome. The existing CSOAI legal/company owners, canonical GSPC method owner, publisher and separate MEOK runtime owners must approve their respective parts.

## Executive decision

**Mission:** make trustworthy claims about bounded AI-agent/model workflows easier to check, correct and use across organizations. **Core service hypothesis:** portable, independently reproducible **measurement + authorized-action + observed-effect + recipient-acceptance evidence**, not another AI model, chatbot, observability stack, model leaderboard, unqualified score or certification business.

Position Council of AI as an evidence/measurement body, CouncilOS as the human-facing workspace, GSPC as the measurement instruments, Layer-0 as custody/provenance, Harness X as bounded verification/control instrumentation, MEOK as separately governed orchestration and H.I.M. as an educational/reviewer-support interface. SovSpace remains a sandbox twin for controlled predictions and counterfactuals. No cross-authority, self-grading or simulation→observed promotion. Existing brands and legal entities retain their owner decisions.

**Business thesis with proof order:** outside question → scoped defined method → reproducible evidence → independently checkable output → a genuinely authorized human decision where required → qualified executor → observed effect → receiving-party acceptance → repeat use. Payment is a separate step and never a proxy for trust.

**What NOT to do:** launch a second crawler, restart old 13-resource jobs, rewrite signed history, create a composite company score, add new subscriptions or SKUs, put a self-voting council in charge of its grader, build another agent runtime or copy competitor code without licence and rights review.

## Capability acceptance ladder — no flattened green score

1. **DISCOVERABLE:** public documentation/listing/tool route exists. Host install or customer use UNKNOWN.
2. **SOURCE_REVIEWED:** method and source revisions reviewed, with licenses and denominator. Measurement or deployment UNKNOWN.
3. **TESTED:** applicable negative/positive tests passed on an exact source head. Public release UNKNOWN.
4. **PROTECTED_RELEASE:** release pipeline succeeded and deployed bytes verified. Browser completion UNKNOWN.
5. **REPRODUCED:** independent implementation/person recovered and checked original exact bytes; scope bounded. Actual effect UNKNOWN.
6. **HUMAN_AUTHORIZED:** separately authenticated authorized person approved a specific action under current revocation/scope rules.
7. **EFFECT_OBSERVED:** permitted qualified executor actually acted; receiving party independently read the effect.
8. **RECIPIENT_ACCEPTED:** external user acknowledges scope/delivery with dated private/public receipt as authorized.
9. **RETURN_USE:** independently attributable subsequent use at a separate time; this is not a second fee unless actually observed.
10. **ECONOMIC_CONTRIBUTION:** recognized receipts minus directly attributable execution/reviewer/hosting/correction/payment costs; use UNKNOWN if not reconciled.

A workflow may have several stages at once; they are not a scalar maturity score. “UNMEASURED” remains an answer, zero observations are not a zero result. x402 payment only means payment, not issuer authentication, permission, delivery or validation. Approval must be current at decision time, not inferred from signed historical bytes.

## Research flywheel that does not contaminate the truth

Source delta / rights-cleared failure / owner-submitted problem
→ classify as candidate, keep source retrieval time and original measurement time separately
→ select only applicable GSPC axes, method and frozen bank; source + rights + data-use review
→ preregister controls: base, no human, irrelevant human, task-relevant information, governed/ungoverned action, sham/negative controls
→ run **bounded offline simulator** or separately authorized *real* experiment (never confuse)
→ independently score with an evidence-blind grader, error bars and false-alarm reporting
→ reaction module recommends bounded recheck or challenge; **advisory** only
→ existing owner decides correction/accepted method/release
→ independent reader verifies exact new artifact and, where relevant, real permitted effect
→ rights-cleared new regression cases enter versioned bank; repeat use and actual utility measured.

### Why this differs from raw model merging
The combined benefit is an empirical interaction effect, not a presumed synergy. Use matched-task factorial designs from #4730 and heldout tests to ask whether MEOK + human information + GSPC verifier actually beats their separate baseline under identical task/compute and missingness controls. A human interface is not authorization; a verified output is not observed effect. Do not update weights or model policy solely from an optimization dashboard.

### Experimental portfolio — bounded and independently scored

| ID | Hypothesis | Experiment / comparison | Actual evidence needed | Current disposition |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| E01 | Human task-relevant information helps over human presence | Frozen matched tasks: none vs irrelevant vs relevant, no model policy adaptation | Consenting humans, calibrated labels, blinded grader and per-condition repetitions | Synthetic design already exists, **real trial unrun** |
| E02 | Independent deterministic referee reduces self-voting errors | 12 identical vs diverse advisory outputs, majority vs nonvoting evidence rule; all-wrong, answer-leak and source-auth controls | New sealed bank, truly independent reviewer custody | Historical leakage finding in #4730; **no BFT proof** |
| E03 | Industrial control charts find a real regression earlier | Frozen timestamped real failure cases, compare Shewhart/EWMA/CUSUM vs standard tests | False positives, detection delay, sample dependence, independent reference standard | #4763 research only; thresholds illustrative |
| E04 | Byte-level changes are not semantic poison | 1/5/25/150 same-size source mutations, with truthful null semantic label | Base-vs-treatment model behavior on held-out runs | Existing #4686 synthetic byte detection; behavior **not tested** |
| E05 | Evidence-linked failures can improve real model routing | Compare a baseline, a compatible merged model, a multi-family router, and an oracle ceiling | Model/weight/dataset rights, equal budget and held-out evaluator | Candidate; no automated MergeKit release |
| E06 | Provenance reduces undetected untrusted-source mistakes | Same apparent hash after fake manifest vs independent source pin; signer key mismatch | External trusted source verification and verifier implementation | Existing negative controls, no universal guarantee |
| E07 | Independent approval/effect gate beats symbolic HITL | Wrong scope, revoked, expired, replay, authorized harmless action | Actual enrolled human, qualified executor and separate receiver | **Highest human acceptance dependency** |
| E08 | SovSpace predicts effects rather than inventing them | Same scenario, preregister outcome forecast, later held-out genuine observation | Prediction scored by independent real outcome, simulation-to-real error | Sandbox only |
| E09 | A correction propagates only to impacted claims | Dependency subgraph replay versus naive full corpus rerun | Exact source join, independent correction, false misses | Claim reactions existing; recheck is advisory |
| E10 | Portable proof yields external value | External party reproduces delivered artifact with no CSOAI login | Actual authorized recipient confirmation + later reuse | Not yet established broadly |

Each experiment needs a signed-off hypothesis, counterfactual definition, outcome unit, randomization, frozen test and train split, source/measurement times, RIGHTS state, reproducibility route, n and uncertainty. Reports cannot turn “NOT_RUN” into zero or PASS. E01–E10 are a prioritization portfolio, not a new blanket daily run.

## Operational rhythm — proposed, NO NEW JOBS CREATED

**Daily existing automation, unchanged:** the read-only public-readback reporter and current scheduled signal/product/reg-watch owners remain where they are. Daily output is at most 3 real deltas and 1 unresolved acceptance dependency. No extra alerts for no-change, no secondary scheduler, no writes from the reporter.

**Daily human operating decision (manual; if capacity exists):** review a single high-priority owner queue. Classify the top unresolved action as SOURCE / TEST / RELEASE / REAL HUMAN / RECIPIENT / COMMERCIAL. Mark missing person as UNSTAFFED, not ASSIGNED. Close only with exact primary receipt. More produced artifacts are not progress if the next stage never becomes accepted.

**Weekly owner review (manual):** one 45-minute scoped agenda with the existing CEO/product/release/legal roles: (a) buyer/receiver evidence, (b) new or regressed product acceptance, (c) rights/corrections, (d) held infrastructure and conflict owners, (e) 3 bounded experiment decisions for the next week. Record who actually attended and accepted the decision. Do not invent a quorum.

**Monthly management review (manual):** qualified deliveries and return users, project gross contribution, support/reviewer load, methods improved and independently reproduced, risk register, public-claim corrections and distribution attribution. Choose stop/continue/retire per workstream. Accounting/legal conclusions need their actual professional owners.

**Quarterly:** actual methodological independence, reviewer competence and conflicts, external standards adoption, customer evidence, legal/insurance, accessible user trials, and explicit budget review; do not renew a stale source automatically.

## Management and SWOT response

**Strength → leverage:** rigorous negative controls, source-pinned methods and open verifier. Put one outside reader through exact reproduction instead of publishing more generated cards.

**Weakness → remediate:** incomplete actual human authority, broad catalog without adoption and partially stale/held infrastructure. Prioritize the real case chain and stop expanding labels, model weights or infrastructure until those gates close.

**Opportunity → test:** the large platforms all optimize their own evaluation/trace workflows. An interoperable receiver-verifiable output that respects source/contract rights may solve a buyer's cross-platform audit pain. Test on their published export formats, not on proprietary data acquired without permission.

**Threat → defend:** cloud platforms can bundle standard tracing/eval affordably; regulations and licensing change; competitors will challenge overclaims. Build a trusted correction and replication trail and avoid unsupported institutional status. Do not claim “the only” or “first” without tested evidence.

## Business model and demand gate

**No new SKU, pricing, subscription promise or revenue projection adopted.** Current offer concept is a **named scoped evidence/reproduction job**. Buyer stages: enquiry; supported question; source/legal scope; accepted deliverable and budget; authorized work; exact output; receiver verification; buyer acceptance; optional settlement; repeat job. Free verifier stays free.

Track an actual unit-economics worksheet only after a job exists: source access rights, labor hours by role, CPU/GPU/provider inference, storage, independent reviewer hours, revisions/disputes, payment fees, tax/accounting allocation and contribution. No £ value assigned to a generated row or a signed hash. A hypothetical grant/market-size figure is not booked turnover.

**Demand gate before catalogue expansion:** at least one real independently accepted external deliverable plus attributable repeat demand that can cover direct delivery and review costs under the owner-approved calculation. One buyer and one repeat demonstrate a narrow signal, not proven scalable product-market fit.
