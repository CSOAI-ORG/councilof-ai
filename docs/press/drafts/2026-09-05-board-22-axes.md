The Council of AI's public measurement board now stands at **22 axis · 22 measured · 0
unmeasured**. Every declared slot on the board has a real graded run behind it.

Two numbers travel together on that board, deliberately. `axes` counts **slots** — a slot is
published so that a gap is visible. `measured_axes` counts slots with a run behind them. Quoting
the first alone would claim measurements that do not exist. Both are **derived** from the axis
arrays the endpoint reads, never typed, so if a future slot is added with no run behind it the
grammar separates the two again on its own.

**Measured is not scored.** Fourteen of the 22 are behavioural axes, where a fleet of models
answers a frozen bank and is graded deterministically. The other eight are financial/domain axes
graded **by rule, with no model, no fleet and no judgement** — issuer-account flags read off the
public ledger and public statistical series. None of the eight has a leader, an accuracy, or a
separation determination, and none contributes to any published mean. An axis with no accuracy
contributes nothing rather than a zero.

Two things the board removes rather than asserts:

- **Our own models are excluded from the public per-axis leaders** on 8 of the 14
  model-comparison axes. A neutral measurement body does not rank its own models against the
  vendors it measures. Their signed cards are untouched — the measurement happened; it is simply
  not published as a public ranking of our own model.
- **Where a named leader had no signed card**, the leader claim is dropped rather than invented
  (3 axes, `public_leader_state=NO_SIGNED_CARD`). The board's promise is that every named leader
  links to the Ed25519 card behind it.

Of the eight financial run artifacts, **one carries an Ed25519 signature and seven are
content-addressed but unsigned**. A content address proves identity of bytes, not signer
authorization, and no signature is inferred from one.

This is measurement, not certification: we do not certify, and we issue no conformity marks. Verification is free,
needs no account, and always will be.
