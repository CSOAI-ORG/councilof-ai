## Title
Engagements — evidence work, scoped and receipted

## Lead
Council of AI helps you inspect what an AI measurement establishes, trace it to its source, and carry that
evidence into your own work. An engagement is one bounded piece of that work, delivered with a receipt you can
re-open later.

## What an engagement is
- **Scoped:** one artefact, one claim set, or one dependency set. Defined before work starts, dated, and
  reference-specific.
- **Receipted:** you receive dated evidence records (our published schema, `csoai.evidence-event/0.1`) plus an
  acceptance receipt stating exactly what was checked, what it establishes, and what it does not.
- **Re-openable:** the result stays retrievable. When the thing you cited changes, a re-run tells you which of your
  conclusions still apply.

## What we offer

### 1. Artifact qualification
You have an evaluation log, model card, benchmark result or dataset. We qualify it: recount what it claims from its
own bytes, state the mapping and versions used, run a negative control, and return evidence records that preserve
every limitation. Missing or malformed evidence stays marked as such — it never becomes a pass.

### 2. Claim maintenance
You cite numbers (ours or others'). We re-read the sources on a schedule, diff by extracted text rather than raw
bytes, adjudicate each difference, and publish corrections. You get a register of what changed and which of your
cited conclusions are affected.

### 3. Dependency change evidence
You operate a set of approved MCP servers or AI dependencies. We watch the published records, and when one changes —
version, endpoint, package digest, declared configuration — you get a dated change record stating which of your
earlier checks may no longer apply.

### 4. Connection audit
We probe every endpoint you advertise (your registries, catalogues, docs) and report which answer and which do not —
with HTTP status and time. A listing that 404s costs more trust than absence; this finds them.

## How it starts
Write to **contact@csoai.org** with the artefact or claim set and the decision it feeds. We reply with a scoped,
dated, reference-specific quote. **Prices are quoted privately** and are never published.

## What this is not
- **Not certification.** We measure; we do not certify, rank, or endorse. No engagement outcome is a grade.
- **Not a security audit.** Change records say "re-check", never "compromised". Connection audits name dead
  endpoints; they do not assess the software behind them.
- **Not assurance of safety.** A recount of an evaluation log does not establish that the model ran as claimed, that
  a benchmark was uncontaminated, or that a system is safe.
- **Not silent.** Every method is stated, every limitation preserved, every correction published.

## FAQ

**Is this a security audit?**
No. A change record tells you that something changed and which earlier checks may not carry over. It does not tell
you whether the new version is safe.

**Do you certify AI systems?**
No. We measure and publish dated evidence with stated limits. Nothing we produce is a grade, a rank or a badge.

**What does it cost?**
Quotes are private, dated and reference-specific, scoped before work starts. We do not publish prices.

**What happens to our data?**
The engagement scope names it. We retain the artefacts needed to keep your evidence re-openable, under the data
class declared in the scope, and nothing beyond it.

**Who has used this?**
See [/traction](/traction/) for dated, qualified records of outside use — and their limits. Nobody there is a
customer; we say so on the same page.

**Can we self-serve first?**
Yes. The board, the verifier and the corrections ledger are free forever. Start with
[/claim-maintenance](/claim-maintenance/) and the public root; an engagement is for when you want the same treatment
applied to your own artefacts.
