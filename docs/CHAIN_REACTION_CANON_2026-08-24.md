## The Waves (the chain reaction, defined)

- **Wave 0 — the signed spine works.** Proven by stranger-executed verification. Without this
  nothing else exists. (Estate: did:web live, signed leaderboard + signals + cards verify PASS.)
- **Wave 1 — verification as a free public utility.** Watches, counters, verify pages, corrections:
  zero-auth, zero-fee, stranger-runnable. (Estate: /gspc-verify, /verify-leaderboard, free Article 50
  passport — all live.)
- **Wave 2 — third parties build ON the rail.** Insurers price off cards, courts cite signed
  records, platforms embed trust fields, enterprises require signed measurement.
- **Wave 3 — network effects.** The challenge ledger becomes the court of record; the MCP/A2A trust
  layers get adopted; the corrections register becomes reputation gravity.
- **Wave 4 — sector/axis replication.** Every regulated vertical gets the sandwich: measure →
  play/fix → re-measure. (Estate: 13 axes firing + first two post-launch axes signed.)
- **Wave 5 — load-bearing infrastructure.** Procurement defaults, referenced-in-regulation, too
  useful to ignore — claimed ONLY when verifiable criteria (271) say so.

## The never-outruns rule (binding)

**No new surface, sector board, axis, desk, dataset, or ledger ships without a signature from day
one.** The trust root is extended (multi-sig, foundation custody — EXP 211/220) BEFORE the estate
grows past its custody capacity. A CI/build gate enforces this: unsigned new surface = red.
