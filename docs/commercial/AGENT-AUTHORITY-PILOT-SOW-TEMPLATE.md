## 1. Commercial objective and eligibility

Customer wishes to assess the enforceability and evidence quality of an **explicitly authorised single agent/tool action** in a controlled sandbox. The engagement assesses the specific software and controls selected below and does not amount to general-purpose security certification, legal compliance advice or incident response.

The customer must be able to show it owns or has written permission to exercise the exact sandbox system and effect destination. No third-party attack, real transaction, external data extraction or production change is authorised through this SOW without separately approved written scope and legal/security review.

## 3. Data and security responsibilities

The customer owns lawful access/permission for the in-scope system and must not provide customer secrets or personal records through the public enquiry page or public GitHub. Only approved private channels may carry confidential scope evidence. Define controller/processor roles, cross-border transfers, security controls, permitted subcontractors, retention/deletion and breach notification in the signed agreement before ingesting personal data.

CSOAI must identify a technical operator, a person with approval authority and an independent reviewer who has accepted the role, competence, schedule and conflicts check. No model, CI test or autogenerated record can substitute for a signed authority decision from the correct party.

## 4. Deliverables

1. Frozen approved method, exact tool/agent versions, input cases, scope and expectations.
2. Raw reproducible run records, controls, failures, uncheckable conditions and independently observed or expressly unknown effects.
3. Machine-readable evidence with source digests and optional *verified* signatures/root proofs, plus a human-readable limitations report. Never claim an unverified digest or test key is production signing authority.
4. Reproduction instructions, artefact manifest, provenance, corrections route and retention/deletion plan.
5. Reviewer exceptions, customer reproduction notes, decision log and delivery acknowledgement.

Use the canonical Council evidence-event and authority-effect formats wherever suitable. GSPC board admission is a separate governed measurement decision, never an automatic SOW deliverable.

## 5. Acceptance and remedies

**Prerequisite:** the parties agree measurable pass/fail and uncheckable thresholds **before** the run. Accepting a report is not accepting the underlying agent as safe. A technically honest report may contain failed or unknown outcomes.

| Checkpoint | Evidence of completion |

| Test execution | Actual fixed-case run log and source pin; denominators, negative controls and all failures retained |
| Effect observation | Separate readback of the exact observable destination; denied cases show no effect or are labelled uncheckable |
| Review | Independent reviewer signs only the claimed *scope* and records dissent/conflicts |
| Delivery | Customer has safe access to required files and reproducible verifier instructions |
| Acceptance | Customer signs a private acceptance record or a written list of exceptions within [AGREED PERIOD] |
| Commercial closure | Invoice independently issued, settled and reconciled; no booking/payment inferred from accepted test |

Define in the signed contract what correction/retesting occurs if agreed acceptance fails, with commercial remedies decided by authorised people rather than by an AI agent. Do not publish private customer evidence without explicit lawful permission.

## 6. Exclusions, claims and communication permission

No automatic GSPC score, regulatory finding, certification, penetration-test authorisation, continuous monitoring, named vendor endorsement, guaranteed attack prevention or permanent protection is included.

Customer logos, commercial references, case studies, external partner tags and announcement copy require explicit separate opt-in approval of **exact claim, actual result, audience, rights and publication date**. A synthetic case must never be described as a live buyer incident.
