# Independent recipient acceptance script

Private test plan. No claim that an external person has completed these steps.

**Packet:** one exact exported source-consistency packet. **Reviewer:** not its producer. **Expected request and digest:** provided through the agreed trusted channel, not accepted solely from the packet. **Time:** record actual completion time; do not prefill a success result.

## Inspector journey
Open the cover sheet and explain, in the reviewer's own words, what was measured and what was not. Locate the original source date, subject and method. Run the independent packet-check command without executing bundled method code. Compare the reported count and statuses with the original source. Identify whether the packet authenticates its publisher; the correct answer for the demonstration is no. Record every ambiguity, missing input and manual intervention.

## Subject-owner journey
Provide a written scope and authority boundary. Identify confidential, personal, licensed or prohibited materials before transfer. Review the claim-to-evidence mapping and disagree with one deliberately planted interpretation. The correction path must preserve the original and show what changed, why and on whose authority. Do not allow payment or the subject's approval to override an independent finding.

## Public-reader journey
Read a suitably redacted public candidate without an account. Find the exact observation, date and limitation. Distinguish indexed rows from new measurements and a signature from a truthful claim. Follow a retained historical URL and see its invalid/superseded status without losing the original bytes. Record where a reader might mistake the result for certification.

## Negative controls
A changed request ID, source bytes, method, row count, observation date, budget, authority or packet digest must fail rather than display a positive badge. Missing information is unknown, not zero. A packet that is internally consistent but lacks an independent issuer trust path must not be presented as authenticated. A withdrawn or superseded observation remains historically inspectable but must not be presented as current.

## Record format
Reviewer role and independence basis; packet/request digests; start/end time; steps completed; observed failures; assistance required; accessibility needs; data-handling permission; acceptance, conditional acceptance or rejection; unresolved issues; signature or acknowledgement only where separately authorised.

Internal automated checks in this release are engineering evidence. They are not independent human acceptance, a customer purchase, a licence clearance or legal advice.
