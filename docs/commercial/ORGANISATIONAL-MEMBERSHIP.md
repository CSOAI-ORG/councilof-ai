# Council of AI organisational membership

## First customer journey

Start with organisations in open-source communities Council already participates in, including the Linux Foundation ecosystem. Identify a named evidence owner and one public output. Article 50 marking is the first offered use case. Record the exact output or digest, detector method and version, observation date, observed mark and any unresolved question. Give the evidence owner a way to verify the result and report a correction. Recheck the output after a change when scope and access permit.

Public evidence and verification stay free for SMEs and all other organisations. A prospective member can enquire about recurring support, such as incorporating evidence into its workflow, scheduled checks or arranging human review. Agree deliverables, review capacity and terms in writing. Paid evidence packs and invoices remain available through the existing catalogue, subject to eligibility and scope. Use only public or authorised inputs.

Decline payment from organisations Council ranks, including via membership or sponsorship. Log the conflict decision before offering payment. Keep sponsorship terms and disclosures apart from evaluators and findings. Record who can accept evidence, resolve an ambiguity or authorise release. Keep the original record and a linked correction.

## Billing and revenue

| Stream | Billing | Reporting |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Organisational membership | Monthly or annual Stripe subscription after written eligibility and scope approval | Annualise active contracted membership. Track new, expanded, reduced and churned recurring value. |
| Sponsorship | Separate written agreement and invoice | Report contracted recurring sponsorship separately. Record one-off sponsorship separately. |
| Machine requests | Existing x402 endpoints and their quoted per-call terms | Report settled external usage net of refunds, distinguishing wallets from customers. Exclude from membership ARR. |
| Evidence packs or custom delivery | Existing GBP invoice process | Report agreed one-off work and collected cash separately. |

Once the legal merchant account, membership terms and recurring price are approved, use an organisation-specific Stripe invoice or Checkout session tied to an approved enquiry. Confirm the first live payment and renewal/cancellation handling before allocating entitlements. Keep customer identifiers and payment reconciliation in the private operations register. Base entitlement on verified billing state. The retired browser checkout stays retired.

At an illustrative annual fee of £5,000, 100 organisations produce £500,000 membership ARR and 1,000 produce £5 million. At £20,000, those counts are 25 and 250. Treat these as scenarios, not price offers or forecasts. The qualified pool, likely conversion and delivery cost need evidence before forecasting. Track accepted independently reproducible deliveries, repeat requests, signed members, renewals and review hours. No valuation follows from the scenarios alone.

## How results improve the service

Use the regulatory watch to queue changes, rerun permitted checks, and route unresolved findings to a named human. Retain dated receipts, corrections, portable fixtures and versioned scorers for reuse where rights permit. Measure how often a recipient reproduces a result, what they corrected and what they used in a decision. Use the confirmed failures and false holds to improve the next run.
