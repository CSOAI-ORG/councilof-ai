## The clean-ask pattern (doctrine lines)

- **Free access honest:** the free tier (detection, verify, leaderboard) is genuinely free and
  says so. No bait-and-switch.
- **Never a paid rank:** no ranked party pays us; the license legs are data-license +
  researcher-access + regulator-evidence (all buyer-not-ranked).
- **Measurement, not certification:** we never claim to certify; we attesta measurement.
- **No fabricated capability on contact:** any claim in an outreach message is provable via the
  public verify path (that IS the pitch).

## What we do NOT do on outreach

- No certification-language in any message.
- No claim we cannot prove via the public verify path.
- No paid-rank offer to any target (the doctrine line).
- No banned codenames or internal-engine mentions in any external note.
