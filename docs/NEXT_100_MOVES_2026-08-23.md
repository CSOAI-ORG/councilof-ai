## What we do NOT do (doctrine lines)

- No certification language; measurement evidence only.
- No fake capability (honest-refusal is correct; never fabricate).
- No banned codenames public; no paid rank (nobody-ranked-pays).
- Never rename a live infra hostname in a de-brand (the lesson).
- Signing key never travels; rotation appends, never erases.
