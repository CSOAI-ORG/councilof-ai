### 1.2 What is missing publicly — the hole

Nobody publishes an **independent, signed, witnessed, monthly record** of:

1. per-chain `totalSupply` of BENJI at a stated block/time (8 chains + BNB UNCHECKABLE), **and**
2. the sum of those supplies **beside** the shares-outstanding / net-assets figure the fund itself files in that month's N-MFP, **and**
3. the *disclosure cadence* actually observed (N-MFP filed on day N; holdings page updated; contracts page changed/unchanged; a chain added/removed), **and**
4. the delta of all three from the previous month,

with `unmeasured[]` stating what is not reconcilable from outside (whitelist membership, in-transit mint/burn between chains, the transfer agent's book itself). The transfer agent has it (private). PwC sees it annually. rwa.xyz shows a number with no signature, no history, no method. The SEC has just asked the public how the official register and the chain should relate — and there is no independent longitudinal record in the docket.

Our BENJI leaves already have the field for it: `unmeasured: ["primary_register_reconciliation"]`. Turning that from UNMEASURED into a **deterministic fact** (on-chain sum at month-end vs the fund's own filed figure, with the difference stated, never interpreted) is the whole move. It is the XRPL reserve-attestation grammar applied to a registered fund.

## 3. The black-swan play

**The sentence.** *When an on-chain register event becomes a regulatory event — a tokenised fund or payment-stablecoin whose public supply, filed figure and monthly disclosure stop lining up in a month that GENIUS (effective 18 Jan 2027), MiCA Art 30/54 or the SEC's new transfer-agent rule now cares about — the only independent, signed, Rekor/OTS-witnessed, monthly time-series of "on-chain supply beside filed disclosure, with what was unmeasured stated", dated from 1 Oct 2026, is ours, and every administrator, auditor, insurer and journalist who needs a dated third-party record that day already knows the verify is free.*

Why it is a black swan in the brief's sense: low probability in any given month (registers are reconciled, whitelists work, attestations land), high payoff when it happens (the record cannot be manufactured after the fact — a leaf dated before the event with a public-log witness is unforgeable, and we will be the only holder), and the estate is uniquely positioned (the grammar, signer, root, witness, OSCAL wrapper and 402 rail all exist; the marginal cost of the series is a cron).

**The three tells that would make it fire (all public, all watchable free):** (1) a month where the sum of per-chain supplies and the filed figure diverge beyond the previous months' band *and* the divergence persists into the next filing; (2) a stablecoin whose stated monthly attestation does not appear by its own stated cadence (the `cadence_observed` field, no interpretation); (3) a chain added or removed on an issuer's contracts page without a corresponding public notice. Each is recorded as a **fact in a leaf**, never as a headline.

**What we must hold BEFORE it happens (the evidence, in order):**

1. **The series.** `benji.onchain.supply.delta` + `stablecoin.cadence.delta` leaves on the 1st of each month from **1 Oct 2026**, GHA-signed, in the root, Rekor-witnessed, OTS-stamped, EAS/XRPL memo when the owner's dust wallet exists. One root anchor per publish, never N leaf anchors. Missing a month breaks the series — the cron is the product.
2. **The method, frozen and citable.** A Zenodo versioned DOI for the reader + the leaf schema + the exact explorer endpoints and block-selection rule ("last block ≤ 23:59:59 UTC on the last calendar day"), so a stranger recomputes the same number. Method changes get a new version and a corrections-ledger line, never a silent edit (C-2026-0902-07 is the precedent for why).
3. **The filed-figure crosswalk.** For each fund/issuer: which public document, which field, which cadence (N-MFP field id, attestation PDF url + sha). `unmeasured[]` lists what is not public (whitelist, in-transit).
4. **The disclaimer.** UK BMR "not a benchmark" + "measurement, not certification" + liability-cap ToS on the interop pages before the first delta (counsel wording).
5. **Docket presence.** Our signed leaves cited in at least one public comment (GENIUS NPRM by 19 Oct; SEC TA proposal within its 60 days) so the record is *in the regulator's own file* with a date.
6. **Recompute by a stranger.** One outside party (Kaggle run, a university, an OSCAL vendor) recomputes one monthly leaf and we publish their run as a `public.notice`. Without it the series is ours; with it, it is a record.
7. **A settled 402 receipt.** One real payment for one bundle so `/api/revenue` shows a non-null count before anyone needs us — a rail that has never carried a dollar is not credible the day it matters.

**What must never be said (on the day, or before):** "under-reserved" · "mismatch" / "discrepancy" (we print two numbers and a difference) · "we caught / we found / we flagged" · any of the eight NEVER-words in the lane brief (the compliance-verdict words, the damage words, the conformity words) · any causal claim about *why* the numbers differ · any AUM/NAV/yield/suitability statement · "verified by Council of AI" (it is *verifiable*, by anyone, free) · "Franklin Templeton client/partner" · "the transfer agent's register is wrong" (we never see it) · "index" / "benchmark" for the series · "proof of reserves" (that is an attestation product with a legal meaning; ours is a supply-and-cadence reading).
