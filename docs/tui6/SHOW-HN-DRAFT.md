# Show HN: CSOAI — Independent AI Measurement Board (22 axes, signed evidence, free verification)

**Title:** Show HN: We measure AI systems on 22 governance axes and publish signed, independently verifiable evidence

**Body:**

We built an independent AI measurement board at councilof.ai. It measures AI models on 22 axes (governance, safety, provenance, continuity, plus 8 financial/deterministic-fact axes) using frozen test banks and deterministic grading.

Key facts:
- 22 axes measured, 14 model-comparison + 8 deterministic-facts
- 335 signed measurement cards (Ed25519, off-device MPC ceremony)
- 257-card public Merkle root, Rekor-witnessed
- 425 stablecoins indexed across 211 chains
- Free verification at councilof.ai/gspc-verify — no account needed
- MCP server at councilof.ai/mcp (12 tools, 8 free)
- x402 payment on Base mainnet for paid evidence

What we don't do:
- We don't certify, accredit, or issue conformity assessments
- We don't claim "safe" or "compliant" — measurement, not certification
- We don't rank our own models against vendors (own-model exclusion)

The board is live at councilof.ai/api/gspc. Every card is independently verifiable from the published bytes.

We're a UK company (CSOAI Ltd, 16939677). Revenue: $0.02 from 1 external payer. We're honest about what we measure and what we don't.
