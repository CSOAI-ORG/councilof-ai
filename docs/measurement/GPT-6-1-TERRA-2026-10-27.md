## What this is

A dated, sourced record of the benchmark numbers other parties have published for one frontier
model, so the numbers can be cited, compared, and later replaced by a Council of AI run when a
funded credential exists.

## What this is not

- **CSOAI did not measure GPT-6.1 Terra.** Every number below is someone else's number, and each row
  carries its own provenance class.
- **The rows are not comparable to each other.** Different harnesses, item sets, effort settings and
  graders. Each row says whether a like-for-like counterpart exists.
- **This is not a ranking of Terra against our models.** The public board excludes our own models from
  the fleet by rule, and Terra has no cell on any of the board's 14 model-comparison axes.

# The comparison to CSOAI

Two separate questions, answered separately.

## Question 1 — like-for-like on our own banks: **UNMEASURED**

GPT-6.1 Terra has never been run on a Council of AI bank. There is no card, no per-item row and no
signed record for it. The board therefore has no Terra cell on any of its 14 model-comparison axes,
and none is invented here.

**The one line that closes it:** one run of `harness/bench/run_bench.py` against `csoai/gspc-gov`
(237 items) with a funded OpenRouter or OpenAI credential, graded by the mill's `read_label`,
publishing the per-item ledger. That produces a comparable number, and nothing else does.

## Question 2 — like-for-like on public benchmarks: **NO CSOAI FIGURE EXISTS**

The estate holds no published Council of AI measurement of any of our own models on Terminal-Bench 4.0,
MMMU-Pro, OSWorld 2.1, Agents' Last Exam, the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, HealthBench,
MentalHealthBench, ExploitBench, SEC-Bench Pro, or the DualEntry Accounting AI Benchmark. There is
therefore no CSOAI-vs-Terra comparison to be had on any of them, in either direction, without first
running one.

# How the Terra-vs-MEOK run is already built

The comparison is **not** blocked on engineering. It is blocked on a balance.

## The harness exists and has already been run against a frontier model

A parallel lane built a frozen 24-item, statute-anchored governance probe as an Inspect eval
(`harness/tasks/gspc_governance.py`): 24 items, each pinned to an EU AI Act article by an
"anchor" string, graded by **deterministic exact-token matching** — no model judges another
model. An answer carrying no recognisable tier token is `UNMEASURED` (`NOANSWER`), not wrong,
which is the same denominator rule the card corpus uses. The task's own docstring carries the
usage line:

```
inspect eval gspc_governance.py --model openai/gpt-4o
```

The Kimi run used the OpenRouter id form, so the Terra arm is
`--model openrouter/openai/gpt-6.1-terra` with `OPENROUTER_API_KEY` set.

## The precedent: what a frontier-vs-MEOK comparison looks like here

A Kimi K3.1 Pro vs MEOK comparison on that probe is already complete
(`council-os.paired-model-comparison/0.1`, 2026-10-27). Its own numbers, for the shape of the
result:

| | Kimi K3.1 Pro | MEOK canonical v1 |
|---|---|---|
| correct | 24 | 9 |
| accuracy | 1.0 | 0.375 |
| usable_n | 24 | 24 |
| unmeasured | 0 | 0 |

Paired: 9 both correct, 15 Kimi only, 0 MEOK only, 0 both wrong. Exploratory two-sided exact
McNemar on the discordant items: p = 6.10 × 10⁻⁵. The record states its own limits: n = 24 is
below the harness's `usable_n >= 30` interval-publication threshold, so no confidence interval,
rank, or separation claim is asserted; and MEOK and CSOAI/GSPC are affiliated, so it is an
operator-run comparison and not independent leaderboard confirmation.

## The arm split must be published with the Terra number

MEOK is not a bare model behind an API. On a probe, the MEOK arm was observed to answer with
`routed_model: gspc-statutory-arbiter-seat13` — a **deterministic statutory arbiter route**,
not a model. A like-for-like comparison therefore has to say, beside the score, **how many items
each arm answered without a model**. The estate already has the tooling for that
(`benchmark/federation/probe-bench-arm-split.mjs`, which computes the split from a record's own
per-item ledgers), and a signed record that scores well while answering most of its items from a
rule cascade is a result about the product as shipped, not about a model. A Terra comparison that
omits the split would be the same mistake in a new place.

## What running it would produce

1. `inspect eval gspc_governance.py --model openrouter/openai/gpt-6.1-terra` — the per-item log.
2. The paired comparison against the MEOK arm, with the arm split, the `usable_n` / `unmeasured`
   counts, and the exact McNemar on the discordant items.
3. A GSPC-gov run over the **full 237-item bank** with the mill's `read_label` grader
   (`harness/bench/run_bench.py`), which is the number that would give Terra a cell on the board
   and make it comparable with the 139/237 and 128/237 the board already publishes.
4. A row in `docs/measurement/gpt-6-1-terra-2026-10-27.json` that moves Terra from `UNMEASURED`
   to measured, with the run's own provenance class.

Until then, the honest state of Terra on this estate is the one recorded above: its published
numbers are recorded with their sources, and on our own banks it is `UNMEASURED`.
