Subject: New I-D: draft-templeman-scitt-framing-space-00

I have posted draft-templeman-scitt-framing-space-00, "Measuring the CBOR Framing Space of
COSE_Sign1 Data-Hash Pre-images" (Informational).
A COSE_Sign1 statement can be serialized into many distinct byte sequences that all decode to the
same data item, so a data-hash over the wire octets is sensitive to that framing while the
signature over the statement is not: two parties can hold the same signed statement and compute
different identifiers for it. The draft measures the size and shape of that framing space rather
than proposing a change, and I would value review of the enumeration and of whether the registry
implications are stated correctly.
