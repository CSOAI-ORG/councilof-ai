actually works. Every step names its door and its honest state. No conformity
opinions, no typed prices, no invented history.

## Hour 2–4 — ASK
- A probe of the named system's reachable surfaces against our frozen tests
  (the walk begins at /api/request-attestation with subject + axis; the door answers
  402 with a challenge until funded, then the workspace walks 3 steps:
  inspect → authorise outside → re-call/inspect then verify).
- Deliverable: a measurement-gap list — what is MEASURED, what is UNMEASURED,
  what is uncheckable and why. Absent ≠ zero (null_rule).

## Hour 4–8 — MEASURE + SIGN
- The measurement run against the frozen method ($METHOD not named here; the

- Output: cards under the card-attestation key (Ed25519 under
  did:web:csoai.org#card-attestation-1), each with signature bytes, public key,
  and preimage rule — verifiable by the stranger verifier.

## Hour 8–12 — ANCHOR + DELIVER
- Root: https://councilof.ai/api/root (permissionless). Rekor + OTS chains as
  the ceremony provides (2026-09-05: 4 OTS receipts, 684 bytes).
- The chosen SKU artefact (docs/product/<sku>.md: file list, schema, size,
  free preview, 402 door path, verify path, ledger it feeds). Non-empty preview
  is a hard rule — nothing is advertised that cannot deliver.

## Hour 12–24 — SETTLE + REPORT
- x402 settlement on Base (eip155:8453) at the SKU's exact payTo; the workspace
  then returns the signed artefact. Nothing is claimed delivered before bytes exist.
- Weekly report template: corrections feed (https://councilof.ai/api/corrections)
  + board delta + next-cycle recommendations.
- The partner's own decision: publish or keep private (never endorsed either way).
