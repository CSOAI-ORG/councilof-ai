## Charter and human-operation mapping

The ten rows attach to the existing 33 specialist definitions; they do not create ten new
authorities or running hives. A specialist observes, tests and proposes within the existing
task grant. Its own measurement must never grant it execution, signing or admission authority.

For the end user, show three separate facts: **what was observed**, **what is still unknown**,
and **what action a named human can approve or stop**. A changed source produces a review task,
not an accusation or automatic sanction. A failed fetch produces an evidence gap, not a zero
score. A digest proves consistency of retained bytes, not truth, legitimacy or safety.

The private governance backend should bind the task, permitted tool, target, limits, expiry
and expected effect to its existing proposal/decision receipt. Recheck that grant immediately
before an effect, retain a result or unresolved-effect state, and make Stop prevent new work
while reconciling anything already dispatched. Source correction and replay belong to the
same evidence trail; they do not create a new public measurement or human approval.

Offline operation can verify local frozen policies and evidence using available trusted keys.
It must label missing current revocations, network receipts or independent review as unknown.
It cannot provide universal tracking or stop software outside its enforcement boundary.

## Interpretation limits

The discovery runner's 3-of-5 threshold was chosen after seeing the data. Its SHA-negative
controls only check payload consistency. Some public responses were capped and the selected
subjects form convenience samples. None of those facts justify silently changing the original
axis definition so that discovery becomes a measurement. The generated audit retains these
limits and the current ten-axis hold explicitly.
