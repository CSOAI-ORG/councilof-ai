## Four ordered batches
A: one fixed release candidate. Research and UX preparation run in parallel, but final source manifest, build, prerender, approvals, release and readback remain ordered. New non-critical ideas move to the next candidate.
B: inspector/public/subject-owner slice. Exercise success, missing evidence, stale source, conflict, revocation, unauthorised access and recovery before external acceptance.
C: one real paid scope. Use one request identity across the commercial/evidence chain while payment, execution, review, signing and witness states remain orthogonal. Verification stays free; prices remain private; payment never purchases a better score.
D: one evidence-led distribution batch. Prepare three useful canonical artefacts, verify the released bytes, then run approved email outreach and measure qualified replies. No mass spam, duplicate directory submissions, calls or LinkedIn outreach.

## Competitive implications
Credo AI and Holistic AI describe organisational inventories, continuous governance, evidence and agent risk controls. Monitoring alone is not a demonstrated unique moat. Vanta's inspected AI-governance page is a waitlist; Drata's inspected page presents agent governance with limited-availability messaging. Arena includes pre-release community evaluation; Artificial Analysis publishes versioned methodology. OpenRouter supplies provider routing rather than replacing evidence admission. Inspect and Promptfoo are reusable evaluation tooling, not rivals that require another private evaluation engine.

The proposed differentiator is an inspector's ability to trace, reproduce, challenge and reuse a scoped observation, with independent review and usable customer delivery. This is a hypothesis to test against competitors and buyer alternatives, not a proven superiority claim. No assertion that CSOAI already equals a regulated credit-rating agency is made.
