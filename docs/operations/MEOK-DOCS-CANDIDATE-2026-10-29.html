Use the public evidence workspace

Historical reference · 30 October 2026. Canonical website documentation work now supersedes this adoption route. Review the adjacent adoption note; do not deploy this standalone file.

MEOK helps you explore published Council of AI evidence and keep a record of your enquiry. This guide describes the public Ask workspace reviewed on 29 October 2026.

Ask, inspect and keep the result

  1. Open the MEOK workspace and choose What does the board show?, or enter a question in Ask the evidence desk.
  2. Select Ask MEOK. Read the response and follow its source links. Check each source's date, scope and stated limitations before using it.
  3. Select Save transcript & events to download the record. Keep the original file if you want to reopen it.
  4. Use Reopen a saved transcript to load that file. Reopening displays the saved answer; it does not refresh the underlying measurements.

If a request fails or you cancel it, do not treat it as a completed enquiry. If a transcript is rejected, retain the original and use the displayed error to decide whether to try a compatible export or start a new enquiry. Edited or incompatible files may not reopen.

What verification means

A transcript's digest can detect a mismatch between its content and its recorded digest. It is not an authenticated signature, an independent measurement or proof that a third party issued the file. The public workspace does not sign its Ask replies.

For a supported Council of AI record, use the public verifier and retain the record together with the result and its stated checks. A response summary cannot replace the source evidence or its verification.

Your saved transcript

The export is a file you choose to save. Review its contents before sharing it, and keep a copy if you need it later. This guide does not promise cross-device recovery or storage of your transcript by MEOK. Read the privacy notice for the service's data-handling terms.

API keys

This public Ask guide does not document an API-key service. Do not infer private workspace access from the availability of the public page.

MCP integrations

This guide does not establish a live MEOK MCP endpoint. Council of AI publishes its own connection guidance; those interfaces and their evidence boundaries are separate from a saved MEOK transcript.

Operator and measurement boundary
