# Nicholas Templeman · Council of AI

Founder of **[Council of AI](https://councilof.ai/about/)**, the public AI measurement service operated by **CSOAI Ltd** (England and Wales, company 16939677).

We measure how AI systems behave on published tests and make the results available for others to inspect. Our work includes frozen test banks, deterministic grading where the method permits it, signed measurement records, free verification and public corrections.

Counts and measurement dates belong to the [live board data](https://councilof.ai/api/gspc). Coverage is distinct from model ranking: a tie remains a tie, an untested comparison remains untested, and a fact run does not rank models. Signed and unsigned records state their status separately.

## Our role

Council of AI measures and publishes evidence. It does not certify or accredit AI systems, issue legal-compliance determinations, or sell ranking positions. Verification is free. Paid evidence work has a defined scope and does not purchase a favourable result. Our own models are disclosed separately from public rankings.

**MEOK is a CSOAI product.** Its [public workspace](https://meok.ai/) reads Council's public evidence and lets users retain a transcript. Product capabilities and availability must be checked on the current MEOK site; they are not endorsements or measurement results.

## Source and contributions

The [Council source repository](https://github.com/CSOAI-ORG/councilof-ai) contains the site, public APIs and measurement tooling. The [board mirror](https://github.com/CSOAI-ORG/gspc-board) provides dated snapshots. The [Hugging Face profile](https://huggingface.co/csoai) links published datasets and tools.

For reproducible defects or measurement challenges, include the record identifier, method version and steps to reproduce. Use the [security reporting route](https://councilof.ai/security/) for sensitive findings.
