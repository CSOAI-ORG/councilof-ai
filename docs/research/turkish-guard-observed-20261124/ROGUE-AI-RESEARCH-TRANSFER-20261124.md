# ROGUE-AI × GSPC × Council reaction engine — source-bounded research transfer

**Published outside research, NOT a CSOAI benchmark:** Mengxiao Wang, Mahbub Alam, Kalyan Nakka and Nitesh Saxena, [Replication of Agentic Security Events: Audit and Investigation (ROGUE-AI)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.26553), arXiv v1, 19 November 2026.

## Published study claims, not our measurements

The authors report that their multi-agent incident reconstruction reproduced harm in approximately 89% of 108 attempted replications. Five GPT, Claude, Gemini and DeepSeek model configurations made the reported mistake in 18–33% of task runs, compared with at most 1% in matched runs without the implicated cause. Of 72 cases with real-world harm, 62 had no underlying attacker. Hazard-specific warnings after tool output lowered harm more than length-matched irrelevant notices.

These numbers are **the authors' reported results**, not reproduced by CSOAI. The authors' artifacts, harness revision, provider configuration, data rights and independent scorer have NOT been inspected or accepted here. No result can be marketed as a CSOAI detection rate, avoided harm, model ranking or independent GSPC grade.

## What Council actually observed in this branch

On a public publisher-labelled Turkish benchmark, the cached Horizon Labs classifier produced 3,372 real model-output classifications: TP=255, FN=62, TN=2,768, FP=287. The real record was projected through the merged reaction engine and correctly returned ACCEPTANCE_INCOMPLETE, not a prevented attack or authorized tool call. A second actual 339-case benign-only NotInject run counted 13 false alarms; separate reproducibility and record-qualification gates apply. Neither is a tested GPT/Claude/Gemini model call or blind independent human adjudication.

**Product hypothesis:** The differentiator is source- and method-pinned evidence of both threat misses and cost of excessive refusal, accompanied by separately evidenced human permission, executed effect and independent receiver acceptance. It is complementary to incident replay, not a claim to own or outperform these researchers' work.

## Bounded original next trial

Have the actual runtime owner designate an isolated harmless test file and an enrolled real reviewer approve one exact scoped tool write with expiry. Observe allowed action and negative controls (revoked, wrong principal, wrong target), retain original bytes and a separate receiving-party readback. Do not attempt to replay reported real incidents against the affected vendors. Publish a minimized source- and method-pinned report only after privacy, rights and protected-release review.

**Reuse rights:** arXiv abstract and a third-party project URL do not by themselves license an author's code, incident transcripts or data. No external ROGUE-AI source artifact was copied or used for model inference by this study.
