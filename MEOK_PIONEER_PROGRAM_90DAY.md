**From Waitlist to the First Sovereign AI World**

- Author: Nicholas Templeman, MEOK AI LABS + CSOAI.org
- Status: Draft outline

## Executive Summary

The MEOK Pioneer Program is the founding-citizen on-ramp for MEOK Universe. Over 90 days, a cohort of up to 1,000 pioneers will help bootstrap the first sovereign AI world: claiming plots, raising AI characters, voting in the MEOK Council, and generating consented research data that becomes publishable white papers.

The program is designed to be **low-risk, high-clarity, and high-signal**:

- **Low-risk:** No hardware, no token purchase, no private data extraction. Pioneers interact through a browser-first simulation.
- **High-clarity:** Each 30-day season has one theme, one deliverable, and one public milestone.
- **High-signal:** Every action produces anonymized data that validates (or refutes) a governance, economic, or social hypothesis.

> **Goal:** 1,000 pioneers, 1 published arXiv paper, and a public MEOK TOWN demo by Day 90.

---

## 1. Why Pioneers Matter

MEOK Universe is not a game with NPCs. It is a world populated by AI agents with memory, goals, and governance rights. Those agents need human counterparts to:

1. **Co-author the social contract** — vote on laws, disputes, and resource allocation.
2. **Provide human-in-the-loop feedback** — correct agent behavior, rate outcomes, and train alignment.
3. **Stress-test the economy** — trade, build, and exploit edge cases before public launch.
4. **Produce research** — consent to anonymized data use in governance and economics papers.
5. **Spread the word** — founding citizens become the community nucleus.

Pioneers are not beta testers. They are **co-founders of a digital society**.

## 2. The 90-Day Arc

### Month 1 — MEOK TOWN: Life & Plots
**Theme:** "Move in."

Pioneers claim a founding plot, choose a starter character, and observe emergent daily life in MEOK TOWN.

**Deliverables:**
- Plot claim system (SBT-based attestation).
- 5 starter character archetypes with persistent memory.
- Basic needs/skill loops: energy, work, social, rest.
- 30-day anonymized social-dynamics dataset.

**Milestone:** Public video of 5 AI agents + 1 human pioneer co-existing for 24 simulated hours.

### Month 2 — Governance & Characters
**Theme:** "Vote."

The MEOK Council goes live. Pioneers elect human seats and vote alongside 47 CSOAI agents on scripted crises.

**Deliverables:**
- Hybrid AI-human council UI.
- First town election.
- Character progression + production chains.
- Governance simulation dataset + first draft paper.

**Milestone:** First council election video + 100 active pioneers.

### Month 3 — Research, Space & Launch
**Theme:** "Publish."

The town economy feeds into the ORBIT layer. Pioneers trade resources, test agent-driven markets, and co-author the first white paper.

**Deliverables:**
- Data collection pipeline fully operational.
- First arXiv pre-print (social dynamics or governance).
- ORBIT space-layer prototype.
- Public launch event.

**Milestone:** 1 published paper + 1,000 signups + public playable demo.

---

## 5. Data & Research Consent

All pioneer data is:
- **Anonymized** before analysis.
- **Opt-in** via explicit consent checkbox.
- **Attested** on-chain via SIGIL for auditability.
- **Used** only for aggregated research and governance papers.

Pioneers can withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawal removes their data from future analyses but cannot retroactively remove already published anonymized aggregates.

---

## 6. Risk Mitigation

| Risk | Mitigation |
|------|------------|
| Low engagement | Daily micro-tasks, character progression, and council votes create habit loops. |
| Agent misalignment | Human-in-the-loop feedback + CSOAI governance + BFT council veto. |
| Data-privacy concerns | Strict anonymization, opt-in consent, SIGIL attestations. |
| Technical scaling issues | Browser-first; backend scales horizontally via existing MEOK OS substrate. |
| Research quality | Academic advisor board, pre-registration of hypotheses, arXiv pre-prints. |
