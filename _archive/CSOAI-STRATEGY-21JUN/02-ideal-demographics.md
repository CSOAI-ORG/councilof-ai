## Primary personas

### 1. Regulatory Mara — CCO / General Counsel / Head of Regulatory Affairs

- Pain points: EU AI Act deadlines, scattered audit evidence, board pressure, personal liability fear
- Budget: £50K–£500K/year
- Needs: Article 50 kit, FRIA support, signed attestations, public verify URLs, 13-framework crosswalk
- Hook: *"Produce a 49-page EU AI Act audit pack with cryptographically verifiable sections in hours, not months."*

### 2. Security Victor — CISO / Head of AI Security

- Pain points: shadow AI, agents accessing sensitive data, no runtime enforcement, vendor claims without proof
- Budget: 10–25% of security budget
- Needs: BFT council, PDCA runtime policy, Watchdog monitoring, DID-based agent identity
- Hook: *"Runtime policy enforcement for every agent call — under 0.1ms latency."*

### 3. Engineering Priya — CTO / VP Engineering / AI Platform Lead

- Pain points: compliance slows shipping, custom governance plumbing, vendor lock-in, integration pain
- Budget: £5K–£100K
- Needs: MCP packs, SDK, open standards, protocol bridges, GitHub repos
- Hook: *"Drop governance into your agent stack with one `pip install`."*

### 4. Sovereign Sarah — CRO / Head of AI Strategy

- Pain points: Big Tech dependence, regulatory fragmentation, reputational risk, need independent certification
- Budget: £100K–£1M+
- Needs: Watchdog Certification, 52-Article Charter, BFT council, NATO-friendly standards
- Hook: *"Independent certification authority for the agentic economy."*
