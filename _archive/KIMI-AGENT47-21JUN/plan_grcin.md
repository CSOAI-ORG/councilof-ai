# Plan: CSOAI Global Regulatory Compliance Intelligence Network (GRCIN)

## Objective
Design the complete architecture and business model for a global AI-powered regulatory compliance monitoring system that:
1. Maintains a comprehensive database of companies × regulations × frameworks × countries
2. Continuously crawls for regulatory updates (daily)
3. Automatically assesses compliance status of any company against any regulation
4. Proactively alerts non-compliant companies before deadlines
5. Provides compliance intelligence to regulators (EU, BaFin, etc.)
6. Creates the world's most advanced governance dataset
