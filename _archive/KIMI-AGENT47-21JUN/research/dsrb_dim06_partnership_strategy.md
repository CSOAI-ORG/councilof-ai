## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

CSOAI has a **time-limited, globally scalable opportunity** to partner with organizations that need a ready-to-deploy digital resilience solution for DORA compliance and beyond. DORA became mandatory on January 17, 2025, affecting **22,000+ financial entities** across the EU and creating compliance costs of EUR 5-15 million per institution [^850^][^853^]. The market urgency is acute: ~100-120 banks across the EU must conduct Threat-Led Penetration Testing (TLPT) every 3 years, yet fewer than 30-40 qualified red team firms exist in the EU — creating 12-18 month wait times [^850^].

**This brief identifies 10 partner categories, 45+ specific partner names, and a prioritized 3-tier approach strategy** that CSOAI can execute immediately.
