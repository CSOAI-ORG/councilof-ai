# Plan V3: Digital Resilience Bank (DSRB) — CSOAI Positioning & Partnership Strategy

## Objective
Deep research into Digital Operational Resilience frameworks (DORA, DSRB, CBEST, TIBER-EU, etc.) used by top banks and their consulting partners. Identify: (1) exactly what boxes CSOAI needs to tick, (2) whether DSRB has taken partners, (3) global partners CSOAI could work with, (4) how CSOAI's existing compliance stack maps to bank resilience requirements.

## Research Dimensions
1. **DORA / Digital Operational Resilience Act** — EU regulation, requirements, timeline, who it affects
2. **DSRB (Digital Resilience Services Banks)** — What it is, which banks use it, partners
3. **Big 4 Resilience Frameworks** — Deloitte, PwC, KPMG, EY — their digital resilience offerings
4. **Global Regulatory Landscape** — CBEST (UK), TIBER-EU, NIST CSF, FFIEC (US), APRA (Australia)
5. **Existing Partners & Providers** — Who's partnered with DSRB/similar globally, competitive landscape
6. **CSOAI Gap Analysis** — What CSOAI has vs what banks require, mapping to existing 13-framework stack
7. **Partnership Entry Strategy** — How to position, who to partner with, what to build

## Deliverable: Comprehensive positioning document (.docx)
