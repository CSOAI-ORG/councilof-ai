## 1. What Banks Require — The DORA Compliance Landscape (~2500 words, 2 tables)
### 1.1 DORA Five Pillars
#### 1.1.1 Pillar 1 — ICT Risk Management: Governance framework, risk assessment, asset classification, protection measures, business continuity (Article 6-13)
#### 1.1.2 Pillar 2 — ICT-related Incident Management: Classification, reporting to NCAs within 4 hours for major incidents, root cause analysis (Article 14-19)
#### 1.1.3 Pillar 3 — Digital Operational Resilience Testing: Threat-led penetration testing every 3 years for significant entities (Article 24-27)
#### 1.1.4 Pillar 4 — ICT Third-Party Risk Management: Register of Information, contract oversight, exit strategies, sub-contracting chains (Article 28-33)
#### 1.1.5 Pillar 5 — Information Sharing: Trusted communities for cyber threat intelligence sharing (Article 45)
### 1.2 The 19 Designated Critical ICT Third-Party Providers (CTPPs)
#### 1.2.1 Full CTPP list: AWS, Microsoft, Google Cloud, IBM, Oracle, SAP, Accenture, Bloomberg, Equinix, Swift, Broadridge, FIS, Finastra, Cognizant, Infosys, TCS, Wipro, Diebold Nixdorf, NTT Data — designated November 2025
#### 1.2.2 What CTPP designation means: direct oversight by Lead Overseer, mandatory reporting, potential penalties up to 1% of average daily worldwide turnover
### 1.3 Penalties & Enforcement
#### 1.3.1 Financial entities: up to 2% of total annual worldwide turnover; board members: up to EUR 5 million personal liability; CTPPs: up to 1% of average daily turnover (~EUR 800K/day for major cloud providers)
