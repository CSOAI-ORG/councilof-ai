# CSOAI Free Global Data Sources Research

## Context
Nick needs free/open data sources globally to train CSOAI's systems — regulatory data, financial data, company data, government data, etc. Must be actionable and concise (user burned out on docs).

## Route: B (Focused) — specific bounded question with clear categories

## Phase 1: Parallel Deep Dive (8 agents)

1. **Regulatory & Legal Data** — EU AI Act, DORA, GDPR, NIST, global regulations, case law
2. **Financial & Banking Data** — Open banking APIs, central bank data, stock markets, SEC
3. **Company & Business Data** — OpenCorporates, LEI, UBO registries, company filings
4. **Government & Open Data Portals** — data.gov, data.europa, UN data, World Bank
5. **Geographic & Demographic Data** — OpenStreetMap, census data, satellite imagery
6. **Cybersecurity & Threat Intel** — CVE, CISA, MISP, open threat feeds
7. **Standards & Frameworks** — ISO, IEC, ITU, open standards databases
8. **Economic & Trade Data** — WTO, UN Comtrade, IMF, customs data

## Phase 2: Synthesis
- One master data catalog with URLs, licenses, formats, use cases
- Rank by relevance to CSOAI's 12 industry hives
- Include API access info where available
