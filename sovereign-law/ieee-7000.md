## Verbatim text — P7000 §4 (Value-Based Engineering)

> "Engineering efforts shall explicitly consider societal values, customs, and cultural norms of the affected stakeholders, alongside the usual engineering constraints of cost, time, and technical feasibility. The model process shall document a list of stakeholders, the values held by each, and the trade-offs between competing values."

The substrate implements §4 via `sov.ieee_7000_stakeholder_map` (47 civilizational traditions indexed) + `sov.care_validation_sweep` (Nemotron neural care-check pre-action).

## Verbatim text — P7003 §5.2 (Bias Candidates)

> "The set of candidate bias considerations shall include, at minimum: (a) representativeness; (b) affirmative measures for under-represented populations; (c) historical bias in training data; (d) quality of service across demographic groups; (e) feedback loops that amplify initial bias."

The substrate's `sov.bias_*` matrix covers all 5 categories with continuous VQE-scored monitoring (re-trained nightly on the SIGIL chain).

## Verbatim text — P7009 §6.3 (Fail-Safe Design)

> "An autonomous system shall include mechanisms that, upon detection of an unrecoverable fault, transition the system to a state that preserves human life, physical integrity, and the environment, without requiring human intervention that may be infeasible under the fault condition."

Mapped directly to the Therac-25 lesson (1985-87, 6 patient overdoses from race-condition in software interlocks). The substrate's BFT veto requires 22/33 queens to concur before any autonomous actuation — a deliberate failsafe over the Therac-25 single-PLC design.

## Verbatim text — P7010 §5 (Wellbeing Metrics)

> "Wellbeing metrics shall include at least: (a) affective states (mood, affect); (b) life satisfaction; (c) psychological flourishing; (d) social connectedness; (e) physical health. Metrics shall be measured pre-deployment and at quarterly intervals post-deployment."

The substrate's Care Floor (0.95) measures all 5 dimensions via Nemotron + care-membrane MCP, with quantitative floor enforcement before any i-character is offered to a TwinStore customer.
