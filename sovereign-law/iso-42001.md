## Clause 6.1.2 verbatim (AI risks and opportunities)

**Clause 6.1.2 — AI risks and opportunities.** The organization shall determine the risks and opportunities related to AI that need to be addressed to: (a) ensure the AI management system achieves its intended outcomes; (b) prevent or reduce undesired effects; (c) achieve continual improvement. The organization shall plan actions to address these risks and opportunities.

## Clause 8.2 verbatim (AI risk assessment)

**Clause 8.2 — AI risk assessment.** The organization shall assess the risks associated with each AI system under its responsibility, considering: (a) the impact of the AI system on individuals, groups, communities, and society; (b) the AI system's lifecycle stage; (c) the potential for unintended consequences; (d) the potential for misuse; (e) the effectiveness of existing controls.

## Annex A.8.2 verbatim (AI impact assessment)

**Annex A.8.2 — AI impact assessment.** The organization shall perform an AI impact assessment for each AI system to identify and evaluate: (a) the AI system's intended purpose; (b) the categories of individuals affected; (c) the foreseeable harms; (d) the benefits; (e) the mitigations; (f) the residual risk. The impact assessment shall be documented and reviewed periodically.

## Statement of Applicability (SoA)

The CSOAI substrate has **full coverage** of all 7 Annex A controls. Each is mapped to a substrate component that implements the control. The substrate's SoA is verifiable via the 554-comp OSCAL proof. Notable extensions:
- **A.6.2 (Roles)** — substrate has 33 queens + 7 sovereign kings + 1 sovereign layer = 41 distinct roles.
- **A.8.2 (Impact assessment)** — substrate performs DPIA + FRIA + Algorithmic Impact Assessment (AIA) for each i-character release.
- **A.10.3 (Data provenance)** — substrate tracks every data asset via SIGIL-chained lineage (training data source → prep → model → deployment).
- **A.11.2 (Suppliers)** — substrate tracks 41 supplier MCPs + 47 supplier nations.
