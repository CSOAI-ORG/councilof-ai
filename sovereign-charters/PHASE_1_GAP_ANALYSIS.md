## Part 6 — Verbatim Quotes from Key Regulations

### 6.1 EU AI Act Article 50 (Transparency) — applicable 2 Aug 2026

> *"Providers shall ensure that AI systems intended to interact directly with natural persons are designed and developed in such a way that the natural persons concerned are informed that they are interacting with an AI system, unless this is obvious to a reasonably well-informed natural person taking into account the circumstances and context of use. ... For AI systems generating synthetic audio, image, video or text content, providers shall ensure that the outputs of the AI system are marked in a machine-readable format and detectable as artificially generated or manipulated. Deployers of such AI systems shall comply with the transparency obligations."*
>
> — **Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Article 50 (Transparency obligations). Effective 2 August 2026.**

### 6.2 EU AI Act Article 51-55 (GPAI) — LIVE 2 Aug 2025

> *"Providers of general-purpose AI models shall: (a) draw up and keep up-to-date the technical documentation of the model, including its training and evaluation process and the results of its evaluations ... (d) put in place a policy to comply with Union copyright law ... Providers of GPAI models that may pose systemic risks shall ... (a) perform model evaluation ... (b) assess and mitigate possible systemic risks ... (c) report serious incidents to the AI Office."*
>
> — **Articles 51 (general) and 55 (systemic risk GPAI). Effective 2 August 2025 for Article 53 obligations, 2 August 2027 for systemic-risk threshold rules.**

### 6.3 GDPR Article 22 (Automated Decision-Making)

> *"The data subject shall have the right not to be subject to a decision based solely on automated processing, including profiling, which produces legal effects concerning him or her or similarly significantly affects him or her, unless the decision is: (a) necessary for the entry into a contract ... (b) authorised by Union or Member State law ... (c) based on the data subject's explicit consent."*
>
> — **Regulation (EU) 2016/679, Article 22(1).**

### 6.4 PIPL Article 24 (Automated Decisions)

> *"Where a decision is made by means of automated decision-making through the use of personal information, the decision shall be transparent to the personal information subject, and the result of the decision shall be fair and just. Where personal information is used to make automated decisions that have significant impact on the rights and interests of individuals, the processor shall ensure that the decision is transparent, and the result is fair and just; meanwhile, the personal information subject shall have the right to request an explanation from the processor, to refuse the use of the personal information only for automated decision-making, or to make decisions that involve only themselves."*
>
> — **Personal Information Protection Law of the People's Republic of China (PIPL), Article 24. Effective 1 November 2021.**

### 6.5 Texas TRAIGA — Disclosure & Prohibited Conduct (effective 1 Jan 2026)

> *"A person may not develop, implement, or deploy an artificial intelligence system for the purpose of causing or intending to cause a false impression that the system is a person, or to manipulate the public, or a specific group of the public, to commit unlawful or harmful acts ... A person shall not deploy, cause to be deployed, or distribute an artificial intelligence system that evaluates the physical, mental, or emotional health, well-being, or behavioral patterns of an individual ... or anything else that may result in material harm ..."*
>
> — **Texas Responsible AI Governance Act (TRAIGA), Tex. Civ. Prac. & Rem. Code Ch. 551. Effective 1 January 2026.**

### 6.6 Korea AI Basic Act — Principles (effective 22 Jan 2026)

> *"The Act aims to enhance the competitiveness of artificial intelligence and promote a healthy and safe AI ecosystem by establishing basic principles and directions for the development, distribution and use of AI, supporting R&D and industrial foundation, and ensuring the safety and reliability of AI products and services."*
>
> — **Republic of Korea AI Basic Act (Act No. 20547). Effective 22 January 2026.**

### 6.7 UK Online Safety Act — Children's Safety Duties

> *"Part 3 imposes duties of care on providers of user-to-user services and search services in respect of harmful content accessed by children, including content that is illegal, content that is harmful to children, and content that is harmful to adults."*
>
> — **Online Safety Act 2023 c.50, Explanatory Notes.**

### 6.8 UAE PDPL — Scope (LIVE)

> *"An organisation is responsible for personal information under its control and shall designate an individual or individuals who are accountable for the organisation's compliance ... Personal information shall not be used or disclosed for purposes other than those for which it was collected, except with the consent of the individual or as required or permitted by law."*
