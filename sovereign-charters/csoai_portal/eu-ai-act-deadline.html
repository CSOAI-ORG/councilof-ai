What Article 50 requires

EU AI Act Article 50 (Transparency obligations for AI systems):

  • AI nature disclosure — users must be informed they're interacting with AI
  • Accessible disclosure — disclosure mechanism must be reachable
  • Appropriate language — language must match user context
  • Consistent surface — disclosure across all user touchpoints
  • No deception — disclosure must not be misleading or hidden
  • Deepfake labelling — generated content must be labelled
  • Emotion recognition disclosure — special notice for emotion/biometric systems
  • Biometric categorisation disclosure — special notice for biometric systems

Non-compliance fines: up to €15M or 3% of total worldwide annual turnover, whichever is higher.
