## 2. CHARTER ARTICLE 0 COMPLIANCE — NEGATIVE LIST

What CSOAI Ltd will **never** do, irrespective of price pressure:

| ❌ Forbidden | Why |
|---|---|
| Take **equity** in any certified institution | Conflicts independence — can't certify what you own |
| Take a **board seat** at any certified institution | Conflicts independence — can't sit on the board you audit |
| Accept **revenue-share** with any regulator | Capture risk — the regulator must remain independent of the audited |
| Charge **success fees** on certification outcomes | Perverse incentive — we'd be paid to certify more, not to certify accurately |
| Charge **per-incident** on Watchdog findings | Same perverse incentive — would monetise bad news |
| **Discriminate** on jurisdiction, language, or industry | Article 0 § 0.1 (Universal Access) |
| **Withhold** cross-walk data from any ratified partner | Article 0 § 0.4 (Cross-Walk Completeness) |
| **Sell** SIGIL chain data to foreign intelligence or surveillance vendors | Sovereign substrate, sovereign by definition |

The **single permitted commercial model** is ISO fee-for-service: a fixed price for a defined service, paid by the consumer of the service, with no contingent or equity component. This is the **CMKC model** (Certification Mark + Knowledge Carrier) translated from the construction industry to AI.

### 4.2 x402 invoice lifecycle

1. Customer calls service (e.g. `sov_x402_invoice(service="article50_passport", tier="pro", quantity=1)`)
2. CSOAI mints an x402 invoice with Ed25519 signature + amount + SIGIL digest
3. Customer pays via Stripe, crypto, wallet, or x402 bus
4. Service executes; SIGIL receipt emitted
5. Invoice paid state anchored to Bitcoin via OTS
6. Refund window: 14 days for any invoice > £100 (Pro/Business), 30 days for Enterprise

### 4.3 Why x402 matters for sovereignty

x402 is **currency-agnostic** (GBP, USD, EUR, ETH, BTC, USDC, Stripe). It is **identity-aware** (each invoice binds to a W3C DID + Ed25519 public key). It is **audit-first** (every line lands on the SIGIL chain). This means a regulator in Singapore can pay for a UK-issued audit in SGD, the invoice lives forever on the chain, and the BFT council can verify the audit was executed by the genuine CSOAI substrate — not a counterfeit.
