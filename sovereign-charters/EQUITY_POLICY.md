## 🚫 Sovereign Equity Policy

Charter Article 0 explicitly binds sovereign operators **against** equity, board seats, revenue-sharing with regulators, and success fees from enforcement. This document provides **detailed interpretation**.

### ✅ Allowed (Charter-Compliant)
1. **Per-Outcome Invoicing (x402)** — paid per delivered service. Article 50 Passport ($0-5), EU AI Act Audit ($25), RAG calls ($0.005), ZAMBA Ask ($0.01).
2. **ISO Fee-for-Service** — fixed fees for standardised services (e.g., £49 Pro tier / £499 Business tier / £4,999 Enterprise / £9,999 Platinum).
3. **Revenue-Share Among Sovereign Operators** — Sovereign operator-to-operator revenue shares (e.g., sovereign cloud shares revenue with charter hosting partner).
4. **Dividends to Sovereign Partner Foundations** — non-profit foundations receiving distributions from sovereign partner entities.
5. **Employee Compensation** — salaries, benefits, equity compensation to sovereign employees (but NOT in certified institutions).
6. **Sub-Licensing** — non-exclusive re-licensing of free services to partner organisations.

### ❌ Prohibited (Charter Violations)
1. **Equity in Certified Institutions** — direct equity ownership in any institution we certify, monitor, or regulate.
2. **Board Seats at Certified Institutions** — no seats on boards of certified institutions.
3. **Revenue-Share with Regulators** — no revenue-share with regulatory bodies (EU AI Office, ICO, NIST, etc.).
4. **Success Fees from Enforcement** — no fees tied to enforcement outcomes (e.g., penalty fines, audit findings).
5. **Equity from Sovereign Cloud Operators** — sovereign operators cannot take equity in certified customers.
6. **Equity Tied to Watchdog S4/S5 Outcomes** — no equity tied to sovereign certification/binding decisions.
7. **Royalty on Sovereign Knowledge** — no royalties on sovereign charters, frameworks, or research.
8. **Patent Lock-In on Sovereign Methods** — Article 0 prohibits sovereignty patents.
