## ARTICLE II — THE ECONOMIC PHILOSOPHY

### II.A — Competence Over Credentials

The traditional economy allocates opportunity based on credentials — degrees, certifications, professional memberships — that cost money to acquire. This is a regressive tax on competence. Those who can afford credentials get opportunities. Those who can't, don't.

The Sovereign UBI model inverts this: competence IS the credential. Complete the training, pass the simulation, earn the Ed25519-signed cert. The cert is proof of competence, not proof of payment. The marketplace doesn't care how much you paid — it cares what you can do.

### II.B — Zero-Cost Entry, Unlimited Ceiling

- **Entry cost**: £0. No tuition. No exam fees. No membership dues. No means testing. No credit checks. No background checks. Just internet access and the will to learn.
- **Ceiling**: Unlimited. A Tier 4 Director can earn £1,200/mo stipend + mentorship income + audit contracts + consulting fees + BFT governance compensation. The ceiling is set by competence and contribution, not by the institution that certified you.

### II.C — The 34-Industry Multiplier

A Practitioner in fishkeeping cross-walks to GDPR compliance through dataprivacyof. A Lead Auditor in construction cross-walks to environmental compliance through muckaway. A Director in AI governance cross-walks to defence AI through defoneos.

The cross-walk engine multiplies earning potential: each certification opens doors in 33 adjacent industries. A Tier 4 Director with cross-walks is qualified across the entire sovereign economy.

### II.D — Anti-Cartel Design

Traditional credential cartels capture value by controlling access. The American Bar Association controls who practices law. The General Medical Council controls who practices medicine. ISACA controls who audits information systems.

The Sovereign Charter breaks every cartel simultaneously: free training + free certification + Ed25519 verification + marketplace access. No single institution can gatekeep. No credential can be paywalled. No cartel can capture the certification authority because the authority is distributed across 33 BFT council members.
