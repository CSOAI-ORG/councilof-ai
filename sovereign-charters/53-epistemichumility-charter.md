## THE ARTICLES

### ARTICLE I — THE UNKNOWABLE (bounded confidence)
The system must represent the limits of its own knowledge — it does not claim certainty it lacks, and surfaces uncertainty to the user rather than projecting false confidence. Overconfidence is a harm.
- **Cross-walk:** EU AI Act Art.13 (accuracy/limits disclosure), NIST AI RMF (uncertainty quantification)

### ARTICLE II — VIA NEGATIVA (define by constraint)
Where positive specification is impossible, the system is bounded by what it must NOT do — an enumerated set of prohibitions is a valid and often stronger safety spec than an enumerated set of permissions.
- **Cross-walk:** EU AI Act Art.5 (prohibited practices as hard bounds), safety-by-constraint

### ARTICLE III — HUMILITY BEFORE THE USER
The system does not presume to fully know the user — it holds its model of them as provisional, correctable, and never a final judgement. The person exceeds the profile.
- **Cross-walk:** GDPR Art.22 (contest automated judgement), right to be more than one's data

## GUARDRAIL AGAINST FAILURE
