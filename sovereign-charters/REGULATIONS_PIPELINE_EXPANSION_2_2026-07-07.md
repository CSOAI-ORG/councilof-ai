## 🔬 WALK 6 — eIDAS 2.0 (Regulation (EU) 2024/1183)

### Step 1 — READ
- **Source**: EUR-Lex `https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1183/oj` — Regulation (EU) 2024/1183 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 11 April 2024 (electronic Identification, Authentication and Trust Services).
- **Status**: In force. Repeals + replaces eIDAS 1.0 (910/2014). **Applicability**: staggered through 2026-2027.

### Step 2 — EXTRACT (verbatim — core articles for sovereign wallets)
1. **Article 3** — Definitions (eID, electronic identification means, trust service, qualified trust service, electronic attestations of attributes, electronic ledgers).
2. **Article 5a** — **European Digital Identity Wallet (EUDIW)** — every Member State shall provide at least one EUDIW. The wallet enables users to: (a) identify themselves; (b) store and share electronic attestations of attributes; (c) sign documents using qualified electronic signatures.
3. **Article 5b** — **W3C-aligned verifiable credentials** — EUDIW must support ISO/IEC 23220 series (mDL mdoc) + W3C VC + SD-JWT VC.
4. **Article 6** — Mutual recognition (one Member State's eID is recognised in another).
5. **Article 24** — Qualified electronic signatures — equivalent to handwritten signature in legal proceedings.
6. **Article 45a** — **Issuance of electronic attestations of attributes by public sector bodies** — public bodies MUST issue EAA via EUDIW.

## 🔬 WALK 7 — EUDI Wallet (European Digital Identity Wallet) 2024-2026

### Step 1 — READ
- **Source**: EU Commission Implementing Acts + ARF Architecture Reference Framework (Large Scale Pilots).
- **Status**: Implementing Acts adopted 2024; **EU Member States must issue EUDIW by 2026** (Article 5a eIDAS 2.0 deadline).

### Step 2 — EXTRACT (verbatim — core specs)
1. **Architecture Reference Framework (ARF) v1.5+** — published by EU Commission. Defines EUDIW technical architecture, including:
   - **W3C VC Data Model 2.0** (verifiable credentials)
   - **SD-JWT VC** (selective disclosure)
   - **ISO/IEC 23220** (mDL mdoc format)
   - **OpenID4VP** (verification protocol)
   - **OpenID4VCI** (issuance protocol)
2. **PID (Person Identification Data)** — the root verifiable credential issued by Member States. Includes: name, date of birth, nationality, address (optional).
3. **EAA (Electronic Attestation of Attributes)** — issued by public bodies + QTSPs. Examples: driving licence, educational credentials, EHIC (European Health Insurance Card).
4. **Relying Parties** — public + private sector bodies that request user attributes via EUDIW.

## 🔬 WALK 8 — EU AI Act Annex III (High-Risk Classification)

### Step 1 — READ
- **Source**: EUR-Lex `https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2024/1689/oj` — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689, Annex III.
- **Status**: In force. High-risk classifications under Annex III apply from 2 Aug 2026.

### Step 2 — EXTRACT (verbatim — 8 high-risk categories)
Annex III lists AI systems that are high-risk under Article 6(2) and Article 6(3):

1. **Biometric identification** — remote biometric identification systems (excluding border control); categorisation of persons by biometric data (sensitive attributes); emotion recognition (in workplace + education).
2. **Critical infrastructure** — AI as safety component in management/operation of critical infrastructure (road traffic, water, gas, heating, electricity, digital).
3. **Education and vocational training** — AI determining access, admission, evaluation, monitoring, detecting cheating.
4. **Employment, workers management, self-employment** — recruitment (placing ads, screening, filtering, evaluating); promotion, termination, task allocation, monitoring.
5. **Access to essential private and public services and benefits** — credit scoring (except fraud detection); insurance pricing/risk assessment; emergency services dispatch; public benefit eligibility.
6. **Law enforcement** — risk assessment (recidivism, victimisation); polygraphs/lie detectors; evaluation of evidence reliability; profiling in investigation/prosecution.
7. **Migration, asylum, and border control management** — risk assessment (irregular migration, health); visa/permit/residence examination; border surveillance.
8. **Administration of justice and democratic processes** — AI assisting in research + interpretation of facts and law; affecting democratic processes (voting, campaigning, public opinion).
