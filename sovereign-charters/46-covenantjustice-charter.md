## PATTERN EXTRACTED (doctrine stripped)
The Abrahamic traditions encode three durable *governance* patterns, independent of any belief:
1. **Covenant** — a binding, mutual, non-unilateral duty between parties, where the stronger party is bound *harder*, not softer.
2. **Due process** — no judgement without hearing; the accused is heard, the witness tested, the record kept.
3. **Jubilee / debt-release** — periodic reset so that error and debt do not compound into permanent servitude.

## THE THREE ARTICLES

### ARTICLE I — COVENANT (mutual, asymmetric-toward-the-weaker duty)
The system's duty to the user is a covenant, not a terms-of-service. The more power the system holds over a user (data, dependency, life-impact), the *stronger* its duty of care — never weaker. Unilateral change to the user's detriment breaches the covenant.
- **Cross-walk:** EU AI Act Art.14 (human oversight), ISO/IEC 42001 (accountability), consumer-protection asymmetry doctrine.

### ARTICLE II — DUE PROCESS (no adverse action without a heard, tested, recorded process)
No AI-driven adverse decision against a user (denial, flag, restriction, termination) without: a stated reason, a route to contest, a tested basis, and an auditable record. The accused is heard before the judgement stands.
- **Cross-walk:** EU AI Act Art.22 / GDPR Art.22 (automated-decision rights), Art.13-15 (transparency), UNESCO AI Ethics (fairness).

### ARTICLE III — JUBILEE (non-compounding of error; the reset)
User error, debt, and adverse history must not compound into permanent exclusion. The system commits to defined reset/expungement paths so a past mistake does not become a life sentence — the governance form of debt-release.
- **Cross-walk:** GDPR Art.17 (erasure), right-to-be-forgotten, spent-convictions/rehabilitation-of-offenders principle.

## GUARDRAIL AGAINST FAILURE
Covenant must not become paternalism (see GuardianOf Art.IV); due process must not become bureaucratic denial-of-service; jubilee must not erase records needed for others' safety (balanced against audit/incident duties). No theological authority is claimed — these are governance patterns only.
