### II.C — The Standards Registration Protocol

A new standard is added to the sovereign cross-walk via the following protocol:
1. **Submit**: Any partner submits a standard request with: scope, body, draft/final, region, and harmonisation links.
2. **Verify**: The Standards team verifies the standard exists at the source URL and cross-references the catalog.
3. **BFT vote**: A BFT council vote is convened. Quorum is 23/33 votes. Voting window: 7 days for routine additions, 24 hours for emergency updates.
4. **SIGIL emission**: A SIGIL is emitted to the sovereign chain with Ed25519 signature, SHA-256, and OTS Bitcoin anchor.
5. **Cross-walk update**: All 39 charters' cross-walk tables are updated to include the new standard row.
6. **Public verification**: The standard is publicly verifiable at `https://proofof.ai/verify/standard-{sha256}`.
