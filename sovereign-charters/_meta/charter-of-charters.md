# Charter of Charters

*Ratified 2026-09-02 · SIGIL `ed25519:sigil:12…`*

## Purpose

This is the meta-charter that governs all 60 charters of the MEOK OS substrate. It is the
authoritative reference for charter structure, amendment process, and ratification.

## Structure

Every charter has:
- Frontmatter (id, title, sigil, ratified_at, BFT 9/13)
- Preamble (the why)
- 7-12 articles (the what)
- Sovereign alignment table per article
- BFT 9/13 ratification record
- Defensive FAQ

## Amendment Process

1. Proposal (any node) — 1 day
2. Second (3 nodes) — 1 day
3. Deliberation (Council) — 3 days
4. Public comment — 7 days
5. Vote (9/13 BFT) — 1 day
6. Ratification (Maternal concurrence) — 1 day
7. Witness (King OCEAN) — 1 day
8. Archive (public root) — 1 day

Total: 16 days

## Sunset

A charter is sunset (deprecated) if:
- It is superseded by a new charter
- It is found to be harmful
- It is no longer relevant
- 9/13 BFT votes for sunset

Sunset requires 9/13 BFT + 7-day public comment + SIGIL-signed deprecation.
