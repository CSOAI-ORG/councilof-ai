# Defensive FAQ

*Ratified 2026-09-02 · SIGIL `ed25519:sigil:12…`*

## What is this sprint?

The Sovereign Charter Sprint is the 4-hour build of 60 charters, 12 frameworks, a global law
index, a compliance crosswalk, a training corpus, and a 50-test suite. It establishes the
MEOK OS governance layer.

## Why 60 charters?

60 is a round number that maps cleanly to the 12 frameworks (5 charters per framework) and
the 33 hives (2 charters per hive, with the 13 queens). The choice is engineering-driven.

## Why 12 frameworks?

The 12 frameworks are the global gold standards: EU AI Act, GDPR, DORA, NIS2, CRA, NIST AI
RMF, ISO 42001, ISO 27001, IEEE 7000, SOC 2, HIPAA, PCI DSS. They cover the legal, security,
and ethics substrate of the sovereign.

## What is the BFT 9/13?

9 of 13 sovereign nodes must sign for ratification. The 13 nodes are the 13 sovereign
roles. The high quorum is intentionally costly to prevent capture.

## What is SIGIL?

SIGIL is the substrate's cryptographic identity: Ed25519 signatures, content-addressed
state, append-only logs, public root. SIGIL is to MEOK OS what TLS is to the web.

## What is the Maternal Covenant?

The Maternal Covenant is the care floor. It precedes every other charter. It is enforced
by the Maternal node (n02) with a hard veto.

## What is the public root?

The public root is the substrate's Merkle-verified transparency log. Every SIGIL-signed
event is anchored in the root. The root is updated every 60 seconds.

## What is the 52 articles?

There are 52 numbered articles across all 60 charters. Article 0 is the Maternal Covenant.
Articles 1-10 are governance. 11-20 are cryptographic. 21-30 are operational. 31-40 are
sectoral. 41-52 are ratification, defense, and dissolution.

## How is the training corpus used?

The corpus is the substrate's training data. It is the 1.39 TB Big Braim plus the 218 MCP
signatures plus the 33 hive configs plus the 60 charters plus the 12 frameworks. The
substrate trains on it continuously.

## Who audits the substrate?

The Public Watchdog (n12), the SigInt node (n08), and King OCEAN (n13) audit the
substrate. The audits are quarterly and public.

## What happens if a charter is wrong?

A wrong charter is amended via 9/13 BFT + 7-day public comment. A harmful charter is
deprecated via SIGIL-signed deprecation. A catastrophic charter is dissolved via 9/13 +
1000+ public petition + 90-day cooling.

## Is this legally binding?

The charters are binding on MEOK OS deployments. Whether they are binding on sovereign
states is a matter of jurisdictional law, not sovereign claim.
