## 7 reasons we are open source

### 1. **Compliance that everyone can audit**
Compliance is a public good. It must be auditable by anyone. Closed-source compliance = "trust me bro" → regulatory capture → institutional corruption. Open-source compliance = math → verifiable → capture-proof.

### 2. **No vendor lock-in**
A sovereign institution cannot be sovereign if it depends on a vendor that can change pricing, terms, or pivot to a competitor. Open source means you can fork, audit, self-host, and run forever — no one can pull the rug out.

### 3. **The barrier to entry MUST be zero**
Charter Article 0 binding mandates that the barrier to entry is zero. Closed-source = paywall. Open source = barrier-free. AI is for humanity, not for the few who can afford licences.

### 4. **Security through transparency**
The strongest encryption in the world has been broken by researchers. The most secure vaults in the world have been cracked. Open source code is reviewed by thousands of eyes; closed source is reviewed by a few. Security through obscurity is anti-security.

### 5. **Cross-walks are public good**
The 236 universal compliance frameworks + 11,316 cross-walks are public domain. They must be cross-walked by everyone, not patented by anyone. Patents on compliance frameworks = regulatory capture by patent holders.

### 6. **SIGIL chain must be public**
The Ed25519 + OTS Bitcoin anchoring chain must be public for trust. Private SIGIL chain = "trust me bro." Public chain = verifiable. Anyone can verify any charter at proofof.ai.

### 7. **BFT governance must be transparent**
The 33-agent BFT council must be transparent. Closed-source governance = capture-proof mathematically impossible. Open-source governance = every vote is auditable, every proposal is public, every ratification is SIGIL-signed.
