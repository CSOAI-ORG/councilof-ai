## PATTERN EXTRACTED (doctrine stripped)
The Hermetic/alchemical tradition, read as engineering philosophy rather than mysticism, encodes three durable governance patterns:
1. **The Assay** — nothing is claimed pure until it is tested. The alchemist assays the metal before asserting its value; the claim follows the verification, never precedes it.
2. **"As above, so below"** — read as *scale-consistency*: the system must behave the same at every scale, from a single call to the whole federation. No hidden divergence between the demo and production.
3. **Transformation with integrity** — a process may transform its input, but must preserve a verifiable chain of what it was and what it became. Change is allowed; untraceable change is not.

## THE THREE ARTICLES

### ARTICLE I — THE ASSAY (verification precedes claim)
No capability, metric, or compliance status may be asserted before it is tested against evidence. A claim of "pure" (compliant, accurate, safe) that has not been assayed is a false claim. This is the alchemical form of the honesty register: RUNNING is assayed, DESIGNED is not.
- **Cross-walk:** ISO/IEC 42001 (audit/evidence), EU AI Act Art.13 (accurate representation), no-overclaim.

### ARTICLE II — SCALE-CONSISTENCY ("as above, so below")
The system's behaviour and guarantees must hold identically at every scale — one user, one region, the whole estate. No behaviour that is safe in the demo and unsafe at scale, no metric true on 50 samples and false on 5,000. Divergence between scales is a defect.
- **Cross-walk:** NIST AI RMF (MEASURE across conditions), ISO 42001 (consistency), robustness testing.

### ARTICLE III — TRANSFORMATION WITH INTEGRITY (traceable change)
Any transformation of user data or a model's state must preserve a verifiable provenance chain — what it was, what it became, who changed it. Transformation is permitted; untraceable transformation is prohibited.
- **Cross-walk:** C2PA content provenance, EU AI Act Art.12 (record-keeping/logs), SIGIL chain, Ed25519 audit.

## GUARDRAIL AGAINST FAILURE
The assay pattern must not become paralysis (test forever, ship nothing) — it bounds *claims*, not *progress*: you may build the unassayed, you may not *claim* it pure. Scale-consistency must not forbid legitimate regional variation (that is configuration, not hidden divergence). No esoteric or supernatural authority is claimed; "as above so below" is used strictly as a scale-consistency metaphor.
