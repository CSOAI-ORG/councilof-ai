# GSPC one

The single public export of GSPC measurements from Council of AI (CSOAI Ltd, 16939677). The file has one row per subject × axis, and each row carries its lane (signed or UNSIGNED), its comparison group and its evidence URL.

- Rows are never ranked or pooled across groups.
- The lab and measurer census rows are unsigned state grids, with no totals and no ordering.
- The humanoid-lab and interpretability-group classes are not yet measurable or measured. They are shown that way, never as zero.
- This is measurement, not certification.

Leaderboards: https://councilof.ai/leaderboards/
