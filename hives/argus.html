Purpose

Argus is the multi-modal fusion centre — ingesting image, video, audio, text, LiDAR, telemetry, and IoT sensor streams. It normalises heterogeneous data into attestable compliance datasets for regulated industries including transport, energy, and critical national infrastructure.

Data Sources & Size

  • Satellite imagery (Sentinel-2 / Maxar) — 2.3M+ scenes indexed
  • Video surveillance feeds — 500+ TB archived (30-day rolling)
  • Industrial IoT telemetry — 8.5B+ time-series datapoints
  • LiDAR point clouds — 90 TB (urban & infrastructure scans)
  • Speech & audio — 120K hours (multi-lingual, 47 languages)
  • Total: ~18 TB structured index + 600 TB raw store
