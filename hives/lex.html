Purpose

Lex processes, indexes, and attests legal and regulatory text at scale — statutes, regulations, case law, contracts, regulatory guidance, and legislative proceedings. Provides cross-jurisdictional legal entity extraction, clause-level compliance checking, and precedent citation graphs.
