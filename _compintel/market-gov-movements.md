## 5 BIGGEST MARKET SIGNALS

1. **The visibility gap is the universal complaint** — 92% of CISOs lack AI-agent visibility, 95% can't contain a compromised agent, 0% have full org-wide AI visibility, yet ~75% are deploying agents with only 21% governance maturity. Demand is acute and unmet. [V]
2. **Gartner is telling enterprises uniform governance = failure**, predicting 40% will decommission autonomous agents by 2027. The market needs **per-agent, differentiated, verifiable** governance — not one-size-fits-all dashboards. [V]
3. **The UK is literally funding your thesis:** £11M AI Assurance Innovation Fund (opens Spring 2026) + Turing/CETAS defence-assurance research + £80M sovereign procurement (July 2026, incl. cybersecurity/defence challenges). Government is paying to *create* a third-party assurance market. [V]
4. **MCP-security emerged as a real, funded, un-consolidated category in <12 months** (~$40M, MCP's own creator advising, live prompt-injection CVEs). This is a green-field runtime-trust layer. [V]
5. **Budgets are flat (4% growth) while demand explodes, and incumbents are expensive** ($150K OneTrust, $19–30K+ Vanta renewals). The gap between need and affordable, transparent tooling is widening — a classic disruption opening for cheaper/open/signed. [V]

## 3 CONCRETE OPENINGS for a sovereign, signed, open-source-first play (SMB + enterprise)

**Opening A — "Signed agent/MCP audit trail" as the answer to the visibility crisis.**
92% can't see their agents and Gartner warns against uniform governance. Ship an **open-source, offline-verifiable (Ed25519-signed) per-agent action ledger** that any org can self-host — every agent/MCP call signed, replayable, independently verifiable without trusting a vendor cloud. This directly attacks lane 3 (Runlayer's lane) but with a *sovereign, no-lock-in, self-hostable* wedge that a startup with $42M VC can't credibly claim. Anchor on the live MCP prompt-injection CVEs (Invariant) as the "why now."

**Opening B — Apply to the UK £11M AI Assurance Innovation Fund (Spring 2026) with a signed System Card.**
The fund exists to seed *novel third-party assurance mechanisms*; Turing/CETAS already documents the JSP 936 / defence-assurance gap. A **signed, offline-verifiable System Card** that validates "deployment-ready" vendor claims (the exact gap Turing named) is fundable, on-brand, and turns a grant into a government reference logo. Pairs with the £80M procurement (cybersecurity + defence challenges, £5M contracts).

**Opening C — Undercut Vanta/OneTrust with a transparent, open, cheap "Governed-SaaS" starter for SMB, upsell enterprise on sovereignty.**
Budgets are flat and incumbents run $19K–$150K+. Ship a **free/open baseline** mapping to the frameworks enterprises now *require* (EU AI Act high-risk, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001) with signed evidence baked in — land SMB at near-zero, then upsell enterprise/defence on **offline verifiability + no single-vendor trust** (the one thing the funded, cloud-native competitors structurally can't match). Positioning: "the signed, sovereign layer *on top of* your Vanta/Palantir — not a replacement for the capability, the proof of it."
