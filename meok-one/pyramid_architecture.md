### 5. Pyramid in Physics — Capital flow analogy (Claude, MEOK Labs)

Think of SOV3 like the **ROOT PRESSURE** of a giant tree:
1. Water is drawn up by **transpiration** (cooling evaporation)
   = The Cooling Provided by Sovereign Substrate (SOV3 emits sigils = cools)
2. Water rises via **capillary action** through narrow tubes
   = Sovereign caps extract high-fidelity gradients from broad substrate
3. Each leaf **transpires** again = each orb/sigil emits (cooling)
4. Water reaches the **highest leaf** = sovereign output, fully grounded

The tree grows downward first (roots expand), then upward (leaves).
**Pyramid grows DOWN first, then CONVERGES to a point.**

### 6. Pyramid in Water Data Transfer

- The tree maintains itself — no external pump needed
