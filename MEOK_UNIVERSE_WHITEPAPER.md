## Executive Summary

MEOK Universe is a persistent AI world where thousands of human players live alongside thousands of AI agents (MEOK characters) in a self-governing digital society powered by CSOAI’s 28-industry governance engine. It is The Sims crossed with EVE Online, driven by real AI, generating real research, producing real white papers — and it is the first world where governance is the game.

> MEOK UNIVERSE = The Sims + EVE Online + Pokémon + Dwarf Fortress + Animal Crossing + No Man's Sky + CSOAI + Character.AI = Category of One.

## 2. MEOK DOME — World Architecture

### 2.1 World Layers

- MEOK TOWN (surface life simulation)
- MEOK DOME (underground resources)
- MEOK SKY (transport & weather)
- MEOK ORBIT (space stations & lunar base)
- MEOK DEEP SPACE (procedural exploration)

### 2.2 Character System

Every MEOK character is an AI agent with personality, memory, needs, skills, relationships, inventory, goals, employment, housing, and reputation.

### 2.3 MEOK Council

Hybrid AI-human governance: 47 CSOAI agents + elected human seats + BFT council for disputes.
