## What's shipped

### The globe (the world at your feet)
- 11 temples across 4 regions (EU, US, APAC, GLOBAL)
- Each temple is a regulation jurisdiction at the right address
- EU temple: 8 frameworks (EU AI Act, GDPR, DORA, NIS2, CRA, AI Liability, DSA, DMA)
- UK temple: 5 frameworks (UK AI Regulation, UK GDPR, OSA, Defence AI, ASGARD)
- US temple: 7 frameworks (NIST AI RMF 1.0, NIST CSF 2.0, EO 14110, Colorado, Texas, California, NYC LL 144)
- CA, CN, JP, SG, UN, ISO, IEEE temples each with their frameworks
- 37+ regulations catalogued total
- Inner-flow workflows (Art. 9 → 12 → SIGIL → SOC 2 for EU; NIST → Colorado → OSCAL for US)

### The LHS — OS + tools (customizable)
- **OS · Tools**: Chat, Search, AI Act, x402, Cascade, Distill, Sigstore, RAG
- **Load SaaS over Globe**: Charts, Docs, CRM, Mail
- **Inner Flows**: SOV, Council, Defoneos, Arcana
- 16 tool tiles total, all clickable, all sovereign-aware

### The center — Sovereign character
- 🐉 animated breathing character
- Greets you by your IP region: "Welcome, sovereign United Kingdom"
- 5+ onboarding suggestions
- Chat input at the bottom that Sovereign replies to (5 categories: EU AI Act, cascade pricing, my region, i-character, cyber check)

### The RHS — sessions, council, BFT
- **SOV3 status card**: 200 OK, v2.0.0, 34 VMs, 13 council nodes, BFT f=4 q=9
- **12-Queen council pills**: All 12 queens + 2 veto (Care, Watch) marked
- **Sessions/Tasks counters**: 1 active, 12 total, 8 tasks
- **Mindsets**: Sovereign, Care, Strategy, Compliance, Defense
- **BFT status**: Quorum 9/13, block 2e9cd9b4, 496 rounds

### The DORADO bar
- 4 steps: 🌅 West → 🌐 Globe → 🛕 Temple → 🌇 East
- Click to cycle (west → east with heavy ontology methods)
- Anchored to bottom of screen

### The temple overlay (the deep-inside view)
- Click any temple to open a deep overlay
- Shows all frameworks + regulations with metadata
- Shows the inner workflow (Art. 9 → 12 → SIGIL for EU; NIST → Colorado → OSCAL for US)
- Color-coded by workflow kind (actuator/decision/evidence)
- Close with × button or Escape key

### PWA install
- "Install MEOK OS as an app" prompt
- Works on iOS Safari, Android Chrome, Windows, Mac
- i-character (digital twin) coming in v2.1
