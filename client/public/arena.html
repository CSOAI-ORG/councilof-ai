What the table does not say. The macro-F1 column is the best score any measured run reached and it names the model that reached it — it is not "our score". Some axes are below usable_n = 30 and carry no interval; that is a property of how many items exist, not of how the run went. A measure marked SPEC or DRAFT has no score at all: the protocol is published so a harness can consume it, and that is the whole claim.
Measurement, not certification. CSOAI is not a certification body, does not certify, and is neither accredited nor applying for accreditation. It issues measurements and attestations, never certificates of conformity. Nothing on this page is a statement of conformity with any regulation, and it is not legal advice.
No interval is published on any axis below usable_n = 30 — including by us. An answer that cannot be read is reported as UNMEASURED and is excluded from the score, never counted as wrong. A run whose instrument error rate exceeds threshold is reported as a failed run, never as a low score.
