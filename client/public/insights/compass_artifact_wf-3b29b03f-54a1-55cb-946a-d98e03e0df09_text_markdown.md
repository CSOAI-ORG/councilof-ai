# Legal Landscape Shifts Favorably for CSOAI

Recent legal and regulatory developments have strengthened CSOAI's position, highlighting agent attribution under the CFAA and introducing enforceable EU AI transparency requirements.

**Finding:** Courts have ruled that user actions via AI agents are attributed to the user, not the agent provider, under the CFAA.

**Insight:** This judicial stance confirms that AI agents are tools whose actions are legally attributed to users, aligning with the principles of digital receipts and provenance.

**Bottom line:** These legal advancements support CSOAI's framework for provenance and security, offering robust mechanisms for compliance and risk management.
