What SOV SPACE accesses — and what it never does

🎙 Microphone (optional): only while you use voice, to hear your commands. Speech is processed in your browser — audio is never uploaded or stored.

📍 Approximate location (optional): to centre the map on your area. Coarse IP-based by default; precise GPS only if you explicitly ask “scan my area”.

🔏 On-device signing: every action is signed with an Ed25519 key generated on your device. The ledger is yours; nothing is sent to a server.

SOV SPACE never: accesses private cameras, runs facial recognition, tracks individuals, or sells your data. These are Layer-0 hard stops.

Public data shown — aircraft, vessels, weather, seismic, news, public traffic cameras — comes from free, consented, public sources.
