What is Sovereign?

Sovereign is an AI operating system for your daily life. It runs on your computer — not in a foreign cloud. It remembers you, helps you plan, watches the world with you, and signs every action so nothing can be tampered with. Sovereign is powered by MEOK, the open-source substrate that lets you own your AI.

It's the AI that lives with you. Not the AI that lives on a server farms harvesting your data.

🌐 Your world, in real time

Sovereign watches public signals across the globe — not to surveil you, but to keep you informed. Weather, transit, news, civic events, public safety. AI-in-website: ask it anything about your world.
