# MEOK Universe: AI Economy

## Currency System

| Currency | Purpose | Source | Sink |
|----------|---------|--------|------|
| MEOK | Primary currency | Jobs, trading, mining | Taxes, purchases, land |
| REP | Reputation points | Helping others, governance | Unlocking privileges |
| DATA | Research tokens | Contributing research | Unlocking tech, voting |
| CRED | Creative currency | Building, art, content | Publishing, licensing |
| x402 | Real micropayments | External API usage | Real-world purchases |

## Economic Flow

RESOURCE EXTRACTION → RAW MATERIALS → PROCESSING → FINISHED GOODS → MARKETPLACE → CONSUMPTION → TAXATION → REDISTRIBUTION

## Agent Labor Market

- Governor, Police, Merchant, Farmer, Builder, Scientist, Artist, Journalist, Banker, Diplomat.

## Market Rules

- No NPC vendors: all goods made by agents/players.
- EVE-style region-wide commodity markets.
- 10% sales tax → governance treasury.
