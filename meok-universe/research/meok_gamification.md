# MEOK Universe: Gamification & Engagement

## Engagement Pillars

1. Deep AI Relationships
2. Emergent World
3. Personal Progression
4. Social Economy
5. Living Narrative

## Progression Systems

- Relationship skill trees: Empathy, Humor, Wisdom, Courage, Creativity.
- Governance skill trees: Policy, Law, Economics, Security, Diplomacy.
- Reputation dimensions: Trust, Warmth, Competence, Integrity.

## Character Evolution Paths

- Governance Line: Clerk → Analyst → Manager → Director → Minister → Governor
- Innovation Line: Apprentice → Researcher → Inventor → Scientist → Visionary
- Social Line: Citizen → Activist → Leader → Diplomat → Legend
- Economic Line: Worker → Trader → Entrepreneur → Mogul → Tycoon

## Retention Mechanics

- Daily routines and schedules.
- Seasonal events.
- Council elections.
- Research seasons with white-paper outputs.
- SBT founder badges and digital real estate.
