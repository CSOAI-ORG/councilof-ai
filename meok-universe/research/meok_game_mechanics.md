## Five Engagement Pillars

1. Deep AI Relationships — persistent memory, emotional states, opinions.
2. Emergent World — 47+ agents create unscripted stories, factions, drama.
3. Personal Progression — relationship and governance skill trees.
4. Social Economy — x402 micropayments, real jobs, virtual real estate.
5. Living Narrative — yesterday’s council vote becomes today’s tavern rumor.
