# MEOK Universe: Human-in-the-Loop Research

## Research Model

Player actions in MEOK Universe generate consented, anonymized data:
- Governance decisions → compliance research
- Trading → economic modeling
- Agent interactions → social dynamics
- Space exploration → procedural generation validation
- Crisis response → AI alignment
- Events → collective behavior

## Pipeline

1. Data Collection (anonymized, consented)
2. Pattern Analysis (AI agents detect trends)
3. Hypothesis Generation
4. Simulation Validation
5. White Paper Production
6. Academic Partnership

## Research Domains & Targets

- Governance voting patterns → ACL, AAAI
- Economic trading data → JEBO, AER
- AI agent social networks → Nature, Science
- Space exploration data → CHI, UIST
- Crisis response simulations → PNAS
- Policy A/B testing → NBER

## Precedent

Sea Hero Quest (4.3M players) generated 17,600 years of dementia research data and was published in PNAS.
