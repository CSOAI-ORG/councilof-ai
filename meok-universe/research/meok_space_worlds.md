# MEOK Universe: Space Worlds

## Layers

### MEOK ORBIT
- Space stations (research, manufacturing)
- Asteroid mining (rare resources)
- Satellite network (global comms, surveillance)
- Lunar base (colony, exploration)

### MEOK DEEP SPACE
- Procedural star systems (seed-based generation)
- Alien factions (diplomacy, trade, conflict)
- Anomaly research (sci-fi discoveries)
- Terraforming projects (planet modification)

## Gameplay Loops

- Resource extraction in orbit feeds the town economy.
- Deep-space exploration generates procedural content.
- Alien diplomacy creates emergent conflict/cooperation.
- Terraforming projects are long-horizon public works.
