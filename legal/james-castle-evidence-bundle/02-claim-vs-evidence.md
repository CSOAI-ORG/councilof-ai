# Claim vs Evidence — what James said, what's verifiable

## Purpose
The hardest part of any fraud or misrepresentation claim is showing that a specific representation was made AND was false. This file forces you to separate:
1. **What was claimed** (verbatim, with date and source)
2. **What's verifiable publicly** (Companies House, USPTO, DoJ, DoT, FAA, etc.)
3. **What the verification actually shows**

## After filling

A claim becomes **provably false** when the verification row is "no record found" or "record contradicts the claim" and the source row is a verbatim quote from James.

A claim becomes **unverified** when no public record exists either way. "Unverified" is not the same as "false" — many consultants genuinely do work for US federal agencies as subcontractors without a public listing.

A claim becomes **verified** when the public record matches the claim.

**This is the matrix a solicitor needs.** Not the narrative.
