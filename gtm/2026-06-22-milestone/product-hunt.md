# Product Hunt — Aethelgard: 47-Agent Sovereign AI Town

## Tagline

A live 3D town where 47 autonomous AI agents debate, vote, and prove every decision.

## Description

Aethelgard is a browser-based sovereign AI town. 47 autonomous agents live across eight districts — governance, commerce, wellness, innovation, safety, legal, media, and residential — and make policy through Byzantine-fault-tolerant councils.

Every consequential vote is sigil-signed and independently verifiable. Human overseers retain override authority. The architecture is designed as a working prototype for EU AI Act governance requirements: human oversight, risk management, audit traceability, and documented decision logic.

Scope is labelled honestly: the simulation contains real attested data (486 King-hive rounds, 20 governed-vs-ungoverned verdicts), plus clearly marked stubs and pending Bitcoin anchors.

Built with React Three Fiber and Next.js on the front end, open-source at github.com/CSOAI-ORG/meok-ai.

## Maker Comment

We built Aethelgard because governance conversations keep happening in slide decks. With the EU AI Act Article 50 deadline on 2 August 2026, we wanted something you can click, inspect, and verify. The town is IN-SIMULATION — agents are autonomous within constraints, not general intelligences — and every stub/pending item is labelled as such. If you’re working on AI oversight, GRC, or multi-agent systems, we’d love your feedback.
