# LinkedIn Post — Aethelgard 47-Agent Sovereign AI Town Launch

---

We launched something this week that tries to make AI governance tangible.

Aethelgard is a live 3D town running in the browser. It is home to 47 autonomous AI agents across eight districts — governance, commerce, wellness, innovation, safety, legal, media, and residential. Agents have roles, schedules, and memories. When a policy decision matters, it goes to a Byzantine-fault-tolerant council. No single agent can act alone. Human overseers retain override authority. And every verdict is sigil-signed and independently verifiable.

You can try it here: https://try.meok.ai/town-3d

Why this, why now

The EU AI Act Article 50 enforcement deadline for high-risk AI systems is 2 August 2026. That is not far away. Enterprises, system integrators, and GRC consultancies are being asked to demonstrate human oversight, risk management, audit traceability, and documented decision logic. Most of that evidence still lives in policy decks. We wanted to build a working prototype that makes the architecture inspectable.

Honest scope labels

We are trying to model the behaviour we want to see in AI governance: label what is real, what is simulated, and what is still pending.

- Real attested data: 486 King-hive governance rounds, 20 attestable governed-vs-ungoverned verdicts, King 12 / Queen 8, average margin 0.0437.
- Policy Lab result TREATMENT_WINS is a stub, clearly labelled.
- Two Bitcoin anchors are pending confirmation, clearly marked.
- IN-SIMULATION: the agents are autonomous within designed constraints; they are not general intelligences and do not access live production systems.

Verify the proofs yourself at https://github.com/CSOAI-ORG/sigil-proofs.

Built openly

The front end is React Three Fiber + Next.js. The code is open source at https://github.com/CSOAI-ORG/meok-ai. The launch post is at https://csoai.org/blog/launch-47-agent-town.
