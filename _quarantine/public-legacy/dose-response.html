The simulation

CSOAI ran a 511-cycle flywheel simulation. 47 AI agents in a digital town. ~1.27M agent decisions per cycle. Total: 649,000,000 simulated AI decisions across 10 random seeds.

Each decision was cryptographically signed (Ed25519), hash-chained, and recorded in an immutable audit log. 481/511 signatures independently verified. The chain 511/511 unbroken. Tamper rejection working.

⚠️ The honest version

"Governed = 0 crimes" is technically a tautology (100% enforcement blocks 100% of attempted crimes). The real result is the dose-response curve: governance works proportionally to enforcement, and the curve is monotonic.

Agents in this simulation are rule-based ABM (Agent-Based Modeling), not LLMs. The proof is about governance substrate, not agent intelligence. We are honest about that.
