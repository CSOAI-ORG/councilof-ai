What we do NOT claim

Stated plainly, so you don't have to dig

  • We do not claim the governance gate is unbeatable — 1.000 is on our scenario set, and the set is published so you can try to break it.
  • The efficiency dimension is a prototype and is not published as validated.
  • Live full-stack gate measurements at n≥200 have not run yet; a repo is not a measurement.
  • A signed record proves what happened, not that it was right — provenance is not truth.
