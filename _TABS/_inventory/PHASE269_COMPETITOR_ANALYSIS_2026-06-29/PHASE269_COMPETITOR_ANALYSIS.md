### 2.1 QUORA POE — *the consumer gateway*

**What it is:** Poe (poe.com) is Quora's multi-model chat UI launched Dec 2022. Lets users chat with GPT-4, Claude, Gemini, Llama, Mistral and 100+ community bots in one interface. Has a creator economy — bot builders earn revenue when users message their bots.

**PMF profile:**
- ~**30M+ MAU** (public estimate from Quora's traffic disclosures + 3rd-party trackers; Quora does not publish Poe MAU separately)
- Demographics: consumer + prosumer; skews tech-curious, knowledge-worker adjacent; English-first international; price-sensitive at the bottom, creator-monetisation-driven at the top
- Subscription: **$19.99/mo Poe Premium** (~600 GPT-4 messages), **$39.99/mo Annual** discount, free tier with daily limits. **Enterprise: none.** No Poe for Business SKU.
- Bot creator revenue: per-message revenue share via Quora Points system

**Why it's a "sovereign-by-design" competitor (loosely):** Multi-model routing is the closest consumer analogue to SOV3's BIG BRAIM 8-model ensemble. Poe lets you "swap the brain" without changing apps.

**Where SOV3 wins against Poe:**
- **No enterprise SKU, no SSO, no data residency, no audit trail** — Poe is consumer only. SOV3 wins on every governance dimension by default.
- **No sovereignty primitives** — data flows to OpenAI / Anthropic / Google US APIs depending on the chosen model. EU customers using Poe are sending prompts to US infra without GDPR Article 28 guarantees.
- **No MCP, no agent framework, no ontology** — Poe is a chat surface, not an OS.

**Where SOV3 loses against Poe:**
- **Brand recognition & viral loop.** Poe's growth engine is "try the new model first, in 30 seconds, no signup". SOV3 has a 33-BFT-council onboarding ceremony. Sir Nick needs a **Poe-style gateway into MEOK** — a free, no-signup, single-page tool that demonstrates a sovereign task (e.g. "EU AI Act Article 50 check on your generated content") and converts to sign-up at the value moment, not the brand moment.

**PMF overlap with SOV3 demographics:** **Low.** Poe users are individual consumers and creators. SOV3 ICPs are CISOs, AI Ethics Officers, defence procurement leads. **Treat Poe as a brand-borrowing threat (free tier sets user expectations), not a buyer-threat.**
