## The legend: koi swims up the waterfall, passes the dragon gate, becomes the dragon

### How it works
1. **Vortex venturi** pumps aerated water up the back of the wall
2. Water spills over the front of each block → cascading waterfall
3. **Koi sculpture** on Block 2 appears to swim UP the falling water
4. **Dragon head** at top breathes the vortex mist as "dragon fire"
5. **Real koi** swim at the base where the waterfall splashes in

### Modular block system
Each block is **370 × 200 × 150mm** — single piece on the Qidi bed.
Blocks stack with **dovetail interlocks + silicone seal + M6 bolts**.

### The koi → dragon transformation (printed in 3 stages)

```
STAGE 1 (on Block 2):          STAGE 2 (on Block 3):         STAGE 3 (at top):
  🐟                            🐟→🐉                          🐉
  Pure koi                     Half-transformed               Full dragon
  Swims upstream               Scales appearing               Breathing mist
  Fins pushing                  Fins becoming wings            Flames from mouth
```

Each stage is a separate print that clips onto the waterfall block behind it.

### Mist/fire manifold at the dragon's mouth
Screw-in interchangeable nozzle with flame-pattern holes:
- **50 holes × 1mm** on the outer ring → visible mist "flame"
- **10 holes × 2.5mm** in the centre → main water jet arc
- Screws in via 50mm thread
- Unscrew to clean or swap patterns

### Wing mount
Wings print flat on the bed (370mm span each), slot into the dragon head with dovetail + M4 bolts.
