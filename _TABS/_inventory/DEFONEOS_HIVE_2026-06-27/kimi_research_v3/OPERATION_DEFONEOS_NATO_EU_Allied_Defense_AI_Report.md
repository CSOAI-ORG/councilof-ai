## SECTION 1: NATO DEFENSE AI PROGRAMS

### 1.1 NATO DIANA (Defence Innovation Accelerator for the North Atlantic)
- **Official Link:** https://www.diana.nato.int
- **What it is:** NATO's flagship dual-use deep tech accelerator, matching startups with defense end-users across 20+ accelerator sites and 180+ test centres. Runs competitive challenge programs twice yearly.
- **2025 Programme:** 10 challenges launched June 2025, proposals due 11 July 2025. Each challenge targets critical defense problems.
- **Funding:** Non-dilutive grants — EUR 100,000 (Phase 1 Boot-camp, 6 months) + up to EUR 300,000 (Phase 2 Scale, 6 months). Total up to EUR 400,000 per company.
- **Eligibility:** Companies headquartered in NATO member nations. Early-stage startups and SMEs with limited defense experience encouraged.
- **IP Rights:** DIANA takes NO rights on IP developed with grant funding.
- **How DEFONEOS can engage:** Apply directly to 2025 challenges. DEFONEOS as a UK-based Defense AI OS is ideally positioned as a dual-use platform. If AI interoperability, data exploitation, or decision-support challenges are listed, DEFONEOS should submit a quad-chart application.
- **Priority: P0**

### 1.2 NATO Innovation Fund (NIF)
- **Official Link:** https://www.nif.fund
- **What it is:** A EUR 1 billion+ standalone venture capital fund backed by 24 NATO Allies, investing in deep tech for defense, security, and resilience. Part of NATO 2030 initiative.
- **Focus Areas:** AI, autonomy, quantum, biotechnology, hypersonics, space, novel materials, energy/propulsion, next-gen communications.
- **Investment:** Initial tickets up to EUR 15M. Seed through Series B. Fund-of-funds program also active.
- **Geography:** Must be headquartered in one of 24 LP nations (includes UK).
- **How DEFONEOS can engage:** Pitch as a Defense AI OS platform serving the dual-use market. Request warm intro or submit deck to investments@nif.fund. Being UK-based is a major advantage (UK is top DSR VC hub in Europe since 2019).
- **Priority: P0**

### 1.3 NATO AI Strategy & Data Exploitation Framework (DEF)
- **Official Link:** https://www.nato.int (NATO AI Strategy, 2021; revised 2024)
- **What it is:** NATO's overarching policy framework for responsible AI adoption. The Data Exploitation Framework Strategic Plan operationalizes data as a strategic resource.
- **Key Components:**
  - Principles of Responsible Use for AI (6 principles: Lawfulness, Responsibility, Explainability, Reliability, Governability, Bias Mitigation)
  - Data & AI Review Board (DARB) — governance forum for best practices
  - Alliance Data Sharing Ecosystem Pilot — breaking down data silos
- **2024 Revision:** Focus on accelerating responsible adoption, delivered at Washington Summit July 2024.
- **How DEFONEOS can engage:** Align product architecture with NATO's Principles of Responsible Use. Position DEFONEOS as enabling NATO's Data Exploitation Framework objectives. Engage with DARB as a standards contributor.
- **Priority: P1**

### 1.4 NATO Allied Command Transformation (ACT) AI Programs
- **Official Link:** https://www.act.nato.int
- **What it is:** ACT is the operational engine driving NATO's AI adoption, developing practical AI solutions and prototypes.
- **Key AI Initiatives:**
  - **AI FELIX:** Digital assistant reducing HQ document processing time by 80%. Expanded across multiple NATO commands. 20,000+ expected users.
  - **AIDA (AI Digital Assistant):** Conversational AI on NATO's classified network using RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation). Document drafting, data queries, multi-agent collaboration.
  - **AI CLAIRE:** Semantic search tool for NATO standards and doctrine content navigation.
  - **PM-ADM (Political-Military Assisted Decision Making):** Strategic decision-support using NLP and knowledge modeling across 24/7 data ingestion.
- **How DEFONEOS can engage:** Position as an enabler for NATO's AI assistant ecosystem. DEFONEOS could integrate with or enhance these tools. Partner with ACT Data Science & AI team through TIDE Sprint Conference.
- **Priority: P1**

### 1.5 NATO Quantum Technologies Strategy
- **Official Link:** https://www.nato.int/en/about-us/official-texts-and-resources/official-texts/2024/01/16/summary-of-natos-quantum-technologies-strategy
- **What it is:** NATO's first quantum strategy (Jan 2024), aiming for a "quantum-ready Alliance." Intersects with AI through sensing, secure communications, and computing.
- **Key Elements:** Post-quantum cryptography transition, quantum sensing for PNT, Transatlantic Quantum Community.
- **How DEFONEOS can engage:** Ensure cryptographic modules in DEFONEOS are quantum-safe ready. Engage with Transatlantic Quantum Community for future-proofing.
- **Priority: P2**

### 1.6 NATO Standardization Office — AI Interoperability Standards
- **Official Link:** https://www.nato.int/cps/en/natohq/topics_49202.htm
- **What it is:** NATO's standardization framework for enabling AI systems to interoperate across Allied forces.
- **Key Standards:**
  - STANAG 4774/4778 — Data confidentiality labeling and cryptographic binding
  - STANAG 5602 (SIMPLE) — Tactical data sharing
  - MIP4-IES — Ground force C2 data exchange

## SECTION 2: EU DEFENCE AI

### 2.1 European Defence Fund (EDF)
- **Official Link:** https://defence-industry-space.ec.europa.eu/european-defence-fund_en
- **What it is:** The EU's EUR 8 billion (2021-2027) instrument for collaborative defense R&D. By 2025, roughly half allocated.
- **2025 Results:** EUR 1.07 billion invested in 57 projects covering AI, cyber defence, drones, counter-drone systems.
- **2024 Results:** EUR 910 million across 62 projects.
- **AI Focus Areas:** Information superiority, situational awareness, decision-making, autonomous systems, multi-domain combat clouds.
- **SME Share:** 27% of 2024 funding went to SMEs (highest share yet). 38% of 2025 participants are SMEs.
- **Eligibility:** Consortia of at least 3 EU Member States + Norway. Smaller consortia (2 entities) allowed for disruptive tech calls.
- **How DEFONEOS can engage:** Join EDF consortia as an AI/SME partner. The 2026 Work Programme introduces Financial Support to Third Parties (FSTP/cascade funding) for ALL EDF projects. Contact UK EDF National Focal Point for guidance.
- **Priority: P0**

### 2.2 EU Defence Innovation Scheme (EUDIS)
- **Official Link:** https://eudis-defence.eu
- **What it is:** A EUR 2 billion scheme (2023-2027) to lower barriers for startups/SMEs entering the defense sector. Part of EDF.
- **Components:** Defence hackathons, spin-in calls, innovation test hubs, Defence Equity Facility, business coaching.
- **2024:** EUR 225 million awarded to ~400 companies.
- **2025:** EUR 336 million (31% of EDF budget) devoted to EUDIS innovation measures. AI technological challenge launched.
- **How DEFONEOS can engage:** Apply for EUDIS cascade funding. Participate in EUDIS defence hackathons. Use EUDIS matchmaking services to find consortium partners for EDF calls.
- **Priority: P0**

### 2.3 European Defence Agency (EDA) — AI Programs
- **Official Link:** https://eda.europa.eu
- **What it is:** The EU's intergovernmental defense body coordinating collaborative capability development.
- **Key AI Initiatives:**
  - **AI in Defence Action Plan (AIDAP, 2020):** Identifies collaboration pathways for defense AI
  - **Hub for EU Defence Innovation (HEDI, 2022):** Platform for defense innovation cooperation
  - **Action Plan on Autonomous Systems (APAS):** Coordinates autonomous systems development
  - **Defence Artificial Intelligence Data Space (DAIDS):** New EUR 2030-target initiative for secure EU defense data sharing
  - **AI4DEF project:** Trustworthy AI assessment framework for defense
  - **CRAI project:** AI-hardened communications and radar systems
  - **AUDROS project:** Autonomous drone services
  - **Trustworthiness for AI in Defence (TAID) White Paper:** Framework for responsible defense AI
- **How DEFONEOS can engage:** Monitor EDA calls for AI projects. Position DEFONEOS as an enabler for DAIDS. Engage with HEDI for partnership matchmaking.
- **Priority: P1**

### 2.4 EU AI Act — Defence Exemptions
- **Official Link:** https://www.europarl.europa.eu/topics/en/article/20230601STO93804/eu-ai-act-first-regulation-on-artificial-intelligence
- **What it is:** The EU AI Act (2024) is the world's first comprehensive AI regulation. **Article 2 explicitly exempts military and defense AI systems from its scope.** However, the Act shapes the ethical debate and may influence future military AI governance.
- **Key Points:**
  - AI systems placed on the EU market or put into service for military/defence purposes are EXEMPT
  - Defence applications fall under national and international law instead
  - EU Parliament calls for regulation and prohibition on lethal autonomous weapons (LAWS)
  - EDF Regulation: LAWS projects must involve "meaningful human control" to qualify for funding
- **How DEFONEOS can engage:** Leverage defense exemption as a regulatory advantage. Build ethical AI practices that exceed civilian standards anyway — this becomes a trust differentiator.
- **Priority: P1**

### 2.5 PESCO AI Projects
- **Official Link:** https://pesco.europa.eu
- **What it is:** Permanent Structured Cooperation — 26 EU Member States collaborating on defense capability development. Multiple AI-integrating projects.
- **Key AI Projects:**
  - **AMIDA-UT:** Automated modeling, identification, and damage assessment of urban terrain
  - **Military Mobility:** AI-enhanced logistics and force movement
  - **European Secure Software Defined Radio (ESSOR):** AI-enhanced communications
  - **Counter-UAS:** AI-powered drone defense
- **How DEFONEOS can engage:** Monitor PESCO project developments for AI integration opportunities. Partner with PESCO project leads as an AI enabler.
- **Priority: P2**

### 2.6 FCAS / EICACS AI Integration
- **Official Link:** https://www.eicacs.eu
- **What it is:** Future Combat Air System (France-Germany-Spain) and European Initiative for Collaborative Air Combat. AI is central to the next-generation air combat system.

- **How DEFONEOS can engage:** Position as an AI interoperability layer for collaborative air combat. Partner with UK entities involved in FCAS-adjacent work.

## SECTION 5: DEFENSE AI STANDARDS & INTEROPERABILITY

### 5.1 NATO Data-Centric Security Framework
- **Key Standards:**
  - **STANAG 4774:** Confidentiality metadata label syntax (XML-based)
  - **STANAG 4778:** Cryptographic metadata binding (SMTP, SOAP, REST, XMPP)
  - **STANAG 5602 (SIMPLE):** Tactical data sharing for coalition operations
  - **STANAG 5516 (Link 16):** Tactical data link for air operations
  - **STANAG 4559 (NSILI):** NATO Standard ISR Library Interface
  - **STANAG 4586:** UAV command, control, and payload data
  - **STANAG 4607 (GMTI):** Ground Moving Target Indicator
  - **MIP4-IES:** Ground-force C2 exchange standard
- **Maturity Levels:** 3 levels from basic metadata labeling to full zero-trust architecture
- **How DEFONEOS can engage:** Build compliance with STANAG 4774/4778/5602 into core architecture. Pursue NATO interoperability certification. This is a MAJOR competitive moat.
- **Priority: P0**

### 5.2 EUROCAE WG-114 / SAE G-34 — AI Aviation Standards
- **What it is:** Joint standardization initiative for development and certification of AI-based airborne and ground systems.
- **Outputs:** "Artificial Intelligence in Aeronautical Systems: Statement of Concerns" (ER-022/AIR6988); Recommended Practice ED-324/ARP6983 for ML system certification.
- **How DEFONEOS can engage:** Monitor standards development. Build compliant frameworks for aviation AI applications.
- **Priority: P2**

### 5.3 AUKUS AI Stack Interoperability
- **What it is:** The emerging trilateral standard for AI system interoperability across AUKUS partners.
- **Layers:** Infrastructure (cloud), accelerated computing (edge AI), data management, modeling, decision-support, autonomous systems.
- **Achievements:** Cross-platform AI model swapping demonstrated on drones; shared network protocols for coalition AI; modular, mission-adaptable AI kits.
- **How DEFONEOS can engage:** Build AUKUS-compatible AI stack architecture from day one. This opens access to US, UK, and Australian markets simultaneously.
