### 2.10 Pricing Psychology Summary: Why This Architecture Wins

**1. The MongoDB Atlas Lesson:** Cloud hosting becomes 70%+ of revenue because managed services outperform self-hosted convenience [^604^]. SOV3 Cloud (the hosted platform) targets the same 70% revenue share within 5 years. The open-source engine is the acquisition channel; the cloud is the business.

**2. The Figma Editor/Viewer Lesson:** Three seat types (creator/contributor/viewer) align cost with value extraction [^521^]. SOV3 applies this: governance creators (those building policies) pay full price, contributors (those editing) pay full price, viewers (executives, auditors, board members) are free. An 8-person governance team pays for 3-4 seats, not 8.

**3. The GitLab Security-Team-As-Buyer Lesson:** GitLab's Ultimate tier ($99/user/month) exists because CISOs require compliance dashboards [^541^]. Developers don't buy Ultimate — security teams do. SOV3's Business tier is architected for the same buyer: the AI governance officer, the compliance lead, the CISO's delegate.

**4. The CrowdStrike Single-Agent Lesson:** Module stacking works when incremental adoption requires zero infrastructure change [^546^]. SOV3's module architecture ensures adding EU AI Act compliance, Watchdog Certification, or MCP Industry Packs is a configuration toggle — not a deployment project.

**5. The Cloudflare Free-Tier-Is-Not-a-Trial Lesson:** Cloudflare's free DDoS protection is "designed as a broad self-serve tier, not a trial with a hard expiration date" [^581^]. SOV3's free tier is genuinely useful for individual developers forever — but deliberately limited to 1 AI system, creating a natural conversion wall at the moment of growth.

**6. The Atlassian No-Negotiation Lesson:** Atlassian publishes one price and refuses to negotiate: "Every customer gets the best price" [^599^]. SOV3 applies this at Team and Business tiers — transparent, published, non-negotiable. Only Enterprise is custom. This builds trust and eliminates sales friction for 90% of customers.

**7. The Snowflake Consumption Ceiling Lesson:** Snowflake explicitly rejected seat-plus-consumption models because "seat licenses create a psychological ceiling that caps expansion" [^547^]. SOV3's per-AI-system add-on pricing ensures governance spend grows with AI adoption — no arbitrary per-seat caps limiting revenue.
