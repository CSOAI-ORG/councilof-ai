### 1.1 Stream 1: SOV3 Cloud (The MongoDB Play)

**The Pattern:** MongoDB Atlas generated **70% of $1.9 billion in annual revenue** by offering a consumption-based cloud service on top of an open-source core [^604^]. Elastic Cloud hit $450.7M in a single quarter by hosting what developers could run themselves [^540^]. The lesson is unambiguous: the managed cloud service always outperforms the self-managed license.

**SOV3 Cloud Pricing Architecture:**

| Tier | Price | Governance Scope | Target Customer |
|------|-------|-----------------|-----------------|
| **Free** | $0/month | 1 AI system, basic PDCA, community support | Individual developers |
| **Starter** | $99/month | 5 AI systems, standard policies, email support | Startups, AI labs |
| **Growth** | $499/month | 25 AI systems, custom policies, priority support | Series A-C companies |
| **Business** | $2,499/month | 100 AI systems, advanced compliance, 99.9% SLA | Mid-market enterprises |
| **Enterprise** | Custom ($50K-$500K+/yr) | Unlimited AI systems, full audit trail, dedicated CSM, air-gapped option | Fortune 500, regulated industries |

**Consumption Metrics (The Snowflake Lesson):** Following Snowflake's explicit rejection of seat-based pricing — *"seat licenses create a psychological ceiling that caps expansion"* [^547^] — SOV3 Cloud prices by governance workload:

- **Per AI system monitored:** $8-$50/month (scales with risk classification)
- **Per policy evaluation (API call):** $0.001 per evaluation
- **Per GB of audit data stored:** $0.50/GB/month
- **Per MCP server connection:** $5-$25/month (per connector)
- **Premium compute (real-time monitoring):** 2x base rate

| **SOV3 Cloud** | **Per-AI-system consumption** | **$9,600-$30,000** |

**SOV3 Advantage:** Every competitor prices by human seat or IT endpoint. SOV3 is the ONLY platform that prices by AI system — the entity actually being governed. As customers deploy more AI agents (the trend is exponential), SOV3 revenue grows automatically without requiring a new sales motion. This is the Snowflake consumption flywheel applied to governance.
