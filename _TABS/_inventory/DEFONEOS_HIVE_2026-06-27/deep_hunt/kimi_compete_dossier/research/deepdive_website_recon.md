# SOV3 EXPLOITABLE GAPS

## Critical Market Gaps to Exploit

### 1. **Monetary Risk Quantification for AI**
- **Gap:** No vendor quantifies AI risk in financial terms (EUR/GBP/USD exposure)
- **Exploit:** Build monetary risk quantification using Fermi estimation or similar
- **Target:** Board audit committees, CROs, prudential supervisors

### 2. **Unified AI Governance + Runtime Security**
- **Gap:** Governance vendors (Credo AI, Holistic AI) lack runtime security enforcement. Security vendors (CrowdStrike, Palo Alto, WitnessAI) lack governance depth
- **Exploit:** Build the first platform that combines deep governance (model registry, policy engine, compliance automation) with runtime security enforcement (AI firewall, inline blocking)

### 3. **Cross-Functional Collaboration Platform**
- **Gap:** Most platforms serve one function (security, legal, compliance, engineering). No platform truly enables collaboration across all stakeholders
- **Exploit:** Build collaboration-first governance with shared workspaces, approval workflows, and evidence sharing across functions

### 4. **SME-Friendly AI Governance**
- **Gap:** Most vendors are enterprise-only (six-figure contracts). Mid-market and SMEs lack accessible AI governance
- **Exploit:** Offer transparent, tiered pricing starting from $500/month

### 5. **Continuous Audit-Ready Governance**
- **Gap:** Point-in-time audits vs. continuous governance. Most platforms produce snapshots
- **Exploit:** Build always-on governance with continuous evidence collection, drift detection, and real-time compliance scoring

### 6. **Agent-to-Agent Governance**
- **Gap:** Current agent governance focuses on single agents. Multi-agent networks (agents calling agents) lack governance
- **Exploit:** Build network-level agent governance with dependency mapping, cascade risk analysis, and inter-agent policy enforcement

### 7. **Open-Source Transparency**
- **Gap:** Most vendors are closed-source black boxes
- **Exploit:** Open-core model with transparent scoring, auditable code, community contributions

### 8. **Comprehensive Framework Coverage**
- **Gap:** Vendors typically cover 2-3 frameworks. No vendor covers EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, ISO 42001, SOC 2, HIPAA, DORA, NIS2 comprehensively
- **Exploit:** Build 10+ framework coverage with cross-framework deduplication
