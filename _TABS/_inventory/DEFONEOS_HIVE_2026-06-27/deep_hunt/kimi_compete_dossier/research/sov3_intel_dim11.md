# Dimension 11: Press & Media Narrative Intelligence

## Executive Summary

The AI governance media landscape in 2026 is experiencing unprecedented expansion, driven by three converging forces: the EU AI Act August 2, 2026 enforcement deadline, the Trump administration's June 2026 AI Executive Order on "Promoting Advanced Artificial Intelligence Innovation and Security," and the rapid enterprise adoption of agentic AI systems creating new attack surfaces. SOV3 enters this environment at a moment of peak media attention but faces a crowded competitive landscape with well-funded incumbents (Cranium AI: $46M raised, TrojAI, SurePath AI: $5.2M, Evoke Security) that have already secured significant press coverage, analyst mentions (Gartner AI TRiSM), and accelerator placements (CrowdStrike/AWS/NVIDIA 2026 Cybersecurity Startup Accelerator).

**Critical Finding:** While AI security/governance as a category is well-covered, the intersection of AI governance with **operational cybersecurity workflows** (SIEM integration, SOC analyst experience, incident response) remains significantly underserved. Most coverage frames AI governance as a compliance exercise rather than a security operations imperative. This is SOV3's primary narrative gap to own.
