4. **Enterprise AI Governance Platforms** - Credo AI, Holistic AI, and others building comprehensive platforms for AI trust, risk, security, and compliance (TRiSK).

### Consolidation Themes

1. **Platform Players Are Buying, Not Building** - Palo Alto Networks, Cisco, SentinelOne, and F5 all acquired AI security startups rather than building internally. This validates the space but creates existential risk for independent vendors.

2. **Acquihire + Tech Premiums** - Protect AI was acquired for >$300M with <$5M revenue. This suggests acquirers are paying massive premiums for technical teams and early market positioning.

3. **Category Convergence** - AI governance, AI security, and data security are converging. Vendors that can't offer end-to-end platforms will be acquired or die.

4. **The "R&D Labs of Larger Companies"** - As Elastic CISO Mandy Andress noted: "For the last 20 years, security innovation has been through acquisition. Startups are the R&D labs of the larger security companies."
