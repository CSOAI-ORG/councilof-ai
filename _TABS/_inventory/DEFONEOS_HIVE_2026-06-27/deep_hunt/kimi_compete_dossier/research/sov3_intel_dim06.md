## Executive Summary

The AI governance and GRC market is a **walled garden of opaque, enterprise-only pricing**. Our intelligence confirms that NO major competitor publishes transparent pricing -- every vendor hides behind "contact sales" gates. The **minimum entry point for AI governance starts at $30,000-$50,000/year** for the cheapest dedicated platforms, scaling to **$1M+/year** for enterprise GRC suites. Implementation costs routinely **match or exceed first-year license fees**. This creates a massive pricing gap for SOV3 to exploit: **the sub-$10,000/year AI governance market is almost completely unserved.**

**Key Findings:**
- The AI governance TCO (5-year) for mid-market organizations: **$495,000 - $750,000** [^15^]
- Enterprise AI governance implementation costs: **$150,000 - $400,000** for full programs [^30^]
- No competitor offers transparent self-serve pricing below $10K/year for AI governance
- Implementation timelines range from **8 weeks** (lightweight) to **9-18 months** (enterprise GRC)
- EU AI Act compliance consulting from boutique firms: **EUR 15,000 - EUR 90,000** Year 1 [^144^]

### Key Pricing War Tactics

1. **Transparent Pricing as Weapon:** Publish pricing prominently. The entire competitive set hides behind "contact sales." Transparency alone is a massive differentiator.

2. **Freemium Entry:** Offer a genuinely useful free tier for AI system inventory and EU AI Act self-assessment. No competitor does this. Credo AI: "No free version or trial." [^51^]

3. **10x Price Anchor:** In all competitive comparisons, anchor against the $100K+ enterprise platforms. "Get AI governance that Fortune 500s pay $150,000 for, starting at $300/month."

4. **TCO Story:** Emphasize that competitor "implementation costs often match or exceed license fees" [^70^]. SOV3's self-serve model eliminates this entirely.

5. **Consulting Replacement:** Position as "the AI governance platform that replaces $50K+ consulting engagements." EU AI Act consulting projects cost EUR 15K-90K [^144^]. SOV3 software delivers continuous compliance at a fraction.

6. **Speed-to-Value:** Lead with "deploy in days, not months." OneTrust: weeks to months [^148^]. MetricStream: 9-18 months [^73^]. AuditBoard: ~4 months [^74^]. SOV3: days.

7. **SMB Exclusion as Evidence:** Every competitor review notes that these tools are "not accessible for SMBs" [^52^], "enterprise-only" [^63^], "minimum $10K/year" [^148^]. SOV3 owns the democratized position by default.
