- **Funder**: MOD Directorate of Industrial Strategy and Exports (DISE), delivered by Innovate UK

- **Deadline**: Open year-round, assessed in 3-month cycles (no fixed deadline)
- **What it funds**: Collaborative projects between UK-registered SMEs (Lower Tier Suppliers) and larger defence suppliers (Higher Tier Suppliers). Developing innovative materials, technologies, processes for defence supply chains. 18-month max duration.

- **Application**: Submit via DASA Online Submission Service; work with DASA Innovation Partner for readiness

## 10. PITCH DECK REQUIREMENTS

### 10.1 UK MOD FUNDERS WANT TO SEE

**Essential Slides (in order of importance):**
1. **The Defence Problem**: Clear articulation of the specific UK military capability gap you're solving
2. **MOD Enduring Capability Challenge Alignment**: Map your solution directly to one of the 5 MOD Enduring Capability Challenges
3. **Dual-Use Potential**: Strong civilian market opportunity alongside defence application
4. **TRL Assessment**: Honest, evidence-based Technology Readiness Level with clear path to TRL 6+
5. **Endorsement/User Pull**: Evidence that military end-users have expressed need or provided feedback
6. **Exploitation Plan**: Clear route to market, including potential MOD procurement pathway
7. **Competitive Landscape**: Why UK defence needs YOUR solution vs alternatives
8. **Team**: Defence sector experience (highly valued); technical credibility
9. **Financials**: Realistic project budget, value for money, and unit economics
10. **IP Strategy**: Patent position or trade secrets; freedom to operate analysis

**Key MOD-Specific Requirements:**
- Demonstrate alignment with UK Defence Industrial Strategy (DSIS)
- Show contribution to UK sovereign capability
- Evidence potential for UK economic growth and job creation
- Address export potential
- Include Defence Lines of Development (DLOD) analysis if appropriate

### 10.2 NATO DIANA EVALUATORS LOOK FOR

**Evaluation Criteria (in priority order):**
1. **Dual-Use Mission**: Solutions must be viable in BOTH defence and civilian markets
2. **TRL Level**: Ideally TRL 3-4 (proof of concept), TRL 5-6 (validated) may be accepted
3. **NATO Strategic Alignment**: Addresses NATO's priority challenge areas
4. **Investor Readiness**: Clear scale-up strategy; additional funding can drive growth
5. **Business Maturity**: Capable of benefiting from accelerator curriculum
6. **Technology Innovation**: Genuinely novel approach with potential for breakthrough
7. **Alliance Value**: Benefit to multiple NATO nations, not just one country
8. **Team Quality**: Technical and business capability to execute

**Application Components:**
- Short Proposal (max 5 pages)
- Quad Chart (one-page summary)
- Company registration on DIANA platform
- Evidence of dual-use potential
- Pathway to commercialisation

### 10.3 DEFENCE VCs CARE ABOUT

**Investment Thesis Checklist:**
1. **National Security Relevance**: Is this technology critical to UK/allied defence?
2. **Dual-Use Commercial Viability**: Is there a strong commercial market alongside defence?
3. **Technical Moat**: Is the technology defensible (IP, expertise, data network effects)?
4. **Government Traction**: Any contracts, pilots, or LOIs from defence customers?
5. **Team Credibility**: Previous defence sector experience? Technical depth?
6. **Market Size**: TAM/SAM/SOM analysis for defence + commercial markets
7. **Regulatory Pathway**: Clear understanding of export controls, security requirements
8. **Unit Economics**: Defensible margins; recurring revenue potential (SaaS model ideal)
9. **Exit Potential**: Strategic acquirers (BAE, QinetiQ, US primes) or IPO path
10. **Alignment with Government Strategy**: DSIS, UKDI priorities, NATO technology needs

**NSSIF-Specific:**
- Must demonstrate both commercial viability AND strategic relevance
- Co-investment model -- who else is investing?
- Willingness to work with government for trials and testing
- Horizon assessment (early vs late stage)
