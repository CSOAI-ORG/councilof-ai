## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The UK defense AI ecosystem is at a critical inflection point. The June 2022 Defence Artificial Intelligence Strategy, the November 2024 JSP 936 regulatory framework, the June 2025 Strategic Defence Review, the September 2025 Defence Industrial Strategy, and the July 2025 launch of UK Defence Innovation (UKDI) collectively represent the most ambitious restructuring of UK defense technology in decades. A £1 billion Digital Targeting Web, a £400M/year UKDI budget, a planned Defence AI Investment Fund, and Palantir's £1.5 billion UK investment signal massive opportunity. **The founder's instinct is correct — there are very few UK companies building a defense AI operating system or platform layer, creating a significant gap for DEFONEOS to fill.**

It identifies decision advantage, efficiency, unlocking new capabilities, and empowering the whole force as priority outcomes — all areas where a Defense AI OS would provide foundational infrastructure.

**Actionable Recommendation:** DEFONEOS should map its 33 Hives directly to the four headline objectives and six "problem spaces" identified in the Defence AI Playbook. Position the OS as the enabling layer for the MOD's "AI Now" adoption at scale.

### FINDING 4.1: JSP 936 — Dependable AI in Defence (November 2024)
**Source:** [GOV.UK — JSP 936 Part 1](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/jsp-936-dependable-artificial-intelligence-ai-in-defence-part-1-directive) & [JSP 936 PDF](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6735fc89f6920bfb5abc7b62/JSP936_Part1.pdf) & [Burges Salmon Analysis](https://www.burges-salmon.com/articles/102jpv4/implementing-dependable-ai-in-defence-key-directives-from-jsp-936/)

**What it is:** The **principal policy framework governing AI adoption in MOD.** Mandates five ethical principles: Human-Centricity, Responsibility, Understanding, Bias/Harm Mitigation, Reliability. Requires Responsible AI Senior Officers (RAISOs) in each TLB. Establishes ethical risk assessment with approval pathways from Minor (TLB-level) to Critical (Ministers). Special attention for AI in "kinetic effects" and "novel/contentious applications." Requires AI ethical risk assessments, model cards, and continuous performance monitoring.

**Relevance to DEFONEOS:** JSP 936 is the compliance framework that ALL defense AI must meet. DEFONEOS must be JSP 936-compliant from day one. This is a competitive moat — compliance is hard.

**Actionable Recommendation:** Build JSP 936 compliance into DEFONEOS as a first-class feature. Every model deployed through DEFONEOS should automatically generate model cards, ethics risk assessments, and audit trails. Appoint an internal Ethics Manager. This becomes a major selling point to MOD buyers.
