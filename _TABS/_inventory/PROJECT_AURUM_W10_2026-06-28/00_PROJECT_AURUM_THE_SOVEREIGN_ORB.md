## 5. THE CAPILLARY COOLING (the physics)

Per the `CAPILLARY_DNA_COOLING_INTEGRATION.md` (the comprehensive 130 KB analysis), capillary cooling works:

- **Washburn equation:** `L = sqrt((γD cos θ t) / (4μ))` for penetration depth
- **Capillary pressure:** `ΔP = 4γcos(θ)/D` (249 Pa for 1mm channel, 1247 Pa for 0.2mm)
- **Phase change:** 10% evaporation = 540x more heat transport than 10°C temp rise
- **Lifting limit:** 0.5mm channel can only lift water ~51mm vertically (capillary pressure alone)
- **Solution:** Photoactuator boost (NIR heating → Marangoni flow) + gravity-assisted condenser

**The graded channel design (mimicking biological vascular networks):**
- **0.2-0.3mm channels** at heat sources (high capillary pressure, close to heat)
- **0.5-1.0mm channels** in transport regions (lower pressure drop, higher flow)

These requirements are fundamentally incompatible at the molecular level."**
