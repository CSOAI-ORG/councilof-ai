## Hacker News

**Title:** We built an open AI measurement board with x402 micropayments — 22 axes, 335 signed cards, Base USDC

**URL:** https://councilof.ai/press/

**Comment (first post):**
CSOAI here. We ship an open AI measurement board that measures 22 axes across models — governance, safety, jail, provenance, conformance, and 8 deterministic-fact axes that read public ledgers directly.

Every measurement is Ed25519-signed, Merkle-rooted, Sigstore-witnessed, and OTS-stamped. Paste any card into the free verifier: three states only (VALID, INVALID, UNCHECKABLE).

The board is free. Issuance and evidence bundles are priced in USDC on Base over x402 — no accounts, no API keys, no Stripe.

Financial coverage: 425 stablecoins indexed across 211 chains, 65 deep on-chain measurements.

Revenue from external customers: $0. The board is free forever.

Happy to answer questions.
