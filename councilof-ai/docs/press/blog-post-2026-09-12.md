# We built an open AI measurement board with x402 micropayments — here's what we measured

**CSOAI Ltd (UK 16939677) ships the public CSOAI board: 22 measured axes, 335 Ed25519-signed cards, and an x402 micropayment rail on Base. Free to verify, priced to issue.**

## What we measure

The board measures 22 axes across AI models:

**14 behavioural axes** (model-comparison): governance, safety, provenance, continuity, conformance, openness, machinery-conformity, care, cross-reality, detector-interop, art5-safeguard, swarm, affect, jail.

**8 deterministic-fact axes**: provenance-controls, reserve-attestation, regulatory-framework, distribution-integrity, custody-disclosure, ai-adoption-components, labour-components, humanoid-labour-index.

Every measurement is:
- **Signed** — Ed25519 under `did:web:csoai.org#board-attestation-1`
- **Rooted** — included in a Merkle tree (169 leaves)
- **Witnessed** — in Sigstore Rekor (logIndex 2791822965)
- **OTS-stamped** — submitted to OpenTimestamps calendars
- **Verifiable** — paste any card into the free verifier

## How to verify (free, no account)

1. Get the board: `curl https://councilof.ai/api/gspc`
2. Get a card: `curl https://councilof.ai/signed/card_index.json`
3. Verify: https://councilof.ai/gspc-verify

Three states only: **VALID**, **INVALID**, **UNCHECKABLE**.

- Revenue from external customers: $0

## The x402 payment model

The board is free forever. Issuance, evidence bundles, data feeds, and proof bundles are priced in USDC on Base over x402.

How it works:
1. Discover: `GET https://councilof.ai/.well-known/x402.json`
2. Challenge: any paid endpoint returns 402 with price
3. Pay: USDC on Base (eip155:8453)
4. Receive: signed receipt JWS
5. Verify: `POST https://councilof.ai/api/receipts/verify`

No accounts. No API keys. No Stripe.
