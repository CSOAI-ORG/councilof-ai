# Comment — OMB 3064-0225, "Reporting Forms and Instructions Associated With Requirements and Standards for FDIC-Supervised Permitted Payment Stablecoin Issuers" (91 FR 46118). Comment period ends 18 September 2026.
Submit via regulations.gov / the FDIC "Submit Comment" link on the notice. This is a Paperwork Reduction Act notice about the FORMS, so the comment is about the forms.

CSOAI Ltd (UK company 16939677) publishes independent measurements of AI and stablecoin systems. We do not certify, grade, or score. One comment on the proposed reporting forms:

Each reported reserve figure should carry, on the form itself, (1) the date and time the underlying data was observed, (2) the method that produced the figure, and (3) the denominator — the number of accounts, custodians or instruments the figure summarises. Without those three fields a reported value cannot be recomputed by a third party who trusts neither the issuer nor its auditor, and a form that cannot be recomputed can only be believed. With them, the same form becomes evidence that survives the issuer's own systems.

Machine-readable submission (a fixed schema alongside the PDF) would let supervisors and the public recompute rather than reread. We publish in that shape and would supply the format on request.
