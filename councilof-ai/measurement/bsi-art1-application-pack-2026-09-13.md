## Why CSOAI Should Join ART/1

CSOAI operates the world's first public evidence operating system for AI measurement. Our 22-axis GSPC (Governance, Security, Privacy, Compliance) measurement instrument is:

1. **Already aligned to BSI/ISO standards:** Our measurement axes map directly to ISO/IEC 42001 (AI Management System), ISO/IEC 23894 (AI Risk Management), and the EU AI Act Article 50 transparency requirements.

2. **Publicly verifiable:** Every measurement card is Ed25519-signed, Merkle-rooted, and anchored to Bitcoin via OpenTimestamps. The methodology is published with DOI: 10.5281/zenodo.21991104.

3. **In production:** 2,419 measurement cards published, 22 axes measured across 425 stablecoins, 22 HuggingFace datasets as frozen evaluation banks.

4. **Crosswalked to UK/EU regulation:** We have published compliance crosswalks for:
   - EU AI Act Article 50 (88/100 readiness)
   - EU MiCA (88/100)
   - EU DORA (72/100)
   - UK FCA AI Guidance
   - CA SB 813 and AB 1405
   - NIST AI RMF (84/100)

## What CSOAI Brings to ART/1

- **Measurement methodology expertise:** Our 22-axis instrument provides a concrete, reproducible framework for measuring AI governance compliance.
- **Cross-standard mapping:** We have already built crosswalks between 11 international regulations and standards.
- **Public evidence infrastructure:** Our signed-card format, Merkle trees, and Bitcoin anchoring provide a template for audit-grade compliance evidence.
- **Industry perspective:** As a UK SME operating in the AI governance space, we bring practical implementation experience to standards development.
