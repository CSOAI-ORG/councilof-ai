When to choose Google AI Principles

Google's AI Principles are a commendable ethical framework — they set out 7 objectives for responsible AI development. But they are not a compliance platform. They don't produce auditable evidence, don't sign anything, and don't map to specific regulatory requirements like the EU AI Act. Google uses them internally; they are not available for third-party AI systems.

Bottom line: Google AI Principles are an ethical compass, not a compliance solution. For signed, regulator-verifiable attestation, MEOK is the purpose-built platform.
