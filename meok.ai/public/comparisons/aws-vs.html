When to choose AWS Service Cards

AWS AI Service Cards are a good transparency initiative — they document intended use cases, limitations, and responsible AI considerations for AWS-managed services. If your AI stack is exclusively AWS and you want their transparency docs, Service Cards are helpful. But they are not a compliance attestation platform. They don't sign anything, don't provide independent verification, and only cover AWS services.

Bottom line: AWS Service Cards are documentation, not compliance. For cryptographically-signed, regulator-verifiable AI attestation, MEOK is the purpose-built solution.
