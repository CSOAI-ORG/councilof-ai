When to choose Azure Content Safety

Azure Content Safety is a respectable content moderation API. If your need is filtering harmful content in AI-generated outputs — profanity, violence, self-harm — it's a good fit, especially within an Azure-native stack. But it is not a compliance attestation platform. It doesn't sign anything, doesn't provide regulator-verifiable evidence, and doesn't cover the EU AI Act or DORA.

Bottom line: Azure Content Safety is a moderation API, not a compliance platform. For regulator-grade, signed AI compliance attestations, MEOK is the right tool.
