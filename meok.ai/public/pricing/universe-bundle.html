Frequently Asked Questions

What is a MCP server attestation?

An attestation is a cryptographically signed assertion that a Model Context Protocol server meets your organisation's compliance, security, and governance policies before it can serve AI agents in production.

Can I switch plans mid-month?

Yes. Upgrades apply immediately with a prorated charge. Downgrades take effect at the next billing cycle. Your evidence and attestation history is preserved across all plan changes.

Do you support on-premise deployments?

On-premise and VPC deployments are available on the Enterprise plan. We'll work with your infra team to deploy into your environment, including air-gapped networks.

Which compliance frameworks are supported?

All plans include SOC 2 mapping. Governance and Enterprise add ISO 27001, ISO 42001, EU AI Act (Article 50), DORA, NIS2, UK CQC, and custom framework builders.

Is there a free trial?

We offer a 14-day free trial on the Starter plan — no credit card required. Professional and above include a 30-day trial with full feature access.

What happens if I exceed my attestation limit?

You'll receive a notification at 80% usage. Once the limit is hit, new attestations queue until the next billing cycle or you upgrade. Enterprise has no limits.

Can I export my data at any time?

Absolutely. All plans support CSV/JSON export of attestations, evidence, and compliance reports. Governance and Enterprise include API-based bulk export.

Do you offer discounted annual pricing?

Yes — annual billing gives you 2 months free (pay 10 months). Enterprise customers also receive a discounted annual rate plus priority onboarding.
