Frequently Asked Questions

How is Enterprise different from Pro?
Enterprise adds custom integrations, organisation-wide management with RBAC, SSO, dedicated support with SLA, white-labelling, and unlimited API rate limits. It is designed for regulated companies with multiple teams and bespoke compliance workflows.
Can we start on Pro and upgrade to Enterprise?
Yes. Migrate at any time. We'll pro-rate your billing and set up your Enterprise workspace within one business day. Email sales@meok.ai to initiate the upgrade.
Do you support invoicing / purchase orders?
Yes. Enterprise customers can be invoiced on net-30 terms. We accept purchase orders and can work with your procurement team on documentation requirements.
What kind of custom integrations do you offer?
We integrate with your existing GRC platforms (e.g. OneTrust, ServiceNow), SIEMs (Splunk, Elastic), identity providers (Okta, Azure AD, Keycloak), and data pipelines via custom webhooks or direct API bridges.
Is there a minimum commitment?
Enterprise requires a 6-month minimum commitment. Annual billing is available at a 15% discount (£15,289/yr vs £17,988/yr).
