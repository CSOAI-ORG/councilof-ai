Frequently Asked Questions

What frameworks does the assessment cover?
The assessment evaluates your compliance posture against ISO 42001 (AI Management Systems), the EU AI Act risk categories, and NIST AI Risk Management Framework. We can also align to specific frameworks you're targeting.
Do I need to already be using MEOK Compliance?
Not at all. The assessment is independent and works with any compliance stack — or none at all. It's designed to give you a clear picture of where you stand and what to do next.
What do I get in the report?
An executive summary (board-ready), detailed findings with risk ratings, a gap analysis matrix, a prioritised remediation roadmap, and custom attestation templates based on your specific findings.
