Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch between plans?
Yes. Upgrade or downgrade at any time. We'll pro-rate your billing for the remainder of the month. Enterprise plan requires a 6-month minimum commitment.
What payment methods do you accept?
All major credit and debit cards via Stripe. Enterprise customers can request invoice billing with net-30 terms.
Is there a free trial?
The free tier at meok-attestation-api.vercel.app/sign gives you 3 attestations per email per day at no cost — no credit card needed. Contact sales@meok.ai for a personalised Pro/Enterprise demo.
Can I get a discount on annual billing?
Yes. Annual billing for Pro is £1,990/yr (save £398). Enterprise annual is £15,289/yr (save £2,699).
How do the 48h Assessment and subscriptions work together?
Buy the assessment to get a full compliance audit, then apply 50% of the fee (£2,500) toward a Pro or Enterprise subscription within 30 days.
Do you offer SSO for Pro?
SSO / SAML / OIDC is available on the Enterprise plan only. Pro uses email + API key authentication.
