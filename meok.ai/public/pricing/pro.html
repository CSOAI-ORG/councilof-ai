Frequently Asked Questions

How does the free tier work with Pro?
The free tier at meok-attestation-api.vercel.app/sign gives you 3 attestations per email per day — enough to evaluate the system. Pro removes all limits, adds cryptographic signing, and gives you API access for automation.
What does "signed attestation" mean legally?
Each Pro attestation is cryptographically signed with a key pair tied to your organisation, creating a tamper-evident record suitable for audit and regulatory submission. The signature can be independently verified.
