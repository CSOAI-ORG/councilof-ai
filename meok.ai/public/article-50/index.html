The Compliance Challenge

Article 50 introduces six distinct obligations that affect every organisation deploying AI systems that generate or interact with content.

Six Obligations, One Deadline

  • Visible labelling of AI-generated outputs
  • Machine-readable metadata marking
  • Deepfake disclosure for image, audio, video
  • AI text disclosure for public-interest content
  • Bot interaction disclosure (chatbots, voice, avatars)
  • Code of Practice compliance documentation
