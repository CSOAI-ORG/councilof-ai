The Gaming AI Compliance Landscape

AI is woven into every layer of modern gaming operations — from personalised game recommendations and dynamic bonus offers to real-time fraud detection, AML screening, and responsible gambling interventions. But this AI-driven approach now sits at the intersection of two major regulatory frameworks:

  • UKGC Licence Conditions and Codes of Practice (LCCP): Social responsibility codes (SR code 3.1.1), customer interaction requirements, and fair and open operator obligations increasingly demand transparent AI decision records.
  • EU AI Act Article 50: Operators serving EU markets (Malta, Sweden, Denmark, etc.) must disclose AI interactions to players — including automated customer service, personalised marketing, and AI-assisted exclusion decisions.

The challenge: Gaming operators run dozens of AI services — CRM models, AML transaction monitoring, player value scoring, chatbot triage, affordability assessment engines — each generating decisions that affect players. Without automated attestation, proving compliance across this estate is a manual, error-prone mess.
