# CSOAI — Measurement doctrine (insurance brief)

**CSOAI Ltd** · UK Companies House **16939677** · https://councilof.ai

## Role

Council of AI is an **independent measurement body** for AI system behaviour. We publish frozen instruments, run systems against them, and issue **signed measurement credentials** (Ed25519, offline-verifiable).

## Grammar

- **Measurement, not certification.** We do not certify, accredit, or issue conformity assessments.
- **13 measured of 14** on the GSPC registry board (live: `GET https://councilof.ai/api/gspc`).
- **UNMEASURED is first-class** — gaps are reported with n and limits, never hidden.
- **Scores are never sold.** No money from anything we rank.

## Relevance to insurance

Underwriters may use our artefacts as **third-party measurement inputs** — analogous to engineering test reports, not regulatory approvals. Any insurability determination remains with the carrier and counsel.
