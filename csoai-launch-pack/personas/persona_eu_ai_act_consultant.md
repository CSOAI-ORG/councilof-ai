## Top 3 pain points (with real complaints)

### 1. "EU AI Act engagements are now 12-month frameworks and I can't deliver fast enough"
Big-4 EU AI Act practices grew **3–5×** between 2023 and 2025 (Big-4 public earnings + IAPP member growth in EU AI practice groups). Typical engagement structure:
- Phase 1: AI inventory + risk classification (£150K–£400K, 8–12 weeks)
- Phase 2: Gap analysis + remediation roadmap (£300K–£800K, 12–16 weeks)
- Phase 3: Implementation support + tooling rollout (£500K–£2M, 12–18 months)

Fatima has 6–10 active engagements at any time. She needs tooling that compresses Phase 1 (inventory + risk classification) from 8–12 weeks to 2–3 weeks. That's a 70% time saving × £150K–£400K/engagement = **£100K–£300K in consultant time saved per engagement**.

### 2. "My clients want a continuous compliance signal, not a point-in-time report"
Big-4 model is point-in-time audit / assessment. EU AI Act requires **continuous compliance**:
- Art 72: post-market monitoring by providers
- Art 73: serious incident reporting within 15 days
- Art 43–47: conformity assessment + ongoing surveillance

Fatima's clients say: "Give us a dashboard we can show the regulator, not a 200-page PDF that goes stale in 3 months." Big-4 firms are notoriously slow to build tooling (they partner with OneTrust / Holistic AI instead). CSOAI's API-first continuous compliance passport model directly addresses this.

### 3. "My juniors spend 40% of their time on evidence collection, not advice"
A typical EU AI Act gap analysis requires interviewing 20–40 stakeholders per client (CTO, CISO, DPO, Head of Data, product owners of each AI system), then mapping their answers to a 9-bullet Art 6 risk classification, an 11-bullet Annex IV technical documentation list, and a 7-bullet Art 27 FRIA template. Junior consultants spend **40–60% of their time on evidence collection** rather than analysis or recommendations.

A tool that ingests existing client artefacts (privacy impact assessments, DPIAs, model cards, vendor questionnaires) and auto-pre-fills the EU AI Act templates would free up Fatima's team to bill higher-margin strategy work.
