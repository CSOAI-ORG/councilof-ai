## 4. EU AI Act enforcement timeline

**Official dates (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689):**
- **February 2, 2025** — Entry into force
- **August 2, 2025** — Prohibited AI practices ban (already in effect)
- **August 2, 2026** — General-purpose AI (GPAI) obligations + Article 50 transparency (28 days away)
- **August 2, 2027** — High-risk AI systems (Annex III) full compliance

**Penalties (Art 99):**
- Prohibited practices: €35M or 7% global turnover
- Other violations: €15M or 3% global turnover
- Misleading info to authorities: €7.5M or 1% global turnover

**Per-company exposure for a typical Series B SaaS at $50M ARR:**
- 3% × $50M = $1.5M fine (vs CSOAI Pro at £499/mo = $6K/year = 250x ROI)

## 7. Top 3 objections to AI governance tools + counter-arguments

### Objection 1: "We already have Vanta/Drata for SOC 2"
**Counter:** SOC 2 ≠ EU AI Act compliance. Vanta automates evidence collection for SOC 2 (an existing framework). EU AI Act requires specific controls (Article 12 logging, Article 14 human oversight, Article 50 transparency). Different frameworks, different controls. Most Vanta customers will need BOTH — SOC 2 for existing customers + EU AI Act for the August 2 deadline.

### Objection 2: "OpenAI/Google already do watermarking"
**Counter:** Their watermarking is included with THEIR products only. If your customers use your product to generate AI content, OpenAI's watermarks don't help. CSOAI issues portable Ed25519-signed compliance certificates that work with ANY AI provider — OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, in-house models. Sovereign by design.

### Objection 3: "We can't trust a UK sovereign AI vendor"
**Counter:** That's the point. CSOAI is the ONLY vendor offering offline-verifiable compliance. You don't have to trust CSOAI's servers — every passport includes the public key and can be verified cryptographically. If CSOAI disappeared tomorrow, every passport we ever issued would still verify. That's stronger than vendor lock-in.
