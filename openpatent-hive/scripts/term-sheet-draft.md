Each Investor whose check size equals or exceeds $250,000 (a "**Major Investor**") shall be entitled to designate **one (1) non-voting observer** to attend all meetings of the Board, in a non-voting capacity, subject to customary confidentiality obligations.

One (1) seat on the Board shall be **permanently reserved** for the founder of CSOAI Limited, who shall serve as Executive Director and chair of the Board (the "**Founder Seat**"). The Founder Seat may not be transferred, diluted, or removed except by the founder's own written resignation or by the founder's death or permanent incapacity. ### 3.4 The Total Seat Count
The Board shall comprise **five (5) seats** as of the Closing:
- **Seat 1** — Founder (the Founder Seat, with the Casting Vote)

## 🜇 · ARTICLE 6 — THE INVESTOR'S SHIELD

### 6.1 Dividend Rights
Holders of the Preferred shall be entitled to receive, when, as, and if declared by the Board, non-cumulative dividends in preference to the holders of the Ordinary Shares, at the rate of 8% of the Original Issue Price per annum. No dividend shall be declared or paid on the Ordinary Shares unless a comparable dividend is simultaneously declared and paid on the Preferred. The right to dividends is **non-cumulative**; if a dividend is not declared in a given year, it does not accumulate.

### 6.2 Most Favored Nation (MFN)
If, within 12 months following the Closing, the Issuer issues any additional Series A Preferred Shares at a per-share price, or on terms, more favorable than those set out in this term sheet (a "**Subsequent Closing**"), each Investor shall be entitled to receive, at the Investor's election, the benefit of the more favorable terms. The MFN shall expire 12 months after the Closing.

### 6.3 Pro-Rata Rights
Each Major Investor shall have the right (but not the obligation) to participate in any Subsequent Closing of equity or equity-linked securities of the Issuer, on the same terms and at the same price as offered to other investors, in an amount sufficient to maintain the Major Investor's percentage ownership of the Issuer on a fully-diluted basis (the "**Pro-Rata Rights**"). The Pro-Rata Rights shall survive any future financing round.

### 6.4 Lockup
Each Investor agrees not to sell, transfer, or otherwise dispose of any shares of CSOAI Limited (or any securities convertible into or exchangeable for shares of CSOAI Limited) for a period of **18 months** following the Closing, without the prior written consent of the Issuer (the "**Lockup**"). The Lockup shall not apply to:
- (a) Transfers to affiliates of the Investor;
- (b) Transfers to the Investor's limited partners, members, or shareholders, pro rata;
- (c) Bona fide gifts;
- (d) Transfers pursuant to a merger, consolidation, or sale of all or substantially all of the assets of the Issuer that has been approved by the Board.

### 6.5 Right of First Refusal (ROFR)
If any holder of the Preferred proposes to transfer any such shares (other than as permitted by Article 6.4), the Issuer shall have a right of first refusal to purchase such shares on the same terms as the proposed transfer. If the Issuer does not exercise its ROFR within 30 days, the holders of a majority of the remaining Preferred shall have a right of second refusal.

### 6.6 Co-Sale (Tag-Along) Rights
If the Founder proposes to transfer any Ordinary Shares in a transaction (other than a permitted transfer), each Major Investor shall have the right to participate in such transfer on a pro-rata basis (based on fully-diluted ownership) and on the same terms.

### 6.7 Drag-Along
If holders of **at least 75%** of the Preferred Shares (voting as a single class) and the Founder approve a sale of the Issuer, all shareholders shall be required to participate in such sale (the "**Drag-Along**"). The Drag-Along shall not be exercised in respect of a sale to a competitor of the Investor, or on terms that would result in the Investor receiving less than the Investor's liquidation preference under Article 7.

### 6.8 Pre-Emptive Rights (Anti-Dilution — Narrow-Based)
If the Issuer issues any equity or equity-linked securities at a per-share price below the Original Issue Price of the Preferred (a "**Down Round**"), the Original Issue Price shall be adjusted downward in accordance with the **broad-based weighted-average** anti-dilution formula (the "**Anti-Dilution Protection**"). The Anti-Dilution Protection shall apply only to Down Rounds and shall not apply to:
- (a) Shares issued in connection with a stock split, stock dividend, or recapitalization;
- (b) Shares issued to employees, directors, consultants, or advisors pursuant to an approved equity incentive plan, in an amount not to exceed 15% of fully-diluted shares outstanding;
- (c) Shares issued in connection with a bona fide acquisition, merger, or strategic transaction approved by the Board;
- (d) Shares issued upon conversion of the Preferred or any other outstanding convertible security.

## 🜈 · ARTICLE 7 — THE ORDER OF THE DRAGON'S BREATH

### 7.1 Liquidation Preference
In the event of any voluntary or involuntary liquidation, dissolution, or winding-up of CSOAI Limited, or a **Deemed Liquidation Event** (defined below), the holders of the Preferred shall be entitled to receive, in preference to the holders of the Ordinary Shares, an amount equal to the **greater of**:
- (a) the **Original Issue Price** plus any declared but unpaid dividends, or
- (b) the amount the Investor would have received had the Preferred been converted into Ordinary Shares immediately prior to such event (the "**As-Converted**" amount).

This is sometimes called a "**1× non-participating preference**" — the Investor takes the higher of (a) or (b), but does not double-dip.

### 7.2 Deemed Liquidation Event
A "**Deemed Liquidation Event**" shall mean:
- (a) A merger or consolidation in which the Issuer is a constituent party, except any such merger or consolidation involving the Issuer or a subsidiary in which the shares of the Issuer outstanding immediately prior to such transaction continue to represent, or are converted into or exchanged for shares representing, a majority of the outstanding shares of the surviving entity;
- (b) A sale, lease, license, transfer, or other disposition of all or substantially all of the assets of the Issuer; or
- (c) A sale, transfer, or other disposition of the 5 LOCKs (or any material component thereof), or of all or substantially all of the `.ai` domain portfolio, in a single transaction or series of related transactions.

### 7.3 Waterfall
After the payment of the liquidation preferences under Article 7.1, the remaining assets of the Issuer shall be distributed to the holders of the Ordinary Shares, pro rata, on an as-converted basis.

### 7.4 Non-Cash Consideration
If the consideration received in a Deemed Liquidation Event is not solely cash, the value of the non-cash consideration shall be determined by the Board in good faith, taking into account the advice of a qualified independent third-party valuation firm.

## 🜉 · ARTICLE 8 — THE PATH BACK TO COMMON

### 8.1 Optional Conversion
Each share of the Preferred shall be convertible, at the option of the holder, into Ordinary Shares at any time, on a 1-for-1 basis (subject to adjustment for stock splits, stock dividends, and similar events). The initial conversion ratio is **1 Preferred : 1 Ordinary** (the "**Conversion Ratio**").

### 8.2 Automatic Conversion
Each share of the Preferred shall automatically convert into Ordinary Shares at the then-effective Conversion Ratio upon the closing of a **Qualified IPO** or upon the written consent of holders of **at least 75%** of the outstanding Preferred Shares.

### 8.3 Qualified IPO
A "**Qualified IPO**" means a firm-commitment underwritten public offering of the Ordinary Shares of the Issuer on the **London Stock Exchange Main Market**, the **New York Stock Exchange**, or the **Nasdaq Global Select Market**, in each case at a per-share price implying a **pre-money equity value of at least $250,000,000**, resulting in gross proceeds to the Issuer of at least $50,000,000.

### 8.4 Reservation of Shares
The Issuer shall at all times reserve and keep available out of its authorized but unissued Ordinary Shares, solely for the purpose of effecting conversion of the Preferred, such number of its Ordinary Shares as shall from time to time be sufficient to effect the conversion of all outstanding Preferred.
