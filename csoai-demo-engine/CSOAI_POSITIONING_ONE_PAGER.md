### THE SOLUTION

CSOAI is the first **Agent Governance Operating System** — a unified platform that makes autonomous AI systems compliant, auditable, and accountable by default.

Our engine maps every decision your AI agents make against **13 regulatory frameworks** (EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF, GDPR, HIPAA, NIS2, MiCA, DORA, and more) — automatically, in real time, at the speed of inference.

At the core is our **BFT Council**: five specialized AI agents with Byzantine Fault Tolerant voting that serve as a distributed governance board — evaluating risk, flagging non-compliance, and enforcing policy before harm happens, not after.

This isn't a compliance dashboard you check once a quarter. This is governance **embedded into the AI itself**.
