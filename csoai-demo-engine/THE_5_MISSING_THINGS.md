## APPENDIX: WHAT IF X GOES WRONG?

**What if the BFT Council API is too slow for a live demo?**
→ Pre-generate 20 responses and cache them. The demo shows "live debate" but loads from cache. 99% of users won't know the difference and the experience is instant.

**What if I can't get Stripe working in a day?**
→ Use Gumroad (takes 15 minutes to set up). Or use a simple "PayPal me $49" link for the first 10 sales. Perfect is the enemy of paid.

**What if nobody buys the $49 report?**
→ Lower it to $19. Or make it "pay what you want" with a $19 minimum. The first sale matters more than the price. You can raise prices later.

**What if the 48-hour build thread gets no engagement?**
→ The thread itself isn't the product — it's the forcing function. Even if only 100 people see it, you still shipped. Engagement is a bonus. Shipping is the goal.

**What if I can't build all of this in one week?**
→ Cut scope, not days. A working demo with email capture is the MVP. The $49 product can launch in week 2. The public build can happen in week 2. But Gap #2 (the decision) MUST happen Monday. Everything else follows from that.

**What if the MEOK.ai community is angry about hibernation?**
→ Frame it as "MEOK.ai's technology is now powering CSOAI.org's agents." It's not dead — it's deployed differently. The 12 civilizations become the backstory for why CSOAI's agents are more sophisticated than competitors'.
