Our mission

We believe that trust in AI should be verifiable, not declarative. Every claim about compliance should be backed by a cryptographic attestation that anyone can verify independently — without relying on screenshots, PDFs, or trust-me statements.

proofof.ai bridges the gap between regulatory frameworks and the MCP ecosystem. Our hive of 32 domain-specialised queens (MoE language models) maps every compliance obligation to machine-enforceable policies, continuously monitors MCP servers for evidence, and produces keystone-signed attestations that carry legal weight.

The result: compliance that's continuous, automated, and cryptographically verifiable — not a once-a-year checkbox exercise.

Why proofof.ai?

The name says it all. In cryptography, a proof is a statement you can verify without trusting the prover. That's the principle behind keystone attestations — you don't have to take our word that an AI system is compliant. The proof of AI compliance is in the cryptographic signature, verifiable by anyone, anywhere, at any time.
