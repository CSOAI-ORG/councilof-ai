Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about the compliance suite pricing.

Can I switch between plans at any time?
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time. Changes take effect immediately upon upgrade; downgrades apply at the start of the next billing cycle. No penalties, no lock-in.
What counts as an attestation?
An attestation is a signed cryptographic statement that an AI system or MCP server conforms to a given compliance framework. Each API call to /sign that produces a keystone certificate counts as one attestation. Verification lookups (/verify/{id}) are free and unlimited.
How does the 48h Assessment work?
You provide access to your AI systems and documentation. Our compliance engineers perform a full gap analysis across all 15+ frameworks and deliver a detailed report with a remediation roadmap within 48 hours. Includes a 30-day follow-up call to address any questions.
Is there a free trial for Pro or Enterprise?
Pro includes a 14-day free trial with full access — no credit card required. Enterprise trials are customised to your environment; contact our sales team to arrange a pilot with a dedicated compliance engineer.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept all major credit and debit cards, Stripe-linked bank transfers, and wire transfers for Enterprise plans. All prices are in GBP (£) and exclusive of VAT where applicable.
Do you offer discounts for startups or non-profits?
Yes. We offer a 40% discount for early-stage startups (< 20 employees, < £1M funding) and registered non-profits. Contact our sales team with proof of eligibility and we'll apply the discount to any plan.
Can I self-host the compliance suite?
Enterprise customers can deploy the full compliance suite on-premise or in their own VPC. This includes the keystone signing service, framework database, and verification portal. Contact sales for pricing and deployment options.
What's your refund policy?
We offer a 30-day money-back guarantee on all annual plans and a 7-day refund window on monthly plans. The 48h Assessment is non-refundable after delivery of the report, but we offer a free re-assessment if the gap analysis is inaccurate.
