◆ Frequently asked
What is an AI governance framework crosswalk?
A control-to-control map so one piece of evidence answers many regulations. CSOAI's holds 1,686 mappings across 26 frameworks — satisfy EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF and ISO 42001 from a single control set.
How is it verified?
Each mapping set is Ed25519-signed and hash-chained — an auditor verifies it offline in a browser, no account. Provenance is cryptographic, not a marketing claim.
Who maintains it?
CSOAI (Council for the Safety of AI), authored by Nicholas Templeman, MEOK AI Labs — v2.1, 2026.
