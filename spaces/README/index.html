Methods and limitations

What a result means

  • MEASURED means evidence exists for an axis; it does not establish a winner.
  • TIE means no separated lead was established. UNTESTED means separation was not established by a test.
  • UNMEASURED is a coverage gap. Missing information is never zero.

Model comparisons and deterministic source facts are different evidence types. Check scope, intake and withdrawal status before quoting a card.
