# Inverse MEOK: evidence-conditioned reasoning geometry — 25 November 2026
Research proposal and executed synthetic counterexamples. NOT real model activation measurements, BFT, a new GSPC axis, consciousness, verified human authority or deployed MEOK training.

## Architecture
Retain the two-path Council, Venturi memory, evidence-fabric, reaction bridge, component-join and existing GSPC instrument guard. Use 12 diverse specialist hypotheses, 1 deterministic evidence arbiter (or 3 separately scoped source/method/effect checks), and the 33 role registry as a slower audit/challenge lane, not 33 necessarily independent credentialed voters. BFT on signed distributed replicas is a different problem from correctness; the existing runtime truth gate and LOCAL_EXECUTOR_NOT_QUALIFIED hold remain.

Define a representation tuple: subject/model+tokenizer+adapter+runtime revisions, input commitment and task bank, layer/token/position/window, transformation and anchor revisions, raw tensor digest/shape/dtype, projection digest and loss, probe/causal intervention method and control, observed output/effect, evidence status and uncertainty. This is a research proposal, not a new canonical schema. Use existing evidence-event and dependency graph; do not store raw sensitive activations publicly. Venturi caches revision-bound validated derived features only; invalidations bind source, consent, model, projection and method revisions. J-Space visualization renders those derived observations with traceable source links, not a neural mind-reading claim. SovSpace rehearses matched with/without interventions; H.I.M. shows competing hypotheses and uncertainty; GSPC separately tests admitted tasks and calibrates the instrument. Learn MEOK only after rights, contamination, regression, independent test and protected review gates.

1,000 two-dimensional linear bottleneck fixtures; six known orthogonal rotations (0,30,60,90,120,180 degrees). Identical decisions at all angles. Known inverse anchor projection restored the original directions; 180° raw discrepancy was not a semantic difference. A nuisance coordinate decoded target labels with 91.1% accuracy but flipping it changed zero decisions. Flipping the causal feature changed every decision. This is a constructed counterexample, not a learned model result or proof of universal anchor alignment.

Council simulation: 10,000 tasks per condition, 8 conditions; 12 vs 33 votes, identical marginal voter competence 70%, common-mode mixture probability 0 or .5; reference observations 90% available, 90% correct conditional on availability; independent vs exact duplicate source. The majority and evidence gates trade wrong-output rate against coverage. Zero observed errors in finite fixtures is not guaranteed zero risk. Independence and competence were assumed by the simulator; real error dependence and unequal reliability must be measured. One or three deterministic checks can protect distinct invariants; a majority cannot override a failed hard requirement. This experiment's three checks are intentionally noisy binary source observations, NOT the proposed source/method/effect hard conjunction. Policy monotonicity only under fixed context/evidence ordering; missing, expired and revoked evidence may reduce permission. No autonomous weight update.

## Next experiments (unexecuted)
- Same open-weight model, different layers and prompt paraphrases; paired label-blind test, decode vs activation patching/ablation vs random directions. Measure effect size, localization, false probe discoveries and confidence intervals.
- Two small open-weight checkpoints: anchor-fitted orthogonal Procrustes / CKA / representational similarity analysis against shuffled-anchor and held-out task controls; test whether intervention effects transfer. Different tokenizers/layer widths need explicit alignment and loss. No universal neuron map.
- Compare 12 same-family, 12 diverse, 33 diverse, single calibrated referee, separate noisy observers and hard source/method/effect conjunction. Blind votes then debate vs no debate, minority escalation, equal token budget and latency; retain unknowns and independent verification.
- Venturi compression: raw vs FP16/int8/vector quantization vs SAE/low-rank projection; reconstruction, causal-effect fidelity, source invalidation, storage and privacy cost. No public raw activations.
- Train small MEOK outcome forecaster on licensed evidence and independent effect labels, compare retrieval-only baseline, process supervision, adapter tuning and constrained monotone risk head. Held-out GSPC banks are never training data. SovSpace counterfactuals are simulation until receiver confirms actual outcome.
- GSPC calibration and drift: matched tasks, exact provenance, exposure quarantine, preregistered negative controls, confidence intervals and cross-model family separation. Evidence that is not observable from closed APIs remains UNCHECKABLE at the neural level.
