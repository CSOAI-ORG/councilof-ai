**Q4 — other comments.** We offer one datapoint for the Bank's planned future work on disclosures, reporting and record-keeping.

We ran a rule-graded check on 16 stablecoin and tokenised-asset issuer accounts on the XRP Ledger (none sterling-denominated systemic stablecoins). The question: is third-party reserve-attestation language present on the issuer's own public page? The published result (https://councilof.ai/interop/financial-measure-run-reserve-attestation.json; `as_of` 2026-09-07T11:30:35Z; `content_id` be700eb070cd3cd5d49019d0f74cd3461ae2c6fe4341b862a046b497b07fa6b7) was:

- **PASS 3**: attestation language found
- **FAIL 4**: page reachable, no attestation language
- **UNCHECKABLE 9**: no issuer-declared page located or reachable

This measures the presence of language on a page, not the quality or adequacy of backing assets, which we do not measure. The point for future disclosure policy is narrow. For more than half of this small set, an outside reader could not check the disclosure from public sources at all. If the Bank sets supplementary disclosure requirements, it may wish to consider:

- a stable, published location for backing-asset disclosures;
- a date on each disclosure;
- a way for a holder to tell whether a disclosure was later changed, such as versioned documents or a published content hash.

That way "not disclosed" and "not findable" can be told apart.

We make no comment on any specific issuer.
