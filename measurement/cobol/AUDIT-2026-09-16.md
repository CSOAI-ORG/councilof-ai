result and nothing more. The cost of a real COBOL measurement is 13+ further frozen items
from a primary source, not compute.

`public/interop/cobol-measure.json` (12 institutions) records whether a public page was
reachable and its sha256. Its own honesty field says a homepage fetch is not a measurement

## A guard that could not fail, found while doing this

`harness/gspc-top100/check_bank_discriminates.py` graded **0 of 17** rows in this bank and
printed `ok ... empty parrot 0/0 = 0.0000`, because the bank's prompt field is `record` and
that name was not in `PROMPT_KEYS`. A checker that grades nothing and reports clean is the
defect this family of checks exists to catch. Fixed the same day: the key list now includes
`record`, `input`, `content`, `statement`, and a bank whose rows parse but grade zero is
UNCHECKABLE with exit 2. Proven with a failing control.
