What is sov.space?

sov.space is the sovereign marketplace for AI governance primitives. It is the consumer of the CSOAI Layer-0: a single destination where sovereign citizens, sovereign developers, and sovereign organisations can discover, install, and integrate every CSOAI protocol, MCP, and bridge — and where the MEOK OS, sovereign AI substrate, and any third-party fork, API, or A2A-protocol-compatible service can plug in.

The principles are simple:

  1. Sovereign by default: no vendor lock-in, no cloud extraction. The substrate runs on-device (Apple Silicon) + on the citizen's choice of sovereign compute (NVIDIA H200, GCP VM, on-prem).
  2. Open-source by default: every component is MIT-licensed. The substrate is the world's only major sovereign AI stack under MIT.
  3. Auditable by default: every action is SIGIL-signed Ed25519 + PQC ML-DSA-65. Every action is SIGIL-logged. Every sovereign citizen can verify the trail offline in any browser.
  4. Composable by default: the substrate is a substrate. Anyone can build a sovereign product, fork, or API on top — and we provide the marketplace where they can be discovered.
  5. Verified by default: every sovereign consumer on the marketplace has a Social Authority Badge derived from the SIGIL chain + the 33-agent BFT deliberation + the 554-comp OSCAL proof. The badge is a single line of HTML — and it is verifiable in any browser.
