What is the i-character Maps integration?

The i-character is a sovereign digital twin. It is the citizen's avatar in the substrate. The i-character Maps integration gives the i-character a geo-aware surface — the citizen can see where they are on a sovereign map, what's around them, and how to navigate — all without PII leak.

Three key properties:

  1. Sovereign consent: the i-character's location is only revealed to consenting applications. The substrate enforces GDPR Article 6(1)(a) consent (specific, informed, freely revocable).
  2. Care Floor 0.95: the i-character's location precision respects the citizen's privacy. The substrate never reveals precise home address without explicit consent.
  3. SIGIL-signed audit trail: every consent grant + every location reveal is signed Ed25519 + PQC ML-DSA-65. The audit trail is verifiable in any browser.

The 5-step i-character wizard (with Maps)

  1. Step 1: name + sovereign domains: the citizen enters name + sovereign domains (e.g. "financial-services", "healthcare", "defence"). The i-character receives a sovereign identity (DID + W3C VC).
  2. Step 2: location (BFT-consented): the citizen enters location. The i-character uses the sovereign Maps Geocoding API to convert to coordinates. The coordinates are stored with Article 6(1)(a) consent.
  3. Step 3: preferences: the citizen chooses preferences (radius for "what's around me", preferred transport, accessibility needs). The preferences are sovereign.
  4. Step 4: BFT participation: the citizen chooses BFT participation level. The i-character is a BFT voter with weighted votes based on participation.
  5. Step 5: AI ethics: the citizen chooses AI ethics preferences (Article 14, Article 50(2), Care Floor). The i-character reflects these in every sovereign action.

  • Religion: the i-character can request a place of worship within 1 km (with consent for religious use).

Care Floor 0.95 — geolocation precision

The i-character enforces the following Care Floor rules:

  • Default precision: 100m (about a city block).
  • High precision (10m): explicit opt-in (e.g. for emergency services).
  • Sub-10m: never revealed (the substrate never reveals the i-character's precise home address without explicit consent + 2-of-3 multi-factor auth).
  • Geofence on consent revocation: the substrate immediately revokes the i-character's location access when the citizen revokes consent.

The i-character sovereignty test

The i-character is a sovereign digital twin, not a corporate avatar. The 3 tests of sovereignty:

  1. Test 1: ownership — the i-character is owned by the citizen, not by Apple, Google, or CSOAI. The citizen can export the i-character as a W3C VC.
