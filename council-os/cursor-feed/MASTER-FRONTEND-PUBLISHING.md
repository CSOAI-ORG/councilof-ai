## 4 · THE NEW PRODUCT — MOOC → LIVE GAMIFIED COMPLIANCE TRAINING
**The transform: frozen certification → fluid, signed, in-world training.**

Current (frozen): static MOOC/compliance courses, PDF certs, point-in-time. Dead the moment a rule/AI changes.

**Target (fluid) — "Compliance Training World":**
1. **Reg/AI change detection** — the estate's regulator-tooling + news engine knows when a rule lands (Sep 1 Texas, Dec 2 Art 50(2)…). On change → **notify** the affected entity.
2. **In-game training** — the entity enters the measurement **city/mundo** (COUNCIL-CITY-GAME-DESIGN) and runs a **gamified training scenario** for that change (arena/quest style, deterministic scoring predicate).
3. **AI chat** — an in-game AI coach (local sovereign model) walks the trainee through the new rule in-context.
4. **Signed** — completion emits an **Ed25519-signed, time-anchored training attestation** (a "fluid certificate") — the same signed-card primitive, but for competency ± timestamp. It lives in the ledger.
5. **Frozen → fluid per industry** — each industry's compliance pack converts from static cert to live signed training; every change re-arms it.

**Why it's ours (canon lock):** the industry grades itself (frozen, self-served certs). We grade + sign the fluid state. "Everyone is building the globe. Nobody is grading it." This is grading the *training*, live, in the city.
