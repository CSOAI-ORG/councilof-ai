Compliance Training World

Compliance certification is frozen. The world changes. The certificate doesn't. Compliance Training World turns every static MOOC and paper cert into live, signed, gamified training inside our measurement city — re-armed automatically the moment a regulation or an AI changes.

Frozen → Fluid

01 · Detect

Rule change → notified

A new obligation lands (EU AI Act enforcement, Art 50(2), Texas portal, a CVSS-9.0 MCP hole, a model drift). The estate's regulator clocks + AI/news engine detect it and notify the affected entity.

02 · Enter

In-game training

The entity walks into the measurement city / mundo and runs a gamified training scenario built for that exact change — arena/quest style, a deterministic scoring predicate grades it.

03 · Coach

AI chat

An in-world AI coach (sovereign local model) explains the new rule in-context, answers questions, and rails the trainee to a provable pass, not a checkbox.

04 · Sign

Signed credential

Passing emits an Ed25519-signed, time-anchored training attestation — a fluid credential that proves competency *as of that moment* and lives in the claims-vs-evidence ledger.

05 · Re-arm

Fluid forever

Every industry's compliance pack is frozen once → fluid always. The next rule change re-arms the training and re-notifies. No more certs that expire into fiction.

06 · Publish

Insight → content

The whole loop auto-generates branded verdicts, cards, and videos for the site — what changed, who's affected, what it proves. See the insights engine.
