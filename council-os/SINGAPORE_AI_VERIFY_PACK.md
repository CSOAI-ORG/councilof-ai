## 1. Why Singapore AI Verify is the best fit

- **AI Verify** (IMDA + AI Verify Foundation, since June 2022) is a *voluntary* AI governance testing framework combining **technical tests** + **process checks** mapped to internationally recognised principles.
- The **Global AI Assurance Pilot** (Feb 2025) "codifies emerging norms and best practices around technical testing" — it is standing up a **third-party testing market**.
- Singapore is championing **ISO/IEC 42119-8** (announced 20 Apr 2026) to standardise GenAI testing methodology, including benchmarking and red-teaming.
- The GenAI framework emphasises **"content provenance such as digital watermarking and cryptographic provenance"** — our signed measurement spine is exactly this.
- The agentic AI framework (Jan 2026) requires "each agent to carry a verifiable digital identity and an audit trail" — our A2A signed-record stack is exactly this.

## 3. What we offer to the Assurance Pilot

1. **Signed measurement cards** — every test run emits an Ed25519-signed, hash-chained card; verifiable with the estate's published public key (SCITT-shaped statement + receipt, RFC 9943-aligned).
2. **Recomputable methodology** — every score is a deterministic predicate; a third party can rerun the harness and get the same verdict.
3. **Neutrality** — Council measures; it does not certify, does not train models on collected outcomes, and holds no auditor conflict (firewall doctrine).
4. **Already-running infrastructure** — 13 axes defined; 3 MEASURED (GovBench n=237, DefBench, MCPBench); arena live with 400+ signed rounds.
