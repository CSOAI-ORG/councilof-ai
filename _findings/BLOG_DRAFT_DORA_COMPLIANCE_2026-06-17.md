## What is DORA?

The Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA) applies to financial entities operating in the EU from 17 January 2025. It requires firms to ensure they can withstand, respond to, and recover from all types of ICT-related disruptions and threats.

UK firms serving EU customers are in scope. UK regulators are also aligning domestic rules with DORA principles.

## The five pillars

1. **ICT risk management** — governance, identification, and mitigation
2. **ICT-related incident management** — detection, reporting, and recovery
3. **Digital operational resilience testing** — threat-led penetration testing
4. **Managing third-party risk** — due diligence on ICT service providers
5. **Information sharing** — appropriate sharing of cyber threat intelligence

## Where AI systems fit in

AI is increasingly part of the ICT infrastructure that financial firms rely on. If your firm uses AI for:

- Credit decisions
- Fraud detection
- Trading algorithms
- Customer onboarding
- Anti-money laundering checks

Then DORA applies to those systems. You must be able to demonstrate resilience, incident response capability, and third-party oversight.
