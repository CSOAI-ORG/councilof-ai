# Blog Draft — R&D Tax Credits: The Most Overlooked Cash Source for UK AI Startups
**Target publication:** csoai.org/blog  
**Date:** 2026-06-17  

---

## Free money you are probably already entitled to

If you are a UK company building AI systems, there is a high chance you qualify for R&D tax credits. Many founders overlook this because "R&D" sounds like lab coats and particle accelerators. It is not.

For tax purposes, R&D is any project that seeks to achieve an advance in science or technology through the resolution of scientific or technological uncertainty.

Building a new AI governance framework? R&D. Designing a novel cryptographic attestation chain? R&D. Creating a BFT council for high-stakes compliance decisions? R&D.

## What can you claim?

For SMEs, the R&D tax credit scheme allows you to deduct an extra 86% of qualifying R&D spend from your taxable profits (from April 2023). If you are loss-making, you can surrender the loss for a cash credit worth up to 14.5% of the surrenderable loss.

In practical terms, for every £100 of qualifying R&D spend, a loss-making SME can receive up to £18.60 in cash.

## What counts as qualifying spend?

- Staff costs (salaries, NI, pension contributions) for people working directly on R&D
- Externally provided workers and subcontractors (with restrictions)
- Software and cloud costs used directly in R&D
- Consumable materials
- Clinical trial volunteers

## Common qualifying activities for AI companies

If your team does any of the following, you likely have a claim:

- Training custom machine-learning models
- Designing new architectures for inference, consensus, or verification
- Building bespoke evaluation or audit tooling
- Resolving uncertainty around model behaviour, fairness, or robustness
- Integrating emerging protocols (A2A, MCP, ACP, x402) in novel ways

## What does not count

Routine development, bug fixes, market research, and commercialisation activities generally do not qualify. The key test is uncertainty: did you genuinely not know whether the approach would work when you started?

## The documentation you need

You do not need a formal lab notebook, but you do need evidence:

- Technical specifications and architecture documents
- Git commit history showing iteration and experimentation
- Notes on failed approaches and why they failed
- Time records for R&D staff
- Contractor invoices and cloud receipts

## Our claim

At CSOAI, we are preparing an R&D tax credit claim for 2025–2026 covering work on Layer 0, sovereign-temple, and the 6-layer cryptographic disclosure stack. Estimated qualifying spend: £74,000. Estimated return: ~£24,420.

If you are a UK AI founder and you are not claiming R&D tax credits, you are leaving money on the table.
