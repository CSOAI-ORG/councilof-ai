## 3. Innovation

### The problem
AI agents are being deployed across healthcare, finance, public sector and critical infrastructure without a standardised trust layer. Existing protocols (A2A, MCP, ACP, x402) assume pre-verified identity and policy compliance. There is no open, sovereign, runtime-enforceable Layer 0.

### Our solution
- **did:csoai** — W3C DID v1.1 compliant decentralised identity for agents
- **Ed25519-signed Watchdog Certificates** — runtime-verifiable compliance attestations
- **6-layer cryptographic disclosure** — SHA-3/512, HMAC, Ed25519, Bitcoin OTS, C2PA, hash-chained audit
- **BFT sovereign-temple council** — 22/33 supermajority governance for high-stakes decisions
- **x402/Stripe ACP compliance pre-check tunnel** — verify before transaction execute

### Why it is innovative
- First open-source, sovereign trust layer designed specifically for agent-to-agent protocols
- 60× price gap vs incumbent governance platforms (£199/mo vs £30K+ enterprise contracts)
- Multi-regulation coverage (EU AI Act, NIS2, DORA, CRA, GDPR, UK AI Bill, ISO 42001) in a single API
- Real-time attestation with public verify endpoints (no login required)
