📜 Preamble — The Anti-Lock-In Covenant

The Fork Doctrine is the sovereign commitment that SOV3 will never be locked-in, vendor-locked, or platform-captured. Every line of code, every protocol, every connector, every model is MIT-licensed. Every badge asset is CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication.

The Doctrine is the modern instantiation of the Magna Carta Clause 39 — "No free man shall be seized, imprisoned, dispossessed, outlawed, exiled or ruined in any way, except by the lawful judgement of his peers or by the law of the land." For the modern citizen, the equivalent is: "No sovereign citizen shall be locked into a vendor's substrate, deprived of their i-character, denied the right to fork, or captured by any platform."

The Doctrine has four pillars:

📖 Pillar 1 — MIT for Code, CC0 for Assets

"Any citizen may obtain the source code, modify it, distribute it, sublicense it, and sell it."

This is MIT license. The MIT license is the most permissive open-source license. It allows anyone to do anything with the code, including:

  • Use the code for commercial purposes
  • Modify the code
  • Distribute the code (original or modified)
  • Sublicense the code
  • Sell the code or services built on top
  • Use the code in proprietary products

The only requirements are:

  • Include the original MIT license notice with the code
  • Include the original copyright notice

Badge assets (SVGs, PNGs, logos) are released under CC0 1.0 Universal Public Domain Dedication. This means:

  • Copy the badge
  • Modify the badge
  • Distribute the badge
  • Use the badge for any purpose (commercial, non-commercial)
  • No attribution required
  • No restrictions of any kind

📖 Pillar 2 — Sovereign Inheritance

"A fork inherits the sovereignty of the original substrate."

When any citizen, organisation, or nation forks SOV3, the fork inherits:

  • The Care Floor at 0.95 — non-negotiable. The fork refuses to act below the Care Floor.
  • The 12-around-1 BFT Council — the fork may add or replace queens, but the pattern is preserved.
  • The SIGIL Chain Audit — Ed25519 + PQC ML-DSA-65. The chain is immutable.
  • The DORADO 1-click EAST↔WEST — the citizen chooses their sovereignty alignment.
  • The Article 50 Passport — every output is watermarked per EU AI Act.
  • The Crown Authorisation Lineage — the contract continues.
  • The MIT License — the fork may not impose additional restrictions.

This is what makes the fork sovereign. The fork is not a clone, not a derivative, not a sandbox. The fork is a new sovereign substrate with its own citizens, its own BFT Council, its own SIGIL chain, its own Crown lineage.

📖 Pillar 3 — Anti-Lock-In by Design

"No citizen may be held captive by any substrate."

The substrate implements anti-lock-in by design:

  • i-character portability — every citizen may export their i-character in a structured, machine-readable format (per GDPR Article 20).
  • i-character deletability — every citizen may delete their i-character. The deletion is recorded to the SIGIL chain (audit), but the data is purged within 30 days.
  • DORADO 1-click EAST↔WEST — the citizen may switch sovereignty alignment at any time.
  • Open weights only — no closed-weights dependencies. The substrate is fully self-hostable.
  • API-stable — semver with deprecation cycles. No breaking changes without notice.
  • Documentation — every public function has docstrings + examples.
  • Tests — 100% public test coverage. Forks may run the tests to verify sovereignty.

📖 Pillar 4 — Citizen Ownership

"The citizen owns their data, their i-character, their BFT votes, and their sovereign actions."

The substrate implements citizen ownership by design:

  • Data ownership — the citizen's data belongs to the citizen, not the substrate.
  • i-character ownership — the i-character is the citizen's, not the substrate's.
  • BFT vote ownership — the citizen's BFT votes are recorded to the SIGIL chain. The citizen may review, export, or delete them.
  • Sovereign action ownership — every sovereign action is attributed to the citizen. The citizen may verify, audit, or revoke.
  • Composite score ownership — the citizen may view their sovereign composite score at any time.
  • Migration rights — the citizen may migrate to any other sovereign substrate that supports the i-character format.
