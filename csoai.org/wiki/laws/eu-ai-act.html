The Act

The EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689) entered into force 1 Aug 2024. The first comprehensive horizontal AI regulation in the world. 113 articles + 13 annexes.

Key Annexes

  • Annex I — High-risk AI systems (8 categories).
  • Annex III — High-risk use cases (8 categories).
  • Annex IV — Technical documentation.
  • Annex V — Post-market monitoring.

Key Articles

  • Art 5 — Prohibited practices.
  • Art 6 & 7 — High-risk classification.
  • Art 9-15 — High-risk requirements (data, transparency, oversight, accuracy, robustness, cybersecurity).
  • Art 50 — Transparency obligations (2 Aug 2026).
  • Art 51+ — GPAI obligations.

Penalties

Up to €35M or 7% of global turnover for prohibited-practice violations. €15M or 3% for most other violations.
