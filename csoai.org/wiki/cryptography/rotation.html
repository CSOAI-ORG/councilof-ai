90-Day Key Rotation

PQC rotation cycle — defensive depth over cryptographic permanence.

The 90-Day Cycle

Every 90 days, the Anchor rotates its keys: a fresh Ed25519 keypair generated, dual-signed with ML-DSA-65, and the old key flagged for retirement. The mapping is published on the SIGIL chain.

Schedule

  • Day 0: New key generated inside Citadel.
  • Day 0: Old key signs a SIGIL announcing the rotation.
  • Day 0: New key signs a SIGIL accepting the rotation.
  • Day 90: Old key expires.
  • Day 180: Old key is destroyed.

Why 90 Days?

NIST SP 800-57 Part 1 Rev. 5 recommends ≤ 1 year for signing keys; CSOAI-ORG chose 90 days for defensive depth. Shorter rotations minimise the damage from a leaked key.

The Encapsulation Step

ML-KEM-768 encapsulates the new key to the next Anchor during handover.
