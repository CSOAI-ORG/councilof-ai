What Citadel Is

Citadel is a Linux container hardening profile for sovereign AI: no outbound network, read-only rootfs, signed-only ingress, every action mediated by the BFT Council. See the Anchor for the keymaster.

What It Is NOT

Citadel is not a fully-verified microkernel. It is a pragmatic hardening layer built on top of standard seccomp + namespaces + capability dropping.

How It Is Built

  • SOPS + age + Sigstore for secrets.
  • Cosign for image signing.
  • Kyverno for policy.
  • CIS v8 baseline for OS. See CIS v8.
  • ISO 27001 + ISO 42001 management layer. See ISO 42001.

Why "Citadel"?

A citadel is a fortress inside a city. The city is the realm; the citadel is the AI; what lives inside is sovereign.

Citadel + Anchor

The Citadel is the wall; the Anchor is the locked chamber inside. Together they make a system whose keys cannot leak even if the wall is pierced.
