What proofof.ai Is

proofof.ai is the public, read-only mirror of the sovereign SIGIL chain. Anyone can verify any emission from any browser. No login. No third-party trust.

API

  • /v1/sigil/events — paginated events.
  • /v1/sigil/single?sigil=... — one event.
  • /v1/sigil/stats — counts.
  • /v1/sigil/export — CSV/JSON/Parquet for auditors.

Why Public?

A private SIGIL is a foreign-controlled system. The Axiom 2 (Audit-by-default) requires public verifiability. A regulator, a customer, a competitor — anyone can prove the chain.

Tiers

  • Free — HMAC-signed (verifiable on proofof.ai).
  • Pro — Ed25519-signed (auditor-grade attestation).
  • Governance — dual-signed Ed25519 + ML-DSA-65.
