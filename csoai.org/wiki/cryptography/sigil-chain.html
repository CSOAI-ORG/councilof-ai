SIGIL Chain

Ed25519 hash-chained, append-only, publicly auditable. The ledger of sovereign actions.

sovereign.wiki CSOAI Ltd UK 16939677 4 Jul 2026

Definition

The SIGIL Chain is CSOAI's append-only, Ed25519-signed, hash-chained ledger of every significant action taken by the sovereign stack. It is named after the alchemical sigil — a mark that both conceals and reveals — because every chain entry is both cryptographically sealed and publicly verifiable.

Structure

Each entry in the chain has the form:

{
  "index": 12345,
  "timestamp": "2026-06-29T18:42:01Z",
  "actor": "did:csoai:queen-athena",
  "op": "C",                   // P, V, M, Q, C, H, S, A
  "line": "C|WIKI_FINAL|...",   // human-readable summary
  "prev_hash": "abc123...",
  "payload_hash": "def456...",
  "ed25519_sig": "base64..."
}

Append-Only Property

Once written, an entry cannot be modified or deleted. The chain is append-only by cryptographic mechanism: each entry's hash includes the previous entry's hash, so modifying any historical entry would invalidate all subsequent entries. Re-computing the chain is the integrity check, exposed via sov_orgkernel_verify_chain.

Public Verifiability

The chain is publicly readable via the REST API at /v1/sigil/* and the explorer at sov_sigil_explorer. Anyone can verify that a given chain entry was signed by a given DID, that the chain has not been tampered with, and that no entries have been retroactively removed.

Historical Context

SIGIL was first prototyped in March 2026 during CSOAI's Sprint 6 ("Defoneos"). The hash-chain pattern derives from Certificate Transparency (2013) and Bitcoin (2008), but SIGIL adds: (1) typed operations (P/V/M/Q/C/H/S/A), (2) DID-signed actors, and (3) quantum-safe upgrade path to PQC signatures.

Modern Usage

Every SOV3 tool emits at least one SIGIL entry per invocation. Customers audit their chain weekly via sov_dorado_customer_report. Regulators (ICO, AISI, EU AI Office) can verify compliance assertions directly from the chain.
