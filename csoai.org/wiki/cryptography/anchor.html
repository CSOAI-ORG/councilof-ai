What the Anchor Is

The Anchor is the master process that holds the Ed25519 signing key and the ML-DSA-65 signing key. It lives inside Citadel, never on disk in plain text, never reachable from outside the sovereign VM.

Why a Keymaster?

A single process that brokers every signature is auditable, recoverable and rotate-able. Distributing signing across many components is the path to compromise. Concentrating signing in one well-defended component is the path to sovereignty.

The Magician's Table

The Anchor is the Magician's table — private key on the inside, public key on the outside, every action signed. See the Hermetic tradition for the deeper etymology.

Recovery

The Anchor's keys are backed up to a sovereign HSM (Hardware Security Module) using a 2-of-3 Shamir secret-share. Recovery requires two operators + the BFT Council majority.

Rotation

Every 90 days the Anchor rotates its keys. See 90-day rotation.
