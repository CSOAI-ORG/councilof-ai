Big Braim

8 category-winning sovereign models wrapped into one brain.

sovereign.wiki CSOAI Ltd UK 16939677 4 Jul 2026

Definition

Big Braim is CSOAI's ensemble brain: a federation of 8 category-winning open-source models, each picked as the sovereign stack's best-in-class for a specific capability. The models are wrapped in SIGIL and routed through the MoE-64 (see MoE-64) gating mechanism.

Routing Logic

When the OOWM Sandwich receives a query, the router (Qwen2.5-3B) classifies it into one of 8 categories (plus an "unclear" bucket that goes to DeepSeek-R1). The chosen category model executes the query. The output is wrapped in a SIGIL event and returned.

Why 8?

8 is the minimum number that covers the capability matrix: fewer and the model is too general (poor at edge cases); more and the routing overhead exceeds the specialisation gain. CSOAI's benchmarking across 384 mixed simulations (the sov_brain_race_run) confirms 8 as the sweet spot.

Historical Context

Big Braim inherits from "model routing" research (2023) and the "mixture of all models" pattern (2024). CSOAI's contribution is the sovereign wrapping: every model in Big Braim is open-source, every inference is SIGIL-emitted, every model swap requires council ratification.

Modern Usage

Customers invoke Big Braim via sov_big_braim_route(query). The tool returns the response, the chosen category, and the SIGIL receipt. The sov_big_braim_status tool reports per-category health and latency.

Limitations

Big Braim does not handle: (1) multimodal perception (use MOM directly for that), (2) council-level deliberation (use the council tools), (3) intuition (use the Intuition Engine — see Intuition Engine). For these, Big Braim delegates to the appropriate layer.
