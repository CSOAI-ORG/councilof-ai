OOWM Anchor

The cryptographic anchor that holds the OOWM Sandwich to the SIGIL chain.

sovereign.wiki CSOAI Ltd UK 16939677 4 Jul 2026

Definition

The OOWM Anchor is the cryptographic linkage between the OOWM Sandwich's output and the SIGIL hash chain. Every inference performed by the Sandwich produces an Anchor: a tuple of (input hash, Mamba state, MoE active experts, MOM perception, SOV3 ruling, output hash, Ed25519 signature).

Purpose

Without the Anchor, the OOWM Sandwich would be a powerful but unauditable system. With it, every output is traceable to its inputs, every state transition is signed, and any party can verify that a given inference actually occurred as claimed. The Anchor is what makes the Sandwich sovereign rather than just capable.

Structure

{
  "anchor_id": "uuid",
  "input_hash": "sha256...",
  "mamba_state": [0.12, -0.45, 0.78, ...],  // 16 dims
  "moe_active": [3, 17, 42, 58],             // 4 of 64
  "mom_perception": {"jurisdiction": "UK", "domain": "legal", ...},
  "sov3_ruling": "ALLOW",
  "output_hash": "sha256...",
  "ed25519_sig": "base64...",
  "prev_anchor_hash": "sha256..."
}

Hash Chain Property

Each Anchor includes the previous Anchor's hash, making the chain tamper-evident: modifying any historical Anchor invalidates all subsequent Anchors. The chain is append-only and publicly verifiable via sov_sigil_explorer.

Historical Context

The Anchor concept derives from Certificate Transparency (CT) logs (Google, 2013). CT logs are append-only, hash-chained, publicly auditable registries of TLS certificates. CSOAI adapts the pattern to AI inference: every AI output is treated as a kind of certificate, and the Anchor is its CT-log entry.

Modern Usage

Customers query the Anchor chain for audit purposes (regulator inspections), for incident response (what did the model do at 02:00 UTC?), and for provenance (where did this output come from?). The sov_orgkernel_verify_chain tool re-computes the hash chain to verify integrity.

Privacy Considerations

Anchors contain hashes, not plaintext. The input and output hashes are one-way: you cannot recover the original text from the hash. The Mamba state and MoE active experts are diagnostic metadata but do not leak user data. Customers with strict privacy requirements can opt for hashed-anchor mode where the state is also hashed.
