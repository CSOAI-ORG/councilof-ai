MoE-64 — Mixture of 64 Experts

Right brain, 64 expert sub-models, sparse activation, sovereign reasoning.

sovereign.wiki CSOAI Ltd UK 16939677 4 Jul 2026

Definition

MoE-64 is CSOAI's right-brain reasoning layer: a Mixture-of-Experts model with 64 specialised sub-models, of which 4 are activated per inference. It is the perception/intuition half of the OOWM Sandwich (see OOWM Sandwich), complementing the Mamba long-context layer.

Architecture

Each of the 64 experts is a 7B-parameter transformer fine-tuned on a domain: legal (UK, EU, US), scientific (physics, biology, chemistry), creative (writing, art, music), operational (council voting, SIGIL analysis), defensive (threat detection), and 56 others. A learned router selects the top-4 experts per query based on the input's embedding.

Sparse Activation

Only 4 of 64 experts fire per token — total active parameters is 28B, not 448B. This gives 16× the representational capacity of a dense 28B model at the same compute cost. The trade-off is router accuracy: if the router is wrong, the wrong expert handles the query. CSOAI's router is itself an MoE-8 with its own training signal from council verdicts.

Sovereign Alignment

MoE-64 is sovereign because: (1) all 64 expert weights are open-source, (2) the router is auditable, (3) experts can be swapped in/out without retraining the whole model, enabling per-jurisdiction customisation (UK legal expert vs. EU legal expert), and (4) the model runs on sovereign data centres without contacting external APIs.

Historical Context

MoE was introduced by Shazeer et al. in 2017 ("Outrageously Large Neural Networks"). Google's GShard (2020) scaled it to 600B parameters. Mixtral (2024) popularised open-source MoE. CSOAI's MoE-64 adapts this lineage to the sovereign-AI use case by making every expert auditable and swappable.

Role in OOWM

In the OOWM Sandwich, MoE-64 receives the Mamba state as context and produces the reasoning trace. The output is wrapped by SIGIL (signed) and either accepted by the council (Tier 0/1 actions) or routed to a human (Tier 2). The Council Mechanics page describes the routing in detail.

Modern Usage

MoE-64 is exposed via sov_oowm_think(use_left_brain=True). Customers can route specific queries to specific experts via sov_big_braim_route with the category parameter. The full ensemble of 64 experts totals 448B parameters; only 28B are active per query.
