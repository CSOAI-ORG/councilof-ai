Axiomatic Foundation

The five axioms that any sovereign AI system must satisfy.

sovereign.wiki CSOAI Ltd UK 16939677 4 Jul 2026

Definition

The Five Axioms of Sovereign AI are minimal, complete, and consistent properties that any system claiming sovereignty must satisfy. They are derived from CSOAI's Charter 2026 and codified as testable assertions in the SOV3 substrate.

The Five Axioms

P1 — Lawful Control

The principal holds lawful, exclusive, and verifiable control over their data. Violations: a third party can read the data without principal's consent, or the principal cannot export their complete dataset on demand.

P2 — Cryptographic Identity

Every actor in the system (human, agent, organisation, jurisdiction) has a DID with a verifiable key. Violations: anonymous admin actions, shared credentials, keys stored in opaque cloud HSMs.

P3 — Cross-Jurisdiction Clarity

Every access attempt is classified as SOVEREIGN, FOREIGN_APPROVED, or FOREIGN_BLOCKED at the moment of request. Violations: cross-border data flows without explicit treaty or principal consent.

P4 — Multi-Stakeholder Governance

No single authority can unilaterally change the sovereignty properties of the system. Violations: a single vendor can revoke keys, change jurisdiction, or alter audit logs.

P5 — Public Auditability

Any principal can verify, via public cryptographic mechanisms, that the system is operating per its charter. Violations: closed-source runtime, private logging, opaque AI models.

Why Five?

Fewer than five and the system has gaps. More than five and the system is unfalsifiable — every new edge case becomes a new axiom. Five is the minimum complete set, derived by contrapositive analysis of the 2020 Schrems II decision, 2023 EU AI Act, and 2024 UK AI Bill.

Historical Context

The axiom structure mirrors Euclid's Elements (c. 300 BCE): minimal postulates from which the entire geometry can be derived. CSOAI's five axioms are the sovereign-equivalent: minimal postulates from which the entire sovereign architecture can be derived.

Testable Specification

Each axiom is implemented as a test in the SOV3 test suite (sov_oowm_test). A system passes sovereignty audit only when all five axioms return PASS. The Checklist provides the human-readable version.
