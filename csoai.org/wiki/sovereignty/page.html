How They Form a Loop

  1. Principle states the axiom: lawful control belongs to the principal.
  2. Property enumerates what a sovereign system must own (data, keys, compute, jurisdiction).
  3. Foreign vs Sovereign gives the operational test: is this access pattern foreign or sovereign?
  4. Sovereign Stack names the layers: substrate → model → governance → interface.
  5. Axiomatic extracts the five axioms (P1–P5) that any sovereign system must satisfy.
  6. Promise vs Property argues that auditability (property) beats promises (SLAs).
  7. Specification formalises each axiom as a testable specification.
  8. Checklist gives a yes/no list for self-assessment.
  9. Methodology walks through building a sovereign system from scratch.
  10. Page (this entry) anchors them in the broader wiki.

Reading Order

For first contact: Principle → Axiomatic → Specification. For builders: Methodology → Checklist → Sovereign Stack. For regulators: Promise vs Property → EU AI Act → Foreign vs Sovereign.
