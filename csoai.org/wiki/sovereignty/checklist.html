Sovereign Checklist

Self-assessment: is your AI deployment actually sovereign?

sovereign.wiki CSOAI Ltd UK 16939677 4 Jul 2026

Definition

The Sovereign Checklist is the human-readable companion to the Specification. It translates each assertion into a yes/no question that a CTO, CISO, or DPO can answer in 30 minutes.

Section 1 — Lawful Control (P1)

  • ☐ Can we export the complete customer dataset in a portable format within 30 days?
  • ☐ Can we honour right-to-be-forgotten within 30 days with cryptographic proof?
  • ☐ Are all data bytes hash-chained to an auditable ledger?
  • ☐ Can customers read their own access logs in real time?

Section 2 — Cryptographic Identity (P2)

  • ☐ Does every actor (human, agent, org) have a W3C DID?
  • ☐ Are all Ed25519 keys generated on the principal's device, never exported?
  • ☐ Are credentials unique per actor (no shared admins)?
  • ☐ Is there a documented path to post-quantum keys (ML-DSA-65, ML-KEM-768)?

Section 3 — Cross-Jurisdiction Clarity (P3)

  • ☐ Does every access attempt return a SOVEREIGN / FOREIGN_APPROVED / FOREIGN_BLOCKED verdict within 100ms?
  • ☐ Is the treaty database refreshed daily from authoritative sources?
  • ☐ Are foreign-blocked attempts observable in a dashboard?
  • ☐ Does the system recognise the eight sovereign realms (UK, USA, EU, CA, AU, NZ, JP, AUKUS)?

Section 4 — Multi-Stakeholder Governance (P4)

  • ☐ Do sovereignty-changing actions require ≥2/3 council majority?
  • ☐ Can any single admin revoke keys, change jurisdiction, or alter audit logs?
  • ☐ Are council members' DIDs public, and are votes cryptographically recorded?
  • ☐ Does the system tolerate Byzantine faults (e.g., up to 4 of 13 nodes malicious)?

Section 5 — Public Auditability (P5)

  • ☐ Is the audit chain publicly verifiable via API?
  • ☐ Are model weights open-source?
  • ☐ Can audit logs be exported in CSV / JSON / Parquet?
  • ☐ Is default retention ≥90 days, configurable by the principal?

Scoring

Yes answersVerdict
20/20Fully sovereign — pass all CSOAI audits
15–19Mostly sovereign — close gaps within 90 days
10–14Partially sovereign — significant gaps, multi-quarter remediation
<10Not sovereign — do not market as sovereign

How to Use This Checklist

Run the checklist at quarterly governance reviews. Document each "yes" with the mechanism that enforces it (not just the promise). Where the answer is "no", identify the gap and the responsible owner.

What It Doesn't Cover

The checklist does not cover legal interpretation — sovereignty in law differs across jurisdictions. It also does not cover cost — a sovereign system costs more than a non-sovereign one. It does, however, cover the technical properties that any sovereign claim must rest on.
