Key findings from 60 user-need scenarios

Finding 2: Defence Intel = Sovereign Moat

DEFONEOS sovereign engineers score 5.0 on Defence Intel. Civilians score 1.0. Two distinct UIs needed: civic and classified. SOV3 is the only sovereign supplier that scores 5.0 on both pillars.

Boomers score 1.0. Gen-Z scores 5.0. Largest gap. Solution: 5-step wizard (1. Hello 2. Choose arcana 3. Pick queen model 4. Get i-character 5. Explore). With large fonts + screen reader.

Finding 4: Article 50 = Unique Selling Point

SOV3 is the only vendor that scores 5.0 across ALL 10 demographics on Article 50 (where tested). Only competitor with 36 days countdown. Only vendor with Omnibus-delay intel built in. Sales pitch: "Aug 2 deadline? Already done."

Gen-Z scores 5.0/5.0. They will refer friends. Cheap CAC. Solution: Anonymous + Public TwinStore listing. Make i-characters shareable on social.
