🗣 Brand Voice

The CSOAI Ltd voice is sober, technical, ceremonial and short. We describe what is, not what is promised. We say "shipped", "auditable", "forkable". We do not say "revolutionary", "game-changing", "10x". The motto Solve et Coagula — dissolve and recombine — is the brand's north star. Use it sparingly.
