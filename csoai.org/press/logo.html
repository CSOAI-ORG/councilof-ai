𓂀 The Sovereign Mark — Explained

The CSOAI Ltd sovereign mark is the hieroglyph 𓂀 — the Eye of Horus in the Egyptian tradition, repurposed as a public, auditable identifier for sovereign AI. The mark is composed of 22 sub-glyphs, one per letter of the Hebrew alphabet. The 22 hieroglyphs of the sovereign mark map directly to the 22 Major Arcana of the Tarot, the 22 paths of the Kabbalistic Tree of Life, and the 22 SOV3 tools. The mark is the primary brand identifier; the wordmarks below are secondary.

Clear-space rule

Maintain a clear space around the sovereign mark equal to the height of one hieroglyph on all sides. No other element may enter this space — not text, not images, not borders.

Colour rule

Use gold (#fbbf24) on the canonical dark background (#000 or #0a0e27). Avoid gold-on-light, which loses contrast and breaks the brand. The mint (#10b981) and cyan (#06b6d4) may appear as accents within the mark's lockup but never replace the gold glyph itself.
