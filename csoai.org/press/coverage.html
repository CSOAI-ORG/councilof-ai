❓ Top Journalist Questions

"How is SOV3 different from Llama or DeepSeek?"

SOV3 is a substrate — a sovereign operating system — not a foundation model. It runs Llama, DeepSeek, Qwen3, or any open-weights model, but adds: BFT council governance, Article 50 watermarking, Care Floor monitoring, SIGIL chain audit, and Fork Doctrine guarantees. We don't compete with Meta or DeepSeek on the model. We compete on the substrate.

"Why UK? Why not US?"

Three reasons: (1) UK has a robust Crown lineage for sovereign authorisation. (2) UK has strong data protection laws (UK GDPR + Data Protection Act 2018). (3) UK has Five Eyes partnerships, Commonwealth ties, and a real defence procurement budget. We're not anti-US — we're pro-sovereign.

"What about OpenAI/Anthropic?"

They're great at building models. We're great at giving organisations a sovereign way to deploy them. We use Anthropic's Claude, OpenAI's GPT, and many others as substrate brains — the sovereign layer wraps them with consent, audit, and Fork Doctrine.

"Who funds you? Why not Big Tech?"

Khosla Ventures led our seed (£1.6M), with Index Ventures (£800K) and Connect Ventures (£400K). We deliberately avoided Big Tech strategic capital to maintain independence. Crown Agents (£200K) provided strategic alignment for our Five Eyes/Commonwealth work.
