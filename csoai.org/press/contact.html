📋 What to Include in Your First Email

To get a fast response, please include:

  1. Your name, outlet, and beat (e.g., "Mark Bergen, FT, AI/tech")
  2. Deadline (date and time of publication/broadcast)
  3. Angle (what story are you working on?)
  4. Format (interview, quote, fact-check, embargoed briefing?)
  5. Duration (15-min call, 60-min podcast, written Q&A?)
  6. Embargo status (are you willing to honour embargo?)

If you are from a major national outlet (BBC, FT, NYT, Wired, Bloomberg, Reuters, AP) and need a same-day response, mention that in your subject line.
