👤 Full Bio

Paragraph 1 — Who

Nicholas Templeman is the founder and director of CSOAI Ltd, a UK-registered company (Company No. 16939677) building sovereign, auditable and forkable AI infrastructure. Founded in 2026 and headquartered in the United Kingdom, CSOAI Ltd operates under a bootstrapped model with no external investors and no foreign data residency. Templeman is the sole director and the architect of the company's flagship product, the SOV3 sovereign substrate, which launches publicly on Saturday 4 July 2026 at 09:00 BST.

Paragraph 2 — What

The SOV3 substrate is the first sovereign-by-design AI substrate: public (source visible), auditable (every decision hash-chained), sovereign (operator-owned end-to-end), forkable (anyone may copy and run it) and free (the Citizen tier costs nothing). It ships with 5 alchemical layers (Nigredo, Albedo, Citrinitas, Rubedo, Philosopher's Stone), 309 MCP tools, a composite sovereign score of 7.305, a Care Floor of 0.95, and a BFT 12-around-1 governance council. Templeman designed SOV3 around three principles: Solve et Coagula (dissolve, then recombine), the Crown Authorisation lineage (1795–2026), and the strict separation of substrate from operator.

Paragraph 3 — How

Templeman's expertise lies at the intersection of sovereign AI infrastructure, open-source licensing (MIT, CC0, OSI-aligned) and ceremonial governance models. He has written and spoken extensively on the Crown Authorisation lineage — the doctrine by which, since 1795, the British Crown has set technical standards in the public interest — and its modern translation into AI governance. His work draws on Byzantine Fault Tolerant consensus, Mamba-2 state-space modelling, and the alchemical tradition as a metaphor for layered, defensible AI systems. He aligns every CSOAI Ltd release with MIT for code, CC0 for badge assets, and OSI principles for community governance.

Paragraph 4 — Why

Templeman founded CSOAI Ltd because, in his words, "AI is infrastructure — and infrastructure must be sovereign." He is concerned that the AI capability of the next decade will be concentrated in a handful of US frontier labs and a small number of state-aligned Chinese models, leaving sovereign jurisdictions with no defensible alternative. CSOAI Ltd's answer is to ship an open substrate that any operator — government, university, defence force, hospital or citizen — can run on their own hardware, in their own jurisdiction, under their own governance. The release on 4 July 2026 absorbs 60+ sovereign charters, 22 open protocols (MCP, A2A, x402, DID, JWT, ANP, AGNTCY, IBC, OIDC, WebSocket, gRPC and others) and 135 wiki entries into a single navigable substrate.
