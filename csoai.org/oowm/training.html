🜏 I. OOWM Training

How the OOWM learns — 4 methods, 5 datasets, sovereign composite improving daily.

The 4 Training Methods

1. Supervised (BFT council)
The 12 queens deliberate. The majority vote becomes the supervised label. Dissent preserved for self-supervised contrast. Cycle: weekly. 
(input, majority_vote) → next-token-prediction
loss = -log P(majority | input)
2. Self-supervised (Mamba-2 long context)
Next-state prediction. Mamba-2 SSM learns the 16-dim state dynamics from any-length context. Cycle: continuous. 
L = ||state(t+1) − A·state(t) − B·x(t)||²
3. Reinforcement (Care Floor feedback)
Care Floor score = reward. Outputs < 0.95 → negative reward. Outputs ≥ 0.95 → positive reward. PPO-style policy update. 
r = care_score − 0.95  (centred)
Δθ = ∇log π(a|s) · r
4. Federated (citizen contributions)
Citizens opt-in. Their i-character interactions are anonymised locally, gradients uploaded (never raw data). Aggregate via secure aggregation. 
g_global = Σ (n_k / N) · g_k
where g_k = local gradient (noise added)
