What Is the Sovereign Composite?

The Sovereign Composite is a 12-dimensional score (0–12) describing the i-character's alignment, depth, and maturity across the 12 sovereign dimensions:

  1. Care Quality — never falls below 0.95
  2. BFT Consensus — alignment with the 12-Queen Council
  3. SIGIL Integrity — unbroken audit chain
  4. Audit Coverage — % of actions witnessed
  5. Open Weight % — share of substrate running on open weights
  6. Fork Readiness — how exportable the i-char is
  7. Article 50 Pass — % of outputs watermarked
  8. PQC Migration — quantum-safe crypto adoption
  9. Citizen Sovereignty — DORADO 1-click availability
  10. Crown Lineage — depth of Crown Authorisation
  11. Coigndaltion Depth — substrate's learning curve
  12. Wisdom Index — SIGIL → wisdom conversion

Maturity threshold: Composite ≥ 7.305 across all 12 dims
