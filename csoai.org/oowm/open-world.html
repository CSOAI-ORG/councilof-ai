Licence Sovereignty

Every feed ingested under one of:

  • OGL-UK-3.0 — Open Government Licence (UK Crown)
  • CC0 — Creative Commons Zero (public domain)
  • MIT — Code corpora (GitHub)
  • Apache 2.0 — Open weights, open data
  • Open — Crown Authorisation, EU Digital Omnibus, public sector

Citizen can: opt-in, opt-out, or customise the ingestion list per feed. Defaults are sovereign + open.

# mcp_federation_search + mcp_bridge_call → ingest
sov_ingest_nightly():

    data = mcp_bridge_call(feed.server, feed.tool, feed.args)
    sigil_digest = sigil_emit("Q|FEED|" + feed.name + "|" + len(data))

  composite_recompute()
