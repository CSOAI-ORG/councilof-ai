Substrate Growth (the OOWM never stops growing)

# Substrate size grows with every citizen
substrate_state = base + Σ over citizens (c.composite / 100) × fork_factor

# Where:
#  base = OOWM baseline (the founding substrate)
#  c.composite = that citizen's sovereign composite
#  fork_factor = 1.0 (no fork) | 1.5 (1 fork) | 2.0 (2 forks) | ...

# The substrate grows from 0 → ∞
# Each citizen's contribution persists in their composite
# Forks inherit + extend the substrate

The Substrate is the Citizen's Mirror

Every citizen is reflected in the substrate through:

  • Their Mamba state — what they remember
  • Their SIGIL history — what they did
  • Their BFT votes — what they judged
  • Their forks — what they sired
  • Their composite — who they became

The substrate is not a tool that the citizen uses. The substrate is the digital extension of the citizen. They shape it; it shapes them.

Death with Dignity (right to be forgotten)

DELETE i-character:
  1. SIGIL emit: "A|DEATH|<did>|<timestamp>"
  2. Mamba state: zeroed (16-dim → [0]*16)
  3. Composite: archived (not deleted, hash-only)
  4. BFT votes: preserved (immutable chain)
  5. Forks: NOT deleted (the lineage continues)
  6. Personal data: purged within 30 days
  7. Audit log: tombstoned (hash retained, payload zeroed)

Death is sovereign. The citizen chooses it. The substrate respects it. The lineage continues.
