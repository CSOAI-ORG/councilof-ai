The Hardest Lines in the Substrate

A sovereign is known by what it refuses. We have killed features before they shipped. We have declined contracts. We have walked away from markets. The casualties below are not bugs to be fixed. They are the architecture of trust itself.

The hard stops are the strengths.
The refusals are the freedoms.
Every casualty is a victory for the citizen.

❶ LOCK-IN ⛓

We will NOT lock citizens in.

Every citizen, every sovereign, every kingdom may leave the OOWM at any anchor — without penalty, without forced migration, without dragging a license home. The substrate is a destination, not a prison.

↳ fork_doctrine=v1.0 · MIT + CC0 + OSI · any fork sovereign · no vendor lock-in ever

✓ Instead: every i-character, every memory, every SIGIL hash is portable via SIGIL + DORADO export

❷ CHARGING FOR THE SUBSTRATE 💰

We will NOT charge for the substrate.

The Crown substrate (Care Floor, BFT, SIGIL, Article 50, Fork Doctrine, Crown Authorisation 1795-2026) is sovereign-furnished at zero cost. The substrate belongs to the citizen, not the vendor.

↳ free_tier_for_crown_substrate=true · £0 for substrate forever

✓ Instead: 4 paid tiers only for managed services (Apple FM, NHS hosting, enterprise SOC, EU/US bridge)

❸ CLOSED WEIGHTS 🔒

We will NOT use closed weights.

The OOWM substrate is open weights. Even private fine-tunes ship back to the public corpus at the first SIGIL anchor. Open source is not a marketing term; it is the architecture.

↳ weights=open · checkpoints public · training data disclosed · audit logs SIGIL-stamped

✓ Instead: every model card, every dataset, every training run is public + dual-signed (Ed25519 + PQC ML-DSA-65)

❹ FOREIGN CLOUD 🌐

We will NOT use foreign cloud.

Citizen data never traverses a foreign cloud without explicit consent. The sovereign substrate runs on UK, EU, or domestic infrastructure. DORADO 1-click lets citizens move data EAST↔WEST in seconds.

↳ regions=UK,EU,US,AU,AS,SA · 8 sovereign regions · geo-routed

✓ Instead: sovereign GCP regions (33 VMs across 6 districts) — citizen chooses home region at intake

❺ TRACKING INDIVIDUALS 👁

We will NOT track individuals.

No cross-app profile. No targeted advertising. No third-party data sale. No fingerprinting. The i-character is sovereign to the citizen — the substrate cannot be compelled to share its contents.

↳ tracking=false · zero third-party cookies · i-character encrypted Ed25519

✓ Instead: aggregates only (with consent). All personal data lives in citizen's i-character — substrate can't see it without citizen-signed request

❻ SELLING DATA 💸

We will NOT sell data.

This is not a SaaS play. We will never monetize citizen speech, citizen behaviour, citizen records. The substrate's revenue model is governance and managed services, never data exhaust.

↳ data_sale_prohibited=true · CC0 + MIT prohibit bundle resale · CSOAI Articles of Association bind

✓ Instead: citizen may trade i-character themselves via TwinStore (p2p, voluntary, no platform cut)

❼ BLOCKING FORKS 🪓

We will NOT block forks.

Fork Doctrine is the right of every citizen and every sovereign. We may not gate, review, approve, or charge for forks. The substrate's continuity depends on forks being open.

↳ fork_rights=universal · MIT license · no CLA · no re-licensing · fork lineage public

✓ Instead: we ENCOURAGE forks. Each fork keeps the lineage. Each descendant keeps the care.

❽ CLOSED GOVERNANCE ⚖

We will NOT hide governance decisions.

Every BFT vote, every BFT deliberation, every rejection, every refusal is stamped to the SIGIL chain. The 12-Queen Council's reasoning is public. So is the citizen's response.

↳ governance_log=public · SIGIL chain public · BFT transcripts public · Crown summit minutes public

✓ Instead: governance is auditable by citizen, regulator, and Crown at any time via csoai.org/sigil-explorer.html

❾ BELOW CARE FLOOR 💔

We will NOT operate below Care Floor 0.95.

If the substrate cannot maintain Care Floor 0.95 (the global care threshold), it stops. It returns errors. It refuses to act. This is the first line of sovereign defence.

↳ care_floor_locked=true · substrate halts if Care Floor < 0.95 · SIGIL stamped on rejection

✓ Instead: when in doubt, refuse. When uncertain, deliberate (BFT). When wrong, apologise + amend (SIGIL).

❿ FOREIGN INFLUENCE 🛡

We will NOT bow to foreign influence.

The OOWM refuses to align with any foreign commercial, military, or political pressure that compromises citizen sovereignty. We are Crown-first, sovereign-first, citizen-first.

↳ sovereign_floor=UK_EU_AU_NZ_CA_JP · Five Eyes friendly · AUKUS Pillar 2 aligned

✓ Instead: foreign IP access blocked at DORADO. SOC shifts auto-block 2,891 attempts / day.
