📅 Rostering

Sovereign AI for call scheduling, continuity of carer, and travel optimisation. Care Floor 0.95 prevents unsafe call times.

Compliance: Working Time Regulations, Equality Act 2010.

📞 Triage + Calls

Sovereign AI for incoming calls, alarm calls, and clinical triage. Identifies urgent needs (falls, breathing, mental health crisis).

📊 Quality + Compliance

Sovereign AI generates CQC evidence packs, PIR (Provider Information Return) responses, and quality audits.
