What is Article 50 watermarking?

EU AI Act Article 50 requires AI-generated content to be watermarked. SOV3 issues a sovereign passport for every output — a JSON-LD document with the SHA-256, SIGIL reference, timestamp, and sovereign composite score. Verifiable at proofof.ai.

What is the Care Floor?

The Care Floor is a numerical threshold (0.95) below which no sovereign action may proceed. Care is a property of the architecture, not a promise of marketing. The substrate refuses — even when the citizen asks — to take actions that fall below the Care Floor.
