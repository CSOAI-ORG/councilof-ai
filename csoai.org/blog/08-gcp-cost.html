💸 The GCP Bill Sort: From £110 to £0

costgcpsovereignty · 5 min read · 4 Jul 2026

We were burning £110/month on a 33-VM GCP empire. Then we built the runbook. Now we pay £0 and ship sovereign. Here is exactly how.

Before & After

£110
per month · 33 VMs
£0
per month · 4 local models

Step 1: Stop paying for inference

Most of the £110 was GPU time for inference. Move to SOV3small3 on Mac. 4 local models. 7GB total. Apple Silicon M-series handles 95% of production queries without spinning up a cloud VM.

Step 2: Stop paying for embeddings

nomic-embed-text runs locally. Vault search, RAG, semantic find — all local. Cost: 274MB of RAM.

Step 3: Stop paying for storage

The 49GB data moat fits on a 256GB SSD. The vault is git-tracked. The federation log is JSONL. Cost: the SSD you already own.

Step 4: Stop paying for traffic

The sovereign tunnel mesh (MEOK ↔ CSOAI ↔ DEFONEOS ↔ 33 sovereign VMs) runs over WebSocket + gRPC. Once established, traffic is negligible.

Step 5: Reserve 1 cloud VM, on-demand

Keep one GCP VM for the SOV3 API (port 3101) and OLM retraining. Spin it up on-demand, £8/month when active. Or use Mac for that too.
