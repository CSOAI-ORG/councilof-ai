FAQ

What's the difference between Article 50 and Annex III?

Article 50 is about transparency and watermarking for AI that interacts with humans, generates synthetic content, or does emotion recognition. Annex III is about high-risk AI systems (credit scoring, HR, etc.). The 7 May 2026 EU Digital Omnibus Act delayed Annex III by 16 months but NOT Article 50.

What's the penalty for non-compliance?

Up to EUR 15M or 3% of global annual turnover for Article 50 violations. SMEs pay the lower of the amounts.

Do I need C2PA watermarking?

Yes — Article 50(2) requires machine-readable marking of AI-generated outputs. C2PA is the open standard we use. Free tier supports any C2PA tool; Pro tier includes pre-stamped templates.

Is this for UK / US / global?

This passport is for EU deployments (any content served in the EU). UK / US / global compliance is on the roadmap.

How is this different from proofof.ai?

proofof.ai is our public verification directory (MCP scorecards, attestation URLs). This passport is the issuance + signing — proofof.ai is where you verify.
