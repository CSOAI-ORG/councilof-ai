## EIS advance assurance — the UK gate that makes the ASA work (owner action)

- **What:** HMRC advance assurance that CSOAI Ltd shares will qualify for EIS (and check SEIS eligibility — company <2 years old, <£250K raised, may qualify at SEIS 50% relief).
- **How:** gov.uk → "EIS advance assurance" → form + business plan + the patent/IP schedule + this scorecard. Free. ~2–4 weeks processing.
- **Why first:** UK angels almost always ask "is this EIS?" on call one. Assured = one friction point gone; the answer "yes, 30% relief" is worth more than any slide.
- **Counsel note (one-off, ~£1–2K):** have a UK startup solicitor confirm the ASA form (post-money, EIS-compliant, 6-month long-stop) and the articles allow its issue. YC SAFE is a US instrument — do not use it for UK investors without this check.
