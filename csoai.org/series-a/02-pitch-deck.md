## Slide 3: Solution

### Sovereign Governance Substrate

- **BFT Consensus Governance:** 12-of-33 Byzantine Fault Tolerant council validates every AI agent action before execution. No single agent can act unilaterally on sensitive operations.
- **SIGIL Ledger:** Ed25519-signed, hash-chained audit trail for every AI action, decision, and output. Cryptographically verifiable provenance — regulators can replay any decision chain.
- **Article 50 Passporting:** Machine-readable compliance passports for AI-generated content with SHA-256 content hashing, HMAC-signed for free-tier verification, Ed25519 for auditor-grade attestation.
- **Self-hosted sovereignty:** Deployable on customer infrastructure. No data leaves the organisation. No third-party API dependency for governance decisions.

**Speaker Notes:** The core insight is that AI governance cannot be an afterthought bolted onto existing tools — it must be an architectural substrate. Our BFT council means no AI agent can execute a sensitive action without cryptographic consensus. The SIGIL ledger means every decision is replayable and auditable. This is infrastructure, not a feature.
