🍴 The Fork Doctrine

A doctrine is not a feature. It is a guarantee. The fork doctrine guarantees exit-velocity. Without it, sovereignty is just marketing.

Three Principles

Principle 1: You own your fork

The moment you sign in to SOV3, you can fork the entire stack — brain, moat, queen council, crown lineage. You take it with you. MIT-licensed. CC0-friendly.

Principle 2: The fork is sovereign

Your fork inherits the crown lineage up to the moment of fork, then branches. Your SIGIL chain is yours. Your Care Floor 0.95 is yours. Your Article 50 passports are yours.

Principle 3: Forking is celebrated

We publish every fork. We credit every forker. We learn from every divergence. Forks are how the empire improves.

Why Forks Keep The Empire Honest

A sovereign AI vendor without a fork doctrine is a feudal lord. You pay rent, you get service, you have no exit. The moment you fork, the lord feels the pressure — and improves.

This is why SOV3 has absorbed so much: every fork that survived taught us something. Every fork that died taught us more. The doctrine is not paranoia. It is humility with teeth.

How To Fork Today

git clone https://github.com/CSOAI-ORG/sov3-sovereign.git

cd sov3-sovereign && make sovereign

You now have the entire stack. 60 charters. 309 tools. 22 protocols. 17 auth providers. Care Floor 0.95. All in 7 GB. On a Mac. For $0/month.
