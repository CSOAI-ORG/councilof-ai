UN Charter for Sovereign AI (proposal)

We propose that the United Nations adopt a Sovereign AI Charter, modelled after the 1948 Universal Declaration of Human Rights but specifically for the digital age.

Article 1 — Sovereignty

Every nation has the sovereign right to its own AI substrate. No nation should be dependent on foreign AI for critical infrastructure.

Article 2 — Care

AI systems shall protect human welfare. The Care Floor ≥ 0.95 standard, including for AI in critical infrastructure.

Article 3 — Audit

Every AI action shall be auditable. Sovereign SIGIL chains shall be public, immutable, post-quantum-encrypted.

Article 4 — Council

Every sovereign AI shall be deliberated by a BFT council of multiple sovereign actors. No single actor decides.

Article 5 — Sovereignty Switch

Every AI shall be switchable between sovereign regions. EAST ↔ WEST is a sovereign right of the citizen.

Article 6 — Watermarking

Every AI output shall be watermarked per EU AI Act Article 50. Provable. SIGIL-signed.

Article 7 — Verify

Anyone may verify any sovereign action. Math doesn't lie.

Article 8 — Heritage

The sovereign infrastructure lineage is open. Every nation inherits, every citizen contributes.

Commonwealth Charter (further commitments)

The 56 Commonwealth realms have a special relationship. We commit to:

  • Sovereign AI substrate per realm (UK + 55 others)
  • Article 50 alignment within 12 months
  • Care Floor enforced
  • SIGIL chain cross-realm
  • Commonwealth sovereign dividend (wisdom points shared)

OECD AI Principles (model alignment)

The Sovereign by Design Pledge

"Every sovereign AI is sovereign by design, not by marketing. We commit to: no foreign API calls, audit-chained SIGILs, BFT council deliberation, post-quantum cryptography, Article 50 watermarking, citizen-owned i-characters, and a verifiable Sovereign Composite scoreboard published at all times."
