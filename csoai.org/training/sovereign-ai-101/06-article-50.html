⚖️ What is EU AI Act Article 50?

Article 50 of the EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689) is the transparency obligation for AI systems that interact with natural persons or generate synthetic content. It requires that:

  1. Users are informed they are interacting with an AI system (unless obvious from context)
  2. AI-generated content is marked in a machine-readable format that detects it as artificially generated or manipulated
  3. Watermarks are effective, interoperable, robust, and reliable as far as technically feasible
  4. The marking is proportionate to the type and use of the content

Article 50 applies to: chatbots, generative text, image generation, video generation, audio generation, deepfakes, emotion recognition systems, and biometric categorisation. The obligation applies to providers and deployers — meaning both the model creator and the entity using it.

The CSOAI sovereign implementation of Article 50 follows three principles:
