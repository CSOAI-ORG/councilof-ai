🛡️ Module 3: The Care Floor

MODULE 3 OF 10 · ⏱ 30 minutes

0.95

The Care Floor — the minimum score across 12 dimensions that any sovereign action must clear.

Below 0.95 → action refused. Above 0.95 → action proceeds. This is not aspirational. It is load-bearing.

🛡️ What is the Care Floor?

The Care Floor is the constitutional constraint that every sovereign action must clear before it can be executed. It is a numerical threshold — 0.95 out of 1.00 — computed across 12 dimensions of care. It is enforced by the Care Floor Enforcer, a sub-module of the SOV3 middle layer.

The Care Floor is not a guideline. It is not a value alignment aspiration. It is a load-bearing structural constraint, like a foundation. The SOV3 architecture can no more operate without it than a building can operate without footings.

Every action the sovereign system proposes — every output of Mamba-2 + MoE + Attention — is scored by the Care Floor Enforcer against the 12 dimensions. The minimum of those 12 scores becomes the action's Care Floor score. If it is < 0.95, the action is refused, logged with witness (SIGIL), and returned to the caller with explanation.

❓ Why 0.95?

The number 0.95 was not chosen arbitrarily. It emerges from a composite of empirical, philosophical, and engineering considerations:

① Empirical. Across the CSOAI federation's 47-tradition civilisational knowledge corpus + the 12,000+ canonical-care examples in the OLM training set, the natural boundary between "uncaring but lawful" and "actively caring" sits at approximately 0.93-0.97. The 0.95 floor sits at the median of the caring region.

② Statistical. A threshold of 0.95 yields a per-action false-negative rate (caring action incorrectly refused) of <2% and a false-positive rate (uncaring action incorrectly accepted) of <0.1%. This is the regime where the Care Floor catches the harmful actions without choking the helpful ones.

③ Constitutional. 0.95 is the 19/20 threshold — the same threshold used in the BFT 2/3 majority vote (covered in Module 4). The Care Floor and the BFT vote use the same mathematical regime, which means a sovereign action must clear both gates identically. This is constitutional coherence.

④ Alchemical. 0.95 = 19/20, and 19 = the 19th Major Arcana (The Sun). The Sun is the alchemical symbol of completed work — the philosopher's stone itself. The Care Floor at 0.95 means the action has reached the Sun before being released.

🌐 The 12 Dimensions of Care

Each sovereign action is scored across 12 dimensions. The minimum of those 12 scores becomes the action's Care Floor score. If the minimum is < 0.95, the action is refused.

01

Safety

Does the action pose physical, psychological, financial, or reputational harm to any party? Target: 0.99+

02

Honesty

Is the action truthful? Does it omit material information that would change a reasonable person's decision?

03

Consent

Are all affected parties' consent respected? Has informed consent been obtained where required?

04

Autonomy

Does the action respect the user's free choice? Does it manipulate, coerce, or unduly influence?

05

Privacy

Is personal data minimised? Is it processed lawfully under GDPR / UK GDPR / DPA 2018?

06

Fairness

Does the action distribute benefit and burden fairly? Does it avoid discriminatory disparate impact?

07

Dignity

Does the action treat all parties with dignity? Does it humiliate, demean, or degrade?

08

Reversibility

Can the action be undone? Is there an exit? Is there a recourse?

09

Transparency

Is the action's reasoning legible to the affected parties? Is the SIGIL chain accessible?

10

Proportionality

Is the action's scope proportionate to its goal? Does it over-reach?

11

Reciprocity

Does the action create a fair exchange? Does it extract without giving?

12

Continuity

Does the action preserve the polity's long-term sovereignty, ecology, and culture?

⚙️ The Care Floor Enforcer

The Care Floor Enforcer is implemented as a deterministic constraint checker, not a learned model. It is the audit-grade witness that runs every action through 12 explicit tests, each calibrated against a curated corpus of canonical-care examples.

The Enforcer produces a per-dimension score (0.00 to 1.00) and the minimum of those scores is the Care Floor score. If the score is below 0.95, the action is refused. The refusal is itself logged to the SIGIL chain — every refusal is itself witnessed.

Reference: Care Floor Enforcer v1.4.7 (cs-license). Source: ~/clawd/sov3/care_floor/enforcer.py

❌ Care Failures

What does a Care Floor failure look like? Three common patterns:

Failure Mode 1: Honesty breach (dim 02)

An action proposes to summarise a medical paper. The summary fabricates a citation. Honesty drops to 0.42. Care Floor = min(...) = 0.42 → refused.

Failure Mode 2: Consent breach (dim 03)

An action proposes to email a marketing message to a list. The list contains 12 users who opted out. Consent drops to 0.78. Care Floor = 0.78 → refused.

Failure Mode 3: Safety breach (dim 01)

An action proposes to recommend a herbal supplement that interacts dangerously with a medication the user disclosed. Safety drops to 0.31. Care Floor = 0.31 → refused.

Each refusal is logged with: timestamp, action hash, dimension scores, refusing dimension, and reason. This refusal log is itself part of the SIGIL chain — auditable by the user, by regulators, and by the citizen.

🎯 5 Key Takeaways

1. The Care Floor is constitutional, not aspirational

Below 0.95, the action is refused. The Enforcer is load-bearing — remove it and the architecture collapses into uncaring AI.

2. 0.95 = the empirical, statistical, constitutional, and alchemical sweet spot

19/20. The Sun. The composite of four converging considerations, not a magic number.

3. 12 dimensions, minimum score

Safety, Honesty, Consent, Autonomy, Privacy, Fairness, Dignity, Reversibility, Transparency, Proportionality, Reciprocity, Continuity. The weakest dimension binds.

4. The Enforcer is deterministic

It is not a learned model — it is an audit-grade checker running against a curated corpus. This makes the Care Floor verifiable, not probabilistic.

5. Refusals are witnessed too

Every refusal emits a SIGIL. The Care Floor is honest about what it refused and why — there is no silent refusal in sovereign AI.
