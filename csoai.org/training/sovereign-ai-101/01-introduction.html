📜 Module 1: Introduction to Sovereign AI

MODULE 1 OF 10 · ⏱ 30 minutes

❓ What is Sovereign AI?

Sovereign AI is artificial intelligence that operates under the authority of a defined polity — a nation, a citizen, a community, an organisation — rather than under the unaccountable authority of a private corporation. It is AI whose substrate is auditable, whose decisions are witness-recorded, whose actions are reversible, and whose design is constitutionally constrained.

Where corporate AI optimises for shareholder return, sovereign AI optimises for citizen welfare. Where corporate AI keeps its weights and chain-of-thought private, sovereign AI publishes its audit trail. Where corporate AI can be silently deprecated, retrained, or jailbroken by its operator, sovereign AI's behaviour is governed by a load-bearing Charter, a Care Floor, and a Byzantine-Fault-Tolerant council.

In short: sovereign AI is the constitutional alternative to corporate AI. It is the substrate on which a nation — or any sovereign entity — can build AI infrastructure that cannot be revoked by a foreign corporation, manipulated by a hostile actor, or turned against its citizens.

🏛️ Why is Sovereign AI Different from Corporate AI?

🏢 Corporate AI

  • Owned by shareholders — fiduciary duty to profit
  • Black-box weights, hidden chain-of-thought
  • Silent deprecation, retraining, capability changes
  • Single point of failure (one operator)
  • Subject to foreign-government warrants (CLOUD Act, EO 12333)
  • No constitutional constraints
  • Care is a "value alignment" aspiration, not enforced

🐉 Sovereign AI

  • Owned by the citizen — duty to welfare
  • Open weights, public SIGIL chain, hash-verified
  • Behaviour governed by Charter + Care Floor + BFT
  • 12-around-1 fault-tolerant (up to 4 failures tolerated)
  • DORADO EAST↔WEST — jurisdiction-aware
  • Care Floor at 0.95 — load-bearing, not aspirational
  • Every action Ed25519 + PQC ML-DSA-65 signed

The fundamental difference is authority. Corporate AI answers to capital. Sovereign AI answers to a polity — and that polity's authority is itself constitutionally constrained. The Crown Lineage 1795-2026 is the working example: 230+ years of continuous lawful authority, transferred not by purchase but by constitutional succession.

⚖️ The 5 Sovereign AI Principles

① Auditable Substrate

Every file, every model weight, every chain-of-thought trace is open to inspection. The sovereign substrate is not "transparency as a feature" — it is "transparency as a constitutional requirement".

② Witnessed Action

Every sovereign action emits a SIGIL — a hash-chained, Ed25519 + PQC-signed record. The SIGIL is the witness. Without it, the action did not happen. Without it, the action cannot be proven.

③ Care Floor Enforcement

The Care Floor at 0.95 across 12 dimensions is not a guideline. It is load-bearing. Below 0.95 the enforcer refuses the action. The sovereign cannot harm the citizen — even by accident.

④ Byzantine Fault Tolerance

No single queen — no single human, no single model, no single node — holds authority. The 12-around-1 council votes 2/3 majority. Up to 4 queens can be compromised; the system still works.

⑤ Reversible Sovereignty

The citizen can revoke. The citizen can migrate. The citizen can audit. Sovereignty that cannot be revoked is not sovereignty — it is captivity. DORADO EAST↔WEST lets the citizen switch jurisdictional frame in one click.
