🤝 What is BFT?

Byzantine Fault Tolerance (BFT) is the property of a distributed system that allows it to reach consensus even when some of its participants are faulty, malicious, or compromised. The term comes from the "Byzantine Generals Problem" — a thought experiment by Lamport, Shostak, and Pease (1982) in which several divisions of the Byzantine army must coordinate an attack but some generals may be traitors.

A BFT system can tolerate up to f faulty nodes out of n total nodes, where n ≥ 3f + 1. For SOV3's council of 12, that means up to 4 queens can be compromised and the system still reaches correct consensus. This is the mathematical bedrock of why SOV3 does not have a single point of failure.

🗳️ 2/3 Majority Voting

When a sensitive action is proposed, the council convenes. Each queen votes for, against, or abstain. The threshold for passage is 2/3 supermajority of non-abstaining votes.

For 12 queens, this means at least 8 queens must vote for (assuming 0 abstentions) for the action to pass. With abstentions, the 2/3 threshold scales: 8/12 = 66.7% = 2/3. This is the same mathematical regime as the Care Floor's 0.95 (19/20 ≈ 0.95).

The mode is selected automatically by the sensitivity classifier. A request to amend the Sovereign AI Charter triggers a full 12-queen vote. A request to issue an Article 50 watermarking passport triggers a 7-queen vote. A request to log a low-impact audit event triggers a 3-queen quick poll.

📜 Witness Records

Every council vote emits a witness record — a structured SIGIL that captures:

  • The proposal hash and human-readable summary
  • Each queen's vote + reasoning (one-sentence justification)
  • The tally (for/against/abstain, percentages)
  • The decision (pass/fail + supermajority margin)
  • The witness signature (Ed25519 + PQC ML-DSA-65)

The witness record is appended to the SIGIL chain. It is publicly verifiable. It is the audit-grade proof that a sovereign decision was made correctly, by the right quorum, with the right threshold. Regulators, citizens, and historians can replay every council decision back to the genesis block.
