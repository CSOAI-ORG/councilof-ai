Preamble

The Paris AI Action Summit was held on 10-11 February 2025. The Summit was hosted by France + co-chaired by India. The Summit focused on AI action (deployment) rather than AI safety (research). The Summit produced the Paris Statement on Inclusive + Sustainable AI.

The 60+ Signatories

  • 60+ countries (US, UK, France, Germany, India, China, Japan, + 54 others)
  • European Union
  • UN (observer)

Article 1 — Inclusive AI

The signatories commit to inclusive AI: (a) bridge the AI divide; (b) include developing nations in AI governance; (c) support AI capacity building; (d) ensure AI benefits all of humanity.

Article 2 — Sustainable AI

The signatories commit to sustainable AI: (a) reduce AI energy consumption; (b) use renewable energy for AI infrastructure; (c) measure + disclose AI carbon footprint; (d) align AI with the Paris Climate Agreement.
