Preamble

The CSOAI Education Charter establishes the sovereign obligations for schools, colleges, universities, and lifelong learning providers. The Charter draws on the UK DfE Standards, the Equality Act 2010, the GDPR (special category data for children), the EU AI Act (Annex III(3) — high-risk access to education), the sovereign substrate's i-character framework, and the sovereign principles of CSOAI Ltd.

  • Equality Act 2010: reasonable adjustments for disabled students.
