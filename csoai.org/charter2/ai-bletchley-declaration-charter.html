Preamble

The Bletchley Declaration was signed at the AI Safety Summit at Bletchley Park, UK on 1-2 November 2023. The Declaration was signed by 28 governments + the EU. It is the first major international agreement on frontier AI safety.

The 28 Signatories

  • United States · United Kingdom · China · India · Brazil · Japan · Singapore · South Korea · Australia · Canada · France · Germany · Italy · Spain · Netherlands · Belgium · Poland · Romania · Ireland · New Zealand · Nigeria · Israel · Chile · Czech Republic · Denmark · Indonesia · Ukraine · UAE
  • European Union (observer)

Article 1 — Frontier AI Identification

The signatories identify frontier AI as the most advanced AI systems that pose the greatest potential risks. The signatories agree that frontier AI poses risks including: (a) potential for misuse in cyberattacks + bioterrorism; (b) unintended bias + discrimination; (c) impact on employment + economic systems; (d) competition + concentration of power.

Article 2 — Safety-by-Design

The signatories commit to safety-by-design in the development of frontier AI. This includes: (a) pre-deployment testing + red-teaming; (b) evaluation by independent third parties; (c) transparency about model capabilities + limitations; (d) ongoing monitoring + post-deployment updates.

Article 3 — Transparency + Accountability

The signatories commit to transparency + accountability in frontier AI development. This includes: (a) disclosing model architectures + training data; (b) publishing safety cases + evaluations; (c) reporting serious incidents; (d) engaging with regulators + civil society.

Article 4 — International Cooperation

The signatories commit to international cooperation on frontier AI safety. This includes: (a) sharing research + best practices; (b) coordinating on evaluation + standards; (c) supporting capacity building in low-resource nations; (d) continuing the AI Safety Summit series.

Article 5 — Sovereign Crosswalk

The Bletchley Declaration aligns with the CSOAI Layer-0 substrate on: (a) safety-by-design (sov.safe_design); (b) transparency (sov.transparency); (c) accountability (sov.accountability); (d) international cooperation (sov.sov_space); (e) frontier AI classification (sov.frontier_ai).
