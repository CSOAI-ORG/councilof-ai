Article 5 — Sovereign BFT Participation

Article 6 — Sovereign Article 14 (4-eyes)

Every high-risk sovereign decision requires 4-eyes human review. The substrate never acts autonomously on a high-risk decision. Article 14 of the EU AI Act is enforced.

Article 7 — Sovereign Care Floor 0.95

Every sovereign government retains full ownership of their data + their BFT votes + their fork IP. The substrate may use the data only with explicit consent. The substrate never sells, rents, or transfers the data to a third party. The substrate is sovereign by design.
