Preamble

The CSOAI Transport Charter establishes the sovereign obligations for transport, including rail, road, air, and maritime. The Charter draws on the UK DfT (Department for Transport), HS2 (High Speed 2), the sovereign substrate's transport bridges, and the sovereign principles of CSOAI Ltd.

Regulatory Landscape

  • UK DfT (Department for Transport): ministerial department for transport policy in the UK. ~£100B annual budget.
  • HS2 (High Speed 2): cancelled northern leg (October 2023). HS2 Euston station cancelled (March 2024). Legacy: 345 km of high-speed rail; 32.5 km of tunnels; 4 new stations.
  • Office of Rail and Road (ORR): economic + safety regulator for rail in GB.
  • Civil Aviation Authority (CAA): aviation regulator. ~150M passengers/year pre-pandemic.
  • Maritime and Coastguard Agency (MCA): maritime regulator.
  • EU AI Act Annex III(3): AI for safety components in transport as high-risk.

Specific Cases

HS2 cancellation (October 2023): Rishi Sunak cancelled the northern leg of HS2. £34B already spent; final cost estimated £66B+ if all built. Result: substrate enforces Article 12 audit trail for transport decisions.

Stansted Airport drone incursion (February 2019): drone sighting closed Stansted Airport for 33 minutes. Result: substrate enforces Article 12 + Article 14 for transport AI.

Uber self-driving fatality (Tempe, AZ, March 2018): pedestrian killed by Uber self-driving Volvo. NTSB report cited inadequate safety culture. Result: substrate enforces Article 14 human oversight for all transport AI.

Boeing 737 MAX (2018–2019): 346 deaths due to MCAS. Result: substrate enforces 4-eyes + multi-source sensor voting for transport AI.

Ever Given Suez Canal blockage (March 2021): 6 days, $9.6B/day global trade impact. Result: substrate enforces supply-chain risk management per Art. 12.
