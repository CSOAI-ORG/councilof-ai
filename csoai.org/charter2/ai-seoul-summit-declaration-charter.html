Preamble

The Seoul Declaration on AI was signed at the AI Seoul Summit on 21 May 2024. The Seoul Summit was co-hosted by the UK + South Korea. The Declaration builds on the Bletchley Declaration and adds specific commitments to frontier AI safety.

The 16 Signatories

  • 10 countries (US, UK, China, Japan, South Korea, France, Germany, Italy, India, Brazil, Australia, Canada, EU + others)
  • OpenAI + Anthropic + Microsoft + Google + Meta (frontier AI labs)
  • 5 developing nations (Nigeria, Indonesia, Mexico, Kenya, others)

Article 1 — Frontier AI Safety Commitments

The signatories commit to: (a) develop frontier AI safely + responsibly; (b) publish safety policies before deploying new frontier AI models; (c) identify + report risks of frontier AI; (d) evaluate AI capabilities + limitations before deployment.

Article 2 — Frontier AI Safety Institute (FASI)

The signatories establish Frontier AI Safety Institutes: (a) UK AI Safety Institute (London); (b) US AI Safety Institute (NIST, Washington DC); (c) Japan AI Safety Institute (Tokyo); (d) South Korea AI Safety Institute (Seoul). The FASIs conduct evaluations + red-teaming + safety cases.

Article 3 — Sovereign Crosswalk

The Seoul Declaration aligns with the CSOAI Layer-0 substrate on: (a) FASI-style evaluation (sov.frontier_evaluation); (b) safety policies (sov.ai_policy); (c) capability identification (sov.capability); (d) risk reporting (sov.risk_reporting).
