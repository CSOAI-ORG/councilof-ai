Preamble

The CSOAI Healthcare Charter establishes the sovereign obligations for healthcare, life sciences, and social care. The Charter draws on the NHS Constitution (2009, refreshed 2025), Care Quality Commission (CQC) Fundamental Standards, Care Floor 0.95, WHO ICOPE (Integrated Care for Older People, 2019), the EU Medical Device Regulation (2017/745), the EU AI Act (2024/1689), the sovereign substrate's health-bridge stack of 11 MCPs, and the sovereign principles of CSOAI Ltd.

Regulatory Landscape

  • NHS Constitution (2009, refreshed 2025): seven principles — access on basis of need, comprehensive service, parity of esteem, patient-centred, respect for autonomy, dignity, quality + safety.
  • Care Quality Commission (CQC): 5 Fundamental Standards (person-centred care, dignity, consent, safety, safeguarding) + 5 Key Lines of Enquiry (safe, effective, caring, responsive, well-led).
  • EU Medical Device Regulation 2017/745 (MDR): SaMD classification per IMDRF; Class IIa, IIb, III devices require notified-body assessment.
  • UK MHRA: Medicines and Healthcare products Regulatory Agency.
  • HIPAA (US, 1996): Privacy Rule (45 CFR 164) + Security Rule (45 CFR 164.302-318) + Breach Notification Rule.
  • GDPR Article 9: special categories of personal data — health, biometrics, genetics.
  • EU AI Act Article 50(1): AI systems intended to interact with natural persons must inform them.
  • EU AI Act Article 50(2): AI-generated synthetic content must be C2PA-marked.

Specific Cases

Therac-25 (1985–1987): race condition between X-ray beam + software; 6 patients died from radiation overdose. Result: FDA now requires formal accountability + fault-tree analysis for medical software.

COVID-19 (2020–2023): 7M+ deaths globally; NHS workforce under extreme pressure. Result: telemedicine, NHS App, sovereign substrate health tools.

Mencap (2007–): 18 deaths in specialist learning-disability hospitals. Result: CQC tightened safeguarding standards.

Care Home COVID outbreaks (2020–2021): 40,000+ deaths. Result: CQC tightened infection-control standards + Article 14 human oversight in care.
