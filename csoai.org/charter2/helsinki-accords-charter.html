Preamble

The Helsinki Final Act (Helsinki Accords) was signed on 1 August 1975 in Helsinki, Finland. The Accords were signed by 35 nations + the EU (then EEC). The Accords established the Conference on Security and Cooperation in Europe (CSCE), which became the OSCE in 1995.

The 10 Principles (the "Decalogue")

  1. Sovereign equality — All states have equal rights + duties.
  2. Refraining from threat or use of force — States shall not use force against another state.
  3. Inviolability of frontiers — Borders are inviolable.
  4. Territorial integrity — States shall not violate the territorial integrity of another state.
  5. Peaceful settlement of disputes — Disputes shall be settled by peaceful means.
  6. Non-intervention — States shall not intervene in the internal affairs of another state.
  7. Respect for human rights — States shall respect human rights + fundamental freedoms.
  8. Equal rights + self-determination of peoples — Peoples have the right to self-determination.
  9. Cooperation among states — States shall cooperate in all fields.
  10. Good faith — States shall act in good faith in fulfilling their obligations.

The 3 Baskets

The Helsinki Accords organised the dialogue into 3 "baskets": (a) Security + Cooperation in Europe; (b) Cooperation in the field of economics, science + technology, and the environment; (c) Cooperation in humanitarian + other fields (human rights, cultural exchange).
