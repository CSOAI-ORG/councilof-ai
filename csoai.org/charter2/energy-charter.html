Preamble

The CSOAI Energy Charter establishes the sovereign obligations for energy production, transmission, distribution, and consumption. The Charter draws on Ofgem (UK Office of Gas and Electricity Markets), the sovereign grid architecture, post-quantum security, and the sovereign principles of CSOAI Ltd.

Regulatory Landscape

  • Ofgem (UK energy regulator, 1986): regulates privatised gas + electricity markets. RIIO (Revenue = Incentives + Innovation + Outputs) framework. ~50 licensed electricity suppliers + 7 major distribution networks.
  • National Grid ESO (Electricity System Operator): balance + manage GB electricity system. 6 GW demand at peak; 350 GW capacity.
  • Energy Act 2004: regulation framework.
  • Energy Act 2013: EMR (Electricity Market Reform) — Contracts for Difference (CfD), Capacity Market, Emissions Performance Standard.
  • Energy Security Bill (2023): measures to reduce dependence on imported gas.
  • EU AI Act Annex III(3): AI for safety components in critical infrastructure (energy) as high-risk.
