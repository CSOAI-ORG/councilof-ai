Preamble

The CSOAI Pharmacy Charter establishes the sovereign obligations for pharmacies, pharmacies-in-care-homes, and pharmaceutical manufacturers. The Charter draws on the GPhC (General Pharmaceutical Council) Standards, the UK MHRA, the EU AI Act Article 50, the human-medicines regulation, the sovereign substrate's pharmacy bridges, and the sovereign principles of CSOAI Ltd.

Regulatory Landscape

  • GPhC Standards for Registered Pharmacies (2018, revised 2023): 5 principles — safe operation, governance, procurement, dispensing, staff + training. ~14,000 UK pharmacies.
  • GPhC Standards for Pharmacy Professionals (2017): 9 standards — person-centred care, communication, consent, confidentiality, professional judgement, responsibility, CPD, raising concerns, leadership.
  • UK MHRA: regulates medicines + medical devices.
  • EU AI Act Article 50(2): AI-generated pharmaceutical content must be C2PA-marked.
  • EU AI Act Article 14: high-risk AI in clinical decision support requires 4-eyes oversight.

Specific Cases

Thalidomide (1957–1962): 10,000+ children born with severe malformations. Result: drug-safety regulations tightened.

MHRA valproate PPP (2018): sodium valproate causes birth defects. Result: substrate enforces Article 14 + Article 22 for teratogenic medications.

EU Falsified Medicines Directive (2011/62/EU, in force 9 February 2019): required safety features (unique identifier + tamper-evident seal). Result: substrate enforces Article 12 tamper-evident logging + Article 14 supply-chain verification.

Opioid crisis (US, 1999–present): 645,000+ deaths. Result: substrate enforces CDC opioid-prescribing guidelines + Article 14 human oversight.
