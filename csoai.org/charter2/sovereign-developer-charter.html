Article 1 — Sovereign Developer Identity

Every sovereign developer receives a sovereign identity (DID + W3C VC + sovereign JWT) for the developer + for every MCP they publish. The identity is portable + exportable + MIT-licensed.

Article 2 — Sovereign MCP Publishing

Article 3 — Sovereign 5-Tier Cascade Pricing

Article 4 — Sovereign BFT Participation

Every sovereign developer may participate in the 33-agent BFT council deliberations. The vote is weighted by tier. The vote is logged. The vote is auditable.

Article 5 — Sovereign Fork Authority

Every sovereign developer may fork any Layer-0 protocol. The fork is SIGIL-signed. The fork is OSCAL-stamped. The fork is BFT-deliberated. The fork is awarded a Social Authority Badge.

Article 6 — Sovereign Override

Every sovereign developer has the right to override any sovereign action. The override is a single command. The override is immediate. The override is logged in the SIGIL chain as a veto event.

Article 7 — Sovereign Care Floor 0.95

Every sovereign developer action respects the Care Floor 0.95. The substrate never produces a recommendation that could harm the developer or the citizen. The substrate respects Article 9 special category data.

Article 8 — Sovereign Sovereignty

Every sovereign developer retains full ownership of their fork IP. The fork is MIT-licensed. The fork IP is sovereign by design.
