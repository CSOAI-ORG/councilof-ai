Preamble

The CSOAI Insurance Charter establishes the sovereign obligations for the insurance sector. The Charter draws on the FCA Consumer Duty (PRIN 2A, 31 July 2023), Solvency II (2009/138/EC, in force 1 January 2016), the Insurance Distribution Directive (2016/97/EU), the EIOPA AI Governance Principles (October 2024), the sovereign substrate's ACORD + Solvency II bridge stack, and the sovereign principles of CSOAI Ltd.

Regulatory Landscape

  • FCA Consumer Duty (PRIN 2A, 31 July 2023): three consumer outcomes — products meeting needs, clear communication, fair value. Applies to all 51,000+ FCA firms.
  • Solvency II (2009/138/EC, in force 1 January 2016): three pillars — Pillar 1 (SCR + MCR), Pillar 2 (ORSA), Pillar 3 (SFCR, RSR). ~5,000 EU insurers.
  • Insurance Distribution Directive (2016/97/EU): IDD. POG (Article 25).
  • EU AI Act Annex III(5): insurance pricing + risk assessment as high-risk.
  • EIOPA AI Governance Principles (October 2024): 6 principles — fairness, transparency, robustness, human oversight, accountability, data governance.
