Preamble

The CSOAI Logistics Charter establishes the sovereign obligations for logistics, supply chain, and freight forwarding. The Charter draws on HMRC (UK tax authority), the EU Article 50 passport for goods, the sovereign substrate's logistics bridges, and the sovereign principles of CSOAI Ltd.

Regulatory Landscape

  • HMRC (UK tax authority): administers UK tax + customs + excise + VAT + import duties + trade statistics.
  • EU Article 50 (in WTO context): applies to Article XXIV GATT (regional trade agreements) + Article V GATS (services). Substrate enforces compliance for cross-border goods.
  • UK Borders (post-Brexit): 1 January 2021 exit. UK Global Tariff replaces EU Common External Tariff.
  • EU AI Act Annex III(3): AI for safety components in supply chain as high-risk.
  • ICS2 (Import Control System 2): EU-wide customs pre-arrival system.
