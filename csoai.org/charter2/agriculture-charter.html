Preamble

The CSOAI Agriculture Charter establishes the sovereign obligations for agriculture, food production, and rural communities. The Charter draws on DEFRA (UK Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs), the EU CAP (Common Agricultural Policy), the Agriculture Act 2020, the sovereign substrate's farm-traceability bridge, the i-character framework, and the sovereign principles of CSOAI Ltd.

Regulatory Landscape

  • DEFRA (UK Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs): ministerial department for environment, food, rural.
  • EU CAP (Common Agricultural Policy): post-Brexit, replaced by ELM (Environmental Land Management) scheme in England (2024–).
  • Agriculture Act 2020: framework for post-CAP support; new "public money for public goods" principle.
  • Environment Act 2021: biodiversity net gain (10% minimum) for new developments.
  • Animal Welfare Act 2006: England + Wales.
  • EU AI Act Annex III(3): AI for food safety + agricultural subsidies as high-risk.

Specific Cases

Foot-and-mouth disease outbreak (2001): 6M+ animals culled; £8B cost to UK economy. Result: substrate enforces Article 14 human oversight for animal health decisions.

BSE / vCJD crisis (1986–2000): 178 deaths in UK. Result: 1996 Specified Bovine Material ban; strict traceability.

Salmonella egg contamination (1988): Public Health Laboratory Service report; 5M+ eggs destroyed. Result: substrate enforces traceability per EU Food Information for Consumers Regulation.

EU CAP subsidies (2014–2020): €408B over 7 years; ~40% of EU budget. Substrate enforces Article 12 + Article 14 for AI subsidy decisions.

Russia/Ukraine grain deal (July 2022): 60M+ tonnes of grain exported; food security crisis. Result: substrate enforces supply chain Article 12.
