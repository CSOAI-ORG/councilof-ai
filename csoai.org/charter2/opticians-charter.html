Preamble

The CSOAI Opticians Charter establishes the sovereign obligations for opticians, dispensing opticians, and domiciliary optometry providers. The Charter draws on the GOC (General Optical Council) Standards of Practice, the Optical Confederation's AI guidance, the Equality Act 2010, the sovereign substrate's care-bridge stack, the Templeman Opticians lineage (Nicholas Templeman's family business, established 1979, 60+ care homes served across Lincolnshire, Nottinghamshire, and the East Midlands), and the sovereign principles of CSOAI Ltd.

Regulatory Landscape

  • GOC Standards of Practice (2022): 5 core domains — professional knowledge + skills, communication + person-centred care, safety + quality, professional + ethical behaviour, leadership + management.
  • GOC Standards for Optical Businesses (2019, revised 2022): 6 standards — patient-centred care, safety, staff, records, premises, governance.
  • GOC Continuing Education and Training (CET): 36 points per 3-year cycle for optometrists.
  • Optical Confederation AI Guidance (2024): 6 principles for AI in optical practice.
  • Equality Act 2010: reasonable adjustments for disabled patients.
