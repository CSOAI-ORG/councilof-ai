Preamble

The ASEAN Guide on AI Governance and Ethics was published in 2024 by the ASEAN Digital Ministers' Meeting. The Guide provides a framework for responsible AI in the ASEAN region (Brunei, Cambodia, Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Myanmar, Philippines, Singapore, Thailand, Vietnam).

Article 1 — AI Governance

The signatories commit to: (a) national AI strategies; (b) cross-border AI cooperation; (c) AI regulation aligned with international standards; (d) AI capacity building in ASEAN.

Article 2 — AI Ethics

The signatories commit to: (a) fairness + non-discrimination; (b) transparency + explainability; (c) accountability + responsibility; (d) human oversight + control.

Article 3 — AI Use Cases

The signatories identify key ASEAN AI use cases: (a) financial services (Singapore, Thailand, Indonesia); (b) agriculture (Vietnam, Thailand, Indonesia); (c) healthcare (Singapore, Malaysia, Thailand); (d) education (all); (e) smart cities (Singapore, Malaysia).

Article 4 — Sovereign Crosswalk
