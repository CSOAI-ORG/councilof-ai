Preamble

The CSOAI Manufacturing Charter establishes the sovereign obligations for manufacturing, supply chain, and industrial operations. The Charter draws on the EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA, 2024/2847, in force December 2024, full applicability December 2027), the UK Made in Britain mark, the SCADA/ICS industry standards, the IEC 62443 industrial cybersecurity standard, the sovereign substrate's supply-chain bridge stack, and the sovereign principles of CSOAI Ltd.

Regulatory Landscape

  • EU Cyber Resilience Act (CRA, 2024/2847, in force December 2024, full applicability December 2027): applies to "products with digital elements" — software, hardware, IoT. Requirements: secure-by-default, no known vulnerabilities, software bill of materials (SBOM), 5-year vulnerability disclosure, CE marking.
  • UK Made in Britain mark: voluntary certification by Made in Britain (since 2011) — ~2,000 member companies. Confirms a product has been substantially manufactured in the UK.
  • IEC 62443 (Industrial Cybersecurity): 14 standards. Part 3-3 (System security requirements and security levels) is the most cited. SL 1-4 (Security Level 1-4).
  • NIS2 (Network and Information Security 2 Directive, 2022/2555): applies to manufacturing operators of essential services.
  • EU AI Act Annex III(3): AI for safety components in critical infrastructure (manufacturing) as high-risk.

Specific Cases

Stuxnet (June 2010): US-Israeli joint operation to disrupt Iranian nuclear centrifuges. Damaged 1,000+ centrifuges. Result: first cyberweapon targeting industrial control systems; IEC 62443 accelerated.

WannaCry (May 2017): ransomware attack on NHS + Renault + Telefonica + Deutsche Bahn. Renault temporarily halted production at 4 sites. Result: NIS2 legislation + IEC 62443 SL-3 compliance.

NotPetya (June 2017): Russian state-actor ransomware; $10B+ in damages globally. Maersk shipping container management offline for 10 days. Result: substrate enforces Article 12 audit trail for every SCADA action.

Triton/TRISIS (December 2017): Russian state-actor attack on Saudi Aramco petrochemical plant safety system. Result: IEC 62443 SL-3 mandatory for industrial control systems.

Colonial Pipeline (May 2021): DarkSide ransomware forced 6-day fuel supply shutdown. $4.4M ransom paid. Result: TSA Pipeline Security Directive 2.

Modern Application (2026)

The CSOAI Manufacturing Charter is implemented through:

  1. EU CRA compliance: substrate enforces secure-by-default + no known vulnerabilities + 5-year disclosure + CE marking + SBOM.
  2. IEC 62443 SL-3: every SCADA action is SIGIL-signed at SL-3.
  3. SBOM CycloneDX: every product ships with a substrate-signed SBOM.
  4. SLSA provenance: every product carries SLSA v1.0 provenance attestation.
  5. Sigstore cosign: every build is cosign-signed + Rekor-transparency-logged.
