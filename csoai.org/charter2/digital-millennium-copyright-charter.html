Preamble

The Digital Millennium Copyright Act (DMCA) was enacted in 1998 (US). The DMCA implements the WIPO Copyright Treaty + the WIPO Performances and Phonograms Treaty. The DMCA criminalises the circumvention of DRM + provides safe harbours for online service providers.

Section 1201 — Circumvention of DRM

The DMCA criminalises the circumvention of technological measures that control access to a copyrighted work. This includes: (a) bypassing access controls; (b) distributing circumvention tools; (c) trafficking in circumvention tools. Penalties: up to 5 years prison + $500K fine (first offense).

Section 1202 — Copyright Management Information

The DMCA prohibits the removal or alteration of copyright management information (CMI). CMI includes: (a) title + author + copyright notice; (b) identifying numbers + symbols; (c) terms + conditions for use.

Section 512 — Online Service Provider Safe Harbours

The DMCA provides safe harbours for online service providers (OSPs) that comply with the notice-and-takedown procedure. The OSP must: (a) designate a DMCA agent; (b) adopt a repeat-infringer policy; (c) accommodate technical measures.
