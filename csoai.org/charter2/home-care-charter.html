Preamble

The CSOAI Home Care Charter establishes the sovereign obligations for home care providers, including domiciliary care, supported living, and care in the home. The Charter draws on the CQC Fundamental Standards, the Care Act 2014, the Equality Act 2010, the Mental Capacity Act 2005, the Care Floor 0.95, the sovereign substrate's care-bridge stack, the Cera integration, and the sovereign principles of CSOAI Ltd.

Regulatory Landscape

  • CQC Fundamental Standards (2014, revised 2025): 5 standards + 5 KLOEs (safe, effective, caring, responsive, well-led).
  • Care Act 2014: well-being principle + prevention + information + advice + personalised care + safeguarding + market oversight.
  • Care Standards Act 2000: regulation of care homes + domiciliary care agencies.
  • Equality Act 2010: reasonable adjustments for disabled care recipients.
  • Mental Capacity Act 2005: best-interests decisions + DoLS 2007.
  • Cera (home care platform, 2016–): 5,000+ carers, 1M+ care hours delivered.
