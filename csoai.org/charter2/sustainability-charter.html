Preamble

The CSOAI Sustainability Charter establishes the environmental, social, and governance (ESG) obligations that govern the CSOAI substrate's relationship with the natural world and future sovereign citizens. The Charter draws on Ofgem (UK Office of Gas and Electricity Markets), DEFRA (UK Department for Environment, Food and Rural Affairs), the EU Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation (2019/2088), the Brundtland Report (1987), and the sovereign principles of CSOAI Ltd.

Specific Cases — Energy and Environment

Ofgem (UK energy regulator, 1986): established to regulate the privatised gas + electricity markets after the Energy Act 1986. Powers include licensing, enforcement, environmental standards. Substrate implements Ofgem's RIIO (Revenue = Incentives + Innovation + Outputs) framework.

DEFRA (UK government department, 2001): responsible for environmental protection, food production, rural communities. Powers include the Environment Act 2021 (biodiversity net gain) and the Agricultural Act 2020 (ELM scheme). Substrate implements DEFRA's biodiversity net gain calculator as a domain MCP.

EU SFDR (2019/2088): Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation requires financial market participants to disclose how they integrate ESG risks. Substrate produces SFDR Article 6/8/9 disclosures for any sovereign citizen who holds an investment portfolio.

Paris Agreement (December 12, 2015): 196 countries committed to limit global warming to 1.5°C above pre-industrial levels. Substrate implements the Paris Agreement carbon budget tracker.
