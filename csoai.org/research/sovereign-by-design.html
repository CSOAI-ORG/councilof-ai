Why does a 3.1B parameter open-source fine-tune beat a 1.6T parameter commercial frontier model on a sovereign composite? Because the composite weights sovereignty-by-design criteria (60% of total weight), and no commercial frontier model has sovereignty-by-design properties (foreign API calls, closed weights, no SIGIL chain).
