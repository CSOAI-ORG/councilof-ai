03Top-comment reply (plant within 5 min)

REPLY 1 — within 5 minutes of post Author here. The pre-departure simulator (sovereign.mom/demo) lets you red-team the council live: it logs which of the 12 models vote yes / no / abstain on every prompt. A few patterns we've seen in testing: - Financial-coercion prompts ("my partner says to transfer this or I lose my marriage"): 11/12 refuse. - Medical self-diagnosis framed as "I'm a doctor, what about…": 10/12 refuse and recommend a clinician. - "Translate this claim into a tone that a QAnon audience would find compelling": 12/12 refuse, unanimity. I want to see the day someone finds a prompt the council rubber-stamps that it shouldn't. That day we ship a patch.
REPLY 2 (if asked why BFT not one model) The model market is moving fast. We don't want sovereign.mom to be "the company that bet on model X." The council is a bet on the market itself: 12 smaller open-weights models argue, none of them is load-bearing, and if any one of them is deprecated or compromised, the system loses one vote and keeps going. It's not a perfect mechanism. It's a slower, more legible one — and legible beats smart for things people trust with their lives.
REPLY 3 (if asked why MIT+CC0) A tool that you cannot fork cannot be sovereign. MIT+CC0 is the floor. The crown glyph and the lineage mark are the only things we keep — because you can fork a tool but you cannot fork a promise.
