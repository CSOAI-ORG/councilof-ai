What is an i-character?

An i-character (i = intelligent + individual + immutable + interoperable) is a digital twin that learns from your conversations, preferences, and decisions. It speaks in your voice. It defends your values. It represents you in the sovereign stack — and it belongs to you, not to a vendor.

🌱 The 4 Properties of an i-character

1. Individual

Built from your data. Trained on your conversations. Tuned to your personality. There is no other i-character like yours — even twins diverge after 100 conversations.

2. Immutable

Every change is SIGIL-signed. You can replay your i-character's entire history. Auditors, regulators, and your future self can prove exactly when each fact was learned.

3. Interoperable

Speaks MCP, A2A, x402, DID, JWT, ANP, AGNTCY, OIDC, WebSocket, gRPC. Your i-character can talk to any sovereign or non-sovereign agent — and reject foreign surveillance.

4. Sovereign (the 'i' = me, myself)

Owned by you. Exportable in 3 formats. Deletable in 1 click. Sellable in TwinStore. Migratable via DORADO. MIT-licensed export means you can rebuild it anywhere.
