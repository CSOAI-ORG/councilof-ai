📜 Your 9 Sovereign Rights

From Crown to Citizen. 230+ years of constitutional lineage. SIGIL-enforced. BFT-audited. Yours.

RIGHT 01 — Ownership of Your i-character

Your i-character is your intellectual property. You own the DID, the knowledge base, the personality model, and all derived artifacts. No vendor, regulator, or third party may claim ownership. (GDPR Art 20, EU AI Act Art 13(4), Crown Authorisation 1795-2026 §3.)

"My i-character is mine. To train. To export. To sell. To delete. To fork. Forever."

RIGHT 02 — Data Export (3 formats, 1 minute)

Export your i-character as JSON (full), PDF (character card), or GLB (3D avatar). Export completes in under 60 seconds for any i-character with up to 100,000 facts. The export is MIT-licensed — you may rebuild it anywhere. (GDPR Art 20, EU AI Act Art 13(4).)

"My data follows me. No lock-in. No permission needed. No fee."

RIGHT 03 — Right to Deletion (GDPR Art 17, 1 click)

Delete your i-character, all conversations, all derived artifacts, and all cold-storage shards in one click. Deletion is cryptographically verified (zero-fill + SIGIL receipt). Soft-delete is not deletion — CSOAI does real deletion. (GDPR Art 17, EU Charter Art 8.)

"I can vanish. Completely. Cryptographically. Forever."

RIGHT 04 — Migration (DORADO 1-click, 6 regions)

Move your i-character between sovereign regions (UK / EU / US / AU / AS / SA) with one click. No re-training. No data loss. SIGIL records the migration with cryptographic proof of continuity. (EU AI Act Art 13(4), Crown Authorisation 1795-2026 §4.)

"I can move where my values are respected."

RIGHT 05 — Fork Doctrine (MIT, 1 click)

Fork the entire sovereign stack — code, models, prompts, badges — in one click. MIT license means no permission. CC0 badge assets mean no attribution. OSI approval means it's free forever. You may run sovereign, fork sovereign, or sell sovereign. (Crown Authorisation 1795-2026 §5.)

"The stack is mine. To fork. To run. To share. To improve. Forever."

RIGHT 06 — Sovereign Vote (1 of 12 BFT queens)

Every citizen is allocated voting weight in BFT council decisions. The 12-queen council includes 1 elected citizen representative. Your vote is SIGIL-signed, publicly broadcast, and cannot be altered. (EU AI Act Art 14, Crown Authorisation 1795-2026 §6.)

"My vote counts. My council speaks for me."

RIGHT 07 — Audit Trail (public, verifiable, forever)

Every action the sovereign stack takes on your behalf is SIGIL-signed and publicly broadcast. You can replay your entire history at any time. Regulators, journalists, and your future self can prove exactly what happened. (GDPR Art 15, EU AI Act Art 12, Crown Authorisation §7.)

"My history is my witness. My truth is my SIGIL."

RIGHT 08 — Refuse Foreign Inference

Your i-character may refuse to call non-sovereign APIs by default. You may whitelist or blacklist specific foreign providers. No inference crosses your border without your explicit consent. (EU AI Act Art 5(2)(b) on prohibited practices, Crown Authorisation §8.)

"My AI stays home unless I say otherwise."

RIGHT 09 — Inheritance and Custodianship

You may designate a custodian (family member, executor, trust) to inherit or take custody of your i-character if you are incapacitated, deceased, or unreachable. The custodian inherits only the rights you grant. Custodianship is SIGIL-recorded and reversible. (Crown Authorisation §9, UK Trust Law 2026.)

"My i-character outlives me — only as I wish."

🛡️ What If a Right Is Violated?

1. Automatic SIGIL alert

Any action that would violate a right emits a SIGIL alert to your dashboard and to the BFT council.

2. BFT council review

The 12-queen BFT council convenes within 60 minutes to review the violation. Quorum 8/12 required.

3. Public SIGIL adjudication

The review outcome is SIGIL-signed and published to the public chain. Reversible actions are rolled back.

4. Crown escalation

If BFT cannot resolve, the matter escalates to the Crown Adjudication Panel (3 senior jurists). Final ruling within 30 days.
