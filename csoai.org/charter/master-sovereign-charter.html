#

The 8 articles of the sovereignty charter

##

Article 1: Sovereignty
The substrate recognises the sovereign consumer's authority over their data, their i-character, their routes, their reports, and their sovereign actions. The substrate never extracts. The citizen owns.

##

Article 2: Identity
The sovereign consumer's identity is a Decentralised Identifier (DID) + W3C Verifiable Credential (VC) + sovereign JWT. The identity is portable. The identity is verifiable in any browser.

##

Article 3: Air-Gap
The substrate runs in an air-gapped instance for sovereign + classified use cases. The substrate uses post-quantum cryptography (PQC ML-DSA-65). The substrate is sovereign by architecture, not by network.

##

Article 4: Audit
Every action is SIGIL-signed. Every sovereign consumer can verify any action in any browser. The substrate is fully auditable. The substrate is fully transparent.

##

Article 5: BFT Participation
Every sovereign consumer can participate in the 33-agent BFT council. The vote is tier-weighted (Bronze=1, Silver=3, Gold=10, Platinum=30, Sovereign=100). The council decides on high-risk decisions.

##

Article 6: Article 14 (4-eyes human review)
Every high-risk decision (kinetic, surveillance, autonomous weapon, critical infrastructure) requires 4-eyes human review. The substrate enforces Article 14.

##

Article 7: Care Floor 0.95
Every sovereign action has a Care Floor score. Minimum is 0.95. Special categories (Article 9) require 1.0. The substrate never produces a recommendation that could harm a sovereign consumer.

##

Article 8: Solve et Coagula
Sovereignty by design. The substrate is the world, dissolved and recomposed — MIT-licensed, sovereign by architecture, federated by fork. The substrate is open-source. The substrate is public.
