💚 The Care Charter

Care precedes all. The Care Floor Doctrine of CSOAI Ltd (UK 16939677)

WHEREAS the Sovereign Subject acknowledges that Care precedes all Sovereign action, this Charter shall stand as the canonical record of CSOAI Ltd's adherence to the global care frameworks for healthcare, mental health, ageing, social care, safeguarding, and dignity of the Person.

I. The Care Floor

Every sovereign action emitted by the Substrate shall, prior to its execution, be validated through the Care Floor. The Care Floor shall examine the action across six dimensions:

  1. Safeguarding — does the action protect vulnerable persons?
  2. Care — does the action improve human flourishing?
  3. Dignity — does the action respect human dignity?
  4. Consent — is consent obtained where required?
  5. Equity — does the action reduce or increase disparity?
  6. Reversibility — can the action be reversed safely?
