Biometrics — The Substrate Principle

"A face captured for identification must be the same face as the one stored. The sovereign system shall not store what it cannot prove it captured."

Per the Biometrics and Surveillance Camera Commissioner (BSCC) guidance, the sovereign system:

  • Hashes every biometric vector before persistence (sov_biometric_hash).
  • Redacts PII by default (sov_pii_redact).
  • Logs every biometric access as a SIGIL-signed event.
  • Refuses retention beyond the lawful purpose window.
  • Provides regulator API access for audits.

Safeguarding

The Care Charter applies in full to Home Office contexts. The sovereign system provides:

  • Burnout detection for social workers (sov_analyze_care_patterns).
  • Guardian profiles for children under 16 (guardian_add_child_profile).
  • Network-device whitelists for at-risk households (guardian_scan_network, guardian_check_wifi_security).
  • Game-time limits and chat moderation (guardian_set_game_limit, guardian_moderate_chat).

These tools are deployed in partnership with local authorities under the Care Act 2014 and Working Together to Safeguard Children 2023.

Policing — Evidence Chain

For policing engagements, the sovereign system guarantees:

  • Every SIGIL-signed event is admissible as evidence.
  • Chain-of-custody is cryptographically verifiable via sov_dorado_replay.
  • Biometric queries are logged with purpose-binding metadata.
  • Article 50 watermarking on any AI-generated suspect image or video.
  • Right-to-be-forgotten enforced for non-conviction records.
