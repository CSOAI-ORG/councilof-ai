Scope

The EU AI Act (Regulation 2024/1689) is the world's first comprehensive horizontal regulation of artificial intelligence. CSOAI Ltd (UK 16939677) commits to full compliance with the Act across all 27 member states, with particular focus on the high-risk classification (Annex III), the GPAI (general-purpose AI) obligations, and the Article 50 transparency + watermarking requirements. CSOAI Ltd is one of the only vendors with the 7 May 2026 EU Digital Omnibus Act delay-aware tooling built in — see Article 50 Passport.

The EU AI Act pillar enters force on Saturday 4 July 2026 09:00 BST, T-minus-4-days from launch.

Article 50 — The Watermarking Imperative

"Article 50 watermarking applies from 2 August 2026 — 36 days from today. The 7 May 2026 Digital Omnibus Act delay only delays GPAI obligations; transparency + watermarking is NOT delayed."

CSOAI Ltd's Article 50 Passport system:

  • Issues machine-readable watermarking passports for every AI-generated text, image, video, audio.
  • Free-tier passports are HMAC-signed and verifiable via proofof.ai.
  • Pro-tier passports are Ed25519-signed for auditor-grade attestation.
  • Enterprise-tier passports are double-signed Ed25519 + NIST PQC ML-DSA-65.
  • Penalties (EUR 15M / 3% global turnover) are absorbed by CSOAI Ltd, not passed to the user.
  • Replicated to all 27 member states where deployed.

The 7 May 2026 Digital Omnibus Delay

The EU Digital Omnibus Act (proposed 19 Nov 2025) delays some GPAI obligations to 2 December 2027. It does NOT delay Article 50 (still 2 Aug 2026). CSOAI Ltd's tooling was built delay-aware from day one:

  • Article 50 passports remain on the original 2 August 2026 timeline.
  • GPAI obligations (training-data summary, copyright compliance, systemic-risk mitigation) are scheduled for 2 December 2027.
  • Penalties for Article 50 violations remain EUR 15M / 3% global turnover.
  • Penalties for GPAI violations remain EUR 15M / 3% global turnover once effective.

GPAI Model Obligations

For GPAI models (including frontier systems with >10^25 FLOPs training compute), the sovereign system provides:

  1. Training-data summary — Public dataset card with sources, copyright-status, and risk profile.
  2. Copyright compliance — opt-out crawler-respect, attribution where due, takedown response within 72h.
  3. Systemic-risk mitigation — red-team evaluation, dangerous-capability testing, behavioural equivalence.
  4. Member-state reporting — Quarterly transparency report to EU AI Office.
  5. Public model card — Per Article 53 of the AI Act, published on the Sovereign Transparency Report.

27 Member State Deployment

CSOAI Ltd deploys the sovereign system to all 27 member states via 8 sovereign regions (UK, EU, US, AU, AS, SA + redundancy), with regional data residency per Article 10. Cross-jurisdictional SIGIL trails are auditable by national regulators via the public SIGIL REST API.
