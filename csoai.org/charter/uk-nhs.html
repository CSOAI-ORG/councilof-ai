Clinical Safety — DCB0129 / DCB0160

Every clinical-safety-relevant tool in the sovereign system carries a Hazard Log and Clinical Safety Case File, with SIGIL-signed evidence of:

  • Intended use, user profile, and patient population.
  • Risk register with mitigations and residual-risk acceptance.
  • Clinical Safety Officer (CSO) sign-off per release.
  • Post-deployment surveillance and incident reporting.
  • Annual safety case review and update.

MHRA UKCA Software as a Medical Device

For SaMD-class tools, the sovereign system provides:

  • UKCA technical documentation aligned to UK MDR 2002 (as amended).
  • Quality Management System per ISO 13485.
  • Risk Management File per ISO 14971.
  • Usability Engineering File per IEC 62366-1.
  • Vigilance and post-market surveillance system.
  • Unique Device Identification (UDI-DI) per MHRA guidance.

NICE Evidence Standards Framework

For AI tools within the scope of NICE evidence evaluation:

  1. Design stage — User need, intended use, target population, comparators.
  2. Effectiveness — Clinical-effectiveness studies (ideally RCT), real-world evidence, outcome measures.
  3. Economic — Cost-effectiveness vs standard of care, budget impact.
  4. Patient & service — Patient-reported outcomes, service-delivery impact.
  5. Implementation — Integration pathway, training, ongoing monitoring.

All five tiers are SIGIL-signed and audit-ready for NICE submission.
