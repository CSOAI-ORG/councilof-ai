The Six Principles

  1. Principle 01 — Lawfulness

    AI applications are developed and used in accordance with national and international law, including international humanitarian law and human-rights law. The sovereign system refuses to deploy into contexts where this principle cannot be attested by the BFT council.

  2. Principle 02 — Responsibility and Accountability

    AI applications are developed and used with appropriate levels of judgement and care; clear human responsibility ensures accountability. The sovereign system implements this at the architecture layer via the 12-around-1 BFT council.

  3. Principle 03 — Explainability and Traceability

    AI applications are developed and used with appropriate levels of explainability and traceability, allowing relevant actors to understand and oversee outputs. The sovereign system provides SIGIL-signed chains, public explorer, and replay tool.

  4. Principle 04 — Reliability

    AI applications are developed and used with appropriate levels of reliability, security, and robustness. The sovereign system provides adversarial testing, drift monitoring, OOD detection, and STRIDE threat modelling.

  5. Principle 05 — Governability

    AI applications are developed and used with appropriate levels of human oversight and intervention capability. The sovereign system provides human-in-the-loop / human-on-the-loop gating at the architecture layer.

  6. Principle 06 — Bias Mitigation

    Unintended bias in AI applications is identified and mitigated. The sovereign system provides demographic matrix 10x6=60 user-scenarios audit pre-deployment, plus ongoing fairness monitoring.
